13Caveats and open items

The conclusions above rest on some estimates that Illumina does not disclose and on sources that disagree. These are the ones that could change an answer.

  1. Segment margins are our estimates. Illumina reports one segment. Consumables and instrument margins assume a ~35% instrument gross margin; segment EBIT is an illustrative allocation (section 2).
  2. The X/6000 dollar split is derived. It assumes high-throughput is about 69% of sequencing consumables, which an installed-base cross-check supports to within about 1%. Integer-rounded share disclosures limit precision: the 3.4x price ratio is known only to about ±10%.
  3. Clinical gigabase growth is inferred. Illumina publishes no clinical gigabase figure. Our 35–50% trailing range depends on the assumed clinical price decline.
  4. Which quarter holds the 53rd week. Bloomberg's period dates put it in Q3'26; management says Q4. We follow management. Its size (management ~0.5 point; mechanically up to ~2%) is unresolved.
  5. SomaLogic's reporting line. The earnings decks book it mostly in microarrays; the 10-Q puts it in service and other revenue.
  6. The clinical and research growth rates do not reconcile exactly to total sequencing consumables growth (they imply about 1 point more). Either the bases differ or price within each segment is worse than shown.
  7. The GRAIL open-offer expiry (~August 2027) is unverified. Illumina's terms page gives a start date and no end date; the 2027 date is inferred from the GRAIL separation documents.
  8. Sell-side models are stale. The newest full broker model in the folder is from 5 August; the stock is up about 36% since. Target changes after that date come from web aggregators, without the notes' reasoning.
  9. Peer multiples mix bases. Peer EV/EBITDA is trailing and GAAP-style; Illumina's is forward and adjusted. We lean on P/E and EV/sales.
  10. Expert calls are mostly July–August 2026, several from former employees who left one to twelve years ago, and six from competitors.
  11. Market share figures are weak. The only survey is from 2023; the rest are expert estimates with undefined bases.
  12. Net debt ranges from $0.33B to $1.36B depending on source and date; it moves enterprise value by about 1% and changes no conclusion.
  13. Unexplained moves. About a quarter of the stock's 7%-plus days since 2023 have no identifiable cause.

What would most improve the analysis: the Q3'26 deck (X shares, pull-through, clinical growth), the 35B price, the first 2027 guide, final CMS 2027 rates, the Q3 13F filings, and any Illumina disclosure of new-product revenue.
