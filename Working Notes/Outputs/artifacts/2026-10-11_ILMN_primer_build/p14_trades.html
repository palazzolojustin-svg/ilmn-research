11What the stock trades on

Illumina's share price has followed one variable more than any other: whether the company's revenue guidance was going up or down. Behind that sits a short list of drivers (clinical consumables growth, NovaSeq X placements, price per gigabase, competitor launches, China) plus, in 2026, a large re-rating driven by index inclusion and momentum. The stock fell 70% from April 2023 to April 2025 and has since risen about 300%.

11.5 The four phases

2023: collapse ($233 to $93). The stock traded on GRAIL's legal fight and on fears that the NovaSeq X would cannibalise consumables. Icahn's proxy fight, the CEO's resignation and two guidance cuts took it down 60%. A new CEO (Jacob Thaysen, September 2023), the end of the GRAIL fight and a December rally set a floor.

2024: a range ($101–155). After the GRAIL spin-off in June, Illumina became a plain sequencing company. Revenue guidance fell, but EPS guidance rose, a $1.5B buyback started and the August strategy day set the 2027 targets. The "clinical cliff" debate (volume up 40% but consumables revenue up only 5%) capped the stock.

Late 2024 to April 2025: the low ($148 to $70). A stack of outside shocks (China's sanctions and import ban, Roche's SBX reveal, tariffs, NIH funding cuts) and a cut 2025 guide produced a 52% drawdown. Executives bought shares near $81.

October 2025 to October 2026: recovery and re-rating ($99 to $278). Growth outside China turned positive in Q3'25 (+24.8% in a day). After a range in early 2026, two beat-and-raise quarters, J.P. Morgan's June upgrade, S&P 500 inclusion and a wave of $300+ targets took the stock up about 119% from 30 April while 2026 EPS estimates rose about 5%. The multiple went from about 25 to about 52 times earnings.

11.6 Positioning and sentiment

  • Sell-side. The average target ($222–234 across two data services) is 16–20% below the price. Brokers raised targets after the stock moved, not before. Even the bears chased: Barclays to $200 and BofA to $168, both still negative W.
  • Insiders. Director Keith Meister sold about 3.06M shares for about $591M between May and early September 2026, in discretionary sales clustered on up days. The CEO and CFO bought near $81 in May 2025 D.
  • Institutions. Institutions own about 89%. Fidelity (FMR) roughly tripled its position in Q2 W. Q3 holdings filings in mid-November will show who bought the September run.
  • Shorts. Short interest is moderate: 6.5% of float on one source, 10.8% on another with a different definition W. Short covering is a possible, not a dominant, part of the rally.
  • Technicals. On 5 October the price was 25% above its 50-day average with an overbought momentum reading; the next day it reversed from a record. These are consistent with a crowded, momentum-driven tape.
