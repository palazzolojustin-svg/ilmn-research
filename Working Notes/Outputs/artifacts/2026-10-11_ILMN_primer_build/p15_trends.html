12How each driver is trending

Most operating drivers are improving: placements, research stabilisation, the shrinking 6000 price drag, operating margin and guidance credibility. The drivers that are worsening are the ones that matter most for 2027–28: the growth rate of clinical consumables, gross margin, cash flow, and the competitive and reimbursement pressure on price. Valuation has moved further than any driver.

The table rates each driver as improving, stable or worsening based on its direction over the last two to four quarters, not its level. The last column says what to look for in the Q3'26 report on 29 October.

Reading the trend table

The improving drivers are mostly about 2026: placements, research stabilising, the 6000 drag fading and a higher margin. The worsening drivers are mostly about 2027–28: price per gigabase, gross margin, competition and reimbursement. That is why we expect another good quarter and a harder argument about next year. The single most informative number on 29 October is clinical consumables growth against a +12% comparison; the second is the X's share of high-throughput revenue. The X pull-through test comes later, with the FY26 figure in February 2027.
