# Expert quotes on competition: Illumina and its rivals

*Prepared 31 Oct 2026. Source: all 23 expert calls in `Working Notes/Sources/ILMN/expert_call/` (Jul–Oct 2026), each read in full. Quotes are verbatim from the transcripts, trimmed with "[...]". Timestamps are the transcripts' own. Tags in brackets mark the topic. An earlier, narrower file pulled only the share-loss quotes: `2026-10-14_expert_quotes_ilmn_share_loss.md`.*

## How to read these

**Experts agree on three things:**
- Illumina keeps its validated clinical work for years. Switching means revalidating, which experts put at $100–200K for a test and up to "$200 million of clinical trials" for an FDA product. They also put it at "four or five years" per lab.
- Erosion happens at the edges: new labs, research cores, start-ups, new projects, Europe and emerging markets.
- Price per Gb matters less than it used to, because sequencing is now only 5–10% of a test's total cost.

**Experts disagree most on:**
- **Roche AXELIOS.** Views run from "the main disruptor" (Natera) and "the one I'd be seriously concerned about" (ex-Illumina scientist) to "will just simply die out" (South Carolina core) and "put up or shut up" (Oxford Nanopore).
- **Share-loss numbers.** One expert says share fell from about 80% to 70%, with "at least 30%" more to come (ex-field scientist, SOPHiA). Another says the mid-60s, flattening near 50% (ex-product director). A third says Roche "could probably pick away 10%-20%" (Oxford Nanopore).
- **Element.** "Almost equal with NovaSeq X" (Natera) vs "not seeing as much adoption" (PacBio).

**Who is speaking matters:**
- **Competitor employees:** Roche (calls 12, 19), PacBio (18) and Oxford Nanopore (22).
- **Element partner:** the SOPHiA field scientist (7).
- **Ex-Illumina staff:** several left one to five years ago.
- **First-hand data:** the core-lab channel checks (2, 14, 16, 17) are the only calls with first-hand volume or pricing data.
- **Interviewer prompts:** these are flagged where the expert only agreed with the interviewer's framing.

**Transcription errors to know:**
- "Xcelris" and "Elio" are AXELIOS.
- One expert calls SBX "sequencing by synthesis"; it is sequencing by expansion.
- "Somagen" is Psomagen.

### 1. Associate Director, Oncology Lifecycle Management, Natera (22 Jul 2026)

- **[Roche]** "one main thing that Illumina is not lacking, but they will feel the competition, is within AXELIOS, the Roche technology [...] I think that would be the main disruptor. I think they will start really, really feeling it very soon with Roche." (00:02:19)
- **[Ultima / segment exposure]** "For many things in oncology, fetal health, women's health, actually, you don't need more than that, and it costs $80-$110. Definitely, we think opportunities of smaller labs, local labs, or some clinical applications like transplant sequencing [...] Definitely, they will start losing some market share." (00:04:25)
- **[Ultima]** "The cost per million reads is somewhere below $0.40, which is tremendous. That's why you have, actually, the somewhere $50 genome." (00:04:25)
- **[Ultima]** "The UG200 is actually pretty expensive. It's comparable with Illumina" (00:04:25)
- **[Switching costs]** "in those big, big companies, Illumina is locked because nobody wants, actually, for an established assay to use something different compared to what you have launched in the market. Also, FDA is not going to love it." (00:07:17)
- **[Segment exposure]** "For new applications, for a diverse portfolio, for new start-ups, for new labs, or for local labs, the barrier to entry [...] is going to be way lower, basically, to switch from an Illumina to something else." (00:07:17)
- **[Element pricing]** "The machine is way cheaper. It's $700,000, compared to more than $1 million that is, basically, the NovaSeq X." (00:10:30)

- **[Roche]** "They have the name, but people, they're not going to mess with their diagnostics that much in order to experiment. [...] You need to prove it first." (00:35:48)
- **[Share]** "Of course, they're going to lose market share, but [...] it will be slow. They will lose, but how much, it's not going to be in six months, they will lose 30%. It will happen gradually and gradually, so they will have time to catch up." (00:47:09)
- **[Switching costs]** "As of now, for me, the barriers to bypass, to change Illumina, are very, very high" (00:47:09)
