# Should the Q4'25 placement surge already be lifting clinical revenue?

*2026-10-30. Sources: ILMN calls Q4'24 to Q3'26 and decks; expert calls; broker notes from May to Sep 2026. Clinical dollars come from the model in `2026-10-30_ILMN_clinical_2027_implied_bridge.py`.*

## Answer

On the CEO's timeline, yes, and it hasn't shown up. NovaSeq X placements jumped to more than 100 in Q4'25 and stayed high (83, more than 95, more than 80). In Q1'26 the CEO said new boxes "generate revenue... approximately 6 months after they install" and expressed "optimism" for H2'26.

Instead, ex-China clinical growth slowed from +20% to +15% and then +14%. The dollar gains also shrank:
- Clinical revenue rose about $15M quarter over quarter in both Q2 and Q3 2026, against about $31M and $16M a year earlier.
- The year-over-year dollar gain fell from about $78M in Q1'26 to about $61M in Q3'26.

The CFO set expectations differently in July. He said clinical customers take "at least 6 to 9 months to reach *normalized* pull-through," and "most of that benefit will come in 2027" (Q2'26 call). On that framing the benefit is not late yet, and the Q4 guide gives the first sign of it (see below).

Three points stand out:
- **The 2025 pattern fits the lag.** About 150 boxes placed in Q4'24 to Q1'25 were followed by clinical growth accelerating to +12% and then +20% in H2'25. The CEO had forecast this in mid-2025.
- **The 2026 surge has not repeated it yet.** About 180 boxes placed in Q4'25 to Q1'26 have not produced the same lift.
- **Scale check.** About 60% of placements are clinical. Assume half of those are new capacity rather than 6000 replacements (JPM's reading of the Q2 call). At the $1.35M-per-year pull-through Illumina guides for an X, the Q4'25 and Q1'26 clinical boxes should add about $18M a quarter once normalized, roughly 4 points of clinical growth. If all of them were new capacity, the figure would be about $37M, or roughly 8 points. Neither shows in Q2 or Q3.

## Why it may not have shown up yet

Each driver below is what management, experts or brokers say. None is quantified by the company.

1. **The boxes are capacity for tests that aren't live yet.** Management says clinical customers are "building out their installed base... for the volume that they can see in their funnel" (Q2'26). Multi-unit orders are tied to "start of new clinical trials." On-market assays stay on the 6000 while "adding X capacity for new tests" (Q3'26). Clinical volume on the X stalled at 78% in Q2 and Q3. A box waiting for a new test to launch generates little revenue until that test launches. Experts put the move of a regulated assay at 12–18 months (Thaysen, Q3'24) and up to about 1.5–2 years at the largest labs.
2. **Price is still falling for the customers who do convert.** There are volume discounts on 5–10-unit orders (Q1'26). The X transition pricing headwind is "lower in 2027 than 2026," so it is still a drag this year. Per-box X pull-through fell from about $1.36M in Q4'25 to about $1.2M in H1'26 (internal estimate, Oct 11 analysis). Barclays reads this as price per gigabase pressure; management reads it as ramp. The data cannot separate the two.
3. **Regions offset the US.** US/Canada clinical grew more than 20% in Q2. Management said US clinical was "well above 14%" in Q3. Europe, the Middle East and Latin America slowed. So the placement effect may be visible in the US and masked overall.
4. **Q4'25 and Q1'26 were flattered.** Q4'25 had "some year-end budget purchasing." Analysts asked about stocking in both Q2 and Q3 and got non-answers.

## The one sign it may be starting

The Q4'26 guide implies ex-China consumables up 5–6% against a +11% comparison, and clinical up about 12–13% against +20%. On a two-year stack that is the strongest quarter in the series: about 33 for clinical, against 25–26 in Q2–Q3. The CFO said the Q4 consumables raise is "entirely from the strong placements and the customer demand." Q4 clinical dollars are still flat quarter over quarter, but Q4 has been flat to down seasonally for clinical (2024: −6%; 2025: +1%).

## What to watch

- Q4'26 clinical. A print at or above +13% (stack 33 or more) supports the lag story; +10% or less undercuts it.
- Whether clinical X share moves from 78% toward the guided 80–85%. Movement means on-market assays are converting, with the price drag that comes with it.
- Any disclosure of new-capacity versus replacement placements. Thaysen declined to give one when UBS asked on the Q3 call.

## Contradictions

- The CEO's Q1'26 "approximately 6 months" and "drive immediately growth on the top line" sit awkwardly beside the CFO's Q2'26 "at least 6 to 9 months to normalized... most in 2027."
- On the share of clinical placements that are new capacity, the CEO said "mostly incremental" (Q1'26); JPM's reading of the Q2 call was "as much as half."
