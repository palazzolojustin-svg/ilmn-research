# Quotes: are NIPT and oncology therapy selection maturing? (2026-11-03)

*Two agent sweeps covered every expert call, broker note, and Roche, GeneDx and 10x transcript, plus every Illumina call, conference and deck from 2023 to Q3'26. Each quote below was re-checked against its source line. Paths are under `Working Notes/Sources/`.*

## Bottom line

- **Experts support the maturity story.** Two experts say it directly:
  - A Roche genomics lead calls CGP "quite saturated."
  - A former Illumina associate director says NIPT is "the nearest clinical use case that is getting to saturation."
- **Illumina management never says either market is mature.** It says:
  - Therapy selection is still the biggest dollar pool for "at least a year or 2," while MRD and early detection grow faster in percentage terms.
  - NIPT is "still growing very nicely."
- **Roche's own market model is the hardest number.** It has therapy selection growing 9%, against 32–35% for monitoring and early detection.

## 1. NIPT is near saturation

1. **Former Associate Director, Illumina (expert call, 6 Aug 2026)**: "The nearest clinical use case that is getting to saturation is non-invasive prenatal testing for pregnancy. Even that, I would say, I think the last few numbers, if I remember correctly, were 70%-80%. There's one pregnancy out of five out there that's not getting the sequencing test…"
   - Source: `ILMN/expert_call/Expert_2026-08-06_Former_Associate_Director_…Multi-Omics_and_Clinical_Genomics_Are.md`, L215–217.
   - Caveat: the figure is from memory. In the same answer he says cancer penetration is "really, really low."
2. **Senior Scientist, Abbott (expert call, 29 Jul 2026)**: "Obviously, there is screening for pregnant people. That has been a common or not extremely growing area, but a constant one for Illumina… I'm guessing maybe 10%-20% of the market is neonatal screening, pregnancy screening."
   - Source: `Expert_2026-07-29_Senior_Scientist_at_Abbott…`, L219.
   - Caveat: hedged, and the speaker has no direct NIPT role.
3. **Thaysen, CEO (Wolfe conference, 19 Nov 2025)**. Asked about customers whose assay is "good enough as it is without requiring more sequencing," he named NIPT: "you could imagine maybe an NIPT assay where customers are saying, look, I do want to move to the X because… I can get better pricing on the X… most of them have already moved…"
   - Source: `ILMN/conference/ILMN_2025-11-19_7th_Annual_Wolfe_Research_Healthcare_Conference.md`, L354–360.
   - Use: management's own example of a test with fixed sequencing depth. NIPT customers take the X for price, not for more data.

## 2. Therapy selection (CGP) is saturated or the slower pool

4. **Precision Medicine and Genomics Lead, Roche (expert call, 29 Jul 2026)**: "I think the CGP, there is a small amount of growth, but I think it's quite saturated. There's a lot of companies in that space… I think the markets that have CGP deployed, I think that will be just a very small increase year-on-year. The liquid biopsy part of the CGP would be a bigger increase."
   - Source: `Expert_2026-07-29_Precision_Medicine_and_Genomics_Lead_at_Roche…`, L331–333.
   - The cleanest quote. Caveat: the speaker is UK-focused and works at Roche.
5. **Roche Diagnostics Investor Day (12 May 2026)**: "Therapy selection for Oncology is a CHF 17 billion opportunity, growing at 9%." In the same passage, early detection grows at 35% and disease monitoring (MRD) at 32%.
   - Source: `ROCHE/transcript/ROCHE_2026-05-12_Investor_Day_Diagnostics_AXELIOS.md`, L431–435.
   - Caveat: the 9% is dollars across the whole segment, including companion diagnostics and pathology.
6. **Thaysen, CEO (TD Cowen, 3 Mar 2026)**: "most of the business today is still in therapy selection, we're now shifting ourselves into the new type of detections like minimal residual disease and… early detection of cancers. So over the next few years, it will still at least a year or 2, it will still be the therapy selection that will be the main part of the business and thereby also dollar-wise the main growth, but percentage growth, the MRD and early detection is definitely starting to kick in."
   - Source: `ILMN/conference/ILMN_2026-03-03_TD_Cowen_46th_Annual_Health_Care_Conference.md`, L190.
7. **Thaysen, CEO (Q2'26 call, 30 Jul 2026)**: "in oncology, we are seeing a shift towards now MRD starting to drive momentum. But still from a dollar perspective, the therapy selection is still the bigger one."
   - Source: `ILMN/earnings_call/ILMN_2026Q2_Earnings_Call.md`, L360.
8. **Former Associate Director, Illumina (6 Aug 2026)**: "The comprehensive genomic profiling is also a great use case, but you do it once."
   - Source: same file as #1, L223.
   - Use: a one-time test grows only with new diagnoses, unlike MRD, which is repeated.

## 3. Counter-quotes a judge may raise

- **Thaysen (JPM, 13 Jan 2026)**: "we're still in the early stages of clinical adoption with less than 20% of patients receiving a relevant NGS test across disease modalities."
  - Source: `ILMN/conference/ILMN_2026-01-13_44th…`, L108.
  - The deck that day put clinical exposure at ~51% oncology, ~25% genetic disease and ~21% reproductive health.
- **Thaysen (Q2'26 call)**: "The rare diseases and screening -- NIPT screening and so on is still growing very nicely" (L362).
- **Former Associate Director, Illumina (6 Aug 2026)**: clinical genomics is "not even going to be close to near saturation for a while" (L215). This is the same expert as #1.
- **Foundation Medicine CEO (Roche Investor Day)**: "CGP adoption is going up" and "we expect the market to essentially double over the next 5 years" (L1187–1189).
- **Dhingra, CFO (Q3'26 call, 29 Oct 2026)**: blames the clinical slowdown on conversion timing ("pace of conversion slow in recent quarters"), not on demand (L79). Clinical grew 14% ex-China, against 20% in Q4'25.

## 4. MRD is small today and years from offsetting the slowdown

No expert says "MRD is X years from being meaningful." The closest quotes:

- **Associate Director, Oncology Lifecycle Management, Natera (expert call, 22 Jul 2026)**:
  - "MRD is smaller, but MRD is expanding by a lot, and in five years from now, it's going to be equal with everything" (L189).
  - "many physicians, they're using MRD, but they don't know what to do with it. You see a positive result, 'What am I supposed to do with it?' This is what I face in Natera every day" (L183).
  - Source: `Expert_2026-07-22_Associate_Director_at_Natera_Inc._…`.
  - Caveat: he is bullish on MRD, with $5B growing to $30B by 2030. The quote helps only on timing: MRD needs about five years to catch up.
- **Former Senior Manager, Global Market Development, Illumina (Tegus, undated)**:
  - "I've always been a bit skeptical about MRD being very popular across the world… the reimbursement landscape outside of the U.S. is going to be very, very challenging" (L363).
  - "there are some clinical trials that have concluded that are looking at deescalation based on MRD, but those did not pan out… I know it's very popular now. I'm still very skeptical about it" (L367).
  - Source: `Expert_Tegus_Former_Senior_Manager_Global_Market_Development_at_Illumina_Inc_Illumina_Inc.md`.
- **Dhingra, CFO (Evercore, 3 Dec 2024)**: "MRD is yet to play out, right? If you take our next three year view, we're anticipating some of the MRD volumes will become accretive, at least during that strategy period" (L192).
  - This is management, not an expert.
- **Our sizing** (`2026-10-17_mrd_market_size_vs_natera.md`): all MRD is roughly 1–3% of Illumina's high-throughput Gb. Growing about 50% a year, it adds only about 0.5–1.5 points to total Gb growth.

## Not found

- No expert says therapy selection will "moderate in a year or two." That wording comes from Thaysen's TD Cowen line (#6), and he does not say "moderate."
- No broker says NIPT is saturated. JPM flags NIPT competition and pricing as risks only.
