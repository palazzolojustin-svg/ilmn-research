# How Big Were Illumina's Revenue Beats? Q4 2024 to Q3 2026

*Prepared 2026-10-30. Data and caveats: `Outputs/datasets/2026-10-30_ILMN_beat_vs_guide_and_street.csv` and its README. Chart: `Outputs/artifacts/2026-10-30_ILMN_beat_vs_guide_and_street.png`.*

## Answer

Illumina has beaten every quarter for eight straight quarters, against both its own guide and the Street. The Q3 2026 beat was the smallest against guidance in two years:

- **Against guidance:** Rest-of-World (RoW) organic growth was 4.7% against a guide of about 4.5%, a beat of +20 bps. Q2 2026 had beaten by +310 bps.
- **Against the Street:** +122 bps (excluding the tariff-refund credit), down from +268 bps in Q2.

In other words, management's Q3 guide had already priced in the slowdown. The big beats in Q4 2025 and Q2 2026 did not repeat.

## Caveats

- The guidance metric changed twice, so 2025 and 2026 are not fully comparable:
  - total Core cc became ex-China cc in Q2 2025;
  - ex-China cc became RoW organic in Q1 2026, which also excludes SomaLogic.
- Including the $37M tariff-refund credit, Q3'26 revenue was $1,129M, a miss of about 22 bps against the Street.
