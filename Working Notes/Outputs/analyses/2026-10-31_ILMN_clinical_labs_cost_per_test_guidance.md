# Are other large clinical labs guiding to lower sequencing cost per test? (2026-10-31)

*Question: besides GeneDx (CFO, Q2'26: "step improvements in 2027" in cost per test as "manufacturers advance their technology"), do any publicly traded clinical labs that buy from Illumina guide to lower sequencing or reagent cost per test? Method: three agents (our corpus, plus two web sweeps of Q1–Q3 2026 calls and filings). Quotes marked "verified" were re-checked against the transcript. All call transcripts are third-party copies (Motley Fool, stockanalysis.com, MarketBeat), not company replays.*

## Bottom line

Yes, but the evidence is narrower than "labs expect Illumina to cut prices." Only three companies give explicit forward guidance on falling cost per test (Guardant, NeoGenomics, GeneDx). Two more say it vaguely (Myriad, Caris). One, Caris, says it is weighing the two non-Illumina suppliers to cut costs. In most cases the saving comes from moving onto Illumina's own NovaSeq X: a lower price per Gb for Illumina, not lost share. Several labs also say they will put the savings into "deeper, not cheaper" tests rather than bank them.

## Explicit forward guidance (strongest)

**1. Guardant Health (GH): Q2'26 call, 30 Jul 2026, CFO Michael Bell (verified).**
- Shield cost per test: "Shield cost per test will reduce by roughly 15% from the current level of approximately $410." The new FDA-approved "higher-throughput, lower-COGS Shield workflow" goes live in August 2026.
- Path to $200: "further scale benefits and major automation initiatives are expected to drive the next wave of reductions… and support our $200 cost per test target in 2028." In Q1'26 the $200 target was expected "to implement in 2027", so it has slipped a year.
- Guardant360 Liquid: "we completed the Guardant360 Liquid transition to NovaSeq X in May, reducing cost per test by approximately $200."
- Gross margin guide: "incorporates lower testing costs for Guardant360 Liquid and Shield in the second half".
- How to read it: the Shield cut is workflow, automation and scale, not only reagent price. The Guardant360 Liquid saving is a move onto NovaSeq X. No non-Illumina platform is named.

**2. NeoGenomics (NEO): Q3'26 call, 28 Oct 2026, CFO Abhishek Jain (verified).**
- Current impact: "We have not yet started to see the impact of the NovaSeq X transition."
- Timing: "we should start to see the impact of the NovaSeq X transition in the second half of 2027… 2028 would become the year when we will start to see a more benefit."
- Scope: heme NGS, its largest NGS revenue line, moves to NovaSeq X "in the first half of 2027" (President and COO Warren Stone). Q3 capex included "over $5 million" on new sequencers and servers.
- How to read it: lower cost per test from moving onto Illumina's X.

**3. GeneDx (WGS): Q2'26 call, 3 Aug 2026 (CFO; previously compiled).** "Step improvements in 2027" in cost per test. A genome costs "nearly twice as much to produce than exome… almost entirely higher reagent costs." This one is closest to directly assuming supplier price-per-Gb deflation.

## Vague forward statements

**4. Myriad (MYGN): Q2'26 call, 30 Jul 2026, CFO Ben Wheeler.** "Over time, we expect continued cost per test improvements to help offset modest reimbursement pressures." No platform, number or timing.

**5. Adaptive (ADPT): Q1/Q2'26 calls, CFO Kyle Piskel.**
- Q2: margin up on "lower assay costs following our NovaSeq X transition". MRD sequencing gross margin was 72% vs 64% a year earlier.
- Q1: "75%… the North Star". The saving has mostly happened already, and there is no 2027 view.

**6. Tempus (TEM): Q4'25 call, 24 Feb 2026, CEO Eric Lefkofsky.** "As costs of sequencing come down," Tempus will reassess "how much you… increase the size of panels". That points to more data per test, not lower cost per test.

## Looking at non-Illumina suppliers to cut cost

**7. Caris (CAI): Q2'26 call, 5 Aug 2026 (verified).**
- President David Spetzler: "There are two competitors out there now against the longstanding sequencing supplier… They create the opportunity to increase our capacity and decrease our cost of goods simultaneously."
- CFO Luke Power: "We're also looking at additional sequencing capabilities, not just what our current existing supplier is."
- CFO Luke Power, same call: "It's always deeper, not cheaper for us… The potential is there for future years to get our COGS way down. That's not where we're focused on right now." He guides H2 gross margin to about the same level.
- How to read it: this is the only big clinical lab on record weighing alternatives to Illumina for cost. It names no vendor or timeline, and Caris is still adding NovaSeq Xs.

## Reported past declines, or no decline (context)

- **BillionToOne (BLLN):** product-level cost per test fell, via "successful negotiations with suppliers" and "higher-throughput sequencing" (Q2'26 10-Q). But CEO Oguzhan Atay: "we expect overall COGS per test to rise gradually over time" as oncology mix grows.
- **GRAIL:** cost of revenue fell as a share of revenue on scale and lower reprocessing. Royalties to Illumina resume on 24 Dec 2026, which offsets that.
- **Natera:** unit costs "largely holding steady" (Q1'26). Our 10-Q math puts cost per test up about 9% y/y in Q2'26. No sequencing-price commentary.
- **Exact Sciences:** acquired by Abbott in Mar 2026, so no longer files separately.
- **Fulgent, Veracyte:** no sequencing-cost commentary.

## What this means for the pitch

- **Supports the short:**
  - Four large Illumina customers (Guardant, NeoGenomics, GeneDx, Adaptive) are cutting cost per test, mostly by moving volume to NovaSeq X, where Illumina earns less per Gb.
  - NeoGenomics' heme move in H1 2027 is more conversion headwind still to come.
  - GeneDx and Caris openly want lower costs from suppliers.
  - Caris is weighing the two challengers, almost certainly Ultima and Element, though it doesn't name them.
- **Cuts against the short:**
  - Nobody has announced moving production to a non-Illumina platform.
  - Most savings go to Illumina's own X, keeping the volume with Illumina.
  - Several labs say they will reinvest in larger panels ("deeper, not cheaper"), which adds Gb.
  - Natera, the largest customer, shows no unit-cost decline.
- **The fair framing:** labs are guiding to lower cost per test mainly by moving onto the NovaSeq X. That confirms the conversion and price-per-Gb pressure is still running into 2027 (NeoGenomics heme, Guardant Shield, GeneDx). Only Caris points at leaving Illumina.

## Gaps and timing

Q3'26 results from Guardant (5 Nov), Caris (4 Nov), Myriad (4 Nov), Tempus, GeneDx and Adaptive are not out yet. All land before the 14 Nov pitch. Check each for cost-per-test, supplier or platform comments, and for any named Ultima, Element or Roche use.
