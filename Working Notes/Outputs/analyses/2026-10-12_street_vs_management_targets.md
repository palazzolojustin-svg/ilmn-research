# Where the Street sits below management's targets, and broker skepticism of those targets

*Prepared 12 Oct 2026. Sources: Bloomberg consensus (10 Oct 2026); research notes from Barclays, J.P. Morgan, Freedom, UBS, Seeking Alpha and Bloomberg Intelligence; all 2024–26 call and conference Q&As. Quotes are verbatim. Growth rates are our arithmetic on each source's own numbers.*

## Management's targets

Set in Aug 2024 and restated through 2026:
- **Revenue:** high-single-digit (HSD) organic growth ex-China in 2027.
- **Operating margin:** ~500bps of expansion to ~26% in 2027. Since SomaLogic this has become "as close… as possible" and "within 50 basis points" (Q4'25 call; TD Cowen, Mar 2026).
- **EPS:** double-digit to teens growth. Dhingra: "the teens go from 13% to 19%."
- **New products:** multiomics and new products add 1–2 points of growth in 2027.

## 1. Where the Street is below

| Target (2027) | Bloomberg consensus | Barclays (31 Jul) | Freedom (5 Aug) | UBS (5 Feb, stale) | J.P. Morgan (31 Jul) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| HSD organic revenue growth ex-China | $4,918M, +6.3% reported; ~+7% ex-China (our calc) | $4,862M, +5.5% | $4,900M, +5.7% | $4,720M, +4.1% | ~$5,047M, +9.1% |
| ~26% op. margin (~25.5% with SomaLogic) | 25.2% (26.5% only in 2028) | 24.3% EBIT | n/a | n/a | n/a |
| Double-digit to teens EPS growth | $6.11, +14% | $5.73, +7% | $5.98, +11% | $5.74, +13% | $6.16, +15% |

**Revenue.**
- Consensus sits at the bottom edge of HSD, roughly +7% ex-China.
- Barclays, Freedom and UBS model mid-single digits. Only J.P. Morgan models HSD.
- *Our calc for ex-China:* consensus Greater China is $211M in 2026 and $189M in 2027.

**Margin.** Consensus is ~80bps below 26% for 2027 and reaches the target a year late, in 2028. Barclays is ~170bps below.

**EPS.** Consensus is inside the range. Barclays is below double digits, and Freedom is at the low end.

**Multiomics +1–2 points.** No published model sizes it. Barclays and Seeking Alpha say it cannot be verified.

**FY26 itself.** The Street is not below. Consensus FY26 revenue is $4,625M against a $4.60–4.64B guide, and EPS $5.36 against $5.30–5.40. Q3 EPS consensus of $1.36 is at the top half of the $1.33–1.38 guide. The doubt is about the 2H margin ramp, not the totals.

## 2. Broker skepticism in written research

**Barclays, Luke Sergott: "If You Seed it, The Consumable Revs Will Come...Right?" (30 Apr 2026; Underweight, $122)**
> "On the guide, mgmt. passed through the beat and now points to the high end of the range of the ROW Organic guide of 2-4%. The 2H margin ramp is embeds a sizable 2Q to 3Q stepup as 3Q tends to be the higher margin Q given the seasonal mix dynamics. So while we are relatively comfortable on the topline guide, the margin guide is not a layup by any means. The business has decent momentum and we can see shares moving higher on continued outperformance. However, we remain UW because we are not sold on the ability to deliver HSD growth in '27."

**Barclays, Luke Sergott: "Once a Prom King Always a Prom King" (31 Jul 2026; Underweight, $160)**
> "The big question is on the durability of HSD/DD growth. We think this is a MSD business and valuation is most expensive in core Tools."

> "The multiomic playbook is beginning to bear fruit and mgmt feels on track to deliver 1-2% TW to growth next year. This is above our expectations and without getting more clarity/disclosure on the business, we have no way to confirm these numbers. But with the momentum in business and exit rate, investors are assuming the +HSD% top line is in the bag and new buyers of the stock have to believe that they can deliver this consistently and that we are likely to see them touch DD.
>
> This scenario assumes clinical continues to crush and the research market stabilizes. The simple math is 60% of the biz grows >20% and if the research market stabilizes, we get DD. Even we can do this math without Excel. But this ignores the competitive landscape that will require continued price concessions, bundling, and favorable terms to fend off more material share losses. As such, we do not envision the market stabilizing anytime soon."

The upside case in the same note: "Upside to numbers assumes multiples expansion on Core ILMN business should mgmt prove to deliver on HSD LT growth framework."

**J.P. Morgan, Casey Woodring: "4Q25 Earnings Recap, With 2026 Guidance" (6 Feb 2026; Neutral, $120)**
> "…the clinical transition continues to see encouraging momentum, but we acknowledge the pushback on the 2H margin ramp implied by the guide."

**UBS, Dan Leonard: "Encouraging Clinical Signal…" (5 Feb 2026; Neutral, $135)**
> "Our Neutral rating on Illumina (ILMN) balances a muted sales growth outlook (lesser than peers') with outsized margin expansion opportunity."

> "…Illumina's +1-3% organic sales growth assumption for 2026 in aggregate compares unfavorably to peers', even as it is in line with our forecast."

**Seeking Alpha, Stephen Ayers (independent): "Illumina: Priced For Perfection It Hasn't Earned Yet" (8 Sep 2026; Hold, $188)**
> "Q1 and Q2 operating margins were 21.9% and 22.5%. Q3 is guided to 24%. To hit the full year of 23.4% to 23.6%, Q4 has to be >25%."

> "Margin expansion is partly due to lumpy government incentives, not solely operational improvements…"

> "The major unsolved mystery is management's claim of "1 to 2 points of growth next year" from new products. To my knowledge, this has not yet been sized by product but is clearly the bridge towards the outperformance ILMN now appears valued for."

**Bloomberg Intelligence, Zandi/Palmer (31 Jul 2026)**
> "…Illumina's 4Q will require a more marked step-up to reach the full-year goals, as 3Q guidance is a touch softer than consensus."

## 4. The other side

- **J.P. Morgan (31 Jul 2026)** calls HSD growth and mid-to-high-teens EPS growth "highly achievable."
- **Freedom (10 Feb 2026)** called the 2027 goals "realistic."
- **Bloomberg Intelligence (31 Jul 2026)** said the 2027 goal "appears increasingly visible."
- **Analysts have recently pushed the other way.** Puneet Souda (Leerink, Q2'26 call) asked "why should we not contemplate something higher than a high single-digit revenue growth for 2027." Thaysen did not raise the target.

## Caveats

- **Consensus mixes bases.** It includes China and is reported growth, while the target is organic and ex-China. Our ex-China conversion uses consensus Greater China lines.
- **Some broker figures are stale.** UBS is from February, before two guidance raises. J.P. Morgan's 2027 revenue comes from its July model update.
- **Few analysts model the components.** Only Barclays gives a 2027 margin. No broker models the multiomics contribution separately.
