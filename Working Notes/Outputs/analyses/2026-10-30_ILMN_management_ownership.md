## Board and group

- **Keith Meister (Corvex), director:** 4,202,657 shares, 2.8%, mostly Corvex funds plus a 368,744-share equity swap. He sold about 3.06M shares between May and Sep 2026, so he now holds roughly 1.1M (~0.7%). That is our estimate.

## Caveats

- Thaysen's May 2025 Form 4 showed 83,390 shares after his purchase, against 40,313 in the proxy.
- The gap is most likely unvested RSUs and PSUs, which Form 4 counts and the proxy excludes. It could also be tax-withholding dispositions. It does not show up as an open-market sale.
- Unvested equity and annual grants are much larger than these holdings. Pay is mostly in stock, so total economic exposure to the share price is higher than shown here.
