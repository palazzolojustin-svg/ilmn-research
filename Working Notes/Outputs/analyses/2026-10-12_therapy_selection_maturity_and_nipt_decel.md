# Is therapy selection maturing? How fast is NIPT decelerating?

Prepared 12 Oct 2026. Two research agents, folder plus web. D = disclosed, C = our calculation, S = secondary or unverified.

## Correction to the 12 Oct note

"Experts expect CGP growth to slow to 15–25% by 2028" was wrong attribution. The +25–35% (2026) → +20–30% (2027) → +15–25% (2028) path is a research agent's forecast (ledger E099), not an expert's. The expert view (E096) is only that CGP is "quite saturated".

**Penetration (where the "maturing" case lives).**
- **Illumina:** CGP adoption below 20% (JPM deck, Jan 2026; management).
- **Advanced cancer overall:** a JAMA Netw Open review of ~26K patients found ~35% got CGP, up from 32% to 39% (S).
- **Lung:** an NSCLC JCO PO study (2024) found CGP in 20% and single-gene-only testing in 80% (S). NSCLC any-NGS rose from 57% to 80% (2020–22, S), and ~90% now get some biomarker test (ISPOR 2026, S).
- **Foundation Medicine's CEO:** a community network treating ~20% of US cancer patients profiles only ~50% of metastatic patients (Roche DX Day; management).
- **Read:** lung is near saturation; other solid tumours are not.

**Other maturity signals (inference, not prints).**
- Share shifting from Foundation Medicine to Tempus and Caris (unverified opinion piece; Roche write-downs not verified). Roche Diagnostics grew +3% in H1'26.
- NeoGenomics growth is price and mix.
- Roche sizes the market at CHF 17B growing ~9%.

**Counter-evidence.**
- Tempus oncology accelerated for five quarters.
- Guardant raised its FY26 oncology volume guide to ~+50% from ~35%.
- New content per test: Guardant360 Liquid CDx adds epigenomic content; Caris WES/WTS; TSO Comprehensive reimbursement.

**Verdict.** CGP test growth is healthy (+18–31%); 2024 was the one air pocket. Maturity is a penetration and share story, plus lung saturation, not visible in volumes. The Illumina risk here is revenue per gigabase (X migration savings, cost-per-test trends), not CGP demand.

**Which lines are clean therapy-selection proxies (not MRD-skewed)?**
- **Clean:**
  - **Caris cases** (MI Profile tissue plus Assure blood profiling): +15% to +22%, mild deceleration.
  - **Guardant360 Liquid alone:** ~+30%, steady.
  - **Tempus oncology:** includes xM MRD, but MRD is only ~9K tests (~5% of oncology). Stripping it out leaves roughly +25% [our rough estimate].
- **Not clean:**
  - **Guardant total oncology** includes Reveal (MRD, >+100%).
  - **Natera oncology** is mostly Signatera MRD.
  - **NeoGenomics total clinical volume** is dominated by non-NGS tests (IHC, FISH, flow cytometry), so its +2% is not a CGP read. Its NGS revenue (+26%) is the better proxy, though it mixes price and volume.
- **Read:** clean therapy-selection volume is about +18–30%, roughly steady, with Caris the mild decelerator.

Tempus therapy-selection volume has run ~+20–24% for six quarters. It peaked around +23–24% in mid-to-late 2025 and eased to ~+20–21.5% in 2026, while the headline accelerated to +31% because of MRD.

**Read.**
- **Caris** decelerated from +31% to +15–18%.
- **Guardant360 Liquid** rose from >20% to ~30%+.
- **Tempus ex-MRD** is ~+20–22%, not the ~+25% estimated earlier. MRD adds ~8–10 points to Tempus's headline growth.
- **Clean therapy-selection growth** is therefore ~+18–30%: decelerating at Caris, accelerating at Guardant360, ~20% at Tempus.
- **Gaps:** Guardant discloses no Liquid or Reveal counts; Tempus Q4'25 oncology volume is not disclosed.

**Update (17 Oct 2026, web sweep of the remaining labs).**
- **Foundation Medicine:** no CGP volume disclosed. Roche's Molecular Lab segment, which includes Foundation, grew +3% CER in H1 2026, and Roche attributes that growth to infectious disease and sequencing (Roche HY26 report).
- **Medicare fee-for-service, 2024:** Foundation's tissue test services fell 11% and its liquid test was flat (CMS data, scrape findings).
- **Don't break out CGP:** Labcorp, Quest, Abbott/Exact (OncoExTra), Myriad (Precise Tumor), Natera (Altera) and Personalis (ImmunoID NeXT).
- **BillionToOne oncology revenue:** +176% (Q2 2026), but off a $13.7M base and including Response.
- **Read:** the fast growers (+18–30%) appear to be partly taking share from the largest incumbent. Market-wide therapy-selection test growth is probably lower, roughly low-to-high teens [our inference].

## 2. NIPT: deceleration path

Natera non-oncology tests (proxy for women's health; includes organ health and carrier tests; C from releases):

| Period | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | Q4'25 | Q1'26 | Q2'26 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| y/y | ~+25% | +15% | +18% | +7% | +7.5% | +9.8% | +12.5% |

- **Natera management:** women's health grew "high-single-digit" seasonally adjusted in Q2'26. A secondary summary says Natera guided mid-single-digit women's health volume growth for 2026 (unverified).
- **BillionToOne:** +51% (2025, 610K tests) → +47% (Q4'25) → +35% (Q2'26). Decelerating, share-driven; ASP +21%.
- **Myriad prenatal:** FY25 −4%; −8% / −3% / −6% (Q2–Q4'25); −9% in Q2'26. Losing share.
- **Market:** US births are 3.61M (2025), −1% y/y (CDC). Penetration is uncertain: ~30% of average-risk pregnancies in 2019–20 against ~70–80% of all pregnancies now (expert). So underlying market growth is about low to mid single digits; anything faster is share gain or add-on screens (Fetal Focus near 200K annualised orders, enhanced Panorama, UNITY).
- **Re-acceleration levers:** ACMG Dec 2025 first-line recommendation for all pregnancies, Medicaid coverage, single-gene and microdeletion add-ons (microdeletion depth ~6 Gb vs 1–2 Gb per standard test).

**Verdict.** NIPT unit growth fell from the 20s to the high single digits and has bottomed around the low teens at the leaders, helped by share gains and add-ons. The underlying market is mature. For Illumina it is ~21% of clinical consumables but a small share of gigabases, and the most exposed pool to cheaper sequencers.
