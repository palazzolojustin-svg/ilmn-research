# Is CGP maturing? What the big CGP labs' own growth rates show (2022 – Q3 2026)

*2026-10-31. CGP (comprehensive genomic profiling) is the large tumor-gene panel used to choose a cancer drug. It is the core "therapy selection" test and a large share of Illumina's clinical consumables. This note tests the claim that CGP is maturing against the reported volumes of the largest US CGP labs, which are all Illumina customers. The headline numbers marked "verified" were checked against the SEC 8-K press releases. Call quotes come from secondary transcripts (MarketBeat, Motley Fool, Earnings Whispers) and should be checked before use on a slide.*

## Bottom line

The lab data only partly supports "CGP is maturing." The evidence splits by sample type:

- **Tissue CGP is slowing.** Caris's tissue test (MI Profile) has grown about 12–13% a year since mid-2025, down from the 30%-plus growth of Caris's total volume in 2023. Roche sizes the whole therapy-selection market at 9% annual growth to 2030, against 32% for MRD (tests for cancer left after treatment) and 35% for multi-cancer early detection.
- **Liquid CGP and the labs gaining share are accelerating.** Tempus's oncology volume growth rose every quarter, from +20% (Q1'25) to +31% (Q2'26). Guardant's core liquid test, Guardant360 Liquid, is growing about 30% or more and accelerating. NeoGenomics' NGS volume is +14–17%.

For the short pitch, the defensible version is narrower than "CGP is maturing." Two claims hold up:

1. Tissue CGP, the larger and older part, is growing in the low teens.
2. The fastest-growing labs are taking share rather than expanding the market. Guardant's CEO says new growth is "mostly depth" in existing physicians rather than new ordering physicians.

The other bearish point is that labs' revenue is growing mostly on price, not volume (Caris tissue ASP +70%, NeoGenomics revenue per test +11%). Illumina is paid per gigabase sequenced, not per test, so higher test prices at the labs don't help Illumina.

## Company by company

### Caris Life Sciences: the clearest evidence of slowing (tissue)

Caris reports "clinical therapy selection cases," which excludes pharma work. Total volume growth has stepped down every year:

| Period | Cases | y/y | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| FY2023 | ~129,000 | ~+33% | S-1/A (calculated) |
| FY2024 | ~163,000 | ~+26% | S-1/A (calculated) |
| FY2025 | ~199,300 | ~+22% | Q4'25 8-K |
| Q1'26 | ~52,800 | ~+15% | Q1'26 8-K |
| Q2'26 | ~59,200 | **+18%** (verified) | Q2'26 8-K |
| FY2026 guide | | ~+20% (verified, reaffirmed Q2'26) | Q2'26 8-K |

The split by test matters more than the total:

- **Tissue (MI Profile):**
  - Q2'25: 42,886 cases, +17.7%.
  - Q3'25: +12.5%.
  - Q2'26: about 48,300, which works out to about +12.6% y/y.
  - On the Q1'26 call, management guided tissue to low-teens growth (secondary transcript).
- **Blood (Caris Assure):** about +50–65% y/y off a much smaller base (about 10,700 cases in Q2'26 vs 7,146).
- **Revenue is not a demand signal.** Molecular profiling revenue grew +55% in Q2'26 (verified), driven by price: tissue ASP of over $4,300 was up about 70%, per the Q1'26 call. Caris reports Q3'26 on 4 Nov 2026.

### NeoGenomics: NGS still growing mid-to-high teens

| Period | Clinical test volume y/y | NGS volume y/y | NGS revenue y/y |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1'26 | +6% (same-store +3%) | +16% | +26% |
| Q2'26 | +1.9% | +14% | +26% |
| Q3'26 | +2% (verified) | **+17%** (verified) | **+29%** (verified) |

Total clinical volume is flat because NeoGenomics is exiting a low-value contract. NGS, which is mostly CGP and heme panels, is still growing mid-to-high teens, and its revenue is boosted by price: average revenue per clinical test is up 11% to $530 (verified). On the Q2'26 call, management said the runway from targeted panels to CGP "is still robust."

### Tempus AI: accelerating

Tempus does not report oncology test counts, only growth rates:

| Q1'25 | Q2'25 | Q3'25 | Q4'25 | Q1'26 | Q2'26 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ~+20% | ~+26% | ~+27% | +29% | +28% | **+31%** (verified) |

The Q2'26 release says "Oncology volume growth of 31% year-over-year, up from 28% last quarter." This includes a small but growing MRD volume of about 9,000 tests in Q2'26, so it is not pure therapy selection, but CGP (xT tissue and xF liquid) is the bulk of it. Tempus reports Q3'26 in early November.

### Guardant Health: accelerating, but the headline is inflated by MRD

Guardant's "oncology tests" combine Guardant360 Liquid and Tissue with Reveal, its MRD test. Reveal has grown over 100% y/y for three quarters and sells at about $600–700 against about $3,000 for Guardant360 Liquid. The headline volume therefore overstates therapy-selection growth.

| Period | Oncology tests | y/y |
|---|---|---|
| Q2'23 | 43,500 | +49% |
| Q2'24 | 49,400 | +14% (trough) |
| Q2'25 | ~64,000 | +30% |
| Q2'26 | ~104,000 | **+63%** (verified) |

- **Guidance:** Guardant raised its 2026 oncology volume guide from about 30% to about 50%, and its revenue guide to about 30% (verified).
- **Core therapy-selection test:** Guardant360 Liquid grew about 30% in Q1'26 and "more than 30%" in Q2'26, per the calls (secondary transcripts). Guardant does not publish a pure therapy-selection count.
- **Guardant reports Q3'26 on 5 Nov 2026.**

### Foundation Medicine (Roche): no disclosure

Roche does not report Foundation Medicine volume or revenue. Its CEO, Malarek, said at Roche's Diagnostics Investor Day (12 May 2026, `ROCHE_2026-05-12_Investor_Day_Diagnostics_AXELIOS.md` L1175) that one of the largest US community oncology practices profiles only about 50% of its metastatic patients, and that this "needs to be north of 90%." That is evidence of under-penetration, not maturity, though it is one practice's anecdote.

## Market-level views

- **Roche, Diagnostics Investor Day, 12 May 2026** (L431–435): "Therapy selection for Oncology is a CHF 17 billion opportunity, growing at 9%." Disease monitoring (MRD) grows 32% to CHF 5B, and multi-cancer early detection grows 35% to CHF 5.3B by 2030. This is the strongest "maturing" data point. It covers the whole therapy-selection market, including companion diagnostics and digital pathology, and is measured in dollars, not tests.
- **Roche Precision Medicine and Genomics Lead, expert call, 29 Jul 2026** (L331–333): CGP is "quite saturated," with "just a very small increase year-on-year" where it is already deployed. The liquid-biopsy part of CGP would grow more.
- **Guardant CEO Eltoukhy, Q1'26 call** (secondary transcript): new growth is "mostly depth," not new physicians, but the market is "still very under-penetrated."
- **JPM survey of 32 clinical labs, 15 Jun 2026:** oncology somatic panels rise from 23% to 26% of clinical sample volume over three years, and labs expect sequencing volume growth of +14.6% in 2027 and +19.1% in 2028. These are Illumina customers surveyed by a broker that upgraded the stock.

## How to use this in the pitch

- **Holds up:** tissue CGP is growing in the low teens (Caris MI Profile about +12–13%; Caris guides tissue to low teens). Roche sees therapy selection as a 9% market, while the high-growth pools (MRD, early detection) are a fraction of its size and sequence less per test today.
- **Holds up:** lab revenue is outrunning lab volume on price. Illumina does not share in test pricing.
- **Does not hold up as stated:** "CGP labs are slowing." Tempus and Guardant are accelerating, and NeoGenomics NGS is +17%. A judge who follows diagnostics will know Guardant just raised its volume guide to 50%. Frame the point as tissue maturing, with liquid and share shifts carrying growth.
- **Timing risk:** Caris (4 Nov), Guardant (5 Nov) and Tempus (early Nov) all report Q3'26 before the 14 Nov competition. Refresh these numbers then.
