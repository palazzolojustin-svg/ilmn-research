### A4. Pattern across the 10 quarters with a revenue guide

- Revenue versus midpoint [Der]: Q1'24 +2.0%, Q2'24 +1.3%, Q3'24 0.0%, Q4'24 +3.2%, Q1'25 about 0.0% (cc top of range), Q2'25 +0.9%, Q3'25 +1.8%, Q4'25 +4.8%, Q1'26 +2.0%, Q2'26 +2.6%. Mean +1.9% [Der: 18.6 / 10]. No quarter was below the low end of its range.
- EPS versus midpoint (8 quarters with an EPS guide or implied guide) [Der]: +34%, +6.7%, +1.6%, +16.7%, +14.5%, +12.5%, +10.0%, +6.9%. Mean +12.9% [Der: 102.9 / 8]. Eight straight beats.
- Margin versus guide: beats of 250 to 420 bps in 2024, +280/+250 bps in Q2/Q3'25, in line in Q1'25, +145 bps in Q1'26, +50 bps in Q2'26. The margin beats are shrinking as revenue beats stay constant, which is consistent with the guide now being set closer to reality and with tariffs, memory and freight costs absorbing some of the revenue upside [Der / MGMT].
- The beats are large relative to the guide, but the **full-year revenue guide was cut in 2024 (twice) and 2025 (once, in March/May)** before the pattern of raises began (see B and E).

## Flags and caveats for the primer

1. **Extraction dependence.** Most numbers are from extracts verified against the originals only where quoted above. The Q3'24 extract contains a garbled full-year revenue sentence; the "approximately $10.70 billion-ish mark" is a transcription error.
2. **Two descriptions of the Aug 2024 FY24 cut.** Dhingra's prepared remarks said about half China/Asia, half mid-throughput and X shipments; his Q&A said "almost 2/3" of the 1.5 to 2 point cut was China (E24Q2). Not reconciled in the transcript.
3. **Oct 2025 margin target.** E25Q3 transcript has "20% non-GAAP operating margins by 2027, excluding Greater China" in Thaysen's prepared remarks; other sources say 26%. Treat 20% as a transcription slip; Dhingra and the slides support 26%.
4. **SomaLogic reporting line.** Decks (microarrays and other) and the 10-Q (service and other) differ; model on the deck presentation but expect the GAAP table to show it in services.
5. **1 to 2 points.** The sources never say whether SomaLogic organic growth, BioInsight or Protein Prep is inside the 1 to 2 points; the wording most explicit about contents is Feb 2026 ("new assays in multiomics, both multiomics and our data... business") and Jul 2026 ("new products").
6. **Peterson question (Q2'26).** The transcript contains an artifact inside the key sentence; the answer is verbatim as printed.
7. **Missing guidance detail.** FY25 pull-through guide, capex and other-expense guidance for FY24/FY25, the March 2025 8-K, and tax/share guidance for Q1'25 and Q2'25 are not in the files; the Q4'24 and Q1'25 decks would fill them.
