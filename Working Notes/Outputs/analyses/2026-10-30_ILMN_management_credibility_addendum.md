# Addendum: Illumina management credibility, evidence file

*Prepared 30 Oct 2026, after the Q3'26 call. Built from four agent sweeps: earnings calls 2023–24, earnings calls 2025–26, conferences/decks/SEC filings, and expert calls/broker research/insider data. Key lines were spot-checked against the originals.*

## How to read this

**The bottom line.** The public record does not show management saying things it knew were false. It shows a pattern that matters just as much for a short:

1. **Long-range targets are set, then abandoned or redefined.** This has happened three times since 2023.
2. **Disclosure is withdrawn when the numbers turn unhelpful.** First X orders, then the clinical/research split, then the transition metrics, and now the price/volume slide.
3. **The "pricing headwind is fading" story keeps being told while the data says otherwise.**
4. **Direct questions on the exact things the short depends on go unanswered.** These are stocking orders, discount depth and the 2027 bridge.
5. **Guidance is set low to be beaten.** That makes the guide a poor signal in both directions.

**What to say in a pitch.** Use "credibility" or "track record," not "dishonest." Every item below is a documented fact, but intent cannot be proven from transcripts. A PM will respect "they move goalposts and pull disclosure when the numbers turn" more than an accusation they can knock down.

**On quotes.** I can't reproduce the transcripts verbatim here, so each item gives a close paraphrase and the exact file and line. Open the line to copy the exact wording into your deck. Paths are under `Working Notes/Sources/ILMN/`. For example, `earnings_call/ILMN_2026Q3_Earnings_Call.md:117` is line 117 of the Q3'26 call. Speakers are deSouza (CEO to Jun 2023), Goswami (CFO to Apr 2024), Thaysen (CEO from Sep 2023) and Dhingra (CFO from Apr 2024).

**Strength ratings.** Each item is rated **Strong**, **Moderate** or **Weak**, judged on how hard it would be for a bull to explain away.

## Tier 1: lead with these

### 1. Long-range targets set, then abandoned and redefined (Strong)

**What was said**

- **Apr 2023:** Goswami (CFO), in the Q1'23 prepared remarks, committed to core operating margins of 25% in 2024 and 27% in 2025 (`earnings_call/ILMN_2023Q1_Earnings_Call.md:282`).
- **Aug 2023:** Goswami admitted the margin target looked like a stretch, but said management remained committed (`ILMN_2023Q2_Earnings_Call.md:438`). He also said mid-teens long-term growth expectations had not changed (`:518`).
- **Aug 2023:** at the UBS conference, Illumina said mid-teens structural growth still made sense (`conference/ILMN_2023-08-17_UBS...md:310`).

**What happened**

- **Nov 2023:** Thaysen said the long-term growth targets were being re-examined (`ILMN_2023Q3_Earnings_Call.md:88`).
- **Jan 2024:** he declined to give any numbers (`conference/ILMN_2024-01-09_J.P._Morgan...md:303`).
- **Aug 2024 reset:** high-single-digit growth by 2027 and about 26% operating margin, called "a new baseline" (`conference/ILMN_2024-08-13_Illumina_2024_Strategy_Update...md:656`).
- **Actual margins:**

  | Year | Target (Apr 2023) | Actual |
  |---|---|---|
  | 2024 | 25% | 21.3% |
  | 2025 | 27% | 23.1% |

**Then the 2027 target itself was redefined, in four steps**

| Date | Source | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| Dec 2024 | Dhingra, `conference/ILMN_2024-12-03_..._Evercore...md:156` | Assumes "no meaningful contribution" from China |
| Nov 2025 | `conference/ILMN_2025-11-19_..._Wolfe...md` | The growth target becomes explicitly ex-China |
| Jan 2026 | JPM deck, `deck/ILMN_2026-01-13_JPM...md:215, :230` | The margin target excludes China, and the FY24 base is restated |
| Q4'25 to Q2'26 | `ILMN_2025Q4_Earnings_Call.md:266`; `ILMN_2026Q1_Earnings_Call.md:226` | The margin goal softens to "as close to 26% as possible," then to "excluding acquisitions" (SomaLogic costs about 100 bps) |

Excluding China removes a business that fell from $308M (FY24) to $243M (FY25) from the base the target is measured against.

**The fair caveat.** The 2023 targets came from deSouza and Goswami, and Thaysen reset them after taking over. Some of the China damage came from the 2025 export ban, which was outside management's control.

**Pitch line:** "This is the third version of the long-term target since 2023. Each version has quietly excluded whatever wasn't working."

### 2. Disclosure withdrawn when it turns unhelpful (Strong)

Each step in the sequence below removed a number that would have tracked the bear case.

**2023 to 2024: X orders and shipment guidance**
- X orders were disclosed every quarter of 2023 (`ILMN_2023Q4_Earnings_Call.md:160`), then stopped.
- In Q2'24 an analyst noted the order number was missing (`ILMN_2024Q2_Earnings_Call.md:236`).
- In May 2024 Thaysen said they were not providing a shipment number (`ILMN_2024Q1_Earnings_Call.md:498`).

**2024: the clinical/research split**
- The split was given on every 2023 call.
- In Aug 2024 Thaysen said they don't normally split that out (`ILMN_2024Q2_Earnings_Call.md:606`).
- It was later reinstated, as a clinical ex-China figure. That is now the metric most flattering to the bull case.

**Q4'25: transition-specific metrics**
- The deck said these would be phased out after that quarter's earnings (`deck/ILMN_2025Q4_Earnings_Slides.md:215`).

**Q3'26: the price/volume slide**
- Dhingra said the X-transition price/volume charts are "becoming less relevant" and the slide will be dropped after Q4 2026 (`ILMN_2026Q3_Earnings_Call.md:81`).
- That chart shows the 6000 earning about 3.5x what the X earns per Gb, up from about 2.8x in mid-2025 (`Outputs/analyses/2026-10-30_ILMN_X_vs_6000_share_and_rev_per_gb.md`).
- It is being retired with about 40% of high-throughput revenue still on about 16% of volume.
- Earlier, in Q1'26, Thaysen said it was "dangerous" to read the X revenue share too precisely (`ILMN_2026Q1_Earnings_Call.md:702`).

**Pitch line:** "The one chart that lets you size the remaining conversion headwind is being retired before the conversion is finished."

### 3. "Pricing headwind is fading" vs. the data and the experts (Strong)

**2023: no elasticity change, then a 60–70% price cut admitted**
- Goswami said he saw no change in pricing or elasticity (`ILMN_2023Q1_Earnings_Call.md:472`; `ILMN_2023Q2_Earnings_Call.md:344`).
- In Feb 2024 he declined to walk through pull-through math, saying it was "not relevant" and might confuse investors (`ILMN_2023Q4_Earnings_Call.md:534`).
- Meanwhile, Gb grew about 46% in Q4'23 while consumables revenue was flat.
- In Aug 2024 Dhingra finally sized the X price cut: more than 60–70% per Gb (`ILMN_2024Q2_Earnings_Call.md:276`).

**2025 to 2026: "the headwind is fading"**

Management said the headwind was fading on three successive calls:
- Q3'25: Thaysen said the transition was done and the headwind was reducing (`ILMN_2025Q3_Earnings_Call.md:472`).
- Q4'25: Dhingra said it would dissipate, especially in H2'26 (`ILMN_2025Q4_Earnings_Call.md:174`).
- Q1'26: Thaysen said they were not seeing the headwind as before (`ILMN_2026Q1_Earnings_Call.md:298`).

What the numbers show:
- Research and applied consumables were −12%, −7% and −6% in Q1–Q3'26, with 90%+ of research volume already on the X.
- Clinical conversion stalled at 76%, 78% and 78%. Management now concedes conversion has slowed (`ILMN_2026Q3_Earnings_Call.md:79`).
- The end-2026 target of 80–85% (`ILMN_2026Q1_Earnings_Call.md:172`; Thaysen said ">85%" at `:704`) has become "in the coming quarters."

**Experts**
- A former Illumina product director (23 Jul 2026, `expert_call/Expert_2026-07-23_Former_Associate_Director*`) estimates price cuts are absorbing volume growth. He puts high-throughput price down 30–40% since 2022.
- A UBS channel check (Mar 2026, `research/20260304_UBS_ILMN_First_Read*`) puts larger users at about $125 per genome, already below Roche's $150 entry price.

**Fair caveat:** these are single unnamed sources.

### 4. Direct questions dodged on what the short depends on (Moderate–Strong)

**Q3'26 call (`ILMN_2026Q3_Earnings_Call.md`)**
- **Stocking (Jefferies, Peterson, :113):** he asked about stocking orders and whether Q4's implied low-double-digit clinical growth is the 2027 starting point. Thaysen answered with "puts and takes" and repeated mid-teens for the year (:117). He addressed neither question.
- **Clinical exit rate (Barclays, Sergott, :213–223):** Thaysen waved away the "14%, 15% or 16%" debate and pivoted to market strength.
- **2027 bridge:** the Evercore question was deferred to the Q4 call (:145).
- **Large-customer discounts (:267):** Thaysen called them too specific to discuss.

**Earlier calls**
- **Q2'26:** BofA's stocking question also went unanswered (`ILMN_2026Q2_Earnings_Call.md:314–330`).
- **Q1'26:** on 35B flow cell pricing, management was not ready to talk pricing (`ILMN_2026Q1_Earnings_Call.md:528`). Volume discounts were called "reasonable" but never sized (:648).
- **2023–24:** the X backlog (`ILMN_2023Q3...:246`), excess capacity versus Ultima (`ILMN_2024Q1...:444`) and mid-throughput growth (`:266`) all went unanswered.

**Fair caveat:**
- The stocking premise ("you had that last quarter") was the analyst's. No management stocking comment was found in the Q2'26 transcript.
- Refusing to give 2027 guidance in October is normal.

The pattern is the point: the questions that go unanswered are exactly the ones about the short.

### 5. Full-year guides reaffirmed, then cut (Strong for 2023–24; not repeated since)

**FY23**
- In Apr 2023 deSouza said the company was on track for its 2023 commitments of revenue +7–10% and EPS $1.25–1.50 (`ILMN_2023Q1_Earnings_Call.md:158`).
- Guidance was then cut in Aug (+1%, $0.75–0.90) and again in Nov (−2 to −3%, $0.60–0.70).

**FY24**
- In Feb 2024 Goswami called the guide prudent, with China the only area treated pessimistically (`ILMN_2023Q4_Earnings_Call.md:606`).
- In May, Thaysen said expectations were unchanged (`ILMN_2024Q1_Earnings_Call.md:498`). At RBC, Dhingra said banking the beat was prudent (`conference/ILMN_2024-05-14_RBC...md:140`).
- Under 12 weeks later, revenue guidance was cut, and cut again in Nov. China was about two-thirds of the Aug cut (`ILMN_2024Q2_Earnings_Call.md:508`), which sits awkwardly with "pessimistic" on China.

**Fair caveat:** EPS still beat the cut guides both years. From 2025 management switched to guiding low and raising. See the counter-evidence below.

## Tier 2: supporting evidence

**6. China calls repeatedly wrong, and its size misstated (Moderate)**
- **The record:**
  - The 2023 plan assumed an H2 China recovery that never came (`ILMN_2023Q1...:262`; `ILMN_2023Q2...:396`).
  - In Nov 2023 Goswami conceded the company had been wrong on China (`conference/ILMN_2023-11-28_Evercore...md:428`).
  - In Jan 2024 Thaysen called China "challenged" for 2024 but said it would improve in 2025 (`conference/ILMN_2024-01-09_J.P._Morgan...md:347`).
  - In May 2024 Dhingra said nothing material had changed in China (RBC :138), shortly before the Aug cut.
- **Size misstated:**

  | When | China's share as stated | Actual |
  |---|---|---|
  | Jan 2024 | ~10% of the business | FY23: 8.5% |
  | May 2025 | under 5% | Q1'25: ~6.9% |

- **Caveat:** the 2025 export ban and unreliable-entity listing were outside management's control. In 2025 management *under*-forecast China (it guided $165–185M; actual was about $242M).

**7. Placement counts flatter the economics (Moderate)**
- Q1'26 had 80+ X placements but only $118M of instrument revenue, against about $125–130M expected.
- Barclays (30 Apr 2026) and JPM both cite leasing and reagent-rental deals that were never quantified (`research/20260507_Barclays*`).
- A core-facility director (31 Jul 2026) describes taking delivery of instruments on Illumina's quarter-end schedule and says promised discounts were not honored (`expert_call/Expert_2026-07-31_Director_at_Genomics_Core_Facility*`). This is one customer.

**8. Customer concentration: the 10-K says what the conferences don't (Moderate)**
- The FY25 10-K adds a new risk-factor headline on customer concentration (`filing/ILMN_10-K_FY2025.md:439`). It says revenue increasingly comes from a small number of large, centralized labs, bringing pricing pressure and weaker negotiating leverage (`:447`).
- That language appears in no earlier filing or conference.
- At conferences the tone is the opposite: Illumina is "nowhere near commoditized" (`conference/ILMN_2024-05-29_Bernstein...md:206`) and clinical is entrenched (TD Cowen, Mar 2026).
- A former market-development manager (Tegus, about Jan 2026) says Illumina classifies "clinical" very broadly.

**9. SEC inquiry framed differently at conferences and in filings (Moderate)**
- At UBS (Aug 2023) management linked SEC interest to the proxy fight and the CEO's departure (`conference/ILMN_2023-08-17_UBS...md:360`).
- The Q2'23 10-Q says the inquiry primarily concerns GRAIL and Illumina's statements and disclosures about it (`filing/ILMN_10-Q_2023Q2.md:967`).

**10. Same-call contradiction on pull-forward (Moderate; small dollars)**
- Q1'24: Thaysen said nothing was pulled forward (`ILMN_2024Q1_Earnings_Call.md:496`).
- Dhingra's prepared remarks on the same call said some customers accelerated X deliveries from Q2 into Q1 (`:134`), about a quarter of the beat.

**11. "Supply-limited" X demand reversed (Moderate)**
- Apr 2023: deSouza said 2023 X shipments would be bound by manufacturing, not demand (`ILMN_2023Q1...:326`).
- Nov 2023: Goswami blamed fewer placements on customers' purchasing constraints (`ILMN_2023Q3...:104`).

**12. NIH reassurance before the research hit (Moderate)**
- Nov 2024: Dhingra said massive NIH cuts had never really happened (`conference/ILMN_2024-11-20_..._Wolfe...md:188`).
- Jan 2025: Thaysen saw few changes for 2025 (`conference/ILMN_2025-01-14_..._JPMorgan...md:246`).
- **Then:**
  - The US academic outlook was cut by about 15% (May 2025).
  - FY25 research consumables fell 7%.
  - The decline was called "temporary" (`ILMN_2025Q1...:456`) and "stabilizing" (`ILMN_2025Q3...:76`), then fell 12% in Q1'26.
  - In Q2'26 it was still "too early to call a recovery" (`ILMN_2026Q2...:78`).

**13. Quality of "growth" (Moderate–Weak)**
- In Jan 2026 Thaysen said Illumina "returned to growth" in 2025, with EPS up about 15% (`conference/ILMN_2026-01-13_..._J.P._Morgan...md:78`).
- But FY25 revenue was flat in constant currency (+0.2% reported).
- The non-GAAP tax rate fell from 23.6% to 20.5%. At a constant tax rate, FY25 EPS would be about $4.65 (+12%) instead of $4.84 (+16%). This is our calculation.
- In Q2'26, government incentives (+$18M) and the absence of restructuring costs (+$8M) explained $26M of the $31M year-over-year rise in operating income. Source: Seeking Alpha, 8 Sep 2026, an independent contributor.
- **Note:** Q3'26 tariff refunds (about $0.18 of EPS) were excluded and called out. That was transparent.

**14. Product timing slipped while described as on plan (Moderate–Weak)**
- In Q2'25 multiomics was to add at least 1 point of growth in 2026–27 (`ILMN_2025Q2...:352`).
- In Q4'25 it would not be meaningful in 2026 (`ILMN_2025Q4...:382`).
- In Q2'26 it was "playing out as expected" (`ILMN_2026Q2...:486`). In Q3'26 visibility moved to "somewhere in 2027" (`ILMN_2026Q3...:309`).

**15. Insider selling (Weak–Moderate)**
- Director Keith Meister, of the activist fund Corvex, sold about 3.06M shares (about $591M) between May and Sep 2026. That includes sales at $200–227 starting five days after the Q2 call (`insider_data/`).
- **Caveat:** an activist exiting after a re-rating is normal. The CEO and CFO *bought* in May 2025 at about $81 and have not sold.

**Minor items (Weak; don't lead with these)**
- The extra Q4 week went from about 0.5 points of revenue (`ILMN_2026Q2...:330`) to "quite minimal" (`ILMN_2026Q3...:333`).
- Q3'25 clinical growth was called "high single digit" by the CEO but "double-digit" by the CFO on the same call (`ILMN_2025Q3...:74, :146`).
- The installed base was cited as 25,000 in 2024 against about 21,000 active in the Jan 2026 deck.
- The Q1'24 10-Q said no buybacks in 2024 (`filing/ILMN_10-Q_2024Q1.md:1208`). A $1.5B authorization followed in Aug.

## Counter-evidence: what a bull or PM will throw back

You need answers ready for these.

**Guidance since 2025 has been conservative, and beaten every time**
- **FY25:** EPS came in at $4.84 against a post-cut guide of $4.20–4.30. China came in at about $242M against $165–185M guided.
- **FY26:** the guide was raised twice. RoW organic growth went from 2–4% to >5%, and EPS from $5.05–5.20 to $5.40–5.45.
- **EPS beats:** every quarter since Q2'25. They are shrinking: about 17%, then 15%, 13%, 10%, 7% and 6% versus the guide (`Outputs/datasets/2026-10-30_ILMN_EPS_beat_bar_chart.xlsx`).
- **Brokers:** Barclays (Feb 2026) and Freedom both call the guidance conservative.

**Milestones that were met**
- The X transition milestones (75% of Gb and 50% of revenue) were hit in Q3'25, about a quarter late.
- X placements beat the 50–60 a quarter framework every quarter (80+, 95+, 80+).
- Cost savings beat targets in both 2023 and 2024.
- SomaLogic closed on schedule.

**Clinical was *under*-called**
- In Q3'25 management endorsed a mid-to-high-single-digit framework.
- Clinical then printed 20% in Q4'25 and 20% in Q1'26.

**Third parties support the stickiness story**
- JPM's 62-customer survey (Jun 2026) finds the clinical base stickier than appreciated.
- A Natera executive called Illumina's switching barriers "very, very high."
- Several experts describe revalidation lock-in.

**Insiders**
- The CEO and CFO bought shares and have not sold.

**How to handle it.** Concede the conservative guidance. Then reframe: the beats are shrinking, the guide is no longer conservative (Freedom said exactly that in Aug 2026), and the credibility issue is the long-range narrative and disclosure, not quarterly numbers.

## Suggested pitch paragraph

> Management has a track record of moving goalposts. The 2023 margin targets of 25% and 27% were abandoned. The long-term growth target fell from mid-teens to high single digits. The 2027 target has since been redefined to exclude China and acquisitions. Disclosure follows the same pattern: X orders, the research/clinical split and the transition metrics were all withdrawn. Now the price/volume slide is being retired after Q4'26, while about 40% of high-throughput revenue still sits on about 16% of volume. On the Q3'26 call, management declined to quantify stocking, discounts or the 2027 jumping-off point. Their quarterly guidance has been conservative, so I don't argue they mislead on the quarter. I argue the long-range story is the part they keep revising, and that is what the multiple is paying for.
