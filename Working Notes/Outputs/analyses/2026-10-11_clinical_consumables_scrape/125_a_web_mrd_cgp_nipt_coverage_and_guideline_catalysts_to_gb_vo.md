## Rough Gb arithmetic (OPINION, my inference)

I did not find a new sourced Gb/test figure, so I did not build a full low/base/high Gb CAGR. Use the Gb/test figures from the earlier agents (84, 85, 88) with the following volume inputs:

- Natera's clinical MRD units grew about 56% y/y, and its oncology tests grew about 57%.
- Volume that grows 50%+ as price per Gb falls about 25% would still grow revenue, which is the scenario in Q2 (E089, E097 context).
- Per-test sequencing depth is the key unknown. Tumor-informed MRD is a low-depth, targeted panel, so test growth overstates Gb growth. Moving to WGS-based MRD would reverse this.
- Illumina's clinical consumables grew 15% ex-China in Q2'26 (known fact). That is far below Natera's test growth, which suggests:
  1. much of Natera's growth is offset by price per Gb;
  2. the testing is low-Gb; or
  3. other sequencers are taking share.
  This is a hypothesis, not a finding.
