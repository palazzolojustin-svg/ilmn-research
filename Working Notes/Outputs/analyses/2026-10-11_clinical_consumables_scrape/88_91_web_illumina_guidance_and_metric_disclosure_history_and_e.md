- **FACT: Illumina first gave FY24 EPS guidance in August 2024, not February.** The Q4'23 release (8 Feb 2024) guided only "Core Illumina revenue that is approximately flat compared to 2023 and a Core Illumina non-GAAP operating margin of approximately 20%". It gave no EPS guide and no Q1 guide. It said the outlook focused on Core Illumina because GRAIL timing was uncertain. [SEC Q4'23 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001110803/000111080324000007/a4q23earningsrelease.htm), 8 Feb 2024. This adds to E140, which has revenue and margin but not the missing EPS guide.
- **FACT: the first FY24 EPS guide was $3.80–3.95 (Q2'24 release, Aug 2024).** It then went to $4.05–4.15 (Q3, Nov 2024), and FY24 non-GAAP EPS was reported at $4.16. The midpoint of the first range is $3.875, so actual EPS beat it by about 7.4%. Revenue went the other way. It was guided flat, cut to –2/–3%, then to ~–3%, and came in at –2% (the ledger shows –2.4%), so the FY24 revenue guide was never beaten from the initial level. [SEC Q2'24](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1110803/000111080324000042/a2q24earningsrelease.htm), [SEC Q3'24](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1110803/000111080324000049/a3q24earningsrelease.htm), [Q4'24 results](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1110803/000111080325000014/a4q24earningsrelease.htm). The operating-margin guide was raised twice in 2024: ~20%, then 20.5–21%, then 21–21.5%.
- **FACT: the initial FY25 EPS guide was $4.50–4.65 (6 Feb 2025), with Core revenue up low single digits and a ~23% operating margin.** It was cut to $4.20–4.30 on tariffs in May 2025, and FY25 EPS came in at $4.84. [Illumina Q4'24 release search result](https://www.illumina.com/company/news-center/press-releases/2025/aefffafa-2be0-4580-8cf0-817322bd5d61.html), 6 Feb 2025. This corrects E140, which gave the initial guide as "~$4.50". The true initial midpoint was $4.575.
