## Key findings

Only a handful of items are new. Most of the assignment could not be answered from public sources.

- **Natera's disclosed Illumina-linked commitments are small.** Natera's purchase commitments to vendors are $11.6M in total at 31 Dec 2025. That is $5.31M in each of 2026 and 2027 and $0.94M in 2028, and all expire in 2028. FACT, from a search snippet of Natera's 10-K. I did not open the filing. [edgar.tools / Natera 10-K FY25 search result](https://app.edgar.tools/companies/NEO/disclosures/commitments-contingencies) (retrieved 2026-10-11, and that URL is labeled NEO, so verify the company). This updates E109. Natera's Illumina dependency is operational, not a take-or-pay lock-in. These commitments are a rounding error against any plausible spend (Natera has about 1M tests a quarter).
- **Natera's 10th Amendment (effective 10 Jan 2025) uses a spend-tier discount.** FACT. It has "7 pricing tiers based on 'Eligible Covered Consumables Spend'", applied to the "Current WGS Product" for WGS-based MRD tests. Reporting and certification conditions attach to the discounts. Both parties agree to "good faith discussions if market conditions for MRD tests change." All percentages and thresholds are redacted ("[*]"). [LawInsider, Exhibit 10.2.10, retrieved 2026-10-11](https://www.lawinsider.com/contracts/5K1yiQbNUCe).
  - OPINION: the discount rises with cumulative spend, so Natera's effective $/Gb falls as it scales. That is a contractual mechanism for the price decline on Illumina's largest-growth clinical account.
  - It also means WGS-based MRD pricing was already renegotiated before the 2026 WGS-MRD launches. It sits next to E109 and E113.
- **Natera supply-agreement background, same snippet source.** FACT. Fees per clinical NIPT test "have been reduced with further reductions based on volume increases." Natera is not bound to Illumina exclusively, but "availability of discounts… may be affected by reduced volumes." This is per-test fee pricing (not per Gb) and cuts both ways: volume buys discounts, and shifting volume away could cost them. The source is an unverified search-engine summary of the 10-K, not the filing text.
- **Guardant's 10-K discloses no dollar purchase commitments to Illumina.** FACT. It also has no sequencer capex breakdown and no COGS by segment. Its supplier language is "a limited number of vendors and would require qualification to transition to a different vendor," plus a "multi-month, multi-lot safety stock strategy." This confirms E110 and adds the safety-stock point. [Guardant 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1576280/000157628026000013/gh-20251231.htm) (fetch summary only).
- **The Illumina roadmap release (23 Feb 2026) gives no price and no date for the 35B.** FACT. It says upgrades "rolled out over the next 18 months," with "staggered starts." It gives 35B as +40% vs 25B and 14B for 10B, with 14B in 20–22 hours. The only customer quote is Texas A&M's Charlie Johnson: "becoming better, cheaper, and faster… data yield for a run has increased by 30% on average." This reconfirms E057/E135. The "Jan 2027 vs H1 2027" dispute (open gap 4) stays unresolved. [Illumina press release, Feb 2026](https://www.illumina.com/company/news-center/press-releases/2026/7b4175f1-a13f-401e-9335-3e5eadbb1fe0.html).
- **MolDX LCDs effective 12 Oct 2026 are mostly irrelevant to Illumina consumables.** FACT. The two finalized LCDs (published 27 Aug 2026) are L40242 (hereditary thrombophilia) and L40222 (non-NGS targeted molecular panels for cancer therapy). Both apply in Noridian Jurisdiction F. [Noridian JF Part B](https://med.noridianmedicare.com/web/jfb/article-detail/-/view/10534/moldx-local-coverage-determination-lcd-finalized-effective-october-12-2026).
  - The NGS-relevant LCDs are still proposed: DL40427 (NGS for hematologic malignancies) and DL40411 (genome-wide methods for copy-number and structural variants in heme neoplasms). Comments ran 27 Aug–11 Oct 2026. [Noridian open meeting page](https://med.noridianmedicare.com/web/jea/policies/moldx/open/agenda-100626).
  - OPINION: the 12 Oct date does not itself change NGS volumes. The heme-NGS LCDs could widen or limit coverage later.
- **PAMA CY2027 detail adds one point to E097.** CMS reported "about 77% of priced codes going down" with about 6,400 labs reporting. FACT. [Forvis Mazars, Oct 2026](https://www.forvismazars.us/forsights/2026/10/cms-releases-preliminary-2027-clinical-lab-fee-schedule-rates) (the −23% genomic sequencing figure and 15%/yr cap are already in E097).
