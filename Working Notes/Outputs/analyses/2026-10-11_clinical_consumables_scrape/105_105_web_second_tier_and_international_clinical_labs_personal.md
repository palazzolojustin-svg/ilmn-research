## Key findings

I found very little that bears on the thesis. For this tier of labs, the web record is thin on platform mix, sequencing cost per test and purchase terms. Several SEC pages came back truncated, so some negatives may reflect the fetch rather than the filing.

- **Fulgent: a top customer is moving testing in-house.** Fulgent's largest customer is internalizing testing. "Revenue from this customer this quarter decreased $4.6 million from the prior quarter," and management expects "continued decline in revenue from this customer through the second half of the year." Precision Diagnostics was $41.5M, down 13% y/y and up 3% q/q. (OPINION on read-through: when customers insource, they buy their own sequencers. That is a small positive for Illumina placements, but it shows that customer concentration is a risk for sequencing-service labs.) [Fulgent Q2'26 call, 2026-07-30, https://www.webull.com/news/15364816053167104]
- **Fulgent's margins are not showing falling sequencing costs.** GAAP gross margin was 30.1% in Q2'26, against 42% a year earlier. Non-GAAP was 31.3%, and management expects a mid-30s% full-year margin. Cost of revenue rose to $59.7M from $47.4M, and gross profit fell to $25.7M from $34.4M. Management blames fixed costs on lower revenue, the billing-system transition and the acquisitions (Bako, StrataDx), not sequencing cost. So cheaper Gb is not visible in this lab's margin. (FACT) [Fulgent Q2'26 10-Q, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001674930/000119312526326136/flgt-20260630.htm; earnings summary at https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/fulgent-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results]
- **Fulgent uses Illumina TruePath.** Management cited this on the Q2'26 call: "Our mean sequencing coverage increased from 40x to 60x to 70x." Repeat-expansion targets rose from 21 to 64. This is a small example of intensity rising at a clinical lab, but it gives no Gb per sample. (MGMT) [Fulgent Q2'26 call, 2026-07-30, webull URL above]
- **Personalis shows no sign of benefiting from cheaper Gb.** Gross margin was 1.8% in Q1'26 against 35% a year earlier, and about 21% in Q2'26. Management blames "unreimbursed test cost" (over 2,000 bp of dilution) and chasing volume ahead of coverage. Clinical volume was 10,384 tests in Q2'26 (+199% y/y, +33% q/q). Clinical revenue was $2.6M, up 442% from a small base, which implies only about $250 per clinical test recognized. That is far below the Signatera ASP of about $1,275 (E089), so reimbursement, not sequencing cost, is the binding constraint. The $250 is my calc: $2.6M ÷ 10,384. (FACT, with a calc) [Personalis Q1'26 call, 2026-05-08, https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/05/08/personalis-psnl-q1-2026-earnings-transcript/; Q2'26 results, 2026-08-04, https://quartr.com/events/personalis-inc-psnl-q2-2026_o08KfBpj]

  - The Q2'26 10-Q cost of revenue was $32.8M for six months against $25.8M, on flat revenue of $37.8M. [https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001527753/000119312526332817/psnl-20260630.htm]
