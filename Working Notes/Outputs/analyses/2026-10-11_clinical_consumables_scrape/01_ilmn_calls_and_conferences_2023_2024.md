## Key findings

Citations use short tags like [EC 2024Q2] (earnings call) and [CF Wolfe 2024-11-20] (conference). A legend at the end maps each tag to its file.

1. **Management has given the relative price of the 6000 and the X many times, but never as one stable number.**
   - The headline was MGMT (Aug 2024): the all-in 6000→X price decline "would easily be more than 60% to 70% on a Gb-to-Gb basis" (CFO Dhingra) [EC 2024Q2].
   - OPINION, my arithmetic from the user's share series: the implied X price per Gb relative to the 6000 was about 55-65% lower in 2024 and about 70-73% lower in 2025-Q2'26.
   - Q3'24: X was 55% of volume and 35% of revenue, so the 6000 earned about 2.3x the X's revenue per Gb.
   - Q2'26: 83% of volume and 59% of revenue gives about 3.4x, or an X price near 30% of the 6000's.
   - Reading: the gap is widening, not fixed. That fits the 25B flow cell, large-account deals and further cuts. It also means the 35B flow cell (about 20% lower $/Gb) lands on a base that was already getting cheaper.

2. **Revenue at risk from the remaining 6000 base (OPINION, arithmetic only).**
   - At Q2'26 mix (6000 = 41% of HT revenue on 17% of volume), converting all remaining 6000 Gb to X pricing at constant Gb cuts HT consumables revenue by about 29%. Offsetting it needs about +41% Gb.
   - If about 20% of 6000 volume never moves (Thaysen's rule of thumb, Feb 2025), the hit is about 23% and the offset needed is about +30% Gb [EC 2024Q4].
   - Reported Gb growth was 30-46% through 2023-24, so volume could offset this if conversion is spread over a year or more. The risk is a lumpy, compressed conversion.

3. **Management's own "20% stays on the 6000" admission (Feb 2025) is the clearest bridge to the user's question 1.**
   - Thaysen: "about 20% stay and shrink over time; the last portion takes much longer than the first 75-80%" [EC 2024Q4].
   - Dhingra (Nov 2024): "some customers will, in his personal belief, never move existing tests off the 6000" [CF Wolfe 2024-11-20].
   - Taken together: the 6000 base contains validated clinical assays that are profitable, sticky and priced at about 3x the X.
   - That is the over-earning mechanism the analyst suspects. Management has not denied that it exists. It has argued the fall is gradual.

4. **Clinical conversion has run much faster than management described in 2024.**
   - Clinical Gb on the X: about 40% (Q3'24) [EC 2024Q3], about 50% (Q4'24) [EC 2024Q4], and about 78% (Q2'26, user-supplied).
   - In Q3'24 and Nov-Dec 2024, management called the move "assay by assay," "usually a year to 18 months," and "gradual… one test per quarter" [EC 2024Q3; CF Evercore 2024-12-03].
   - It also rejected the "clinical cliff" framing. Thaysen: "I've heard this clinical cliff a few times, and I don't think that's the way we look at the business" [EC 2024Q3].
   - It happened faster than the "gradual" framing implied. Management's volume and revenue targets were roughly met, but the cliff framing was dismissed.

5. **Management's transition targets were mostly met on volume and slipped slightly on revenue.**
   - Aug 2024: about half of HT revenue on X "by mid-2025," and about 75% of volume by H2'25 [EC 2024Q2; CF Strategy 2024-08-13].
   - Actual (user-supplied): revenue share was 44% in Q2'25 and 51% in Q3'25, so the half-way point landed one to two quarters late.
   - Volume was 78% in Q3'25, so the 75% target was met.
   - The first quarterly path was "about 5 pts of revenue shift per quarter." Reported steps: roughly 25-27% (Q2'24), 35% (Q3'24), 40% (Q4'24). The 10-point jump in Q3'24 was called "accelerated transition."

6. **Volume has so far beaten price, but "elasticity" has meant two different things.**
   - Reported Gb growth: Q3'23 +29%, Q4'23 +46%, Q1'24 >35%, Q2'24 >40%, Q3'24 >40%, Q4'24 >30% [EC calls].
   - The matching consumables revenue was roughly flat to +7% (Q2'24 flat; Q3'24 +7%).
   - Dhingra on the 40% vs ~5% gap: "Largely price… about, say, 25% decline on the price" [CF Wolfe 2024-11-20].
   - Management's own long-term model (Strategy Day) was 25% volume growth against a 15% price headwind, which Dhingra said implies "mid-to-high single-digit" HT consumables growth [CF Strategy 2024-08-13].
   - The model implies volume must keep growing at 25% or more just to deliver mid-to-high single digits.

7. **Clinical elasticity is mostly new assays, not repricing of old ones.**
   - Goswami (Nov 2023): "assays validated on the 6K will not immediately switch to the X… What goes onto the X are newer assays that were marginally viable or not viable on the 6K" [CF Evercore 2023-11-28].
   - Thaysen (Q3'24): customers are "adding markers, including methylation markers, which need substantially deeper sequencing."
   - This supports question 2, but it ties volume growth to specific test launches (MRD, liquid biopsy, CGP, WGS) rather than a general cheaper-price response.

8. **Pricing-structure shift for clinical.**
   - Aug 2024: "I don't think we're going to do global pricing going forward as we've done before" (Thaysen) [CF Strategy 2024-08-13].
   - Feb 2025: Dhingra described "active talks with quite a few clinical customers on separate pricing for emerging tests, enabling much higher volume as a distinct line rather than cutting prices across the whole portfolio" [EC 2024Q4].
   - Reading: large clinical customers are getting application-specific price tiers. That is evidence of customer bargaining power and supports the "35B / lower $/Gb" risk.

9. **Parallel use and low flow-cell fill.**
   - Mgmt explicitly said customers keep 6000s: "Most customers… especially in clinical, do not intend to decommission their 6000s" (Goswami, Apr 2023, only 10-12 decommissions expected from early X buyers) [EC 2023Q1].
   - Actual: about 110 deactivations by end-2023 [EC 2023Q4] and about 330 in FY25 (user-supplied).
   - Dhingra Dec 2024: "most of the current panels stay on the NovaSeq 6000… while customers validate new assays" [EC 2023Q4] / "Many clinical customers bought fleets of X systems, and new clinical trials and new tests launch on X" [CF Evercore 2024-12-03].
   - Jefferies' Q2'26 parallel-lab question is therefore a long-standing pattern, not a new one.

10. **Under-filled flow cells raise effective price per Gb.**
    - Goswami (Sept and Nov 2023): customers "never batch samples at the rate needed to fill a flow cell… they pay a slightly higher effective price that we recover" [CF MS 2023-09-11; EC 2023Q3].
    - OPINION: that is real revenue support that shrinks as the 25B/35B fill rates improve or as customers aggregate volume. It is not quantified anywhere in 2023-24.

11. **Credibility pattern: serial guidance cuts and dropped long-term targets (see "Management statements later contradicted" below).** The main points:
    - 2023 core guidance: +6-9% (Feb-Apr) → flat (Aug) → -3% to -4% (Nov).
    - 2024 core guidance: flat (Feb) → -2% to -3% (Aug) → about -3% (Nov). Instruments went from "high-teens decline" to "mid-30s decline."
    - Margin targets: 25% (2024) and 27% (2025) → "stretch" (Aug-Sep 2023) → abandoned for a new 500 bp-by-2027 framework (Aug 2024).
    - The mid-teens revenue growth target was reaffirmed in Aug 2023, then not reaffirmed in Jan 2024, then replaced by high single digits by 2027.

12. **Disclosure was selectively shut and reopened.**
    - X pull-through: refused (Aug 2023, "only once ramp-up has stabilized"), then refused again with "the math is not relevant" (Feb 2024), then disclosed as ">$1M" in Aug 2024 once it looked good.
    - X orders and placements, advanced-pipeline probabilities and mid-throughput placements were dropped or "rewound" (details below).
    - The 6000 pull-through was recast on a "net installed base" in Feb 2024 (denominator shrinks, metric rises).

## The 6000→X economics, by topic

### Price per Gb and the "pricing transition"
- FACT (company statements): the wording "pricing transitions" first appears in the Q4'23 call as a drag on consumables. Consumables were $687M, flat y/y, with "stronger X consumables largely offset by expected decline in NovaSeq 6000 consumables and 'pricing transitions'" [EC 2023Q4].
- MGMT (CFO Goswami, Q4'23): the price transition is "very similar to the price transition" seen moving from HiSeq to the NovaSeq 6000. [EC 2023Q4]
- FACT (reported): Q2'24 Gb output +40% while HT revenue grew ~1% [EC 2024Q2]. Q3'24 Gb +40% vs consumables +7% [EC 2024Q3].
- MGMT (Kumar, RBC question): the price headwind was framed as ~40% in Q2'24. Dhingra's answer was that it was "higher early on and shrinks as more volume moves to X" [EC 2024Q2].
- FACT: analyst framing of per-sample price from Nov 2023: "about $600 to about $200" (the host's framing, not management's) [CF Evercore 2023-11-28]. The "$200 genome… in a production environment" was said by Dhingra in May 2024 [CF Bernstein 2024-05-29].
- MGMT: 25B flow cell "cuts cost per genome to about $200 but requires enough samples to fill the flow cell" (analyst Schenkel's description, Feb 2024). The Q3'23 Souda question described the 25B as bringing "a price uptick" because not all customers fill it. Goswami: "higher prices per gigabase until flow cells are fully loaded" [EC 2023Q3].

### Volume statements
- FACT: sequencing activity on connected instruments is reported in Gb since Q2'23 (the earlier metric was run counts; the change was announced to "reflect higher output per run") [EC 2023Q2].
- FACT: Gb y/y: Q3'23 +29%, Q4'23 +46%, Q1'24 >35%, Q2'24 >40%, Q3'24 >40%, Q4'24 >30%. The 10-year average was ~25% [CF Evercore 2024-12-03].
- MGMT (Thaysen, Feb 2025): the Q4'24 slowdown to >30% "is not the main message"; longer term "mid-20% range" [EC 2024Q4].
- MGMT: Gb is "not a predictor of near-term revenue" (Dhingra, Aug 2024) but "directionally correlated with revenue over time" (Goswami, every call 2023 to Q1'24). The phrase changed.

### Validation timelines
- MGMT (Goswami, Nov 2023): "burn-down of 6K inventory before the X is installed; then a few validation runs that take roughly a quarter… then ramp" [CF Evercore 2023-11-28]. Customers stock up on the X initially.
- MGMT (Schwartz, May 2024): lag from launch to adoption is "one quarter to maybe two," longer for clinical because of "revalidation and redocumentation" [CF RBC 2024-05-14].
- MGMT (Thaysen, Q3'24): 12 to 18 months to move a customer's 6000 work to the X, assay by assay.
- MGMT (Thaysen, Q3'24): "sometimes the validation cost outweighs the savings of moving an existing assay, so they may keep it on the old platform" [EC 2024Q3]. This is the sticky-base mechanism in management's own words.
- MGMT (Goswami, Nov 2023): some assays still run on the HiSeq six years after the HiSeq→6000 transition. He called it "astounding."

### Shelf life and inventory
- MGMT: X consumables carry a committed shelf life of "three to four months" versus a minimum of six for the 6000 (Dhingra, Dec 2024) [CF Evercore 2024-12-03]. This makes X consumables revenue more sensitive to year-end destocking than the 6000's.
- MGMT: "not much stocking on the 25B… 10B very early in the stock-up phase" (Goswami, May 2024) [EC 2024Q1].

## Competition and customers' bargaining power (as seen in 2023-24)

- FACT (analyst questions): Quest and LabCorp "announced or expanded collaborations with a competitor" (Burstein, Aug 2024). Thaysen declined to give revenue share and said there is "a little bit over rotation" on price for "a very limited application space" [EC 2024Q2]. This is an early read-through on large clinical labs diversifying.
- FACT: Ultima's $100 genome was raised twice (Feb 2024 by Piper; May 2024 by Morgan Stanley, whose question on excess HT capacity and Ultima got no direct answer in the transcript) [EC 2023Q4; EC 2024Q1].
- FACT: Element's 100 AVITI units were put to Goswami in Sept 2023. He said Element "talk[s] about placements, not orders or purchases," and suggested Element boxes may be placed at customers who already bought an Illumina box [CF MS 2023-09-11].
- MGMT: competition outside China is "very much according to expectations, with a little share decline from new entrants" (Goswami, Nov 2023) [EC 2023Q3]. Win rates "stabilized in Q3 and Q4" 2023 and were "range-bound" in 2024 [EC 2023Q4; EC 2024Q2].
- MGMT: Dhingra, Dec 2024: new mid-throughput entrants fail on scalable business models, and customers add "an 11th instrument from another vendor if they have 10 Illumina boxes" [CF Evercore 2024-12-03]. So mid-throughput parallel use is acknowledged.
- FACT: UK Biobank proteomics and a single-cell deal went to rival vendors (per analysts; Feb 2025). Thaysen did not comment on the deals. Savant's question referred to a competitor "giving away instruments" [EC 2024Q4].
- MGMT: Roche is mentioned only as "Roche news in a couple of weeks" (Wolfe's question, Feb 2025). AXELIOS (launched 2026) has no 2023-24 coverage in my files.
- OPINION: pricing power signals. Management repeatedly said it is "very selective" on which customers get discounts, based on volume potential (Goswami, Sept 2023). That implies customer-specific price discrimination, which is where large clinical labs will have leverage.

## Further price cuts

- FACT: no 35B flow cell appears in these 2023-24 sources. The relevant precedents are the 25B launch (Oct 2023) and later the 25B 100/200-cycle kits (Q4 2024).
- OPINION: precedent for price pressure.
  - Management has not committed to avoiding price cuts. Thaysen (Jan 2024): "Illumina offers a premium product and customers accept premium pricing," but "Illumina is coming out with more attractive pricing, quality and more capacity" on mid-throughput XLEAP [EC 2023Q4].
  - Dhingra (Aug 2024): "We don't think this is the exit point or the equilibrium" (about pull-through) [EC 2024Q2].
  - Barnard (Aug 2024): "nowhere near the end of our density roadmap," meaning flow-cell density keeps rising and $/Gb keeps falling [CF Strategy 2024-08-13].
- MGMT: the XLEAP (NextSeq) pricing transition is "significantly smaller and likely more proactive" than on the X side. Dhingra acknowledged a margin drag that is "generally manageable." Teardown: Brennan cited a $9/Gb XLEAP figure vs on-label NextSeq in "the teens or low 20s" [EC 2024Q2].

## Gross margin and the "over-earning" question

- FACT (Core non-GAAP gross margin): Q1'23 65.2%, Q2'23 67.0%, Q3'23 66.0%, Q4'23 64.7%, Q1'24 67.1%, Q2'24 69.4%, Q3'24 70.5%, Q4'24 67.4%.
- MGMT: Goswami said the 6000 consumables are "highly profitable" and that losing them hurts gross margin (Nov 2023) [EC 2023Q3]. Gross margin then rose by about 380 bp from Q4'23 to Q3'24 while 6000 consumables fell, so the net effect of the transition on margin was less negative than feared.
- MGMT: X consumables were "designed so margins at scale would be comparable to NovaSeq 6000 consumables," lower early on, normalizing through 2024 (Goswami, Feb 2024) [EC 2023Q4].
- MGMT: Q3'24 gross margin +450 bp y/y: "a more favorable mix toward consumables accounted for roughly half" and productivity the rest [EC 2024Q3].
- OPINION: the 2024 gross margin improvement is explained mainly by (a) instrument mix falling (instruments were -42% to -29% y/y) and (b) COGS productivity. The data do not isolate the 6000's contribution. I cannot confirm that clinical consumables margin is inflated by the 6000 base from these sources. The only signal is management's "highly profitable" description of 6000 consumables and its "comparable at scale" claim for the X.
- Gross margin guidance: "closer to the 70% range" (Dhingra, Aug 2024) and "high 20s" operating margin as top quartile.

## Management statements later contradicted or quietly dropped

1. **Guidance cuts.** 2023 core growth went from +6-9% to flat (Aug) to -3% to -4% (Nov). Brennan (Nov 2023): "a series of guidance reductions, longer than peers" [EC 2023Q3]. 2024 went from "approximately flat" (Feb) to -2% to -3% (Aug) to about -3% (Nov). Dhingra called himself "disappointed in our lower revenue guidance" [EC 2024Q3]. Instruments: "high-teens decline" → "mid-30s decline" (Aug 2024) [EC 2023Q4; EC 2024Q2]. FACT.
2. **Operating margin targets.** 25% (2024) and 27% (2025) given Apr 2023 [EC 2023Q1]. In Aug 2023 Goswami said it would "seem like a stretch" while remaining "very committed" [EC 2023Q2]. By Sept 2023: "We are at 20% and close to flat for this year" [CF MS 2023-09-11]. Replaced in Aug 2024 by "500 bp expansion through 2027" off ~21%, and a 26% goal for 2027 (cited Feb 2025). FACT.
3. **Mid-teens growth target.** Reaffirmed Aug 2023 ("have not changed"). On Jan 9, 2024, Thaysen: "I'm not ready to provide any numbers at this point." By Aug 2024: "high single digit by 2027." Mid-teens is gone without an explicit retraction. FACT / MGMT.
4. **"No decommissions" for clinical.** See table. The Q1'23 estimate was 10-12; actual ~110 by end-2023 and ~330 in FY25.
5. **"Customers swapping two 6Ks for one X? … that's never played out in our history"** (Goswami, Nov 2023) [CF Evercore 2023-11-28]. The 6000 base fell from ~1,770 (end-2023) to ~1,700 (Q3'24) to ~1,360 (end-FY25), while X went from 352 to 630 to ~890. Net HT instruments: 2,122 → ~2,330 → ~2,250. OPINION: the user's brief data suggest some consolidation, but I can't determine the replacement ratio from these sources.
6. **Pulling forward.** Q1'24: Thaysen "We didn't pull anything forward" (Vatnsdal question) while Dhingra's prepared remarks cited customers "pulling forward delivery of a few NovaSeq X instruments from Q2 into Q1" as a driver of the beat, quantified as about one-fourth of the overachievement [EC 2024Q1]. FACT: direct contradiction inside one call.
7. **China cut breakdown.** In Q2'24 Dhingra's prepared remarks said about half of the cut was China and broader Asia; in Q&A, about two-thirds of the 1.5-2 point reduction was China [EC 2024Q2]. Flagged by the summarizer as possibly different scopes.
8. **Dropped or changed disclosures:**

## Read-through from end customers and competitors (limited to 2023-24 corpus)

- FACT: clinical customers in 2023 were "a tale of two cities" (well-funded customers placing fleet-expansion orders; others converting slowly). Many clinical customers "are not profitable" and manage cash (Goswami) [CF Evercore 2023-11-28; EC 2023Q2].
- MGMT: Oncology customers face "profitability pressure that may slow test development" (deSouza, Apr 2023) [EC 2023Q1]. A diagnostic customer in NIPT/MRD was "validating different platforms" (Souda, Nov 2023). Thaysen: "I'm here, I'm ready to fight for it" [EC 2023Q3].
- MGMT: Thaysen (Nov 2023): fewer than 50% of US cancer patients get sequencing at diagnosis; monitoring penetration is only 1-2 pts [CF Piper 2023-11-29].
- MGMT: FDA's LDT rule (Apr-May 2024): Dhingra, "if they don't, somebody else will"; the rule could delay grandfathered tests by "a year longer or whatever" [CF Bernstein 2024-05-29]. Thaysen: the rule "plays to our strength" through single-site PMA and Illumina's regulatory infrastructure [EC 2024Q1].
- MGMT: "We're not banking on any one individual program" for population genomics; ~30 programs running, ~50 in the funnel; <5% of revenue (Schwartz, Aug 2023) [EC 2024Q1; CF UBS 2023-08-17].
- MGMT: biotech customers outsourcing sequencing to core labs and CROs, which benefit from HT pricing (Dhingra, Nov-Dec 2024). That moves volume from mid-throughput (higher $/Gb) to HT [CF Wolfe 2024-11-20; CF Evercore 2024-12-03].
- OPINION: readthrough to the thesis. Parallel running is built in: large clinical labs buy "fleets" of X for new tests while leaving 6000 assays alone. Diversifying vendors (Quest, LabCorp) is documented only through analyst questions in this period.
