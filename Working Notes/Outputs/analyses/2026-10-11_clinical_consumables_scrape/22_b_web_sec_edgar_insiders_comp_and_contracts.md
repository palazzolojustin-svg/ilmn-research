4. **The Thaysen May 2025 purchase is confirmed by secondary summaries, not yet by the raw Form 4.** A summary of the Form 4 filed 14 May 2025 says Thaysen bought 12,350 shares at $80.86 ($998,674), taking direct holdings to 83,390 (+17%). Later Thaysen filings are consistent with ~83K: 82,089 after an Oct 2025 sale; 89,883 after a Feb 2026 PSU vest; 83,339 after Feb 2026 withholding. [FACT, secondary; see Thaysen section]
5. **The 2025 PSUs use operating margin and relative TSR, with no EPS or revenue PSUs.** This settles gap 8 for the 2025 grants. Each executive's PSU is split equally between 3-year average operating margin and 3-year rTSR against the Nasdaq Biotechnology Index, paying 0–250%. The 2025 annual bonus used revenue and non-GAAP operating income goals and funded at 77%. [FACT; proxy per search extract]
6. **Natera's supply agreement has a most-favored-pricing clause that references GRAIL.** The Eighth Amendment (effective 31 Dec 2023) says Natera gets "Volume-Based Net Prices that are no less favorable than... provided to an Equivalent customer or to GRAIL". This answers the price-protection part of gap 5. Pricing tiers are redacted. [FACT; Natera 10-K FY23 Ex. 10.28]
7. **Natera's pricing is tiered by annual spend, with test-based credits.** The Eighth Amendment has a 20-tier universal-consumables table keyed to annual sequencing spend, a five-tier NIPT credit structure keyed to annualized test volume, and a capped R&D/clinical-trial discount. All percentages and dollar amounts are redacted ("[*]"). [FACT]
8. **The Tenth Amendment (effective 10 Jan 2025) adds a seven-tier discount table for WGS-based MRD consumables.** It also has a re-opener: "If market conditions for MRD Tests materially change during the Term, either Party may initiate and the Parties will engage in good faith discussions regarding the discounting structure". Natera's WGS-MRD pricing is therefore contractually negotiable with Illumina, and it was written to "accommodate Natera's anticipated increased demand". [FACT; Natera 10-K FY24 Ex. 10.2.10]
9. **The Eleventh Amendment (effective 2 Mar 2026) adds a tariff pass-through and a price-protection clause.** New §7(g) passes through tariff charges above a threshold, with 30-day notice and retroactive application. A separate clause says the inflation-adjusted net price "shall not increase further" except for material cost increases beyond Illumina's control. Discount tiers now cover "WES-Based MRD" and "WGS-Based MRD" tests with monthly reporting. [FACT; Natera 10-Q Q1'26 Ex. 10.1]
10. **Guardant's agreement (Amendment #5, 1 Jan 2021) runs to Jan 2033 with auto-renewal.** Guardant can terminate for convenience on 90 days' notice, and nobody else is named as holding that right. The amendment "updated the list of specific hardware and consumables, including associated pricing terms". There is no unredacted exclusivity or minimum language, so Guardant could leave on 90 days' notice (E110 adds the 90-day detail to what is already known). [FACT; Guardant 8-K Jan 2021]
11. **Illumina's Q2'26 10-Q shows deferred revenue shrinking and the backlog shortening.** Contract liabilities were $333M at 28 Jun 2026 vs $346M at 28 Dec 2025 (current portion $261M vs $270M). Remaining performance obligations were $1.0B, with ~83% due in 12 months and ~11% in the following 12, versus $1.1B at Q1'26 with ~85%/9%. [FACT; 10-Q Q2'26]
