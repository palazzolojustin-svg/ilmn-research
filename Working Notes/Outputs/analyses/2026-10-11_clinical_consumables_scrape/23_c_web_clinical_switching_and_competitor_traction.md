## Key findings

I found little new, and no named clinical production switch. Six items add to or correct the ledger.

1. **Caris wording and capacity (adds to E092).** The ledger had the "two competitors" language but not the capacity context. The Q2'26 transcript quotes: "There are two competitors out there now against the longstanding sequencing supplier, and their throughput and their cost is significantly lower Higher and lower, respectively, than what's available." It continues: "they create the opportunity to increase our capacity and decrease our cost of goods simultaneously." On capacity: "Our current capacity is about a billion dollars a year of revenue and we're just about to triple that. So that'll be about three billion a year in revenue." MGMT. [Caris Q2'26 call transcript, earningswhispers.com, Aug 2026](https://earningswhispers.com/transcript/CAI/Q22026)
   - The excerpt returned names no supplier or platform. It gives no validation timeline and no Illumina-versus-alternative COGS figure.
   - OPINION: a tripling of capacity is the volume Caris would dual-source if it validates a second platform. It is also a large Illumina volume opportunity if Caris stays single-source. The transcript does not say which.
   - The excerpt also mentions Caris Detect (its MCED test, i.e. multi-cancer early detection) as a capacity driver. Dual-source risk therefore covers the new high-Gb products.

2. **Element Series E, 9 Jun 2026 (new, adds to E129).** Samsung Electronics put in $175M. The round was "upsized", with other investors in it, but the total, valuation and revenue were not disclosed. The prior Series D was $277M in Jul 2024. Element reports customers in "more than 40 countries across academic, pharmaceutical, biotechnology, and clinical research organizations" and names no clinical customer. Funds go to VITARI, AVITI Dx and tissue profiling on AVITI24. FACT/MGMT. [Element IR](https://www.elementbiosciences.com/news/element-biosciences-announces-upsized-series-e-to-accelerate-global-growth-across-ecosystem-of-genomic-multiomic-and-clinical-research-solutions), [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/news/note/element-biosciences-raises-upsized-175m-series-e-with-samsung-electronics-backing)
   - OPINION: Element is funded through the 2026–27 VITARI ramp. The Illumina litigation can't starve it of cash.

3. **Munich ruling for Element, 14 Jul 2026 (new, not in the ledger).** The Regional Court of Munich I found Illumina Inc. and Illumina GmbH infringe Element's German patent DE 112008000363 ("Device for detection and its use"), an imaging method. [Business Wire, 14 Jul 2026](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260714274374/en/), [ip fray](https://ipfray.com/munich-court-finds-illumina-infringes-element-sequencing-patent-orders-injunction-and-product-recall/), [Element](https://www.elementbiosciences.com/news/element-biosciences-prevails-in-german-patent-case-against-illumina)
   - Direct and indirect infringement. The judgment names MiSeq and MiSeqDx, plus "any other Illumina imaging system that functions as described."
   - Injunction on infringing systems and consumables in Germany.
   - Recall of systems and reagent kits put into circulation since 31 Mar 2023, with refunds and return costs.
   - Joint and several liability for damages, plus an order to render accounts of quantities, customers, prices and profits.
   - I could not confirm from the snippets whether NovaSeq X is covered or whether the ruling is stayed pending appeal. The ip fray full text is paywalled. This needs follow-up (ILMN 10-Q legal note, Illumina statement).
   - The link to Illumina's earlier Delaware suit against Element (May 2025) comes from ip fray's summary. Element's suit is described as a countersuit.

4. **Element antitrust order (adds to E120 and gap 20).** The A&O Shearman summary gives the reasons for the 28 May 2026 dismissal (case 25-cv-08026-NW, Judge Noël Wise). FACT, via a secondary summary. [A&O Shearman](https://www.lit-antitrust.aoshearman.com/northern-district-of-california-dismisses-dna-sequencing-antitrust-claims-against-illumina-with-leave-to-amend)
   - Alleged conduct: "bundled discounts (e.g., 50%+)" said to be below cost; discounts conditioned on exclusive use; "threatening to raise the price of consumables and instrument services to coerce customers into buying new instruments"; statements about Element's "impending demise."
   - Dismissal grounds:
     - Exclusive dealing failed because Element did not allege "specific provisions that locked customers."
     - Tying failed because the allegations were internally inconsistent.
     - Predatory pricing failed because Element gave "no support for defendant's costs, and offered no sense of scale."
     - Disparagement failed because the statements were non-actionable opinion.
   - No named customers appear in the summary.
   - OPINION: the order gives no support to a "minimum commitment" theory. The 50%+ bundled-discount allegation is the only discount figure. The amended complaint text and the 23 Jul reply are still unobtained, so this part of gap 20 stays open.

   - The snippet is unverified. The fetch of the filing's financial pages found no Illumina mention.
   - The Dec 2025 10-K already names Illumina as sole NGS supplier (E110).
