9. **The NHS WGS contract with Illumina is small and runs on the NovaSeq 6000.** Genomics England extended and varied its 2019 WGS services contract with Illumina Cambridge for £4.67M. The original scope was 300k whole genome equivalents, with an option to 500k, on NovaSeq 6000. The extension is meant to cover the move to distributed WGS in the NHS. FACT (Find a Tender notice 032387-2025, 2025; Genomics England). Distributed WGS means more labs may buy their own instruments, which is positive for X placements and consumables. It also means more procurement exposure to Roche, Ultima and Element.
10. **Competitive datapoints since Q2.**
    - Ultima's UG200 was selected on 22 Sep for the Mayo-led Precure project (1M biospecimens), with Helix as lab (Ultima/HPCwire, 2026-09-22). The Precure founding partner is Thermo Fisher, an Illumina competitor.
    - Hartwig Medical Foundation (Netherlands) expanded its collaboration with Ultima and adopted the UG200.
    - BaseClear will be Ultima's first certified service provider in Europe.
    - Roche AXELIOS has booked pre-orders and early users include CNAG (Spain), Broad, Hartwig, Sanger and Foundation Medicine (GenomeWeb). Foundation Medicine plans to onboard AXELIOS across its European sites. Large labs will run it in parallel with their Illumina instruments before switching.
    - Latvia's National Research and Innovation Institute agreed to develop new genomics technologies with Element Biosciences (lvportals.lv, ~Sep 2026), within days of Latvian institutions signing a cooperation MoU with Illumina.
11. **Product roadmap items are research-use-only (RUO) and mostly 2027.**
    - The WGS-based MRD (minimal residual disease, cancer-relapse monitoring) kit is in early access, with wide availability in 2027. It targets sensitivity of ~10 ppm and a 5-day workflow, and it runs on NovaSeq.
    - Illumina also said "several more" oncology WGS products and an ultra-sensitive MRD workflow are to follow.
    - SpliceAI2 was released on 8 Oct, along with DRAGEN 4.6 (late Sep). These are software and AI releases with no direct consumables-price effect.
    - FACT (Illumina press releases, 2026-05/06 and 2026-10-08; GenomeWeb; Diagnostics World News 2026-06-08).

No Q2 transcript mention was returned. This lets the Jan 2027 ~20% lower $/Gb figure stay unconfirmed in public sources. OPINION: Q3 is the likely venue.
