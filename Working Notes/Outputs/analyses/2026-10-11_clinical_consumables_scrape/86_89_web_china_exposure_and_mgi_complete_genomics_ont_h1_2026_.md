[Illumina Q2'26 release](https://www.illumina.com/company/news-center/press-releases/2026/29061.html), fetched 2026-10-11.

Taiwan and Hong Kong stay inside "Greater China", so the ex-China carve-out removes more than mainland revenue. FACT. [10-Q Q1'26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001110803/000111080326000092/ilmn-20260329.htm). This adds a point to E145, which has only the recast.
6. **The China regulatory timeline is confirmed, and the 10-K says case-by-case approval remains.**

   - 4 Mar 2025: told it "would no longer be permitted to export sequencing instruments into China."
   - 5–10 Nov 2025: instrument exports allowed again, "subject to approval on a case-by-case basis."
   - Illumina stays on the UEL, and the Q2'26 10-Q still lists the UEL as an uncertainty.
   - FACT. [10-K FY25](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1110803/000111080326000024/ilmn-20251228.htm), [MedTech Dive](https://www.medtechdive.com/news/Illumina-China-export-ban-lifted/804880/). The 10-K also says an impairment is possible if operations were significantly curtailed.

The ledger's "exposed to local displacement" question is more about 6000-class volume in the rest of the world than about China dollars. OPINION.
