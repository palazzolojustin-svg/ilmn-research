- **Twist NGS growth, FQ3'26 (FACT).** NGS revenue was $61.8M, up 12% y/y from $55.3M and up 8% sequentially from $57.4M. Total revenue was $118.4M, up 23%. Twist guides to sequential NGS growth again in FQ4.
  - Source: Twist 8-K Ex. 99.1, 2026-08-03, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001581280/000158128026000044/twst-2026630xex991.htm
  - Twist's NGS growth is slower than Illumina's reported ex-China clinical consumables growth (15% in Q2'26). That is not like-for-like, because Twist sells probes and library-prep products and Illumina sells consumables.
- **Twist diagnostics, FQ3'26 (FACT).** Diagnostics revenue was $43.8M, up 15% y/y and up 10% sequentially. The attribution is "growth among top accounts", which fits a concentrated customer base.
  - Source: Pulse2 and Twist press release summaries, https://pulse2.com/twist-bioscience-gene-shipments-jump-56-as-revenue-reaches-record-118-4-million/
  - MGMT, via MarketBeat's paraphrase: demand is "especially for molecular residual disease testing and proprietary enzyme-enabled workflows". Tumor-informed MRD is driving a higher probe count per patient. This is a weak, indirect support for deeper MRD sequencing intensity.
