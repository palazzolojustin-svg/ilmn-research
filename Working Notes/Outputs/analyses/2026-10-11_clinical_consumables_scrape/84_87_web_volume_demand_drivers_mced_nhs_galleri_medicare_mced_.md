## Key findings

Overall, I found no public-sector or policy program that adds more than about 1–3 points to clinical Gb growth through 2027. Most Gb is already running or already committed, and several of the big programs are not on Illumina.

- **The GRAIL FDA panel result is new and not in the ledger.** On 23 Sep 2026 the FDA Molecular and Clinical Genetics Devices Panel voted 6–4 that Galleri is effective, voted unanimously that it is safe, and voted 7–2 with 1 abstention that benefits outweigh risks. FACT. [Nasdaq/RTT via search, Sep 2026](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/fda-panel-recommends-approval-grails-galleri-multi-cancer-early-detection-test); [GRAIL release](https://grail.com/?p=28484). An NPR report says the FDA gave no decision date. FACT. [STLPR/NPR, 2026-09-23](https://www.stlpr.org/npr/2026-09-23/will-fda-advisers-recommend-a-multi-cancer-blood-test-for-approval-theyll-vote)
- **The MCED Act is law.** The Nancy Gardner Sewell Medicare MCED Screening Coverage Act was signed on 3 Feb 2026 inside the FY26 spending package. FACT. [AZBio](https://www.azbio.org/?p=48713); [Prevent Cancer Foundation](https://preventcancer.org/policy-advocacy/multi-cancer-early-detection/coverage-and-legislation/).
  - Medicare coverage starts in 2028 and only for FDA-approved MCED tests. It allows one test a year per beneficiary, with age limits that widen over time.
  - So MCED Gb from Medicare cannot arrive before 2028, and then only if the FDA approves. The ledger's "MCED not in the number today" (E078) is consistent with this.
- **GRAIL's timeline.** It filed the final PMA module on 29 Jan 2026. FACT. [GRAIL via search](https://grail.com/press-releases/grail-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results/). H1'26 volume was >117K tests (+42%), and Q2'26 revenue was $42.6M (+24%) on >61K tests (+35%). This matches E178. Cash was $861.6M at 30 Jun 2026 (FACT, same release).
- **NHS-Galleri did not hit its primary endpoint.** Stage III–IV cancers combined did not fall significantly. Stage IV of 12 prespecified aggressive cancers fell 22% and 26% in rounds 2 and 3. FACT. [GRAIL Q2 release summary](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/grail-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results). An NHS rollout is therefore not a near-term volume source. OPINION.
- **Galleri's sequencing depth is not public.** My searches and the GRAIL release returned nothing (this confirms the ledger's E178 gap). All Gb below is an assumption.
- **Truveta's sequencer is not named for the sequencing itself, and Regeneron does the work.** The Truveta Genome Project targets up to 10M exomes from leftover clinical samples, with 17 health systems. Regeneron invested $119.5M and Illumina $20M in the Series C ($320M). Regeneron's Genetics Center does the sequencing. FACT. [Fierce Biotech, JPM25](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/medtech/jpm25-regeneron-illumina-and-more-back-truveta-10m-genome-sequencing-project); [BiopharmaTrend](https://www.biopharmatrend.com/news/truveta-raises-320m-to-sequence-10m-exomes-and-build-largest-genetic-database-1109/).
  - I found no timeline and no Illumina contract value.
  - The Regeneron Genetics Center is a documented Ultima user. It used the UG 100 for the UK Biobank proteomics work and has a development agreement with Ultima dating from 2022. FACT. [BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/ultima-genomics-announces-ug-100-sequencing-platform-selected-for-uk-biobanks-groundbreaking-human-proteome-study)

## Aggregate view

- The sum of identifiable programs that Illumina plausibly captures today is about 20–40M Gb a year (OPINION, calc from the table). Illumina's HT base is roughly $2.0B (69% of $2.94B, E003 logic) at a blended price of about $2.50/Gb (E071), so about 800M Gb. That is a rough, cross-checked-only-once estimate.
- 30% growth on that base is about 240M Gb of new Gb a year. These programs are mostly already running, so the incremental part is perhaps 5–15M Gb, or about 1–2 points of growth.
- **Answer to your question:** these drivers do not add more than 5 points before 2028. Only MCED at 5M or more tests a year could, and it needs FDA approval, Medicare coverage from 2028, and several years of ramp. OPINION.
- Against a mid-teens price decline, public-sector programs do not change the arithmetic in E083–E084. The volume case rests on commercial oncology (MRD, liquid biopsy) and genome conversion, not on government programs.
