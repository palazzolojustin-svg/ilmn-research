7. **Incentive metrics changed in 2025: EPS PSUs were dropped.** Starting in 2025 Illumina no longer grants PSUs based on EPS targets. It now grants operating-margin PSUs (OM PSU) and relative-TSR PSUs (rTSR PSU). The 2026 proxy lists the key pay measures as revenue, non-GAAP EPS, operating income, operating margin and rTSR. [FACT; 10-Q FY2025 note and DEF 14A 2026 via search summaries] **This contradicts the assumption that "EPS units were reinstated"**: I found no evidence of reinstatement, and the 2026 proxy still lists non-GAAP EPS as a company measure (probably in the annual bonus). I could not retrieve CD&A pages (fetch truncated), so weights, targets and payout percentages are unverified. Severity: medium, because the thesis is about margin quality. An operating-margin PSU rewards cost cuts and mix, which the old-6000 base inflates.

## Read-through to the thesis

- **Pricing power and volume offset.** The Element case (OPINION) shows the competitive dynamic the analyst is probing: Illumina's volume-for-discount arrangements are being tested in court. The May 2026 ruling found no pleaded exclusive dealing or below-cost pricing, which supports management's right to keep using discounts. A successful amended complaint would be a risk to the commitment-based volume offset.
- **Incentives.** Operating-margin PSUs plus a non-GAAP EPS measure mean executives are paid on margin. This is consistent with the idea that the unconverted 6000 base props up margins, though pay design alone does not prove over-earning.
- **Insiders.** Management is not selling meaningfully, while the largest outside holder-director (Corvex) cut heavily through $200–227. Reading this as a valuation call is OPINION.

## Contradictions and stale data

- The brief mentions "EPS units reinstated". I found the opposite: EPS PSUs were discontinued from 2025. Non-GAAP EPS remains a listed pay measure, but I could not confirm whether it is in the annual bonus or the 2026 PSUs.
- Search summaries of the "10% workforce cut / $100M expense plan / April announcement" are from a 2023 SDBJ article (GRAIL FTC period) and are **not** 2025–26 events. Ignore them.
- A parameter.io/Blockonomi piece cited a "66.4% gain in 2024" and "S&P 500 return on September 21" while describing Meister's Sept 2026 sales. The year context looks mixed and I did not verify it. Do not rely on it.
- Reported share prices vary by source (Stock Titan weighted-average ranges versus Quantisnow single prices). The Thaysen Oct 2026 transaction was described by one tool as 10b5-1, but the Form 4 shows no flag, and the code is F.
