3. **The remaining sequencing consumables growth is much lower than the ex-China clinical growth figures suggest.**
   - FACT: Sequencing consumables were $775M in Q2'26 vs $740M in Q2'25 (+4.7%). Q1'26 was $726M vs $696M (+4.3%). YTD was $1,500M, +4% [Bloomberg quarterlies; 10-Q Q2'26].

4. **Filings stopped mentioning the NovaSeq 6000 decline.**
   - FACT: The FY24 10-K and Q1'25 10-Q said NovaSeq X consumables growth was "partially offset by decreases in consumables across our other high-throughput instruments, as customers transition to NovaSeq X, and across our mid-throughput instruments."
   - FACT: The FY25 10-K and Q3'25 10-Q say only "demand for high-throughput consumables as customers continue to transition to NovaSeq X". The Q1'26 10-Q says "as the NovaSeq X installed base continues to grow." The Q2'26 10-Q adds only "partially offset by a decrease in mid-throughput consumables" [Sources/ILMN/filing].

5. **"Reduced pricing" disappeared from the gross margin discussion after Q1'25.**
   - FACT: Q1'25 10-Q: Core gross margin was "relatively flat… offset by lower product margins, primarily due to reduced pricing."

Q1'26 reports margin up on "higher product margins". Q2'26 cites instruments mix, memory chips, freight and SomaLogic.
   - OPINION: Per-Gb price declines are now never named as a margin driver, and Q2'26 describes consumables as "higher-margin" without qualification. This is consistent with the X carrying a higher margin even at lower price, but the text gives no price-vs-volume disclosure.
6. **The customer concentration risk factor is new in the FY25 10-K.**
   - FACT: FY25 10-K, line 447: "A portion of our revenue is increasingly derived from a small number of large, centralized laboratory customers… Increased customer concentration may result in greater pricing pressure, reduced negotiating leverage, and increased exposure to credit risk."
   - FACT: The risk-factor heading was also expanded: "…and increasing customer concentration makes us more dependent on key customers." The FY24, FY23 and FY22 10-Ks have the heading without that clause, and "centralized" appears in none of them.
   - FACT: The FY24 10-K already said "We have faced, and expect to continue to face, increased pricing pressure from competitors who offer sequencing products."
   - FACT: No single customer exceeds 10% of revenue in FY23–25.
   - OPINION: This is the first time management has told shareholders in the filings that the customers shifting to the X have bargaining power. It matches the thesis on concentrated clinical customers.
7. **Q1/Q2 2026 contained a large rise in government incentives.**

8. **Gross margin has not benefited from the mix of X consumables in 2026.**

## Pricing, customers and contract terms in the filings
