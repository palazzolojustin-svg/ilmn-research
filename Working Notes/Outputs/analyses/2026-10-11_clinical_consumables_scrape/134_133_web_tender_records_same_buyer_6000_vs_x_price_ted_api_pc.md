## Key findings

**Partial result.** I found two new Spanish tender data points for NovaSeq X pricing. I found no same-buyer NovaSeq 6000 vs X price pair, and no 6000 trade-in or decommission terms. Nothing here confirms or contradicts the "more than 60-70%" per-Gb claim or the 35B ~20% claim.

- **FACT: Barcelona FRCB-IDIBAPS awarded 13 NovaSeq X 25B 300-cycle kits for €208,000 ex-VAT, or €16,000 per kit.** The estimated value was €249,600 (€19,200 per kit), so the award came in 16.7% below the estimate. The buyer is Fundació de Recerca Clínic Barcelona-IDIBAPS. The project is PMPER24/00011, funded by ISCIII and NextGenerationEU. It was published 11 Apr 2025 and awarded 5 Jun 2025 to Diagnostica Longwood S.L., an Illumina distributor in Zaragoza, not to Illumina directly. Award criteria were 50% price and 50% quality. [Publications Office of the EU, procurement summary, fetched 2026-10-11: https://op.europa.eu/en/web/public-procurement/procurement-details/-/procurement/21279da3-5c1f-491d-8c7b-e21c35d651aa]
- **OPINION (calc): that award is about €2.00/Gb awarded, or €2.40/Gb at the estimate.** I assumed ~8,000 Gb per 25B 300-cycle flow cell (Illumina's nominal spec, which I did not re-verify here). The €2.00/Gb is close to Illumina's launch claim of ~$2.00/Gb list for the X (E055). It sits below the ex-Illumina AD's "~$2.50/Gb" for the 25B (E058). It is below the core-facility 25B median of ~$3.11/Gb (E051), which includes labor and overhead. Pricing is in euros and not converted to USD.
- **FACT: the CIBER flow-cell tender has an average estimated value of about €12,800 per flow cell.** CIBER (Centro de Investigación Biomédica en Red) is the buyer. It tendered 155 flow cells plus 70 more for NovaSeq X Plus, a total of 225 and €2,880,000. It was published 28 Jul 2025, closed 2 Sep 2025, and the aggregator showed "awaiting award". The flow-cell type is not stated in the aggregator text, and the €12,800 is derived from the total value, not a published unit price. [bidsonar.com, 2025-07-28: https://bidsonar.com/contracts/494801-2025]
- **OPINION (calc): if the CIBER cells are 25B-300, €12,800 would be about €1.60/Gb.** That is 20% below the IDIBAPS award. This is a plausible reading of a volume-tiered price, since CIBER is 17x the size of the IDIBAPS order, but I could not confirm the type. This is the closest thing to a volume-discount signal, and it is not usable until the pliego is read. Closes E-gap 4 only partially.

It was signed 19 Apr 2024 by Hospital Universitario La Paz (FIBHULP), with delivery through 30 Jun 2027. Sequencing is shared between La Paz and Son Espases. The project is ISCIII-funded (PI22-01743). [Madrid contracts portal, 2024-04-19: https://contratos-publicos.comunidad.madrid/sites/default/files/PCON/2024-04/ppt_pas_17-2024.pdf] The budget and award price are in a separate pliego (cláusulas administrativas) that I did not find.

The value is shown as €1.00, which is a placeholder, so there is no price information. [Publications Office of the EU: https://op.europa.eu/en/web/public-procurement/procurement-details/-/procurement/82eb7f2c-2f02-4f85-8c65-61482f7f69ba]
- **FACT: an old US federal data point exists for a 6000 S4.** HHS contract 75N93020P01105 shows a NovaSeq 6000 S4 v1.5 300-cycle kit committed at $14,688 on 14 Aug 2020. That implies ~$14.7K for ~3,000 Gb, or ~$4.9/Gb, using ~3,000 Gb as the S4 nominal output (an assumption). It is a 2020 federal price, not comparable to Spain 2025. [ProPublica coronavirus-contracts: https://projects.propublica.org/coronavirus-contracts/contracts/75N93020P01105]
