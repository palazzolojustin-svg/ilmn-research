# The most compelling quotes for the ILMN short (2026-10-31)

*A shortlist for the pitch, grouped by argument. Each quote was checked against its source in this session or comes from a verified ledger entry. Use the speaker and date as given. Judges will test anything that sounds like hearsay, so experts are labelled by role.*

## 1. Illumina is buying volume with price, and the price keeps falling

1. **Dhingra, CFO, Q3'26 call (29 Oct 2026):** "Approximately 84% of high-throughput volume and 60% of revenue is now on the X."
   - Why it matters: 84% of the gigabases bring in only 60% of the dollars. Each Gb on the X earns about 30% of what it earned on the 6000.
2. **Thaysen, CEO, Q1'26 call (30 Apr 2026):** "for us, that conversation is based on an elasticity game where if we go in and provide a very aggressive pricing, we also see a very aggressive volume growth… on the 35B pricing, we are not ready at this point to go out and talk about pricing."
3. **Thaysen, CEO, Q3'26 call (29 Oct 2026):** "if you come to Illumina and are willing to sign up for a large volume, we are, of course, very willing to also talk about what the right price point is for that."
4. **Former Illumina product director (expert call, 23 Jul 2026):** "maybe the price would drop by half, but they would only increase their sequencing by about 25%… the price decrease hasn't really caused that acceleration in adoption and sequencing intensity that it had in the past."
5. **Former Illumina product director (expert call, 24 Jul 2026):** "once you're at that $100 genome or a $1 per gigabase, going cheaper just isn't really affecting your bottom line that much… The actual sequencing now might only be 5%-10% of the total cost of some of those workflows. Reducing it another 10%, 20%, 30%, it just isn't really moving the needle."
   - Why it matters: price cuts no longer buy volume, so they just cut revenue.

## 2. The "headwind will fade" promise keeps slipping

6. **Goswami, CFO (JPM, Jan 2024):** "after year one, you don't see that same level of price reduction come through."
7. **Dhingra, CFO (Strategy Update, Aug 2024):** "by the time we get to the mid next year, the impact of pricing should continue to abate."
8. **Dhingra, CFO, Q3'26 call (29 Oct 2026):** "we expect the year-over-year pricing headwinds from the X transition to be lower in 2027 than in 2026."
   - Why it matters: "goes away" in 2024 became "lower in 2027". The end date has moved out by two years.
9. **Thaysen, CEO (TD Cowen, 3 Mar 2026):** "don't take that means that everything is coming out in June '27 or something like that."
   - Then, between 13 and 31 Oct 2026, Illumina's roadmap page quietly moved the 35B, the 14B and Q70 from H1 2027 to H2 2027. Snapshots are saved.

## 3. The next price cut is already coming (35B), and customers are budgeting for it

10. **Customer quoted in a UBS expert-call note (4 Mar 2026):** "Already at $175 / genome with his discounts, and Illumina's new 35B flow cell likely brings Illumina pricing to $150 anyway."
11. **Barclays (6 Aug 2026):** "The 35B flow-cell will need to be priced to compete with this should ILMN want to hold on to its share."
12. **Guardant, CFO Michael Bell (Q2'26 call, 30 Jul 2026):** "we completed the Guardant360 Liquid transition to NovaSeq X in May, reducing cost per test by approximately $200… support our $200 cost per test target in 2028."
13. **NeoGenomics, CFO Abhishek Jain (Q3'26 call, 28 Oct 2026):** "We have not yet started to see the impact of the NovaSeq X transition… 2028 would become the year when we will start to see a more benefit."
    - Why it matters: Illumina's conversion headwind is still in front of it, not behind it.
14. **GeneDx, CFO (Q2'26 call, 3 Aug 2026):** a genome costs "nearly twice as much to produce than exome… almost entirely higher reagent costs." He expects cost per test "relatively flat" in H2'26, with "step improvements in 2027."

## 4. Customers are lining up alternatives

15. **Caris, President David Spetzler (Q2'26 call, 5 Aug 2026):** "There are two competitors out there now against the longstanding sequencing supplier… They create the opportunity to increase our capacity and decrease our cost of goods simultaneously."
16. **VP, Harvard/MIT-affiliated genome center (expert call, 31 Jul 2026):** "We are likely to acquire a couple AXELIOS boxes… We could buy more Illumina NovaSeq X Pluses if more volume appears, but right now, no plans on that."
17. **Brigham and Women's single-cell core (Tegus, ~Oct 2026):** "for a large biotech or pharma using Roche is maybe four or five out of five. It's very highly likely… The presence of Roche would drive the sequencing prices lower."
18. **Former Illumina product director (expert call, 23 Jul 2026):** "because of the competition out there, specifically with companies like Element and Ultima and now Roche, it's harder, just like with those big research customers like the Broad, it's harder to command that price premium. They're seeing quite a bit of price erosion in there as well."

## 5. The growth engine is narrower than it looks

19. **Dhingra, CFO (JPM, Jan 2026), on the "15x" exome-to-genome story:** "the whole genome still makes a smaller fraction of the overall volumes… it's still much, much smaller."
20. **Roche Precision Medicine and Genomics Lead (expert call, 29 Jul 2026), on comprehensive genomic profiling, the biggest oncology use:** "I think the CGP, there is a small amount of growth, but I think it's quite saturated… the markets that have CGP deployed, I think that will be just a very small increase year-on-year."

## Use with care

- **#10 and #14** come from third-party write-ups or transcripts (a UBS note and a Webull transcript). Cite the source.
- **#20 and #17** are single experts. #20 is a Roche employee, so he has a bias.
- **A judge will counter with stickiness.** The PacBio lead says Illumina is "very sticky", the JPM upgrade says the same, and Natera's unit costs are flat. Have the answer ready: sticky volume at a falling price per Gb is still falling revenue per Gb.
