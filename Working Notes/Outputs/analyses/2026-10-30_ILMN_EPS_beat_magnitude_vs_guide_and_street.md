# How Big Were Illumina's EPS Beats? Q4 2024 to Q3 2026

*Prepared 2026-10-30. Data and caveats: `Outputs/datasets/2026-10-30_ILMN_EPS_beat_vs_guide_and_street.csv` and its README. Chart: `Outputs/artifacts/2026-10-30_ILMN_EPS_beat_vs_guide_and_street.png`. Companion revenue analysis: `2026-10-30_ILMN_beat_magnitude_vs_guide_and_street.md`.*

## Answer

Illumina beat EPS in all eight quarters, but the beats have shrunk steadily.

- **Against the Street:** the peak was +18 cents (+17.8%) in Q2'25. Q3'26 was +8 cents (+5.9%), the smallest beat since Q1'25.
- **Against guidance:** the beat fell from about +15–17% in mid-2025 to about +6% in each of the last two quarters.

The revenue beat against guidance collapsed in Q3'26, to +20 bps. The EPS beat held at about 8.5 cents because the 25% operating margin was 100 bps above guide. So the Q3 beat came from cost control, not revenue upside.

## Caveats

- **Q4'24 and Q4'25 had no quarterly guide.** Both midpoints are implied by the full-year guide, and both quarters were pre-announced at JPM in January.
- **Q4'24 is core EPS ($0.95).** The consolidated figure ($0.86) showed as a miss.
- **Q3'26 excludes tariff refunds.**
