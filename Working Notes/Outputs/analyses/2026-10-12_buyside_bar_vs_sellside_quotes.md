# Broker statements on the buy-side bar versus sell-side consensus

*Prepared 12 Oct 2026. Covers all ILMN research notes (Nov 2025 to Sep 2026), the Barclays sector note, 2025–26 calls and conferences, the clinical-consumables scrape files, and a web search (12 Oct). Quotes are verbatim. Key quotes were re-checked against the sources.*

## Bottom line

No broker in the folder, and nothing found on the web, gives a numeric buy-side "whisper" for revenue, EPS, consumables or placements. Only two pieces of evidence quantify where investors sit:

1. **Barclays, 31 Jul 2026.** Investors treat high-single-digit (HSD) 2027 growth as "in the bag" and new buyers must believe it can "touch DD" (double-digit). That is above consensus: Bloomberg FY27 revenue growth is +6.3%, sequencing consumables +8.8%. Barclays itself calls Illumina a mid-single-digit (MSD) business.
2. **Seeking Alpha (Stephen Ayers, independent), 8 Sep 2026.** A reverse DCF of the share price implies a low-double-digit revenue CAGR and free cash flow margins near 30%.

The direction moved over the year. In February the buy-side was more skeptical than the guide and read it as a "market freeze". By July and August the buy-side was above the Street.

The one other numeric investor expectation is J.P. Morgan's: before AGBT, most investors expected Roche to price at about $150 per genome, some as low as $100.
