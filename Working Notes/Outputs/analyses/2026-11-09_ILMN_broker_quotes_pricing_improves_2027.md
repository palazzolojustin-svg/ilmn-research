# Broker quotes: does the Street expect Illumina's pricing to improve next year? (2026-11-09)

*Source: every broker note in `Sources/ILMN/research/`. The newest notes are from 5–6 Aug 2026; there are no post-Q3 (29 Oct) broker notes in the folder.*

## Like-for-like discounting specifically

No broker says like-for-like discounting will improve, meaning price cuts on the same product independent of the 6000-to-X mix shift. No broker separates like-for-like price from mix at all. The bullish brokers attribute all price erosion to the X conversion (quotes 1–4 below). That carries an implicit assumption that like-for-like price holds once conversion is done.

The only brokers that address price on the X itself are bearish:
- **Barclays (7 May 2026), L7:** "Bears are going to point to the in-line consumables revs and what appears to be lower price/Gb on NSX volumes." At L224, Barclays reports that management called the quarter-to-quarter price-per-Gb analysis "a frivolous exercise" and blamed mix.
- **Barclays (6 Aug 2026), L13:** "continued price concessions, bundling, and favorable terms."
- **UBS expert-call write-up (4 Mar 2026):** a customer is "already at $175 / genome with his discounts, and Illumina's new 35B flow cell likely brings Illumina pricing to $150 anyway." This describes the next like-for-like step down.

## Brokers expecting the price headwind to fade

1. **JPMorgan, upgrade to Overweight (15 Jun 2026), L165:** clinical labs "have transitioned (or are in the process of transitioning) testing to the NovaSeq X… with the associated pricing headwinds largely gone exiting this year."
2. **JPMorgan, Q1'26 recap (6 May 2026), L174:** "with >90% of research and applied volume already transitioned to NovaSeq X, management expects the associated pricing headwinds to ease through the year." (This relays management's view.)
3. **JPMorgan, Q4'25 recap (11 Feb 2026), L173:** "the NovaSeq X pricing headwinds will begin to ease for its research customers in 2H26." (Also relaying management.)
4. **Freedom Broker (6 May and 5 Aug 2026), catalyst list, L208 / L228:** "Completion of the NovaSeq X transition. Finalizing the clinical migration and normalization of demand in research should help mitigate the impact of pricing erosion."
5. **Bloomberg Intelligence (Jan 2026, repeated Jul 2026), L31 / L109:** "Sequencing consumables look to be at the inflection point where the transition to the NovaSeq X is driving enough volume to offset pricing headwinds."

## The counter view

- **Barclays (12 Feb 2026), L9:** "With Roche supposedly coming in at $100/genome, we think that there could be another round of pricing HWs facing the business in '27 and beyond, which would weigh on outyear growth."
- **Barclays (6 Aug 2026), L13:** "this ignores the competitive landscape that will require continued price concessions, bundling, and favorable terms to fend off more material share losses."

## The expectation built into the numbers

Bloomberg consensus (10 Oct 2026) has sequencing consumables growing +8.8% in 2027. If gigabase volume grows about 30%, that implies revenue per Gb falls about 16% (1.088 ÷ 1.30 − 1). The Q3'26 implied decline was about 19%. So consensus assumes price erosion eases by about 3 points. This is our back-solve; no broker models price per Gb.
