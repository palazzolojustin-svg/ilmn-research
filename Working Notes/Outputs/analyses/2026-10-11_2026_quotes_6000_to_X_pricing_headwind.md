# 2026 quotes on the NovaSeq 6000-to-X pricing headwind

*Prepared 11 Oct 2026. Every 2026 statement in the folder about the pricing headwind from customers moving work from the NovaSeq 6000 to the NovaSeq X. Quotes are copied verbatim from `Working Notes/Sources/ILMN/`. Ellipses, bracketed notes and [sic] marks are ours. Transcripts are Seeking Alpha prints, so garbled transcription is reproduced as printed and flagged.*

## What the quotes show

Brokers wrote about the conversion headwind in 2026, but almost never in numbers:

- **J.P. Morgan** is the only firm with a figure. It cites a "-DD%" (double-digit) price decline as history, and calls the clinical transition pricing headwind "largely gone exiting this year".
- **Barclays** is the only firm arguing the opposite. It suspects "lower price/Gb on NSX volumes" and possible discounting.
- **Everyone else** (Freedom, Ameriprise, Bloomberg Intelligence) relays management's conversion percentages and describes the headwind in words.

On the calls and at conferences, analysts asked the pointed questions: Souda on the initial price hit, Peterson on "over-earning", Nambi and Mikson on 6000 pull-through and the stalled revenue share. Management answered each time without a number.

Background terms:
- **Gb (gigabase):** a billion DNA letters, the unit of sequencing output.
- **HT:** high-throughput.
- **Pull-through:** annual consumables revenue per installed instrument.
- **NSX:** Barclays' shorthand for NovaSeq X.
