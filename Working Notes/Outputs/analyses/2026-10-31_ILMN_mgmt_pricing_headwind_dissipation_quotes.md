# Every time management said the conversion pricing headwind would fade (Jan 2024 – Oct 2026)

*2026-10-31. These are all the management statements in the folder's Illumina earnings calls and conference transcripts saying the 6000-to-X pricing headwind would fade, ease, abate or dissipate. Each was checked against the transcript; file and line references are given. Analyst questions are left out unless needed for context.*

## The pattern

Management has promised the headwind would fade for nearly three years, and the date has kept moving out:

| When said | Who | Promised timing |
|---|---|---|
| Jan 2024 | Goswami (CFO) | After "year one" the price drop "goes away" |
| Aug 2024 | Dhingra (CFO) | "by the time we get to the mid next year" (mid-2025) |
| Nov 2024 | Dhingra | "by second half of 2025… largely annualized" |
| Jan 2025 | Dhingra | 2H25 "should begin to abate" |
| May 2025 | Dhingra | "over time… fades away" (no date) |
| Oct 2025 | Thaysen / Dhingra | Research only: "pricing headwinds easing"; clinical still "conversion pricing headwinds" |
| Feb 2026 | Dhingra | Research: "especially in the second half of 2026" |
| Jan 2026 | Dhingra | 2026 "should be a lesser impact than '25" |
| Mar 2026 | Thaysen | "dissipate… throughout the year"; "shouldn't be worse than '25" |
| Apr 2026 | Thaysen | "the price headwind is getting behind us" |
| Oct 2026 | Dhingra | Research: "minimal". Clinical: "lower in 2027 than in 2026" |

The language has also weakened over time. It went from "goes away" (2024), to "largely annualized" (2H25), to "lesser impact than '25" (2026), to "lower in 2027 than in 2026". The latest promise is only a *smaller* headwind in 2027, a full year later than first stated. At no point did management put a number on it.

## Quotes in date order

### 2024

**Joydeep Goswami, CFO, J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference, 9 Jan 2024** (`conference/ILMN_2024-01-09_J.P._Morgan_42nd_Annual_Healthcare_Conference.md` L327)
> "You're going to see the growth in sequencing offset a little bit by the drop in price. But in subsequent years, that goes away. So, you keep seeing the elasticity go up from multiple over multiple years. But after year one, you don't see that same level of price reduction come through. So that's just a little bit of the dynamic that explains why we're seeing elasticity, but not seeing that revenue go up."

**Ankur Dhingra, CFO, Q2 2024 call, 6 Aug 2024, prepared remarks** (`earnings_call/ILMN_2024Q2_Earnings_Call.md` L144)
> "In terms of pace of shifting mix from 6K to X, to date, on average, roughly 5 percentage points of high-throughput sequencing consumables revenue has moved from NovaSeq 6000 to NovaSeq X PLUS each quarter. As this transition progresses, the impact of price continues to reduce. If this trajectory holds, almost half of high throughput sequencing consumables revenue should transition to the NovaSeq X by mid-2025, continuing to reduce the impact of pricing transition and converting volume growth into higher revenue growth thereafter."

**Dhingra, same call, Q&A (Tycho Peterson)** (L392)
> "The 2025 references, half of the revenue should transition over into the X. And with every 5, 7-point mix, the impact of the price continues to go down and the contribution of that volume into revenue continues to increase."

**Dhingra, Strategy Update, 13 Aug 2024** (`conference/ILMN_2024-08-13_Illumina_2024_Strategy_Update_Special_Call.md` L378, L614, L682)
> "As more volume moves to X overtime, the impact of price reduces and we see a higher portion of that volume translate into revenue. This underlying demand combined with reduced impact of client's transition gives us confidence in our high-throughput consumables growth moving forward."

> "We have achieved one milestone here, anticipating hitting the second milestone of getting half of the revenue in X by about mid-next year or so and the impact of pricing. So the volume been growing very, very well. And by the time we get to the mid next year, the impact of pricing should continue to abate as the volumes mix."

> "But as X transitions, as the impact of price comes down, we will get a much larger share of that volume growth flowing into our revenue. After a point with our clinical customers, it's going to be a long tail…"

**Dhingra, Wolfe Research conference, 20 Nov 2024** (`conference/ILMN_2024-11-20_CFO_Ankur_Dhingra_Hosts_Wolfe_Research_2024_Healthcare_Conference.md` L332)
> "The way I'm tracking at it is still at the aggregate level to say, by second half of 2025, if -- by that time, if we can get more than 75% of the volume moved over to X, we will start getting to a point where the price transition is getting largely annualized, right? It is in the denominators and a larger part of our volume growth should start flowing into the revenue and gets us closer and closer towards that high-single-digit growth model that we've set for our three years."

**Dhingra, same conference** (L368)
> "During the year, our thinking is still the same that we should have the impact of the transition is higher during the first half and by the time we get to the second half, the pricing impact on the transition should begin to reduce given that a larger set of the customers of the volume should have moved to X… So '25 generally thinking half one higher impact, half two it should begin to abate. We should begin to see signs of that reduced impact."

**Dhingra, Q4 2024 call, 6 Feb 2025, Q&A** (`earnings_call/ILMN_2024Q4_Earnings_Call.md` L218)
> "Our top line is -- top line growth is more towards -- skewed towards the second half of the year. As you make the transition to X and the pricing transition expecting to come down a little bit."

**Dhingra, Q1 2025 call, 8 May 2025, prepared remarks** (`earnings_call/ILMN_2025Q1_Earnings_Call.md` L138)
> "We continue to make progress in the framework we previously disclosed. That in the second half of 2025, approximately 50% of high throughput revenue and approximately 75% of GB shipped will be on the NovaSeq X series. With continued strong underlying sequencing volume growth and strong adoption of X, over time, the price effect of lowered mix of 6K consumables fades away, and a much larger part of high GB growth translates to revenue growth."

**Thaysen, Q3 2025 call, 30 Oct 2025, prepared remarks** (`earnings_call/ILMN_2025Q3_Earnings_Call.md` L124)

**Dhingra, same call, prepared remarks** (L204)
> "Looking ahead to 2026, we see 3 key trends. First, in clinical, we expect dynamics similar to this year, strong volume growth and continued transition to X. Second, in research and applied, we anticipate conditions to remain muted, consistent with the latter half of 2025, with pricing headwinds easing now that 91% of high throughput volume has transitioned to X."

**Dhingra, same call, Q&A** (L628)
> "In terms of the research market looking forward, you have it right in the sense vast majority of our -- the volumes in the research market have transitioned to X, implying that as and when those markets return to growth or even in a stable environment, the actual pricing headwind should start to dissipate from that and resulting into a better revenue performance or revenue growth performance."

**Thaysen, Wolfe Research conference, 19 Nov 2025** (`conference/ILMN_2025-11-19_7th_Annual_Wolfe_Research_Healthcare_Conference.md` L208)
> "when we lay out the strategy, the whole underlying logic in the strategy was that the main driver is going to be the transition to the X. And we continue to see that as the main driver. We have to -- the faster we can get through the transition, the faster we get the pricing, of course, situation behind us."

### 2026

**Thaysen, J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference, 13 Jan 2026** (`conference/ILMN_2026-01-13_44th_Annual_J.P._Morgan_Healthcare_Conference.md` L212)
> "Now we have approximately 80% of the volume. You're starting to see the impact of the price transition be lower than actually the volume performance. So we -- the math is working and it's working according to the expectations."

**Dhingra, same conference** (L292)
> "On the X transition side, it's great to get to a point where we're past the 75% mark, now being into the 80s. And we expect that the price effect of that transition would continue to reduce over time, should be a lesser impact than '25, going into '26 per se."

**Dhingra, Q4 2025 call, 5 Feb 2026, prepared remarks** (`earnings_call/ILMN_2025Q4_Earnings_Call.md` L174)
> "The research transition is nearing its end, as now roughly 90% of the high-throughput sequencing volumes for these customers have transitioned to X. As a result, we expect pricing headwinds from the transition to continue to dissipate for our research customers, especially in the second half of 2026. Clinical volume is now more than two-thirds converted to the X, and we believe pricing dynamics going forward will be tied more to new applications like whole-genome sequencing adoption, which drives higher volumes."

**Thaysen, TD Cowen conference, 3 Mar 2026** (`conference/ILMN_2026-03-03_TD_Cowen_46th_Annual_Health_Care_Conference.md` L158)
> "I mean the transition is still going to happen here in '26. So there's definitely still some pricing headwind. It should start to dissipate over the -- throughout the year. So it shouldn't be worse than '25."

**Thaysen, Q1 2026 call, 30 Apr 2026, Q&A** (`earnings_call/ILMN_2026Q1_Earnings_Call.md` L298, L464)
> "And as we also reminded ourselves is that we are now had-have converted most of our Xs in the research business. So when this market comes back, we're not seeing the price headwind as we did before, and thereby, we will see -- we can see growth coming that way."

> "And since we are, of course, transitioning more and more of the revenue on to the consumables business over to the X platform, the price headwind is getting behind us, and we continue to see that being strong growth."

**Dhingra, Q3 2026 call, 29 Oct 2026, prepared remarks** (`earnings_call/ILMN_2026Q3_Earnings_Call.md` L77, L79)
> "Approximately 84% of high-throughput volume and 60% of revenue is now on the X. In research and applied markets, 90% or greater volume has been on the X for each of the last 5 quarters, signaling the conversion to X is largely complete. Thus, we expect minimal transition pricing headwinds from these customers going forward."

> "In clinical, 78% of volume is currently on the X, and we have seen the pace of conversion slow in recent quarters… We anticipate clinical conversion to reach 80% to 85% in the coming quarters. And as such, we expect the year-over-year pricing headwinds from the X transition to be lower in 2027 than in 2026."

## Caveats

- **Q2 2026 call.** Management made no explicit statement there on the headwind fading. It was asked about "over-earning" on the 6000 and called it "a standard part of the business."
- **"Lesser" is not "gone."** Several statements promise a smaller headwind, not an end to it (Jan 2026, Mar 2026, Oct 2026). The 2024 statements were stronger ("goes away", "largely annualized").
- **Research versus clinical.** From late 2025, the "dissipate/minimal" language is confined to research. For clinical, the most management has said is "lower in 2027 than in 2026."
