# The NovaSeq X 35B flow cell: strongest evidence in the corpus (2026-10-31)

*This note ranks every piece of evidence we hold on the 35B. It draws on two agent sweeps (primary sources, and our own scrapes and evidence ledger), with the key items re-checked against the source files.*

## Update, 31 Oct 2026 (late): Illumina quietly pushed the 35B back to H2 2027

**Where this comes from.** I checked for ASHG news (20–24 Oct, Montreal). Illumina issued no 35B announcement or price. But Illumina's own roadmap page has changed:
- **Wayback copies from 4 Mar, 16 Jun, 16 Sep and 13 Oct 2026** all list "H2 2026: HT mode increases reads for better sequencing economics" and "H1 2027: Q70 duplex/Q50 simplex; 14B; 35B flow cell".
- **The live page on 31 Oct** has only two headings, H1 2026 and **H2 2027**. Q70, the 14B and the 35B all now sit under H2 2027, and the HT mode item is gone.
- Snapshots are saved in `Outputs/datasets/2026-10-31_ILMN_roadmap_page_snapshots/`.

**When and how.** The change was made between 13 Oct and 31 Oct, around the Q3 call (29 Oct), where management did not mention it. Search indexes still show the old H1 2027 version, so as far as we can tell it has not been reported.

**What it means for the thesis.** The effect is mixed:

- *For the 2027 pricing math (cuts against the short).* A 35B in H2 2027 hits fewer months of 2027, so the modeled 2027 price-per-Gb hit from the 35B shrinks, maybe by half or more. The HT mode removal also takes away a hidden H2 2026 price-per-Gb cut. The 2027 revenue-at-risk figures below should be scaled down for timing.
- *For competitiveness and credibility (supports the short).*
  - Illumina's answer to Roche AXELIOS ($150 genome) and to Ultima and Element is now six months later.
  - Q70 is the accuracy upgrade Illumina pitched for MRD (tests for leftover cancer), the fastest-growing clinical application, and it is delayed too.
  - The roadmap was changed silently, two weeks after Illumina's own page promised H1 2027 and around a call where the 35B was asked about directly.
  - It fits Barclays' line about "inherent technological barriers" (6 Aug 2026).
- *For 2028.* The 35B price cut moves rather than disappears, so the full-year hit lands in 2028.

**Item 1 below is superseded on timing:** read "H2 2027" for "H1 2027".

## Bottom line

No 35B price exists anywhere: not on Illumina's order page, in a tender or distributor listing, on a core rate card, or in a customer quote. Three things are hard facts:

- **The product and its timing.** Illumina's roadmap and press release give H1 2027 and about 40% more output than the 25B.
- **Management's refusal to price it.** Thaysen declined to give a price three times, in Mar, Apr and Oct 2026.
- **A cap on Illumina's largest contract.** Natera's supply contract stops a new flow cell from raising its price per Gb.

The size of any cut, about 20–29% per Gb, rests on one former employee's guess and one UBS calculation. Present it as an estimate, not a fact.

## Tier 1: hard facts

**1. What the 35B is and when it arrives.**
- Illumina's innovation-roadmap page lists the 35B and 14B for "H1 2027".
- The 23 Feb 2026 press release gives the 35B 40% more output than the 25B, with no price (E214, E057).
- It is a new flow cell for the existing NovaSeq X installed base, not a new instrument. Customers can switch with no capital purchase, so it can replace 25B volume quickly.
- "January 2027" comes only from a customer's hearsay (31 Jul 2026 expert) and should not be cited.

**2. Management has refused to price it three times.**
- **TD Cowen, 3 Mar 2026:** "No, we have not priced that yet… we're not going to price it before we are ready to launch it… We don't see any value in coming out too early with pricing."
- **Q1'26 call, 30 Apr 2026 (L528):** "if we go in and provide a very aggressive pricing, we also see a very aggressive volume growth… on the 35B pricing, we are not ready at this point to go out and talk about pricing."
- **Q3'26 call, 29 Oct 2026 (L241 onward):** Stifel asked whether the 35B is needed to win large projects against $50 genomes. Thaysen answered "We love elasticity. We can address any opportunity that we need to," with no 35B detail.
- The pricing language consistently points to trading price for volume.

**3. Natera's contract caps price per Gb on new products** (E212; Natera Q1'26 10-Q Ex. 10.1, effective 2 Mar 2026).
- §7(l): new product versions must keep a comparable "inflation-adjusted… price per gigabase of sequencing".
- §7(k): bars further inflation-adjusted price increases.
- Our correction pass confirmed this is a ceiling, not a floor. A 35B cannot reset Natera's price per Gb upward, and nothing protects Illumina's revenue per Gb.
- If Illumina prices the 35B lower per Gb, the largest clinical customers get the benefit. If it doesn't, they can't be charged more.

**4. Roche has set a price umbrella.**
- AXELIOS lists at $150 per genome with "no volume-based discounting" (Roche Investor Day, 12 May 2026; launched 29 Jun 2026).
- Illumina's list is about $200 per genome (JPM, 3 Mar 2026).
- Element (~$100 genome) and Ultima ($50–100) sit lower still, per expert and broker reports.

## Tier 2: estimates of the cut (medium strength)

**5. UBS: a "path to ~$150/genome"** if the 35B is "priced consistent with the 25B flow cell" (UBS First Read, 3 Mar 2026, L17).
- Read as the same price per flow cell for 40% more output, that is about a 29% cut in price per Gb ($200 to about $143).
- UBS's expert call (4 Mar 2026, L38) added that the customer is "Already at $175 / genome with his discounts, and Illumina's new 35B flow cell likely brings Illumina pricing to $150 anyway."

**6. Former Illumina associate director, 23 Jul 2026.**
- Size of the cut: "They may be down to like $2.50 per gigabase, and maybe that drops to $2 flat with this 35B" (L151). He was more definite at L555: "that is going to be a pricing decrease. They're definitely going to do that."
- On demand: "is dropping the price by 20% going to spur excitement and huge demand? Probably not" (L161).
- He is the only source for a price-per-Gb figure. It is speculative, from someone who left Illumina in Jul 2025.

**7. Barclays, 6 Aug 2026 (L15):** "The 35B flow-cell will need to be priced to compete with this should ILMN want to hold on to its share, but the inherent technological barriers could ultimately prevent this." Barclays gives no numbers. No broker models the 35B (E411).

## Tier 3: supporting context

**8. Price per Gb is already falling without a new list price** (E261).
- A Texas A&M customer quoted on Illumina's roadmap page: "usable output per run has increased by roughly 30 percent". At an unchanged flow-cell price, that is about 23% cheaper per Gb.
- The roadmap also lists an "HT mode" for H2 2026 that "increase[s] reads for better sequencing economics".

**9. Core labs are already cutting 25B and 10B prices** (core-lab dataset, Mar–Oct 2026).
- 25B: UConn −50%, Northwestern −14%, UCSD −9%.
- 10B: Kansas −40%, Case Western −29%.
- This shows price pressure on the 25B base before the 35B arrives. Cores resell, though, so they are an imperfect proxy for clinical contract prices.

## What the cut would cost (our calculations)

- **Break-even volume.** A 20% cut in price per Gb needs 25% more Gb just to hold revenue flat.
  - Stacked on management's mid-teens price decline, the effective cut is about 32%, so Gb would need to grow about 47%.
  - That figure may double-count, if the mid-teens decline already includes the 35B. Management hasn't said.
- **FY27 revenue at risk** at constant Gb, from a 20% cut:
  - about $77M at 25% adoption
  - about $153M at 50% (25B customers only)
  - about $306M at full adoption
- **Clinical 2027 base case.** With conversion to 87%, the 35B at −20% on half of X volume, and −5% residual decline, clinical Gb must grow about 27% for flat clinical high-throughput revenue.
- **Our 27E variance** (2026-10-18): a modest 35B step-up in discounting costs about $27M of revenue, within a total of about −$87M against street, or about −8% EPS.

## What cuts against the short

- **Revenue per flow cell can rise.** If price per Gb falls 20% on 40% more output, each flow cell sells for about 12% more than a 25B. Unless volume shifts away, revenue per flow cell goes up.
- **Big accounts are already below list.** At $125–175 per genome against a $200 list (UBS expert), their effective cut from the 35B may be smaller than the list-price arithmetic suggests.
- **Clinical demand is sticky.** JPM (15 Jun 2026) says pricing "alone does not seem to be enough to fully convert customers," so Illumina may not need a deep defensive cut in clinical.
- **The 35B won't reach every customer.** It is a very large flow cell, and only high-volume labs can fill it. Small labs, which are struggling to fill even the 25B, won't adopt it.
- **The cut is not yet announced.** It could be smaller or later than modeled, or offered only to large contracts.

## Gaps

- No 35B price, list price per Gb, or per-genome price.
- No dated launch.
- No information on which customers or contracts get it.
- No management figure for its effect on revenue per Gb.
- Our last check for a listing was 11–12 Oct, before ASHG (the American Society of Human Genetics conference). An announcement at ASHG (20–24 Oct) has not been checked.
