# NovaSeq X vs NovaSeq 6000: Share of Volume, Share of Revenue and Revenue per Gb, Q4 2024 to Q3 2026

*Prepared 2026-10-30. Data: `Outputs/datasets/2026-10-30_ILMN_X_vs_6000_share_and_rev_per_gb.csv` (see its README). Chart: `Outputs/artifacts/2026-10-30_ILMN_X_vs_6000_share_and_rev_per_gb.png`. Background on why the ratio widened: `2026-10-12_6000_vs_X_ratio_widening_decomposition.md`.*

## Answer

In Q3 2026 the NovaSeq 6000 carried 16% of Illumina's high-throughput volume but 40% of its high-throughput revenue. Per Gb, that means the 6000 earned about **3.5x** what the X earned. The gap has widened from about 2.8–2.9x through mid-2025. It has held at about 3.3–3.7x since Q3 2025, peaking at 3.7x in Q1 2026.

The volume shift is nearly done: the X's share of volume rose from 66% to 84%. The revenue shift lags well behind, rising from 40% to 60%. In other words, about 24 points of Illumina's high-throughput revenue still sit on 16% of the volume. As that tail moves to the X, each Gb that moves earns roughly 70% less.

## Caveats

- **Rounding.** Shares are rounded to whole points, so each ratio is good to about ±0.2x. The Q1'26 peak (3.5–3.9x) and the Q2'26 dip (3.2–3.6x) are within that noise of each other.
- **Management's view.** It says research and applied conversion is complete (90%+ of volume on the X for five quarters). Clinical is at 78% and expected to reach 80–85%. On that basis it expects a smaller transition price headwind in 2027 than in 2026.
- **Disclosure ends.** Illumina will stop showing these shares after Q4 2026, so this series ends with Q4 2026.
