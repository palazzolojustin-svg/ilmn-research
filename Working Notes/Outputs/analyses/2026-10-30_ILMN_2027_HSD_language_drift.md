# How management's language on 2027 high-single-digit growth has softened (Mar–Oct 2026)

*Prepared 30 Oct 2026, from a direct search of the Q4'25 to Q3'26 calls and the Jan and Mar 2026 conferences. Paths are under `Working Notes/Sources/ILMN/`. All entries are paraphrases. Open the cited line for the exact wording.*

## Summary

Management's language on 2027 peaked in March 2026. At TD Cowen, Thaysen said Illumina should "easily" grow high single digits, and "at least" high single digits even against heavy competition.

By the Q3'26 call (29 Oct), the commitment had shrunk:

- The goal was described as "in that range."
- Details were deferred to the Q4 call.
- Management said it wanted to "show we can do" high single digits before talking about more.
- Asked directly whether the long-range targets were intact, the CEO did not say yes.
- The margin answer cited the 400 bps already achieved, not the 26% target.

## Context

- The Q3'26 analyst who asked about the 2027 framework (`:141`) noted that the Q4 jump-off implies roughly 6% RoW growth.
- Getting from about 6% to high single digits needs the 1–2 points of new-product contribution that management now hedges.
