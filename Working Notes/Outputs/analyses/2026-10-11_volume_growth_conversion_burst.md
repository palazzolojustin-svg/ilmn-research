# Is part of the >30% volume growth a one-time conversion burst?

Prepared 11 Oct 2026, in answer to: "Part of the ~30% volume growth comes from conversions and new placements. When conversions wind down, volume growth will slow if each conversion sparks a one-time burst. Tell me when management guides this."

Sources: ILMN calls Q2'23–Q2'26; Strategy Day Aug 2024; Wolfe Nov 2024 and Nov 2025; Bernstein May 2025; JPM conference Jan 2026; JPM note and customer survey Jun 2026; expert calls Jul–Aug 2026; evidence ledger E027, E204, E303.

## Short answer

Partly right, and management has already signalled the slowdown: it puts long-run whole-business volume growth at ~25%, against ">30%" now. But there are two caveats.
- **Current placements are not mainly conversions.** Management says recent clinical placements are "mostly incremental", meaning new capacity rather than 6000 replacements.
- **Conversion volume was close to revenue-neutral.** Volume offset price in year one, so when conversions end, the price drag ends too.

The variable that matters is underlying, non-conversion volume growth (~15–25%) against continuing X price erosion. Management gives no numeric 2027 volume or conversion guide.

## Evidence for a conversion burst

- **Reported growth spiked during research's conversion.** Volume grew +29% in Q3'23, +46% in Q4'23, >35% in Q1'24 and >40% in Q2–Q3'24, while research customers moved to the X. Since Q4'24 it has been reported only as ">30%", a floor that would hide any slowdown.
- **Research went quiet once converted.** Research volume was "robust" through Q1'25, "muted" from Q2'25 (~80% converted) and "below 30%" in Q3'25. This is confounded by NIH funding cuts.
- **Converted customers needed big volume jumps.** For 40 converted clinical customers, revenue held flat in year one despite a 60–70% price cut. That needs about 2.5–3.3x the gigabases, though much of it is new tests rather than like-for-like moves [ledger E303].
- **Illustrative arithmetic.** High-throughput (HT) gigabase share on the X went from 69% to 83% in a year. If converted work runs at roughly 2–3x its old volume, conversion step-ups explain ~8–17 points of the ~33-point growth. That contribution shrinks as the remaining 6000 pool (17% of HT gigabases, a fifth of which may never move) runs out [our calculation].
- **Management and customers point lower.** Long-run volume is "mid-20%" (Bernstein, May 2025) and "approximately 25%" for the whole business (JPM, Jan 2026). JPM's customer survey expects only +11–19% volume growth in 2027–28.
- **Experts agree.** One former Illumina manager (left 2024) says customers "sequenced everything in the freezer"; another says earlier price cuts brought "5X to 10X more" sequencing from pent-up demand.

## Evidence against

- **Recent clinical placements add capacity.** They are "mostly incremental… rather than conversions of 6000s" (Q1'26 call). That reflects test demand, not a one-time burst.
- **Converted cohorts grow in year two.** Revenue and volume both "accelerated in year 2" (Q3'25 deck), though early converters are the most motivated, a selection bias. Illumina said in January it will stop reporting cohorts.
- **Clinical has real underlying drivers.** Larger panels, exome-to-genome (~15x data), MRD and therapy selection. Management says clinical stays "above 30%" for "a while".

## The revenue twist

Conversion volume was close to revenue-neutral, so its fading removes both the volume boost and the price drag. Revenue then depends on underlying volume growth against price erosion on the X:

| Underlying volume growth | X price per Gb | Approx. revenue growth |
|---|---|---|
| +25% (management) | −10% (35B year) | +12% |
| +20% | −10% | +8% |
| +15% (survey) | −10% | +3–4% |
| +15% | −15% | −2% |

Consensus 2027 sequencing consumables growth of +8.8% therefore needs underlying volume of about +20% or more alongside a 35B-sized price cut.

## Follow-up: is the buy-side clinical bar out of reach even on management's numbers?

**Not quite.** On management's own inputs, total sequencing consumables grow about +12%, above consensus (+8.8%). For clinical specifically, the outcome turns on price per gigabase, not volume.

Clinical revenue per gigabase has fallen about 20–28% over the past year. That figure includes the remaining 6000-to-X conversion, which is still running in clinical (78% converted, against ~90% in research).

The grid below shows clinical revenue growth for each combination of clinical volume and clinical revenue per Gb [our calculation].

| Clinical volume growth | Rev/Gb −10% | −15% | −20% |
|---|---|---|---|
| +25% | +12.5% | +6% | 0% |
| +30% (management: "above 30%") | +17% | +10.5% | +4% |
| +35% | +21.5% | +15% | +8% |

The buy-side bar for clinical is roughly mid-teens. RBC calls it "durable mid-teens", and with research at −2% consensus implies ~15–17%. Hitting it needs about 30% volume with a −10% price decline, or 35% volume with −15%.

- **Is −10% a reasonable assumption?** Only if the conversion drag and the price cut on the X are both mild. The price decline within the X was only about −4% in H1'26. But in 2027 the 35B (about −20% per Gb for big users) arrives, the clinical tail keeps converting (~$90–130M of drag), and contracts only let prices fall. A clinical revenue-per-Gb decline of −12% to −18% looks likelier.
- **Our base:** ~30% volume and about −15% price gives ~+10–12%. That is 3–6 points short of the bar.
- **The disagreement with the bulls is about price, not volume.**

## Measurement caveat

The metric covers gigabases on "connected" high- and mid-throughput instruments; China's machines are not connected. No source says whether X units are connected more often than 6000s. If they are, conversions would mechanically inflate measured growth. This is worth asking management.

## When management will disclose relevant data

There is no numeric 2027 volume or conversion guide today; management is only "committed" to high-single-digit revenue growth. The checkpoints:

1. **Q3'26 call and deck, 29 Oct 2026:**
   - the volume-growth wording (does ">30%" survive?);
   - clinical % on the X (78% in Q2; end-2026 target 80–85%, though the CEO said "over 85%" in Q1);
   - X share of HT gigabases and revenue;
   - placements.
2. **JPM Healthcare Conference, mid-January 2027 (date unverified):** preliminary FY26 installed base and pull-through, and often long-range volume commentary.
3. **Q4'26 results, early February 2027 (date unverified):** the FY27 guide, placement framework, pull-through guide by platform, 6000 decommissions and the X base. This is the first likely explicit 2027 framework.
4. **35B launch, H1 2027:** pricing.
