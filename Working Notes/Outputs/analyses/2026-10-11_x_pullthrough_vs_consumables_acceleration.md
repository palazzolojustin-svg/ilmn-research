# NovaSeq X pull-through vs the consumables-acceleration thesis

Prepared 11 Oct 2026, in answer to: "The second derivative of pull-through growth seems to be slowing, while the buy side believes a huge consumables acceleration is coming."

All quarterly X and NovaSeq 6000 dollar figures are our derivations [Der], not company disclosures. They rest on high-throughput (HT) being ~69% of sequencing consumables and on the rounded X share of HT revenue (43%, 44%, 51%, 55%, 55%, 59%). Installed bases are rolled from disclosed placements, with ~890 X at end-2025. This method understated the disclosed FY25 X pull-through by ~3–5%.

## 1. Per-machine X pull-through: flat to down, with no ramp yet

The table shows the X's quarterly consumables revenue converted to an annual rate per installed machine, using the average installed base for each quarter.

| Quarter | Avg X base | X consumables $M | Annualised pull-through | y/y |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1'25 | 660 | 206 | $1.25M | |
| Q2'25 | 715 | 225 | $1.26M | |
| Q3'25 | 768 | 263 | $1.37M | |
| Q4'25 | 842 | 287 | $1.36M | |
| Q1'26 | 930 | 276 | $1.19M | −4.9% |
| Q2'26 | 1,018 | 316 | $1.24M | −1.3% |

- **Per box, there is no ramp yet.** Pull-through peaked in Q3–Q4'25 and is below last year in both 2026 quarters.
- **The second derivative turned up, not down.** The y/y change improved from −4.9% to −1.3%, but from a negative level. Two quarters of rounded data are noisy.
- **All of the X's growth is installed-base growth.** X consumables rose 34% and 40% y/y while the average base grew 41% and 42%.
- **Flat dollars per box while each box sequences more is the price-erosion signal.** Gigabases are growing above 30%, so the X's price per gigabase is falling.

## 2. Why consensus can still assume acceleration without per-box acceleration

HT consumables grew only 4.4% and 4.7% in Q1–Q2'26. X revenue rose 34–40%, but the NovaSeq 6000 fell 18–23% and was still ~41% of HT dollars. As the 6000's share shrinks, its decline subtracts less, so the same X growth produces faster total growth. That is mechanical, not a demand inflection.

Much of 2027's base growth is also already locked in. The average X base should rise roughly 30% in 2027 from machines placed during 2026.

## 3. What 2027 needs

The scenarios below use FY26 HT of ~$2.15B (X ~$1.29B, 6000 ~$0.86B) and hold mid- and low-throughput consumables flat.

| Scenario | X base growth | X pull-through | 6000 change | HT growth | Seq. consumables growth |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bear (35B cuts, new boxes run light) | +25% | −5% | −25% | +1% | +1% |
| Our base | +28% | flat | −20% | +9% | +6% |
| Consensus-like | +30% | +2% | −18% | +12% | +8.5% |
| Bull (new boxes ramp) | +30% | +5% | −15% | +16% | +11% |

Consensus 2027 sequencing consumables growth of +8.8% needs HT to grow about 12%. That means X pull-through holding or rising while the base grows ~30% and the 6000 declines no faster than ~18%. The 2026 evidence (pull-through −1% to −5%, 6000 −18% to −23%) sits closer to our base case.

## 3b. What drives consensus's acceleration (H1'26 +4.5% to FY27 +8.8%)?

**Question:** is the acceleration from the placement wave and from China normalising?

**Answer:** the placement wave, yes; China, no. Five pieces sit behind the acceleration:

1. **The placement wave (the main stated reason).**
   - About 280 X systems were placed in Q4'25–Q2'26, around half adding clinical capacity.
   - The CFO says "most of that benefit will come in 2027" because new boxes need 6–9 months to ramp. At full use that is roughly $300M a year of consumables, about 10% of the line, before price [working file 03, B5d].
   - **Our view:** real, but the first half shows the X's per-box pull-through flat to down so far.
2. **China is not normalising.**
   - Consensus has Greater China falling further: $243M (FY25) to $211M (FY26E), $189M (FY27E) and $187M (FY28E). Quarterly y/y is −14%, −16%, −9% and −6% through 2027.
   - The only help is a smaller drag: China's subtraction from growth drops from about 1 point to about 0.5 point.
3. **The price drag from the 6000 tail fades.** Once most work is on the X, the mix-driven fall in revenue per gigabase shrinks, so the same volume growth yields more revenue (section 2).
   - **Our view:** right for the tail, but the 35B and contracts move price pressure onto the X itself.
4. **Research gets easier comparisons.** After two years of declines, consensus implicitly has research flat to +5%, depending on the clinical assumption.
   - **Our view:** too optimistic. We use about −2%.
5. **New products** (Protein Prep, single cell, TruPath) book mostly in consumables. Consensus includes little visibly.

**One factor works against 2027:** the 53rd week in Q4'26 inflates the 2026 base.

**Does the placement wave account for ~10 points of 2027 clinical growth?** No. The ~$300M is a rough full-use run-rate, about 10% of total sequencing consumables, not of clinical [03 B5d, order-of-magnitude sketch].
- **Only part of it is clinical.** About 60–70% of placements went to clinical, so ~$190–210M.
- **Part is already in 2026.** Boxes placed in Q4'25–Q1'26 ramp through 2026, and X consumables already grew 34–40% in H1. Perhaps half lands as 2027 y/y growth: ~$100–150M, about 5–8% of FY26 clinical ex-China consumables (~$1.95B). This is before the 35B price cut.
- **It is not additive to volume growth.** Labs buy capacity because their test volume is growing; the consumables come from those tests. The placement wave is how the clinical volume growth shows up, not an extra source on top of it.
- **Consensus has no clinical line,** so no exact share of consensus can be attributed.

## 3c. Clinical placement mix up, clinical consumables down: a smoking gun?

**The user's note:** the clinical share of X placements went from 60/40 to 70/30 (Q1 to Q2'26) while clinical consumables growth slowed from +20% to +15%. Is management being dishonest?

**The premise is weaker than it looks.**
- **Q1 was a floor, not a point estimate.** Q1'26 was disclosed as "more than 60%" clinical (Q1 call; JPM 1Q recap, 6 May 2026). In Q2 the CFO said the mix "is still about 70% clinical, 30% research", and JPM wrote the mix "remained" about 70/30 (Q2 call; JPM 2Q recap, 5 Aug 2026). Q1 may already have been ~70%. A shift from 60/40 to 70/30 is not established.
- **No dishonesty is evident.** The disclosures are consistent with each other. Management's explanation for the slowdown matches the regional data: US/Canada +13% organic and EMEALA flat in Q2.

**Why the tension still matters.**
- **The 2027 bull case runs from placements to consumables,** so record clinical placements alongside slowing clinical consumables is the right thing to probe. In Q2 the CEO said clinical customers are "building installed base ahead of volume in their pipelines".
- **Two readings are possible:**
  - *Lag (bull):* new clinical boxes take 6–9 months to ramp, so consumables reaccelerate by Q4'26 or Q1'27.
  - *Capacity ahead of demand (bear):* boxes are bought ahead of uncertain volume, possibly pulled forward before AXELIOS or won with volume discounts and leases. Per-box pull-through stays diluted and price per gigabase slips. Evidence so far: X per-box pull-through is flat to down y/y.
- **Tests:**
  - clinical consumables growth in Q3–Q4'26 (re-acceleration versus ~13% or below);
  - X revenue growth against installed-base growth;
  - instrument revenue per placement;
  - the FY26 pull-through actual in February 2027.

## 4. The tell

The boxes placed in Q4'25–Q2'26 (~280) should reach normal use in H2'26, given the 6–9 month ramp.
- **Q3–Q4'26:** if X consumables grow faster than the average base, pull-through turns positive y/y and the bulls' ramp is real.
- **The X share of HT revenue:** watch it in the Q3 deck on 29 Oct.
- **February 2027:** the disclosed FY26 pull-through figure, and the 35B price.
