# NovaSeq X 10B internal lane prices at university and research cores (tracker, 17 Oct 2026)

*Source: the overnight thesis scrape findings (`Outputs/datasets/2026-10-12_thesis_scrape_findings.csv`). Core rate cards include labor and overhead, so they are a proxy for Illumina's price, not Illumina invoices. Most cores reprice once a year (1 Jul, 1 Aug or 1 Sep).*

## Coverage

There are 10B internal lane prices for **21 core facilities**:
- **18 US:** Pitt, KUMC, UCLA, MD Anderson, Illinois Carver, UChicago, Georgia GGBC, Northwestern, TGen, NCI, UC Berkeley, TxGen (Texas A&M), Fred Hutch, Harvard Bauer, UCSD IGM, UCSF CAT, UC Riverside, UW-Madison.
- **3 outside the US:** TCAG, OICR, EPFL.

Only **8** have a before-and-after 10B price in 2026.

## Last 2 months vs prior 6 months (cores with prices observed live in Sep–Oct 2026)

There are 7 qualifying cores: UCSD, Northwestern, Fred Hutch, Carver, Harvard, UCLA and UCSF. TGen is excluded because its latest data is from July 2026.

- **Last 2 months (mid-Aug to mid-Oct):**
  - UCSD −17.6% (new sheet, pending approval)
  - Northwestern −6.3% (if FY27 rates took effect 1 Sep)
  - Fred Hutch +3.0% (timing unconfirmed)
  - The other four were flat
  - **2 of 7 (29%) cut; average −3.0%.**
- **Prior 6 months (mid-Feb to mid-Aug):**
  - Carver −5.2% (1 Aug)
  - Harvard +0.8% (1 Jul)
  - **Average −0.6%**, or −3.6% if UCLA's −21% cut took effect 1 Jul 2026, which the data cannot confirm.
- **Conclusion:** the data does not robustly show an acceleration in the last two months. Annual repricing dates dominate the timing. What it does show is a consistent, large cut in 10B prices in the latest annual cycle.
