# What ex-China clinical growth does 2027 need?

*2026-10-30. Model: `2026-10-30_ILMN_clinical_2027_implied_bridge.py`. Data: `../datasets/2026-10-30_ILMN_clinical_exChina_2yr_stack_implied_2027.csv`.*

## Answer

Illumina's ex-China clinical consumables need to grow about **20% in 2027** for total revenue to grow 8.5%, the midpoint of management's high-single-digit (HSD) target. That assumes research is flat and the other revenue lines follow Bloomberg consensus. The two-year stack would need to reach about 36 points. It has run at 25–28 since Q4 2025.

The 2026 exit point looks healthier than the simple two-year-stack math suggested. The Q4 2026 guide implies clinical growth of about +12–13%, a stack of about 33. The full year lands at about 15%, in line with management's "mid-teens."

## How Q4 2026 is implied

Illumina does not disclose clinical dollars. They are back-solved each quarter from the deck's ex-China sequencing consumables dollars and the disclosed clinical and research growth rates. That gives FY25 clinical of about $1.68B, or 61% of ex-China consumables. This matches management's "about 60%."

Two routes give a similar Q4:

- **Route 1, the Q4 consumables guide.** The CFO guided Q4 ex-China consumables to "5%, 6%" growth (Q3'26 call). If research declines 6%, as it did in Q3, clinical comes out at +12–13.5%. The base case is +12.7%.
- **Route 2, the full-year clinical guide.** A full year of exactly 15% ("mid-teens") requires a Q4 of +11.6%. A full year of 14% would need +7.7%, and 16% would need +15.4%.

Q4 is the tough comparison (+20% a year earlier), so a +12–13% print still means a two-year stack of about 33. That would be the strongest in the series. Pull-through from the roughly 360 NovaSeq X placements over the past year is what management says supports this.

## How 2027 is backed into

The bridge starts from 2026E revenue of $4.65B, the guide midpoint excluding tariff refunds, and splits it as follows:

- ex-China clinical consumables: ~$1.94B
- ex-China research and applied consumables: ~$0.99B
- ex-China sequencing instruments: ~$0.46B
- Greater China: ~$0.21B
- ex-China services, microarray, SomaLogic and other: ~$1.05B

Revenue up 8.5% means $5.05B in 2027, or about $395M of growth.

The other lines are held as follows:

- **Research and applied:** flat, as specified.
- **Instruments:** −6.0%, Bloomberg consensus 2027 versus 2026 (about −$28M).
- **China:** −10.2%, consensus (about −$22M).
- **Services, microarray and other:** +4.7%, consensus (about +$49M).

Together these net to roughly zero. Clinical therefore has to supply almost all of the $395M, about +$396M on a $1.94B base, or **+20.4%**.

## Sensitivities

The required clinical growth moves with both the total-growth target and the research assumption. The table shows the main grid.

| Total 2027 growth | Research −5% | Research flat | Research +3% |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7.0% | +19% | +17% | +15% |
| 8.0% | +22% | +19% | +18% |
| **8.5%** | +23% | **+20%** | +19% |
| 9.0% | +24% | +22% | +20% |

Changing one other input at a time (8.5% total, research flat):

- China flat instead of −10%: clinical needed falls to +19.3%.
- Instruments flat instead of −6%: +19.0%.
- Services, microarray and other at +8%: +18.6%. This reflects management's claim that multiomics and new products add 1–2 points in 2027.

Two cross-checks frame the result:

- **Consensus revenue.** Bloomberg consensus 2027 revenue of $4.92B (+5.8% on $4.65B, set before the Q3 print) needs only about **+14%** clinical growth.
- **The current run-rate.** If the two-year stack simply holds at 26, 2027 clinical grows about +11% and total revenue only about **+4.4%**.

## Takeaways

- **HSD is a clinical re-acceleration call.** HSD in 2027 is not a "steady state" outcome. It requires clinical to re-accelerate to roughly its Q4'25 to Q1'26 peak pace (+20%) for a full year on a tougher base. Alternatively, research has to turn positive and multiomics has to beat the 1–2 points management cites. Management's own levers are the 6000-to-X pricing headwind shrinking in 2027 and the six-to-nine-month pull-through lag on recent placements.
- **Consensus sits closer to the run-rate.** The Street's pre-Q3 numbers (~6%) imply mid-teens clinical, closer to the current run-rate. JPMorgan is the exception, at 9.1%.

## Caveats

- **Basis.** The 8.5% is applied to total reported revenue. Management has not said whether HSD means total, organic, or ex-China.
- **Clinical dollars.** Clinical dollars are model estimates. Rounding in the disclosed growth rates moves them by about ±$10M a quarter.
- **Stale consensus.** Consensus growth rates for instruments, China and services date from 10 Oct 2026, before the Q3 print. China returned to growth in Q3, so the −10% China assumption may be too harsh.
- **Q3'26 ex-China consumables.** The figure of about $752M is implied, because no Q3'26 deck is in the folder.
