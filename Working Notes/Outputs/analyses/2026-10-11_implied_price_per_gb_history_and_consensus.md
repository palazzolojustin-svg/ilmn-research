# Implied revenue-per-gigabase change: 10 quarters of history and 10 quarters of consensus

Prepared 11 Oct 2026.

**Method.** Implied change in revenue per Gb = (1 + revenue growth) ÷ (1 + Gb growth) − 1. It blends price, flow-cell mix and the 6000-to-X shift.

**Inputs.**
- **Revenue:** Illumina's disclosed ROW (ex-China) sequencing consumables growth, total and clinical [working file 01, section 5A; earnings decks].
- **Gb growth:** management's floor for gigabases on connected high- and mid-throughput instruments: ">35%" in Q1'24, ">40%" in Q2–Q3'24 and ">30%" from Q4'24 on.

Because Gb growth is a floor, the deflation shown is the minimum. Clinical Gb grew "well above" 30% in 2026, so clinical deflation is understated.

**Forward.** Revenue is Bloomberg consensus sequencing consumables (10 Oct 2026) with China removed, estimated at 76% of consensus Greater China revenue. Gb growth is a 30% proxy, because no volume estimates exist.
- **No consensus clinical line exists.** The clinical column backs out clinical growth assuming research & applied falls 2% a year, which is our view.
- **Calendar:** Q4'26 includes the 53rd week, which flatters Q4'26 and toughens Q4'27.

## Reading

1. **History: the deflation is easing.** Total revenue per Gb went from about −22% to −27% in 2024 to about −15% to −19% in late 2025 and 2026. Clinical eased from about −21% to −25% to about −8% to −12% as most clinical work moved to the X. Q2'26 ticked back up for clinical (−11.5% against −7.7%), coinciding with the clinical slowdown.
2. **On a 30% Gb proxy, consensus is not heroic on price.** It implies total revenue per Gb falling about 15–16% a year, close to the recent −15% to −19%. For clinical it implies about −10% to −11% alongside ~16% clinical growth, similar to the last three quarters.
3. **So the real bet in consensus is volume holding near 30%.** If Gb growth slows to management's long-run ~25%, the allowable total deflation shrinks to about −12% to −13%. At 20% it shrinks to about −9%. That is exactly when the 35B (about −20% per Gb for big users) arrives.
4. **Clinical: ~16% revenue growth needs clinical Gb growth well above 30%** if clinical deflation reverts toward the −12% to −15% seen in 2025. That ties back to whether the placement wave is real demand or capacity bought ahead of it.
