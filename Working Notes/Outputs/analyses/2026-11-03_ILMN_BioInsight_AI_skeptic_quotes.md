# Skeptical quotes on Illumina's AI and data push: BioInsight and the Billion Cell Atlas (2026-11-03)

*One agent swept every expert call, broker note, Illumina call and conference, and the 10x, PacBio, ONT and TMO transcripts. The top quotes were re-checked against their source lines. Paths are under `Working Notes/Sources/`.*

## Bottom line

- **No expert or broker attacks BioInsight or the Billion Cell Atlas by name.** Experts never mention either one, and no analyst on the calls pushed back on them.
- **The skepticism in the corpus is indirect.** It makes four points:
  - Multiomics is a short-term workaround for running out of samples.
  - Illumina is late to proteomics and single-cell.
  - AI may need *less* data, not more.
  - Others are building the same perturbation datasets.
- **The cleanest Street line is Barclays, which says it cannot verify the 1–2 point growth target.**

## What management admits

- **Revenue isn't visible yet.** Dhingra, CFO (Q3'26 call, 29 Oct 2026), L309: "we anticipate that somewhere in 2027, it does become more visible."
- **The upside depends on AI.** Thaysen, CEO (same call), L305: "depending on how that AI opportunity plays out… this could be substantially bigger."
- **It is early and lumpy:**
  - "still in the early days" (Q4'25).
  - "the timing of strategic partnerships and data deals can be lumpy" (Q1'26).
  - "a multiyear opportunity" (CFO, Q4'25).
- **Size.** The target is 1–2 points of 2027 growth, but that bucket also includes SomaLogic, Fluent and spatial, so BioInsight's own share is not disclosed. The atlas had over 300M cells and six pharma partners at Q2'26, and was more than half complete at Q3'26 with GSK added. No dollar revenue has been given.
