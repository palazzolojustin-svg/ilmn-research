# Are Natera, Tempus, Caris and Guardant slowing net new tests?

Prepared 11 Oct 2026. Sources: company quarterly releases and 8-Ks (Q1'25–Q2'26); clinical-consumables ledger (E081, E089, E092–E094, E293–E294, E379, E432, E438–E439, E452–E453). Figures marked (d) are derived; see the caveats.

## Answer

No. Test growth is flat to accelerating at Natera, Tempus and Guardant. Caris has slowed modestly (+22% to +15–18%), but its FY26 guide implies re-acceleration in the second half. So Illumina's clinical consumables slowdown (+20% to +15% in Q2'26) did not come from slower test growth at its biggest customers. It points instead to:
- price per gigabase (X migration, contracts that only let prices fall);
- mix toward low-sequencing tests;
- region (the Middle East).

## What this means for Illumina

- **Test demand is not the problem; conversion of tests into revenue is.** Customer test growth of +18% to +63% sits alongside Illumina clinical revenue growth of +15%.
- **Mix dilutes the volume.** The fastest-growing lines include shallow tests: Natera's amplicon Signatera is under 1 Gb per draw, and Shield is a low-depth screen. Migrations to the X (Guardant) bank savings as margin.
- **Contracts cap price.** Natera's and Caris's contracts only let prices fall.
- **Upside is real but early.** The deeper tests (Signatera Genome, whole-genome MRD, Caris WES/WTS and whole-genome tests, Guardant's larger panel) are launched, but their volumes are small or undisclosed.

**Caveats.** Several quarters are derived or missing: Natera Q2'25 total, Q4'25 oncology and Q1'25 MRD; Tempus Q2'26 hereditary; Caris Q1'25. Full transcripts were not read.
