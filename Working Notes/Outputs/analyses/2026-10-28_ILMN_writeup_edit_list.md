# Edit List: "ILMN Writeup UIC" (Google Docs PDF, reviewed 2026-10-28)

The PDF text was checked against the folder's data: Core Notes 03 and 05, the core-lab tracker of 17 Oct, the price-per-Gb analysis of 28 Oct, and the expert call of 23 Jul.

## A. Substantive and factual fixes (do these first)

1. **"75% of the labs we tracked cut NovaSeq X 10B rates."** The denominator is wrong. You tracked 21 cores, and 6 of the **8 that repriced** cut. Say "75% of the labs that repriced."
2. **"Price cuts have directionally accelerated over the last few months."** Your own tracker says the opposite: "the data does not robustly show an acceleration in the last two months," because cuts land on annual repricing dates. Delete this, or replace it with the cycle-over-cycle point (10B list held flat and 6000 list raised about 15% in 2022–24, versus cuts now).
3. **"Buy-side implies top-line growth doubling… in line with the sell-side's view of the 27E bogey."** Bloomberg consensus (10 Oct) has FY27 revenue growth of **+6.3%**, against +6.5% in FY26. That is no acceleration, so the buy-side bar is *above* the sell-side, not in line with it. Either say the buy-side sits above consensus, or say the "bogey" is management's HSD target and the buy-side's LDD, not the sell-side's.
4. **Pick one number for the buy-side bar.** The writeup uses "doubling," "+750 bps of acceleration" and "LDD rev growth" in three places. From organic growth of about 4–5%, +750 bps gives about 11–12%. Use that consistently, and say which base you mean (FY26 organic, reported or ROW).
5. **The 35B "20% implied discount."** The expert (former Illumina associate director, call of 23 Jul 2026) left Illumina in July 2025 and was speculating ("maybe that drops to $2 flat"). Write "a former ILMN product lead expects…", not "will be released at."
6. **"Implies a higher cadence of discounting relative to prior releases."** This has no comparator, and the folder's data cuts against it. At core-lab medians, the 10B→25B step was about **−32% per Gb** ($4.16 to $2.81), so a 20% step is *smaller*. If you mean frequency, give the dates (25B in late 2023, 35B in about H1 2027). Otherwise cut the claim.
7. **"Resell ILMN's sequencing 'at cost.'"** Cores charge cost plus labor and overhead, and their external rates run about 13% above internal. Also, NCI, TGen, Fred Hutch, OICR and others are not universities. Say "20+ academic and research sequencing cores, whose rates track reagent cost plus overhead."
8. **"Element's VITARI release at $100/genome in H2 2026."** The $100 genome is Element's claim, and VITARI had not visibly shipped as of 12 Oct (pre-orders only). Say "Element's VITARI, marketed at ~$100/genome and slated to ship in 2H26."
9. **"Consumables (~74% of Q2 mix)."** Q2'26 consumables were about **73.5%** ($852M of $1,159M). 74% is the FY25 figure. Write "~74% of FY25 revenue" or "~73% in Q2'26."
10. **"Clinical/commercial labs (~65% of Q2 mix) and academic labs."** The 65% is a share of *sequencing consumables*, not of total revenue. The other ~35% is "research & applied" (academic, government, pharma and applied), not just academic labs. Also note that in Jan 2026 management put clinical at ~40%, so the definitions vary.
11. **"ILMN consumables are at ~$200 per genome while Element and Ultima are closer to $80–100."** Consumables are not priced "per genome." Say "ILMN's list cost per genome is ~$200; Element and Ultima claim $80–100." Those are claims and targets, not shipped street prices.
12. **"Management is being asked on calls why they cannot guide up… to ramp to HSD."** This misstates the Q2 call. Leerink asked why **2027 shouldn't exceed HSD**, and Thaysen declined to raise the target. That actually strengthens your catalyst: management won't go above HSD while the buy-side sits at LDD. Rewrite it that way.
13. **"We are doubtful of a ~6% assumed improvement in implied rev/Gb discounting."** This is unclear. Define it, for example: "the ~6-point easing in the implied rev/Gb decline (from about −21% to about −15%) baked into consensus."
14. **"A core clinical large account guided for 'step improvements in 2027.'"** Name or describe the account, and cite the source (call, date). As written it can't be checked.
15. **"FY28E EPS has only been revised up 12%."** Give the period (since when?).
16. **Timing.** Q3'26 results land around now (last year's were 30 Oct). Your Q2 figures (mix, 17%/41%, 3.4x) will be one quarter stale within days. Plan to refresh after the print, or say "as of Q2'26."
17. **Header.** Add the share price, price target or downside, and the date. Say that the P/E is non-GAAP (52.1x on $5.36 26E EPS is consistent with $43.7B EqV and ~156M shares).

## B. Clarity and wording

18. "provides DNA sequencing machines including its old machine (NovaSeq6000) and the new larger-volume instruments (NovaSeqX)." Mixes singular and plural; use "legacy" and "higher-throughput." Suggested: "ILMN sells DNA sequencers, chiefly the legacy NovaSeq 6000 and its higher-throughput successor, the NovaSeq X."
19. "ILMN's products are sold between a customer split between…" Repeats "between." Suggested: "Customers split between clinical labs (~65% of sequencing consumables) and research & applied."
20. "Based on an expert call with a company former ILMN's new high-volume consumable…" This is garbled. Suggested: "A former ILMN product lead expects the new 35B flow cell to launch at a ~20% lower price per Gb."
21. "smaller, volume-inelastic labs that lack the volume elasticity." Redundant; cut "that lack the volume elasticity."
22. "the 3.4x rev/Gb delta between the X and 6000." A ratio is not a delta. Say "the 6000's 3.4x revenue-per-Gb premium over the X."
23. "The buy-side and the street are both baking in acceleration and take management's guidance… at face value, we believe that…" This is a comma splice. Split it into two sentences.
24. "this cause expectations to rationalize." Write "this would cause expectations to reset."
25. "whereas we view discounting getting worse." Write "whereas we see discounting worsening."
26. "comparing the core pricing lists between machines." Write "comparing core-lab price lists across flow cells."
27. "comes in at the low end": the low end of what? Say "at the low end of HSD (~7%)."
28. "north star" and "bogey" are informal. They are fine in a pitch, but use them once each at most.
29. "ILMN is overearning on the NovaSeq 6000 with a residual 17% of volume but 41% of high-throughput revenue." Make both measures the same: "17% of high-throughput Gb but 41% of high-throughput revenue (Q2'26)."
30. "ahead Element's" → "ahead of Element's."
31. "ILMN… they." The company is singular: "why it cannot."

## C. Mechanical

32. **Missing spaces: not real.** The run-together words in the extracted text ("buysideconversations", "hasre-rated" and so on) are PDF export artifacts. Checking character positions shows a normal space-width gap at every one, so the document itself is fine. There is one real spacing error: a double space in "doubtful of a  ~6%".
33. **Product names.** "NovaSeq6000" → "NovaSeq 6000"; "NovaSeqX" → "NovaSeq X". Use one form throughout (full names on first use, then "6000" and "X").
34. **Hyphenation.** "buyside" vs "sell-side" is inconsistent; use "buy-side" and "sell-side." Write "topline" and "top-line" one way ("top-line" as an adjective). "overearning" → "over-earning." "in-line with" → "in line with." "6000 to X Conversion" → "6000-to-X Conversion."
35. **Units.** "750bps" → "750 bps"; "rev/gb" → "rev/Gb"; "$80-100" → "$80–100."
36. **Period labels.** Mixed formats: "26E," "27E," "FY28E," "FY 2027," "Q4 26," "H2 2026." Pick one style, for example FY27E, Q4'26 and 2H26.
37. **Define abbreviations on first use:** rDCF (reverse DCF), HSD and LDD (high-single-digit and low-double-digit), EqV, rev/Gb.
38. **Layout.** The "Relative to the industry…" sentence sits in a narrow right-hand column, one or two words per line. It looks like text wrapping around an image or a text box. Fix the wrap or move the sentence back into the paragraph.
39. **Header format.** Write "SHORT: Illumina, Inc." with a space after the colon, to match the other section labels.
