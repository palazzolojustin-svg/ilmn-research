# Illumina NovaSeq X innovation-roadmap page: snapshots, Mar–Oct 2026

**What this is.** These are copies of Illumina's NovaSeq X innovation-roadmap page, saved to document a timeline change made between 13 Oct and 31 Oct 2026.

**Page:** https://www.illumina.com/systems/sequencing-platforms/novaseq-x-plus/applications/innovation-roadmap.html

**Files**
- `wayback_<timestamp>_innovation-roadmap.html` — Wayback Machine captures (raw `id_` copies, decompressed) from 4 Mar, 16 Jun, 16 Sep and 13 Oct 2026.
- `live_20261031_innovation-roadmap.html` — the live page, downloaded 31 Oct 2026.

**The "Innovation timeline" section in each copy**

| Capture | H1 2026 | H2 2026 | H1 2027 | H2 2027 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 Mar, 16 Jun, 16 Sep, 13 Oct 2026 | 5B and 1.5B 600-cycle flow cells; staggered start | "HT mode increases reads for better sequencing economics" | Q70 duplex / Q50 simplex; 14B (10B +40%); 35B flow cell | (none) |
| 31 Oct 2026 (live) | same | **(heading removed)** | **(heading removed)** | Q70 duplex / Q50 simplex; 14B (10B +40%); 35B flow cell |

**What changed**
- The H2 2026 "HT mode" item was removed.
- Q70, the 14B and the 35B moved from H1 2027 to H2 2027, a six-month slip.

The page still says "Timeline and performance are estimates and are subject to change." The change falls within the two weeks around the Q3 2026 call (29 Oct), where management did not mention it.

**Method:** Wayback CDX listing, then `id_` raw captures and a live `curl` download. Text was extracted by stripping the HTML.
