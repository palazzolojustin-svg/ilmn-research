# ILMN: clinical sequencing consumables growth excluding China, Q1 2024 to Q3 2026

**What it is:** year-over-year growth in Illumina's clinical sequencing consumables revenue outside China ("Rest of World", RoW), for every quarter Illumina has disclosed it.

**Compiled:** 2026-10-30, directly from the original Illumina earnings decks (Q3'25, Q4'25, Q1'26, Q2'26) and the Q3'26 earnings call transcript in `Working Notes/Sources/ILMN/`.

**Coverage:** Illumina first published this split in the Q3 2025 deck, with history back to Q1 2024. Nothing earlier exists ex-China; 2023 calls gave only total (China-inclusive) clinical growth. Q3 2026 comes from the call because no Q3 2026 deck is in the folder.

## Columns

- `period`: fiscal quarter or full year.
- `clinical_consumables_exchina_yoy_pct`: YoY growth, %.
- `basis`: decks label these "actual" / "reported" (includes currency effects; not organic). The Q3'26 call did not say.
- `source`: the document the figure was taken from.
- `note`: management colour or caveats.
