# ILMN KPI summary table (2026-10-19)

**Files:**
- `2026-10-19_ILMN_KPI_summary_table.xlsx` (values, ready to paste)
- `Outputs/artifacts/2026-10-19_ILMN_KPI_summary_table.png` (an image of the same table)

Both are built by `scratchpad/kpi_table.py`.

**What it is:** one compact table of Illumina's key KPIs, with current management guidance alongside.

**Columns:**
- FY24 and FY25, reported
- Q3'25 to Q2'26, reported
- Q3'26E to Q2'27E, consensus
- FY26E to FY28E, consensus
- Management guidance

**Rows:**
- Revenue: dollars and y/y growth
- Ex-China revenue growth
- Sequencing consumables: total and ex-China growth
- Clinical and research & applied growth, ex-China
- Instruments growth
- NovaSeq X placements
- X share of high-throughput Gb
- Gb growth
- Implied revenue per Gb
- Adjusted gross and operating margin
- Adjusted EPS and its growth

**Sources:**
- Reported figures: `2026-10-17_ILMN_numbers_table.xlsx` (ILMN decks, 10-Q/10-K and calls).
- Estimates: Bloomberg consensus, 10 Oct 2026.
- Guidance: Q2'26 call (30 Jul 2026), the Feb 2026 FY26 bridge, and the 2027 targets reaffirmed at JPM in Jan 2026.

**Caveats:**
- **FY24** is Core Illumina, excluding GRAIL.
- **No consensus** exists for the clinical/research split, X placements or Gb. The FY26E clinical/research cells are guidance midpoints.
- **Ex-China revenue growth** is reported, so it includes FX and SomaLogic. Guidance is organic.
- **X placements** are disclosed as lower bounds.
- **FY26** is a 53-week year.
