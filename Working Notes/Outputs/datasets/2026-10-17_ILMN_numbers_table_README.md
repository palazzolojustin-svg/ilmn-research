# ILMN numbers table (2026-10-17)

**File:** `2026-10-17_ILMN_numbers_table.xlsx`. Built with live formulas and recalculated in Excel with zero formula errors.

**Sheets**
- **Quarterly:** Q3'25–Q2'26 reported plus Q3'26E–Q4'27E Bloomberg consensus. Paste-ready for Google Docs.
- **Annual:** FY25 reported plus FY26E–FY30E consensus. Paste-ready.
- **Addendum:**
  - GAAP-to-non-GAAP adjustments, Q3'25–Q2'26 and FY25.
  - Commentary on consumables margins.
  - Walk-down of $1 of revenue: FY25 non-GAAP and GAAP, plus FY26E non-GAAP.
- **Data:** every input (blue) and every derived formula (black), from Q1'24 to Q4'27E and FY23 to FY30E. The presentation sheets link here.

**What the tables cover:**
- **Revenue:** consolidated and ex-China revenue.
- **Placements:** NovaSeq X placements, and the clinical share of placements.
- **Instruments:** total instruments, and sequencing instruments ex-China.
- **Consumables:** total consumables, sequencing consumables, and sequencing consumables ex-China.
- **Clinical vs research:** ex-China sequencing consumables growth for each.
- **Volume and pricing:** Gb volume, X share of high-throughput Gb and revenue, and implied revenue per Gb at 30% Gb growth.
- **Margins and costs:** non-GAAP gross, operating, EBITDA and net margin; GAAP gross and operating margin; COGS, R&D, SG&A, total opex, stock comp and tax rate as a percentage of revenue.
- **EPS:** non-GAAP EPS.

Growth rows show y/y, q/q and 2-year stacks. Margin and cost rows show y/y and q/q changes in bps.

**Sources:**
- ILMN earnings decks and reconciliations, 10-Q/10-K and calls (Q1'24–Q2'26).
- Bloomberg consensus annuals and quarterlies, 10 Oct 2026.
- Company FY26 guidance for the FY26E clinical and research split.

**Bases and caveats:**
- **2024 is Core Illumina (ex-GRAIL).**
- **Ex-China lines** are the company's Rest-of-World disclosure. Consensus has no ex-China split by line.
- **X placements** are disclosed as lower bounds. There is no consensus for placements, Gb or the clinical/research split.
- **Gb growth** is disclosed only as a floor (">30%").
- **Estimate margins** use Bloomberg's consensus margin lines, because its dollar lines don't tie to those margin lines in 2028E–30E.
- **Adj. EBITDA** = adj. operating income + depreciation.
- **FY26 is a 53-week year.** Management says the extra week falls in Q4'26.

**To paste into Google Docs:** in Excel, select the table range (A4 to the last column of the last note row), copy, then paste in Docs and choose "Paste unlinked". Set the page to landscape (File > Page setup) so the 11-column quarterly table fits.
