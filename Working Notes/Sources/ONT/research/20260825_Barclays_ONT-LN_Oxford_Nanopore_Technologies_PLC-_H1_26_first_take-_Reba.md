# **H1 26 first take: Rebasing the midterm** 

New 2030 targets are -10%/-35% below cons on rev/adj EBITDA and imply a 19% 26-30 rev CAGR . Speaking to the co, mid-teens growth more of a base; we look for further details on the call. **Oxford Nanopore reported H1 26 results today, with revenues broadly in line with the trading update published in July (see H1'26 Trading Update Review, 14 Jul) and Gross Profit -2% behind Bloomberg consensus, although inline with comments at the trading update. New 2030 targets announced of >$700m of revenue (-10%, vs Bloomberg cons £584m/$782m at spot), and adj EBITDA of >15% (implies $105m EBITDA, -35% vs $161m Bloomberg consensus). Top-line guide implies ~19% CAGR in 2026-2030 (when taking base in USD, $345-$355m as per company), and commentary of "organic constant-currency revenue growth of approximately mid-teens per annum and accelerating". Speaking to the company, mid-teens is more a base with acceleration expected as higher growth areas become a bigger share of revenue, but we look for further clarification on the call. Consensus estimates 20.8% and 21.9% constant currency revenue in 27/28, respectively. New guide excludes potential upside from a new licensing agreement and other uncertain elements.  We expect the below-consensus expectations for 2030 to weigh on shares today, but note we believe the market has been looking for a rebase of targets to shift away from a mode of consistent downgrades to expectations. We reiterate our Overweight rating and believe valuation does not accurately reflect the growth opportunity relative to peers.**

#### **H1 26 segment performance** 

- **EMEAI:** Revenue grew 23.8% ccy to £55.6 million (vs. Barc. £54.1 million), driven by continued momentum in Clinical and BioPharma end markets, alongside robust Research performance despite disruption in the Middle East and the roll-off of large research programmes. 

- **AMR:** Revenue grew 12.5% ccy to £39.1 million (vs. Barc. £39.8 million), driven by broadbased growth across end markets despite customer order timing headwinds, contract delays and continued pressure on the US research funding environment. 

- **APAC:** Revenue declined 8.4% ccy to £22.0 million (vs. Barc. £22.6 million), driven by a 15.7% decline in China alongside the completion of the PRECISE II programme in Singapore, partially offset by growth elsewhere in the region. 

#### **FY26 guidance updated** 

- **Revenue growth of 16-20% at constant currency excluding the newly announced $20m cross-licensing agreement (vs. Barc. 16.5%). Including the agreement, FY26 constantcurrency revenue growth is expected to be approximately 23-27%** (vs. prior 21-25% and Barc. 21.5%) **.**
