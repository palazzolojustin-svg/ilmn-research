### Oxford Nanopore Technologies PLC 

# **H1'26 Trading Update Review** 

Cut to 2026 underlying growth weighed on shares, but additional opportunities provide support to numbers. We expect underlying growth to accelerate in 2027 driven by Clinical and Biopharma, and see potential upside to our medium-term Biopharma estimates where we are below management expectations. 

Following publication of ONT's H1'26 trading update (H126 Trading update: Sales miss, 2026 guidance adjusted, 13 July), we update our model ahead of H1'26 results on 19th August. The trading update was characterised by a cut to underlying growth guidance (now expected to be 16-20%), driven by weakness in China, Middle East and Americas. Management highlighted potential additional collaboration and licensing opportunities that could push growth back up to the previous guidance range of 21-25%, but with some element of this non-recurring. There is limited disclosure around the phasing of these contracts and how much is recurring. We assume these opportunities generate ~£11m of additional revenue in 2026 (i.e. 5% additional growth), and that 50% is recurring in 2027. As such, we now forecast 21.5% constant currency growth for 2026 (16.5% underlying) and see an acceleration in growth across all regions in H2. For 2027, we expect to see a headwind to growth from these additional agreements, but an underlying growth acceleration (from 16.5% to 21.1%) supported by self-help and easier comps in China along with conversion of Clinical and Biopharma revenues across EMEAI and AMR. We forecast 21% constant currency growth 2025-2028E, and see potential upside to our forecasts from Biopharma. Management has indicated Biopharma could account for 20% of Group sales by 2027; however, our risk-adjusted estimates see Biopharma at just 14% of sales by 2028 (Guidance cut a clearing event?, 25 March). Despite a slew of guidance cuts, we believe valuation does not accurately reflect the growth opportunity relative to peers and reiterate our Overweight rating, whilst reducing our price target to 160 GBp (from 172 GBp). 

**Key takes from CFO meeting: 1) 2026 revenue expectations:** Confidence in H2 growth comes from converted, ramping business. AMRs and APAC ex-China are expected to be swing factors between the 16-20% guide, with EMEAI and China expectations relatively fixed. New opportunities that could push them back into the 21-25% range are to be discussed in more detail once signed. Revenue at the mid-point of new guide is slightly more H2 weighted than the previously communicated 45:55 split, but not drastically. **2) 2027+ growth:** Management do not see a fundamental issue that changes their view on continuing growth rate of the business; they can see a reacceleration of growth to 21-25% in 2027. China comp will be easier. **3) Earnings:** Adj EBITDA breakeven is still targeted for 2027, despite the expansion of the C-Suite.

## Notes from CFO meeting 

Following publication of H1'26 trading update, we joined a meeting with the CFO. We highlight three key takeaways with our notes below: **1) 2026 revenue expectations:** Confidence in H2 growth comes from converted, ramping business. AMRs and APAC ex-China expected to be swing factors between 16-20% guide, with EMEAI and China expectations relatively fixed. New opportunities that could push them back into the 21-25% range to be discussed in more detail once signed. Revenue at mid-point of new guide is slightly more H2 weighted than the previously communicated 45:55 split, but not drastically. **2) 2027+ growth:** Don't see a fundamental issue that changes their view on continuing growth rate of the business; can see a reacceleration of growth to 21-25% in 2027. China comp will be easier. **3) Earnings:** Adj EBITDA breakeven still targeted for 2027, despite the expansion of the C-Suite. Still comfortable with their guidance of 62% gross margin for the year, and in both H1 and H2. ONT will publish full H1'26 results on 19 August, 2026. 

##### **<u>Our notes</u>** 

- **China:** 

- °<sup>H1 performance below expectations, largely reflects China where they are in the middle of</sup> restructuring commercial organisation, and where the market is weak. Competitors have all shown a decline in the market.  Still in the middle of the commercial reorganisation which has weighted on performance, and don't expect to see an immediate rebound from this. 

- °<sup>China comprised 10% of revenue in 2025, now 7% in H2'26.</sup> 

- °<sup>Always expected low-to-no growth for China.</sup> 

- **Americas:** Performance below expectations, largely timing issue. Have signed the contracts they need to see H2 acceleration, with these in the ramp phase. 

- **Applied Industrial:** Seeing slower growth due to slowing plasmid growth due to multiplexing/ higher utilisation per flow cell, and more pricing intensity between service providers. 

- **2026 swing factors:** China still declining in H2. Some headroom built into the AMRs expectations; not assuming all the opportunities are going to happen. EMEAI more of a fixed expectation. 

- **2026 revenue split:** Revenue weighting at mid-point of new guide is slightly more H2 weighted than previously communicated 45:55 split, but not drastically. 

- **Expectations beyond 2026:** Management do not think there is a fundamental issue with performance/ market. Expect they can see a reacceleration of growth in FY27. 

- **Adj EBITDA breakeven:** Still confident in their ability to achieve this, despite C-Suite buildout. 

- **New opportunities:** Will talk in more detail once signed, but have talked for some time about penetrating different markets. 

- **UK Biobank:** Ramped and performing strongly, significant contribution in H2'26.
