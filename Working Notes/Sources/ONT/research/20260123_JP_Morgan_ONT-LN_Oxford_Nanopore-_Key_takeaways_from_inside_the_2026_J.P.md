- **PromethION and GridION product development expected in 2026.** As part of improving and further developing nanopore platforms, GS highlighted plans to sequence 4 genomes per PromethION flow cell to drive improved flow cell utilisation. The capability to sequence 2 genomes per flow cell is in early access launch. For GridION Q v2 in late-stage development, R10 and RNA Chemistries will be included for broader adoption with clinical and biomanufacturing QC customers. GS also reiterated that PromethION Q is expected to launch end of 2026. 

- **Market share gains in the Americas driven by an experienced salesforce.** NK noted that ONT gained share in the Americas, with customers placing smaller orders, leading to ONT seeing modest growth in the Americas research and government segment. GS noted that these market share gains in the Americas have been driven by their experienced salesforce, particularly the former head of sales from a large sequencing peer. He highlighted the overall $20-25bn market opportunity for ONT’s technology, of which $13-14bn is an opportunity unique to nanopore because of their differentiated sequencing platform capabilities. 

**• ONT remains confident in the differentiated capabilities of its technology relative to new entrants like Roche SBX sequencing.** GS noted that the Roche Sequencing by Expansion (SBX) technology, which is expected to launch this year, only shares the nanopore sensing element with ONT, but is still fundamentally a sequencing by synthesis technology. Similarly, GS also noted that while Element Biosciences are expecting to launch an IVDR offering in 2026, marking a potential entry into diagnostics, though he does not see either Roche’s sequencing by expansion technology or Element Bioscience’s IVDR diagnostics entry as posing a competitive risk to ONT, given these are shorter-read focused technologies. GS reiterated the differentiated value proposition of ONT’s platform, highlighting its ability to deliver richer insights, faster time to results, and broad accessibility. GS noted that no competitor currently combines all of these capabilities within a single platform, leaving ONT well positioned and highly differentiated, irrespective of recent or upcoming competitor product launches.

## Investment Thesis, Valuation and Risks 

**Oxford Nanopore** _(Overweight; Price Target: 190p)_ 

#### **Investment Thesis** 

In 2026, we expect ONT performance to be driven by operational execution on expansion into Biopharma and Clinical/Applied use of Nanopore sequencing, which should improve confidence in ONT’s ability to deliver strong double digit Revenue growth beyond their 2024-27 mid-term growth outlook. We forecast a 2025-30 Revenue CAGR for ONT of 27%, driven by expansion into Biopharma and Clinical as well as expanding use in Research (increasing use of methylation). In 2026, we expect >30% Revenue growth for ONT, as academic and government funding headwinds abate, and should now be reflected in the base, with Biopharma/Clinical and Applied and strong Research demand ex US expected to underpin an acceleration in Revenue growth. On Adj. EBITDA, we forecast a -£56m Adj. EBITDA loss in 2026, a £34m y-o-y improvement, driven by the topline growth together with 200bps of Gross margin expansion, which should increase confidence in ONT’s ability to achieve their Adj. EBITDA breakeven target by 2027. Beyond the fundamentals, we expect market focus to remain on the ongoing CEO search, which should lift the potential overhang over commercial strategy. On our forecasts, ONT is currently trading on 3.5x 2026 EV/Sales, a discount to peers Illumina (c. 5x 2026 EV/Sales) and PacBio (c. 6x 2026 EV/ Sales), despite ONT offering a superior growth profile relative to both, given we forecast a 2025-28 Sales CAGR for ONT of 31%, ahead of Illumina’s 5% 2025-28 Sales CAGR and PACB’s 13% 2025-28 Sales CAGR ( _note Illumina and PACB are both covered by US Life Sciences and Tools analyst Casey Woodring)._ We move to a Jun-27 PT of 190p, 5.5x 2026 EV/Sales, which we see as undemanding for a strong mid-term growth story. 

#### **Valuation** 

We move to a Jun-27 PT of 190p, 5.5x 2026 EV/Sales, which we see as undemanding for a strong mid-term growth story. 

#### **Risks to Rating and Price Target** 

Key downside risks to our Overweight rating include: 

- Competition from well-established short-read Optical sequencing companies, in particular from Illumina, with their very large installed base, and competitive pricing and accuracy. 

- Future competition from other medium-longer read sequencing companies, such as PacBio, with their competing Optical sequencing approach, or Roche with their SBX Nanopore product, albeit at present this is just short read and doesn’t capture methylation. 

- Eventual competition from other long-read Electrical sequencing companies, as and when ONT patents expire. 

- Failure to execute on margin improvement / reaching profitability, due to either pricing pressure and hence slower Gross Margin improvement, or due to higher OpEx, as the company continues to invest in the business.
