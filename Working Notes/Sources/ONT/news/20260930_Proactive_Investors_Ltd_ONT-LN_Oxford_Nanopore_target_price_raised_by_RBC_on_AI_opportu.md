

# **Oxford Nanopore target price raised by RBC on AI opportunity** 

_Author: Ian Lyall  /  First published: 14:30 30 September 2026_ 



RBC Capital Markets raised its target price for Oxford Nanopore Technologies PLC (LSE:ONT) to 300p from 225p, saying the sequencing company is well placed to benefit from growing demand for biological data as artificial intelligence (AI) reshapes drug discovery. 

RBC retained its 'outperform' rating on Oxford Nanopore, whose shares have risen 19% over the past two weeks, compared with about 30% for US sequencing peers, according to the broker. 

The broker said Oxford Nanopore generates differentiated biological data by combining long reads, native methylation and structural variant detection in a single sequencing assay. 

RBC said AI models increasingly require richer biological inputs, while DeepMind's AlphaGenome model has highlighted the value of long-range genomic context for predicting gene function. 

The UK Biobank's 50,000-sample project, being run by deCODE Genetics, is expected to provide a near-term test of the opportunity, with results due by the end of 2026. 

RBC estimated the project could generate about $20 million in revenue at its assumed $400 per genome, while a followon covering the remaining 450,000 samples could represent about $180 million. 

The broker said wider opportunities include population biobanks, pharmaceutical databases and AI-native drug discovery companies, with more than $4 billion invested in AI drug discovery companies in 2026. 

Oxford Nanopore's PromethION devices already use Nvidia GPUs for real-time basecalling, while the Ellison Institute of Technology owns about 17.5% of the company and has strategic priorities in health and generative biology. RBC cut its revenue forecasts for 2026, 2027 and 2028 by 5%, 11% and 14% respectively, to £256 million, £299 million and £353 million, but said it remained broadly in line with consensus. 

The broker based its 300p target on a 7 times 2027 enterprise value-to-sales multiple and a further 10% premium for potential AI-related newsflow.
