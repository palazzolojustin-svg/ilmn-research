#### EXPERT SENTIMENT 

- Short-read sequencing remains the dominant and most trusted technology in clinical labs, and is unlikely to be displaced in the near term. Illumina is expected to remain a major player due to its widespread adoption and established reputation, but will need to continue evolving and diversifying to maintain its competitive edge. 

- While competition is increasing and some market share may be lost, Illumina's entrenched position and comprehensive product portfolio make it difficult for competitors to fully displace them.

- Competitive pricing and bundled workflow solutions are increasingly important, but switching away from Illumina is seen as risky and uncommon, especially in clinical settings. 

- Illumina's main vulnerabilities are in areas where competitors can offer more integrated, turnkey solutions or address specific workflow pain points, particularly as NovaSeq 6000 is phased out.
