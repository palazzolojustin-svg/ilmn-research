## **SUMMARY** 

## **Overview** 

The discussion focused on trends in sequencing technologies, particularly the utilization of platforms from companies like PacBio, Illumina, and 10x Genomics. The expert, a former director of a sequencing center, shared insights into the evolution of sequencing technologies and their applications in precision medicine, particularly in immune repertoire profiling. The conversation also touched on the expert's transition to directing a new center focused on precision medicine and the launch of a company dedicated to using long-read sequencing for immune profiling. 

## **Key Insights** 

- The expert highlighted a significant shift in sequencing trends, noting a decrease in Illumina's academic utilization due to competitive pricing from large CROs. However, Illumina remains a strong contender with new developments in single-cell and spatial transcriptomics. 

- The expert expressed strong enthusiasm for PacBio's long-read sequencing technology, emphasizing its accuracy and potential for future-proofing data in immune repertoire profiling. 

- The expert's company, Clareo Biosciences, is actively expanding collaborations globally to leverage long-read sequencing for improving precision genomic medicine approaches. 

- The expert is optimistic about the potential of AI to enhance integrated pipelines for multi-omics data analysis, which could revolutionize clinical and translational medicine.
