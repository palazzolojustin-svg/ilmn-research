EXPERT BIO 

Former Associate Director of Portfolio Strategy at Illumina Inc. 

Director, Global Product Marketing - Oncology at Bio-Rad Laboratories. Reporting to the Vice President of Oncology, the expert leads a team of product managers for new product development and product lifecycle management for digital droplet-based oncology assays. The expert also supports the global oncology strategy by collaborating with R&D, market development, business development, sales, and marketing teams to drive innovation in cancer diagnostics. 

Prior to Bio-Rad Laboratories, the expert was the Associate Director, Portfolio Strategy at Illumina, leaving in April 2024. Reporting to senior leadership, the expert served as the global strategic lead for the development and accelerated adoption of genomic applications in oncology and pharma drug development. The expert drove adoption of genomics in biopharma, including target identification, biomarker discovery, and patient stratification, while also accelerating market and product development in precision oncology testing modalities such as therapy selection and minimal residual disease (MRD) monitoring. 

The expert has implemented strategic initiatives in precision oncology, driven multi-omics adoption in cancer research, and provided leadership in portfolio and market development for cutting-edge genomics applications.
