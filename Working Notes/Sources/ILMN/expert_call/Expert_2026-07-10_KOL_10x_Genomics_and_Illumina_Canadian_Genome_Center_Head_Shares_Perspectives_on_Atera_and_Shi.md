## **SUMMARY** 

## **Overview** 

The discussion focused on advancements and trends in genomics platforms, particularly those from Illumina, 10x Genomics, and emerging technologies like Ultima and Roche's AXELIOS. The expert from a Canadian Genome Center shared insights on the utilization and future potential of these platforms in genomic sequencing, single-cell analysis, and spatial biology. The conversation also touched on the impact of macroeconomic factors on purchasing decisions and the evolving landscape of genomic technologies. 

## **Key Insights** 

- The expert emphasized the continued dominance of Illumina platforms for sequencing, noting their full utilization and increasing demand due to national initiatives. 

- Ultima's platform is gaining traction due to its cost-effectiveness, particularly for high-throughput applications, although it requires bespoke analytical pipelines. 

- The expert expressed excitement about the potential of Roche's AXELIOS to compete directly with Illumina, highlighting its speed and flexibility as key advantages. 

- There is a growing interest in single-cell and spatial technologies, with 10x Genomics' platforms seeing high utilization, particularly in industrial applications. 

- The expert noted that while new platforms like Atera are promising, they will coexist with existing technologies like Xenium due to specific application needs.

## **Market Strategy** 

The expert highlighted the need for genomic centers to maintain multiple platforms to drive competition and technological improvements. 

There is a strategic focus on integrating new platforms like Atera while continuing to leverage existing technologies for specific applications. 

The expert anticipates a shift in some applications from single-cell to spatial technologies as platforms like Atera mature. 

## **Stage & Timing** 

Illumina remains the predominant platform, but emerging technologies like Ultima and Roche's AXELIOS are gaining ground. The genomic center is in the process of integrating new platforms and evaluating their impact on existing workflows. The expert expects significant developments in the market over the next few years, driven by increased competition and technological advancements.
