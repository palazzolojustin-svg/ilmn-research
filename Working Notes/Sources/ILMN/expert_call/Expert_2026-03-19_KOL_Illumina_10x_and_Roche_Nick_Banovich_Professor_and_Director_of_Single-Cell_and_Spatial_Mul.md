# **Key Insights** 

The expert highlighted the challenges and improvements in NIH funding, noting a significant drop in grant success rates due to multiyear funding mechanisms.

- The expert expressed skepticism about the current readiness of AI models, like Microsoft's GigaTIME, to replace direct molecular measurements in clinical and research settings.
