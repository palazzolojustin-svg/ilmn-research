#### **SMART SUMMARY** 

## **Summary** 

The interview covered sequencing output, platform usage, funding sources, and competitive dynamics in a genomics core facility. Discussion included drivers of demand, platform evaluation, pricing trends, and future outlook for sequencing technologies. 

CHANNEL EXPERT BACKGROUND 

- Director of a genomics core facility at a research and teaching center, overseeing advanced sequencing 

   - operations supporting academic and external research, with experience in evaluating and operating a range of short-read and long-read sequencing platforms. 

CHANNEL EXPERT SENTIMENT 

- Cautiously optimistic about sequencing output growth, projecting 5-10% annual increases, contingent on stable funding and political environment. 

- Sees Illumina as likely to gain share in short-read sequencing due to single-cell and spatial transcriptomics 

   - growth, while PacBio's long-read share may slightly decrease despite lower costs. 

- Views Roche AXELIOS one as a promising but niche competitor, with Ultima posing a more significant long-term threat to Illumina due to lower costs. 

TAKEAWAYS 

- Annual sequencing output is approximately 18,000-20,000 gigabases, primarily supporting academic 

   - research with some external and commercial users. 

- Short-read sequencing (Illumina) accounts for 85-90% of output, driven by single-cell and spatial 

   - transcriptomics; long-read (PacBio) is 10-15%, mainly for de novo assembly and Fiber-seq applications. 

- Recent advances in single-cell library prep (10x Genomics FLEX v2, OCM) and spatial transcriptomics (Visium HD) are increasing demand for short-read sequencing, while imaging-based platforms like Xenium are shifting some demand away from sequencing-based approaches. 

- Funding is stable, with 80% from federal grants, 15% institutional/philanthropic, and 10-15% commercial; 

   - output and consumable spending grew ~5% year-over-year, in line with volume. 

- The lab is evaluating Roche AXELIOS one but has concerns about reusable sensor degradation and cost; currently, all short-read is on Illumina and all long-read on PacBio, with no immediate plans to change unless 

   - real-world performance and pricing improve.
