### **SUMMARY** 

### **Overview** 

The discussion focuses on the current state and future outlook of the sequencing and single-cell markets, particularly concerning companies like Illumina, PacBio, and 10x Genomics. The conversation provides insights into the challenges faced by core facilities in the sequencing industry due to budget constraints, increased competition, and evolving market demands. The expert shares their experiences and perspectives from managing a DNA core facility at a major university. 

### **Key Insights** 

The expert highlights a significant decrease in sequencing volumes, primarily attributed to budget cuts and grant cancellations, which have led to a 15% reduction in work compared to previous periods. 

- There is a notable shift in funding priorities towards human research, impacting the types of projects and applications being pursued. 

- The expert expresses concerns about aggressive pricing strategies by new market entrants, which are creating competitive pressures and forcing established facilities to lower prices. 

- Despite challenges, there is optimism about new technologies, such as Illumina's 5-base kit, which could drive interest and utilization by offering comprehensive genomic insights.
