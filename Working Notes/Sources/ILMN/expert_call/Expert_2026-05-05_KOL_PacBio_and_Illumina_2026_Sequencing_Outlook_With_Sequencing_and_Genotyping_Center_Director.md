### **Key Insights** 

The expert notes a significant increase in sequencing activity and revenue, attributed to accelerated spending due to federal funding uncertainties. This trend may not sustain long-term as funds are finite. 

- There is a growing interest in single-cell and spatial technologies, with a shift towards long-read sequencing applications. The expert expresses skepticism about the long-term viability of Illumina's TruPath technology as a competitor to PacBio's Revio, citing historical challenges with similar technologies.
