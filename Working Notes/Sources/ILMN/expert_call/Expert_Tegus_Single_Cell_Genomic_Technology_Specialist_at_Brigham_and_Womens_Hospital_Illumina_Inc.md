# Summary 

The client spoke with a single-cell genomics expert who primarily uses Illumina sequencing but has experience with Nanopore and PacBio, and has analyzed data from Roche’s AXELIOS platform. The expert highlighted AXELIOS’s advantages in turnaround time, real-time data generation, scalability, pricing, ease of use, and maintenance, noting that their analysis found no substantial differences in key quality metrics compared to Illumina. They emphasized that broader adoption of new platforms like Roche’s will depend on accumulating comparative data, overcoming established workflows, and building trust, which is particularly challenging in ongoing projects and smaller academic labs. Larger clinical and commercial labs are more likely to adopt new technologies due to greater resources and willingness to innovate, while academic labs tend to be more conservative. The expert noted that Roche’s flexibility with smaller batch sizes and faster run times could benefit users with limited budgets and highthroughput needs, but Illumina’s established ecosystem and customer support create high switching costs. Widespread adoption of new platforms will require more published data and could be delayed by price competition from incumbents. Overall, the expert expressed optimism that increased competition will drive down prices and that new entrants could become significant players, especially in clinical and large-scale commercial settings.
