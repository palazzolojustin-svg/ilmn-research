# **Getting Ready for the Main Event - Hulk Hogan vs. The Big Show This Year At AGBT** 

1Q guide below sets up a 2H-weighted ramp in the face of what is expected to be stiff competition in the 2H of the year. Shares have held in relatively well vs. the group and we see downside to shares from headline risk at the AGBT industry conference at the end of the month. However, we do see... 

_...plenty of momentum in the business on the clinical side as they exited close to 20% clinical sequencing consumables growth in 4Q and the oncology NGS testing market is showing little signs of slowing down. With Roche supposedly coming in at $100/genome, we think that there could be another round of pricing HWs facing the business in ’27 and beyond, which would weigh on outyear growth._ 

ILMN keeps up the trend with a 1Q guide coming in much softer than expected and resulting in a 2H weighted ramp in the business. On the one hand, the macro is stable. Clinical seq cons continued to ramp w/ LB and NGS-based assays across oncology testing, leading to ~20% growth ex-China and research has improved as the year progressed. So with what is expected to be a better year for research budgets, the thinking is that we should see at least MSD+ growth in sequencing consumables. However, mgmt’s guide is for a modest improvement in sequencing consumables for the year and instruments to be slightly down to flat. With this setup, we would expect investors to assume a healthy dose of conservatism to be baked into the guide. However, with plenty of competitive launches coming this year, investors are reading into the guide as portraying some type of market freeze and/or some decel in Clinical. And with a steep drop-off in HT placements in 1Q off of a really strong 4Q, the thinking is that there was some pullforwards / backlog flush ahead of these competitive launches. Management was clear that this is not the reason for their guide and that they do not see a fully recovered market dynamic throughout the year at the moment. There are still funding issues on the research side and some of their biggest accounts hail from institutions that are in the cross-hairs of funding cuts w/in the current administration, so everything is not back to normal. Outside of these dynamics, mgmt. continues to invest across the portfolio to diversify into multi-omics, workflow simplification/automation, and building out a comprehensive analytical software ecosystem around DRAGEN. Given the guide, the macro, and the setup into what is expected to be some meaningful announcements at AGBT, investors are likely hitting pause and taking a wait and see approach as to the year shakes out. As we have seen with the rest of the group, investors have little risk appetite for a 2H-weighted guide, especially with a guide baking in some improvement

## Key Takes from the Call 

**ILMN launched BioInsight in 4Q for AI enabled drug development including interest + key partnerships from large pharma** 

- BioInsight supports discovery and drug development through data, software and AI with tools to perturb biology using CRISPR at genome wide levels and using AI to analyze and build predictive biological models 

- The "Billion Cell Atlas" is claimed to be the most comprehensive map of human biology for drug discovery. It was built using single cell , CRISPR-based perturbation and AI, and has seen strong interest from biopharma partners including initial collaborations with AZN, MCK and LLY. 

**Multiomics investment is increasingly a long-term growth pillar for ILMN, using scale of the platform to capture more of the workflow** 

- ILMN launched Illumina Connected Multiomics, a software platform that integrates genomics, proteomics, single-cell, and ABE genomics data 

- The company remains on track to introduce its spatial transcriptomics solution and its Constellation mapped-read technology in in 1H’26 

- Mgmt. expects multiomics combined with BioInsights to drive at least 1%-2% revenue growth in 2027, with momentum accelerating in subsequent years
