# **Illumina (ILMN)** 

Life Sciences Tools & Services 

## Solid Q1 Beat and Raise; Stock Reaction Appears Unjustified [Flash Note] 

**Illumina delivered a strong Q1 print, with revenue, margin and EPS all above expectations. Q1 revenue of $1.091B beat consensus by $18M and Freedom Broker by $16M, while nonGAAP EPS of $1.15 beat consensus and Freedom Broker by $0.1. The after-hours stock reaction is mixed (roughly -1%) that looks unjustified given the combination of NovaSeq X strength, margin leverage and higher FY26 EPS guidance. We would expect positive momentum over the next 1-2 trading days, as the market should focus more on clinical-led instrument/ consumables demand and less on still-weak research/applied trends.** 

**The beat was broad enough to be considered high quality.** Revenue rose 4.8% YoY to $1.091B, with ROW organic growth of 3.5%, above management’s guidance range. The key driver was stronger-than-expected instrument demand: Illumina placed more than 80 NovaSeq X systems versus a 50–60 quarterly target, while sequencing instruments revenue rose 9% YoY. Clinical remained the main demand engine, with ex-China clinical sequencing consumables up 20% for the second consecutive quarter. Non-GAAP operating margin expanded 150 bps YoY to 21.9%, and non-GAAP EPS rose 19% YoY to $1.15. 

**FY26 guidance moved higher, but not aggressively.** Revenue guidance was raised by $20M at the midpoint to $4.52–$4.62B, broadly above our prior $4.54B estimate at the midpoint. Non-GAAP EPS guidance increased to $5.15–$5.30 from $5.05–$5.20, above our prior $5.06 estimate and implying estimates up. Operating margin guidance was raised 10 bps at the midpoint to 23.4–23.6%, broadly consistent with the view that margin expansion remains on track. Q2 guidance of $1.12–$1.14B revenue and $1.20–$1.25 EPS is slightly above our prior Q2 revenue estimate of $1.10B and broadly in line on EPS at $1.21. 

**The real surprise was the evidence that NovaSeq X demand is stronger than assumed and that the instrument outlook is moving from stabilization toward modest growth.** The consumables segment remains the primary growth engine in 2026 and instruments is likely to stabilize rather than grow. The print therefore improves confidence in the 2026–2027 growth bridge, especially as new NovaSeq X placements should support future consumables revenue. Research/applied weakness remains a constraint, with ex-China research and applied consumables down 12%, but the market is likely to look through this given the stronger clinical signal, buyback authorization, and upgraded EPS guidance.

###### **_Freedom Broker's Analyst Rating Calculation Disclosure_** 

The calculation of the Analyst Rating (“AR”) is disclosed below. 

Analyst Rating (AR) = (Expected Return) / (Annual Volatility) 

1. Expected Return is calculated using the formula: Expected Return = (Price Target / Current Price) − 1 

This figure represents the anticipated percentage change from the stock’s current price to the analyst’s target price. 

2. Annual Volatility is calculated as: 

Annual Volatility = (Standard Deviation of Daily Returns over the Past Year) × √252 

Volatility measures how much the stock price fluctuates over time. To calculate this, Freedom Broker uses the standard deviation (a statistical measure of how much daily returns vary from the average) of the stock’s daily price returns over the past year, adjusted to reflect a full year by multiplying it by the square root of 252 – the approximate number of trading days in a year. 

Once calculated, the AR indicates how many standard deviations the analyst’s expected return is away from the current price. Investors should carefully review this entire report, including the definitions of all ratings, and should not base investment decisions solely on a stock’s stated rating.
