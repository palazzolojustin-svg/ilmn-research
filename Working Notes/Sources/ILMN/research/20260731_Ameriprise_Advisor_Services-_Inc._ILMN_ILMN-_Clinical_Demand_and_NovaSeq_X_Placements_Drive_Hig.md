## Clinical Demand and NovaSeq X Placements Drive Higher Guide 

### Key Takeaways 

- ILMN delivered Q2’26 revenue and Adj. EPS above consensus and guidance. 

###### **Q2’26 Results** 

Illumina’s Q2’26 revenue of $1.159B grew 9.4% y/y (6.5% y/y organic), beating consensus of $1.131B and 4.0% y/y organic growth. Q2’26 Adj. EPS of $1.31 beat consensus and the company’s internal guidance of $1.23 and increased 10.1% y/y from the Core ILMN Adj. EPS of $1.19 in Q2’25. 

###### **Clinical Capacity Expansion Supports Outlook** 

More than 95 NovaSeq X placements marked a second consecutive quarter above ILMN’s historical target of 50 to 60 systems, with ~70% of placements directed to clinical customers. Management attributed demand primarily to customers adding capacity for expected testing volumes, including multi-unit orders from large clinical laboratories and support for new clinical trials. Clinical sequencing consumables increased 15% y/y in rest-of-world (ROW) markets, led by U.S. growth above 20% y/y, while oncology remained the largest application and molecular residual disease (MRD) gained momentum. In our view, the expanding NovaSeq X installed base strengthens visibility into future consumables growth, although normalization typically requires at least six to nine months. Instrument growth could moderate against more difficult comparisons, per management, while research demand remains uncertain. **_Continued on Page 2…_**

###### **Market Dynamics** 

Management continues to view clinical sequencing as ILMN’s principal growth driver. ROW clinical sequencing consumables increased 15% y/y in Q2, compared with 20% y/y in Q1, while U.S. clinical demand remained above 20%. Management attributed most of the change to near-term conditions in the Middle East and Latin America rather than a deterioration in underlying U.S. demand. Oncology remained the largest clinical application, with therapy selection representing the largest revenue pool and molecular residual disease (MRD) beginning to contribute more visibly. Rare disease and screening applications also continued to grow. 

The NovaSeq X transition advanced further in Q2. The platform represented 59% of high-throughput consumables revenue and 83% of high-throughput gigabases shipped. Clinical volume reached 78%, while research and applied volume reached 92%. Management expects clinical conversion to reach 80% to 85% by year-end. Management also indicated that the continued operation of NovaSeq 6000 systems is a normal transition dynamic rather than an identifiable consumables headwind. 

Research and applied sequencing consumables declined 7% y/y in ROW markets but increased 9% q/q. Management observed greater U.S. funding activity and customer inquiries late in Q2, although it remains too early to identify a sustained recovery. The FY’26 outlook

###### **Strategic Developments** 

ILMN continues to expand its strategy across core sequencing, multiomics, and biological data services. The company launched an earlyaccess whole-genome molecular residual disease (MRD) research workflow designed to shorten assay-development timelines and lower development costs. The workflow operates on NovaSeq systems and could reinforce the platform’s role as customers expand sequencingintensive oncology applications. Management also indicated that the NovaSeq X roadmap provides customers with greater confidence that the platform can support evolving workflows over several years. 

ILMN launched StrataMap Spatial, a sequencing-based spatial workflow that broadens its ability to support tissue-analysis applications. Management reported customer interest above its internal expectations following the launch. The acquired SomaLogic offerings were rebranded as SomaScan and SomaSeq, with the business tracking toward the high end of the company’s transaction expectations. Most multiomics products, including single-cell and TruPath offerings, are recorded within consumables revenue. Management continues to expect new products to contribute an incremental one to two percentage points of revenue growth in 2027. 

BioInsight combines sequencing perturbation tools, computing, and AI to develop multiomics datasets and interpretation tools for Pharma customers. The Billion Cell Atlas had delivered more than 300M cells by quarter-end, and ILMN added three partners after the quarter, increasing the total to six. The company has begun recognizing revenue from the program, which is included in sequencing service and other revenue. Management characterized the opportunity as early but indicated that the data could support drug-discovery programs and the development of predictive biological models. 

###### **2026 Outlook** 

ILMN increased FY’26 organic revenue growth (ex-ROW) guidance to ‘greater than 4.0% y/y’ and increased revenue guidance to a range of $4.60B to $4.64B (previous $4.52B to $4.62B), above consensus of $4.571B prior to the earnings release. ILMN increased FY’26 Adj. EPS guidance to a new range of $5.30 to $5.40 per share (previous $5.15 to $5.30), projecting growth of 10.5% y/y at the midpoint. The range was ahead of consensus of $5.23 prior to the earnings release. 

For Q3’26, ILMN guided to revenue of $1.14B to $1.16B, ROW organic growth of ~4.5%, Adj. EPS of $1.33 to $1.38, and an Adj. operating margin of ~24%. The Q3 margin outlook would represent ~150 bps of sequential improvement from Q2, supported by a higher consumables mix, cost actions, and operating-efficiency improvements. Management expects Q4 to remain the largest revenue quarter of the year. An extra fiscal week in Q4 could contribute ~0.5 percentage point to revenue growth, primarily through consumables.

###### **Capital Allocation** 

Q2 operating cash flow was $201M and free cash flow was $162M, compared with $234M and $204M, respectively, in Q2’25. Management attributed the lower cash flow primarily to the timing of tax payments and higher inventory as the company secured supplies of critical components for the next several quarters. Capital expenditures were $39M. ILMN ended Q2 with ~$1.17B in cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments, compared with ~$1.99B of total debt and gross leverage of ~1.6x last-12-month EBITDA. 

In Q2, ILMN repurchased ~0.9M shares for ~$122M at an average price of $129.07 per share. The company had ~$1.8B remaining under its share-repurchase authorizations at quarter-end and plans to continue repurchasing shares opportunistically. In our view, the remaining authorization provides capital-return flexibility, although the pace of repurchases could continue to vary with valuation, cash generation, acquisition-related requirements, and balance-sheet priorities. 

###### **Valuation** 

ILMN shares trade at a current 12m forward P/E ratio of 36.3x, which is a discount to the stock’s 5-year average forward P/E ratio of 72.2x. The large discount reflects in part the recent divestiture of **GRAIL Inc (NASDAQ; GRAL; $71.14)** which occurred on June 24, 2024 and

In our view, ILMN’s valuation framework should balance improving clinical sequencing demand, elevated NovaSeq X placements, higher FY’26 guidance, and the potential for better consumables growth against uncertainty surrounding research funding, Greater China, the timing of multiomics revenue, cost inflation, and execution against the company’s 2027 financial objectives. The stock’s investment case increasingly depends on whether recent instrument placements convert into sustained consumables pull-through and whether ILMN can achieve higher revenue growth while expanding margins. 

###### **Investment Thesis** 

In our view, Illumina offers investors a vehicle to invest in the genomic medicine of the future through exposure to the large diagnostic markets related to diagnosing, treating, and monitoring diseases with genetic or hereditary origins, including cancer. We believe the launch of NovaSeq X and X Plus, ILMN’s next-generation production-scale sequencing system that can sequence a human genome, can potentially be a multi-year revenue growth tailwind for the Instruments franchise. The company believes the next-generation sequencing (NGS) total addressable market (TAM) is $125B and only ~$10B of it is being serviced today. Innovation in the areas of single-cell analysis, spatial analysis, and liquid biopsy includes further potential growth opportunities for ILMN. For more aggressive investors with multi-year time horizons, we view the improving financial profile and continued recovery as an opportunity to accumulate the stock.
