### Illumina Inc. 

# **If You Seed it, The Consumable Revs Will Come...Right?** 

Big instrument upside surprise, which is what we expect the bulls to point to. Bears are going to point to the in-line consumables revs and what appears to be lower price/Gb on NSX volumes. Also, with such a big beat on instruments only translating to 50bps topline beat, it is hard to declare a... 

_...clear victory. However, w/ few beat/raises in the space this Q and the momentum behind the business, we expect shares to be up tomorrow and for the bull/bear debate to rage on._ 

CEO Jacob Thaysen and team really put it on the industry from an instrument perspective. Everyone thought it was going to be a tough instrument Q given the reads from every other tools company, especially around A&G capex demand. Apparently ILMN didn’t get the memo and beat our placement estimates by >50% and what is more is that they are expecting to maintain the strength in 2Q, eliminating the idea of a pull forward. Clearly the leasing program is working b/c the big NSX placement number didn’t lead to a linear beat on inst revs, but we understand the strategy of seeding the market with as many boxes as they can. To mgmt’s point, a customer is not going to lease a box just to have one because this isn’t 2014 and sequencing is not a vanity research project anymore. Said another way, the consumables should come. But on that front, we would think that there would have been a beat on sequencing consumables given how the ramp up time on new instruments is much faster than in the past. We think that part of the issue is around price/Gb on NSX where we saw an increase in NSX Gb output as a % of total output but % of NSX consumable revs remained flat at 55%. Mgmt explained that there is plenty of noise around NSX capacity, types of flow-cells used and that the general trend is still moving higher. We give them the benefit of the doubt as we recognize that we have imperfect data and this is 1 quarter of action. However, it warrants watching as the cynical view is that ILMN is discounting ahead of Roche’s launch and in response to Ultima. 

On the guide, mgmt. passed through the beat and now points to the high end of the range of the ROW Organic guide of 2-4%. The 2H margin ramp is embeds a sizable 2Q to 3Q stepup as 3Q tends to be the higher margin Q given the seasonal mix dynamics. So while we are relatively comfortable on the topline guide, the margin guide is not a layup by any means. The business has decent momentum and we can see shares moving higher on continued outperformance. However, we remain UW because we are not sold on the ability to deliver HSD growth in ’27. 

On valuation, shares are trading at the lower-end of the group on an EV/EBITDA basis, but on the higher end on PE. We set our PT to $122 to account for the beat and our estimates going up for the year. Our new PT is based on 23.5x PE on our ’26 EPS of $5.17 vs. 21x on $5.11 prior. The

## Key Takes from the Call 

**Clinical volumes remain the clear growth engine, however X consumables as a percent of revenue remained flat qoq at 55% while overall X Gb % increased 500bps to 76%** 

- Clinical sequencing consumables grew **~** 20% ex-China for the second straight Q and represented >65% of sequencing consumables revenue and continues to gain mix 

- °<sup>76% of clinical volume now on NovaSeq X, expected to reach 80–85%+ by YE26</sup> 

- Mgmt. urged that the trying to discern Q to Q dynamics on how revenue as a % could be flat while Gb increased 500bps was a frivolous exercise. This is due to mix, e.g. if all new volumes went exclusively to 25Bs on X while new volumes on the 6k could have gone to smaller panels that generate more revs per Gb 

**Research volumes remain pressured given slow funding disbursement and continued hesitancy to spend** 

- Research & applied consumables were down ~12% ex-China in Q1 due to funding caution, however mgmt. expects gradual improvement but does not embed recovery in FY guidance (although guide implies less decline through the year) 

- ~90% of research volumes are already on the X which mgmt. says positions the co. for cleaner growth when demand returns as pricing comp dynamics are behind them 

**NovaSeq X placements were strong due to clinical demand with backlog ~1 Q of revenue** 

- More than 80 Xs were placed in 1Q, with >60% tied to clinical customers 

- Demand was driven by incremental capacity adds, multi-unit orders, and new clinical trials
