# Illumina (ILMN) 

### _An Ameriprise Investment Research Group publication_ 

Sector: Health Care Investment style: Mid Cap Growth Christopher K. Macino, CFA, Director, WMS Research, Equity May 01, 2026 

### Investment Opinion: Buy 

## Clinical Momentum and NovaSeq X Demand Support Raised Guidance 

###### **Q1’26 Results** 

Illumina’s Q1’26 revenue of $1.091B increased 4.8% y/y (1.2% y/y organic), beating consensus of $1.073B. Q1’26 Adj. EPS of $1.15 beat consensus and the company’s internal guidance of $1.05 and increased 18.6% y/y from the Core ILMN Adj. EPS of $0.97 in Q1’25. Adj. operating margin expanded to 21.9%, up 150 basis points y/y. Results were driven primarily by better-thanexpected NovaSeq X placements and clinical consumables demand at the high end of expectations. 

###### **Business Segment Performance** 

Performance in Q1 was led by continued clinical adoption and higher NovaSeq X placements. Sequencing consumables revenue of $726M increased 4.3% y/y, with rest of world (ROW) organic growth of 5% y/y. Clinical sequencing consumables grew 20% y/y in ROW for the second consecutive quarter, reflecting expanding use of sequencing-based diagnostics and more sequencing-intensive applications. Sequencing instruments revenue of $118M increased 8.3% y/y, supported by more than 80 NovaSeq X placements, above the company’s prior quarterly target range of 50 to 60 systems. **_Continued on Page 2…_** 

### Key Takeaways 

- ILMN delivered Q1’26 revenue and Adj. EPS above consensus and guidance, supported by NovaSeq X placements and clinical consumables growth. 

- Clinical sequencing consumables grew 20% y/y in ROW for the second consecutive quarter. 

- NovaSeq X demand exceeded expectations, with more than 80 placements and a pipeline supporting higher instrument expectations. 

- Research and applied consumables remain pressured, but funding improvement in this market could provide upside if spending normalizes. 

- Management raised FY’26 revenue and Adj. EPS expectations, reflecting Q1 outperformance and confidence in clinical demand.

###### **Market Dynamics** 

Management continues to view clinical markets as ILMN’s primary growth engine, supported by reimbursement progress, new assay launches, and customers shifting toward more information-rich testing approaches. Clinical sequencing consumables grew 20% y/y in ROW during Q1, matching the 20% y/y growth rate from Q4’25. Management noted that the clinical business is not showing signs of deceleration, and growth above the company’s current mid-teens outlook could represent upside. 

The NovaSeq X transition continues to support clinical and research workflows. NovaSeq X represented 55% of high-throughput consumables revenue in Q1, consistent with Q4’25, while the platform represented 82% of high-throughput gigabytes (Gb) shipped. Research and applied volume is now ~90% transitioned to NovaSeq X, while clinical volume reached 76%, with management expecting the clinical transition to progress further to around 80-85% by year-end. 

Research and academic markets remain more cautious. Research and applied sequencing consumables declined -12% y/y in ROW, reflecting ongoing uncertainty around funding. Management noted that National Institutes of Health (NIH) funding commentary appears

###### **Strategic Developments** 

ILMN continues to advance its strategy across core sequencing, multiomics, and data services. The company closed the SomaLogic acquisition in January, with the business tracking in line with expectations on revenue and profitability. Management also highlighted TruPath, which enables whole-genome sequencing with deeper insight while reducing hands-on time to ~10 minutes. Customer interest is notable in rare disease, where TruPath can consolidate several test types into a single workflow. 

The NovaSeq X roadmap remains a key strategic lever. ILMN introduced an 18-month roadmap that includes 14B and 35B flow cells, staggered flow cell runs, and improved Q70 performance. Management noted that these updates should improve flexibility, throughput, and workflow efficiency. Spatial transcriptomics also remains on track for launch later this year, with early access users generating data in challenging sample types. 

###### **2026 Outlook** 

ILMN reaffirmed FY’26 organic revenue growth (excluding China) guidance in a range of 1.0% to 3.0% y/y, but slightly increased revenue to a range of $4.52B to $4.62B (previous $4.5B to $4.6B), above consensus of $4.54B prior to the earnings release. ILMN increased FY’26 Adj. EPS guidance to a new range of $5.15 to $5.30 per share (previous $5.05 to $5.20), projecting growth of 8.0% y/y at the midpoint. The range was ahead of consensus of $5.11 prior to the earnings release. 

Management now expects sequencing instrument revenue growth to be flat to low-single digits y/y on a CC and ROW basis (slight improvement), supported by NovaSeq X demand and a placement pipeline that remains above prior expectations. 

For Q2'26, ILMN guided to revenue of $1.12B to $1.14B (3%-5% organic growth y/y), above consensus of $1.11B. ILMN guided to Q2’26 Adj. EPS in a range of $1.20 to $1.25, slightly below consensus of $1.25, and adj. operating margin of ~22%. The Q2 margin reflects a higher instrument revenue mix, inflationary cost pressures from electronic components and freight, and a full quarter from the acquisition of SomaLogic. Management noted that mitigating actions have begun and are expected to become visible from Q3 into Q4, supporting meaningful margin improvement in 2H’26.

###### **Capital Allocation** 

Cash flow generation remained solid, with Q1 operating cash flow of $289M and free cash flow of $251M. ILMN repurchased ~2.0M shares for $242M during the quarter at an average price of $120.85 per share. ILMN's Board of Directors authorized an additional $1.5B in share repurchases on April 28, 2026, with ~$400M remaining on the existing authorization at quarter-end. The company ended Q1 with ~$1.16B in cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments, and gross leverage of ~1.5x gross debt to last 12 months EBITDA. 

ILMN closed the SomaLogic acquisition on January 30 for a net cash payment of $363M, plus potential royalties and milestone payments. Subsequent to quarter-end, ILMN paid the first $25M milestone tied to achievement of certain 2025 targets. The Board also authorized an additional $1.5B in share repurchases, increasing flexibility for opportunistic capital return. 

###### **Valuation** 

ILMN shares trade at a current P/E ratio of 23.7x forward year earnings estimates, which is a discount to the stock’s 5-year average forward P/E ratio of 72.2x. The large discount reflects in part the recent divestiture of **GRAIL Inc (NASDAQ; GRAL; $54.48)** which occurred on June 24, 2024 and the return to a normalized ‘Core Illumina’ Adj. EPS. In our opinion, as the launch of NovaSeq X and X Plus continues, ILMN could generate solid earnings leverage in the near future as the company moves past the initial launch expenses and generates complementary disposable revenue from the instrument sale. In our view, the current valuation may not reflect the opportunity related to next-generation sequencing (NGS) oncology testing markets that the company is targeting.

###### **Investment Thesis** 

In our view, Illumina offers investors a vehicle to invest in the genomic medicine of the future through exposure to the large diagnostic markets related to diagnosing, treating, and monitoring diseases with genetic or hereditary origins, including cancer. We believe the launch of NovaSeq X and X Plus, ILMN’s next-generation production-scale sequencing system that can sequence a human genome, can potentially be a multi-year revenue growth tailwind for the Instruments franchise. The company believes the next-generation sequencing (NGS) total addressable market (TAM) is $125B and only ~$10B of it is being serviced today. Innovation in the areas of single-cell analysis, spatial analysis, and liquid biopsy include further potential growth opportunities for ILMN. For more aggressive investors with multi-year time horizons, we view the recent weakness as an opportunity to accumulate the stock.
