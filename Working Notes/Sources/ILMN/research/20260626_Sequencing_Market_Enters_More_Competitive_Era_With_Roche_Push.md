# **Sequencing Competition: Roche and Illumina** 

Read Research Report: Global Life-Science Technologies 2026 Midyear Outlook 



Jonathan Palmer Team: Health Care BI Senior Industry Analyst 

Sequencing Market Enters More Competitive Era With Roche Push 

Contributing Analysts Adeline Zandi 

(Bloomberg Intelligence) -- The sequencing market is entering a more competitive phase as new platform architectures begin to challenge the durability of Illumina's long-standing leadership. Axelios could test whether differentiated technology and Roche's channel access are enough to loosen Illumina's grip on high-value sequencing workflows. (06/26/26) 

1. Roche's Axelios Tests Illumina’s Sequencing Moat 

Contributing Analysts Adeline Zandi 

Illumina's NovaSeq X remains the entrenched standard, with scale, familiarity and workflow depth. Customers have built pipelines, bioinformatics, purchasing patterns, validation processes and staff expertise around Illumina, making platform switching difficult. Roche has a credible path given Axelios' differentiated sequencing-by-expansion approach and the company's deep diagnostics reach across hospitals, labs and oncology workflows. If Axelios performs as advertised, it could pressure Illumina's pricing power, accelerate sequencing adoption in routine diagnostics and give Roche greater exposure to high-growth oncology applications. 

Disruption to Illumina is unlikely to be immediate, but Roche could still chip away where workflow or economic advantages are clear. (06/26/26)
