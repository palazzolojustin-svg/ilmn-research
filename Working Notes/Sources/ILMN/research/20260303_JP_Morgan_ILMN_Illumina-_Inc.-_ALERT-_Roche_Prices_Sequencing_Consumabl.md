# **Illumina, Inc.** 

### **Neutral** 

###### **ILMN, ILMN US** 

#### ALERT: Roche Prices Sequencing Consumables at $150 per Genome vs. ILMN’s $200 per Genome 

Price (25 Feb 26):$121.87 **Price Target (Dec-26):$120.00** 

This afternoon at the AGBT conference, Roche (ROG SW, covered by J.P. Morgan analyst Richard Vosser) held its sequencing solutions workshop and lunch, where the company announced sequencing consumables list price of $150 per genome for its Axelios 1 instrument (including synthesis reagents and consumables but not including library prep costs). This price assumes users reuse sensor modules 20 times (see more on this below). In terms of product specification, the SBX duplex is capable of 16 genomes per run, 64 genomes per day, translating to ~$2,400 per run. For comparison, ILMN’s sequencing consumables are priced at ~$200 per genome for the NovaSeq X instrument. Based on our conversations, most investors expected Roche to price its consumables ~$150 per genome, with some estimating as low as $100.

Turning to other takeaways from the workshop, Roche is framing the Axelios 1 around cost per data output, highlighting ~$0.06 per million reads in sequencing consumables, translating to SBX simplex capabilities at >40B reads per run, ~160B per day, translating to a ~$2,400 list price per run. Lastly, regarding the cfDNA panel, Roche noted that pricing will vary by application but noted that, if you assume ~1.1 Mb panel, 2,000x coverage, and 96 samples /run, this results in a $25/sample price. Turning to sensor module reusability, the company presented data demonstrating that performance is maintained when its sensor module (the Axelios’ version of a flow cell) is reused over 20 times across one week. While this data tracks reusability over one week, the company noted that the sensor module can be used for longer than one week. In addition, Roche highlighted that 15/16 sensor modules successfully reached 20 runs of reuse. Lastly, Roche noted that the intended product configuration is designed to support up to 20 uses.

## Investment Thesis, Valuation and Risks 

**Illumina, Inc.** _(Neutral; Price Target: $120.00)_ 

###### **Investment Thesis** 

Although we continue to see strong clinical traction for NGS over the long-term with potential ramping contributions from the multiomics pipeline, the ongoing competitive concerns and pressured pricing dynamics leave us on the sideline with a Neutral rating. 

###### **Valuation** 

Our Dec. 2026 price target of $120 is derived using a DCF methodology with a CAPMderived WACC of 10.2% and terminal growth of 4.0%. 

###### **Risks to Rating and Price Target** 

Upside risks include: (1) A faster-than-expected recovery in the academic end market; (2) Stronger growth in the clinical end market driven by increased adoption of sequencingbased clinical applications; (3) Broader uptake of whole genome sequencing; (4) and expanding use cases for multiomic offerings. Downside risks include: (1) Ongoing uncertainty or further pressure in academic research funding; (2) Intensifying competition and potential pricing pressure in the sequencing market; (3) And execution risks related to the launch and adoption of new products.
