### _An Ameriprise Investment Research Group publication_ 

Sector: Health Care Investment style: Mid Cap Growth Christopher K. Macino, CFA, Director, WMS Research, Equity February 06, 2026 

### Investment Opinion: Buy 

## Clinical Market Strength Continues, But Research & Academic Markets Still Pressured 

### Key Takeaways 

- ILMN Q4’25 revenue and Adj. EPS beat consensus driven by momentum with its NovaSeq X transition and growing sequencing volumes, particularly in clinical markets. 

###### **Q4’25 Results** 

Pre-announcing Q4’25 revenue ahead of the _JPMorgan Healthcare Conference_ last month, Illumina posted Q4’25 revenue of $1.159B, up 4.0% y/y on a constant currency (CC) basis, beating consensus of $1.099B. Q4’25 Adj. EPS of $1.35 beat consensus of $1.21 and the company’s internal guidance of $1.29 and increased 42.1% y/y from the Core ILMN Adj. EPS of $0.95 in Q4’24. Excluding Greater China, revenue grew 7% CC y/y, driven by a significant acceleration in the clinical business. Adj. operating margin expanded to 23.7% (up 400 basis points y/y), reflecting solid operating leverage and the continued benefit of cost optimization initiatives enacted earlier in the year. 

###### **Business Segment Performance** 

Performance in Q4 was anchored by robust clinical demand, where consumables revenue grew 20% y/y excluding China. Total sequencing consumables revenue of $755M increased 8% y/y, driven by high-throughput volume growth as customers ramped utilization of NovaSeq X. **_Continued on Page 2…_**

###### **Market Dynamics** 

Management views the clinical market as the company’s primary growth engine heading into 2026, reinforced by a 20% y/y expansion in clinical consumables revenue excluding China during Q4’25. This momentum is driven by the broader adoption of sequencing-intensive applications, particularly whole-genome sequencing and comprehensive genomic profiling within oncology and genetic disease testing. Consequently, ILMN assumes robust double-digit to mid-teens growth for the clinical segment in FY’26 as customers continue to scale assays on the NovaSeq X platform. 

Conversely, the Research and Academic markets remain measured. While consumables revenue in this segment stabilized in Q4, management assumes no fundamental improvement in demand dynamics for FY’26, projecting a mid-to-high single-digit revenue decline for Research and Applied consumables. Regarding funding, management noted that recent announcements have provided improved line of sight into the National Institutes of Health (NIH) budget. However, despite this high-level clarity, uncertainty persists regarding the specific timing and mechanics of grant distribution to individual institutes. As a result, ILMN has adopted a prudent outlook, assuming that Academic purchasing behavior will remain cautious until funding flows fully normalize. 

###### **Strategic Developments** 

Management highlighted significant progress across its three strategic pillars: core sequencing, multiomics, and data services. A key milestone was the completion of the SomaLogic acquisition in January 2026, which integrates high-plex proteomics with ILMN’s NGS scale. This addition is expected to accelerate the multiomics roadmap, with a spatial transcriptomics solution on track for introduction in 1H’26. Additionally, the company launched BioInsight, a new business unit focused on data and AI-driven drug discovery. Its inaugural

###### **2026 Outlook** 

ILMN issued FY’26 organic revenue growth (excluding China) guidance in a range of 1.0% to 3.0% y/y, or sales in a range of $4.5B to $4.6B, ahead of consensus of $4.41B prior to the earnings release. ILMN also issued FY’26 Adj. EPS guidance in a range of $5.05 to $5.20 per share, projecting growth of 5.9% y/y at the midpoint. The range was ahead of consensus of $5.07 prior to the earnings release. 

Management anticipates sequencing instrument revenue to be down low-single digits to flat in FY’26, targeting 50-60 NovaSeq X placements per quarter. Greater China remains a headwind, with revenue expected between $210M to $220M as the company works through import license dynamics. Excluding the impact of the SomaLogic acquisition, operating margins are projected to expand approximately 130 basis points.

###### **Capital Allocation** 

Cash flow generation remained solid, with Q4 operating cash flow of $321M (FY’25: $1.1B) and free cash flow of $267M (FY’25: $931M). ILMN returned capital to shareholders by repurchasing ~337k shares for $42M during the quarter. The company ended FY’25 with a solid balance sheet position, holding $1.63B in cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments, and a gross leverage ratio of ~1.6x. The $350M upfront payment for the SomaLogic acquisition was funded with cash on hand subsequent to quarter-end. 

###### **Valuation** 

ILMN shares trade at a current P/E ratio of 26.0x forward year earnings estimates, which is a discount to the stock’s 5-year average forward P/E ratio of 72.2x. The large discount reflects in part the recent divestiture of **GRAIL Inc (NASDAQ; GRAL; $94.06)** which occurred on June 24, 2024 and the return to a normalized ‘Core Illumina’ Adj. EPS. In our opinion, as the launch of NovaSeq X and X Plus continues, ILMN could generate solid earnings leverage in the near future as the company moves past the initial launch expenses and generates complementary disposable revenue from the instrument sale. In our view, the current valuation may not reflect the opportunity related to next-generation sequencing (NGS) oncology testing markets that the company is targeting. 

###### **Investment Thesis** 

In our view, Illumina offers investors a vehicle to invest in the genomic medicine of the future through exposure to the large diagnostic markets related to diagnosing, treating, and monitoring diseases with genetic or hereditary origins, including cancer. We believe the launch of NovaSeq X and X Plus, ILMN’s next-generation production-scale sequencing system that can sequence a human genome, can potentially be a multi-year revenue growth tailwind for the Instruments franchise. The company believes the next-generation sequencing (NGS) total addressable market (TAM) is $125B and only ~$10B of it is being serviced today. Innovation in the areas of single-cell analysis, spatial analysis, and liquid biopsy include further potential growth opportunities for ILMN. For more aggressive investors with multi-year time horizons, we view the recent weakness as an opportunity to accumulate the stock.
