### **Overweight** 

###### **ILMN, ILMN US** 

Price (30 Jul 26):$205.09

ILMN’s 2Q beat demonstrated continued momentum in clinical consumables, growing +15% ROW, albeit down from the +20% trend seen the past two quarters, which was attributable to pockets of softness in the Middle East and Latin America in the quarter though U.S. clinical consumables grew >+20%. EPS growth moving forward as highly achievable, with upside from a more pronounced acceleration in research end-market demand and emerging opportunities in multi-omics and BioInsight. ###### **<mark>Summary Investment Thesis and Valuation</mark>** 

##### **Investment Thesis** 

ILMN’s clinical franchise is anchored by major CLIA labs that have largely transitioned to the NovaSeq X and face meaningful switching friction, supporting a stickier-than-appreciated user base and a multi-year test volume runway that underpins our +HSD organic growth forecast through 2030E. Structural tailwinds across therapy selection, MRD, and MCED are further amplified by rising sequencing intensity as labs adopt WGS, RNA, and multiomic workflows. Competitive risk from Roche’s Axelios 1 appears manageable near-term given unresolved concerns around reusable flow-cell contamination, and higher DNA input requirements, while incremental upside comes from a recovering U.S. A&G end market into 2H26/2027 and ILMN’s expanding multiomics portfolio (Fluent, SomaLogic, StrataMap). With +mid/high-teens adj. EPS growth and a leading ~4-5% FCF yield, ILMN offers scarcity value at a 4.1x EV/EBITDA premium to Core Tools (well below its historical ~15x premium) making the relative valuation attractive even after the recent run-up. As such, we reiterate our Overweight rating. 

##### **Valuation** 

Our Dec. 2027 price target of $230 is derived using a DCF methodology with a CAPM-derived WACC of 9.8% and terminal growth of 4.5%.
