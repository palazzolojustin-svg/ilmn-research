# **Illumina (ILMN)** 

Life Sciences Tools & Services 

## Steady Plans for 2026 

### **Rating:** Buy **Price Target:** $140.00 

- Revenue is back on a growth trajectory, and organic growth should continue in 2026. 

- Operating margin improved to a “healthy” level and retains expansion potential in line with 2027 targets. 

- The Consumables segment is driving revenue and signals improving demand. 

- Improved predictability in the regulatory environment underpins stabilization in Instruments. 

- Expanding the services portfolio enhances revenue diversification and opens new avenues to monetize Illumina’s infrastructure. 

- **We raise our target price from $130 to $140 and maintain a Buy rating.** 

**Revenue Back on a Growth Track.** Total revenue in 4Q25 rose 5% YoY, reinforcing a return to growth after declines in 2024 and 1H25. Performance across major regions was positive except China, where regulatory constraints drove a 31% YoY decline in 4Q revenue. With shipments into China resuming, the company maintains a presence in this large market. However, a return to growth there is not yet in sight: 2026 guidance implies quarterly revenue averaging roughly the 3Q-4Q25 level, which we read as stabilization of commercial operations. A renewed growth trajectory will likely depend on easing regulatory constraints, in particular removal from the UEL (Unreliable Entities List). 

**Consumables: Lead Growth Engine.** Consumables (+6.8% YoY) remain the primary revenue driver on rising activity among clinical customers. Management highlights stronger demand for routine sequencing ex-China, with clinical consumables growing 20% YoY in 4Q and 16% YoY in 2H25. The upgrade cycle to NovaSeq X high-throughput systems is nearing its later stage: the consumables revenue share generated on NovaSeq X increased from 44% at end-2Q25 to 55% by year-end. Additional upside in consumables from the upgrade cycle remains in the clinical business, where NovaSeq X already accounts for 71% of total gigabases, a share that should continue to rise in 2026.

**NovaSeq X Transition Through 2026.** Management expects the NovaSeq X transition to complete in 2026, with 50–60 systems shipped per quarter on average and a larger portion likely in 4Q (seasonally stronger for shipments). Roughly 60% of systems should go to clinical customers with high utilization, which in our view will drive consumables growth through 2026-2027. 

Shipments and utilization for research are not strong yet, but management cited stabilizing regulation and funding. Whether this proves a leading indicator will be clearer through 2026. Based on current funding dynamics, a positive surprise is possible, though our base case for research revenue remains moderately negative for now. 

**Service and Other: Reaccelerating with New Offers.** Service and Other returned to growth (+2.4% YoY) after four consecutive quarters of declines and has continued growth potential in 2026. The BioInsight launch can support segment revenue next year. Importantly, BioInsight, and specifically the Billion Cell Atlas product, gives Illumina an additional monetization path within the still-soft research segment, enabling sales not only of instruments but also potentially higher-margin services. 

**Margins: Tariff Headwinds Offset by Operating Leverage.** Adjusted gross margin declined 40 bps YoY to 67%, driven mainly by ~205 bps of tariff headwind. Despite this, product gross margin remained healthy at ~70% for the year. Service margin improved meaningfully in 2H25 to 57% (vs 50% a year earlier). Together with lower adjusted OpEx, this lifted adjusted EBIT margin by 400 bps to 23.7%. We view operating profitability as firmly re-established above 23% for 2025, consistent with pre-GRAIL consolidation levels. Adjusted EPS was $0.05 above the top end of guidance for the quarter and the year. 

**Capital Allocation: Conservative and Shareholder-Friendly.** Illumina generated $931M FCF in 2025. $1.63B in cash and short-term investments and 1.6x gross leverage provide substantial financial flexibility for M&A and capital returns. Management repurchased $740M in 2025 ($42M in 4Q) and retains $643M of remaining authorization, to be used opportunistically. The company also demonstrated willingness to pursue strategic acquisitions, closing SomaLogic in January 2026 for $350M upfront plus potential royalties and milestone payments. Strategically, the SomaLogic platform broadens Illumina’s presence in proteomics, strengthening its multiomics positioning. Overall, the model balances organic growth and integration of acquired assets, with excess liquidity returned via buybacks. 

**2026 Guidance: Constructive Set-Up.** Revenue guidance of $4.5-4.6B is above consensus and implies 4-6% YoY growth – constructive relative to three years of declining Core Illumina revenue. Guidance includes a 1.5–2% contribution from SomaLogic. Consumables should remain the main organic growth driver, while Instruments are expected to be slightly negative; we see research as a potential source of upside during the year. Operating margin guidance is 23.3–23.5%, including a ~100 bps negative impact from SomaLogic. Adjusted EPS of $5.05-5.20 implies ~6% YoY growth

#### **Investment thesis** 

Illumina remains the standard-bearer in next-generation sequencing (NGS), supported by a large installed base of systems and an increasing mix shift toward clinical applications. Growth in consumables sales and expanding free cash flow are underpinned by the company’s expansion into new verticals, including multiomics and data/AI platforms. Illumina is positioned for a re-acceleration of revenue growth and margin expansion. 

The long-term attractiveness of the NGS market is supported by structural growth drivers, including broader adoption of genomics in oncology, neonatal screening, and expanding use of NGS across biopharma and population-scale programs. Illumina represents one of the few pure-play exposures to the long-term expansion of the global genomics market. Key risks include intensifying competition, a challenging macro environment, particularly in the research segment, and regulatory pressure in China. 

#### **Potential growth drivers** 

1. **Mid-term revenue growth acceleration.** A shift toward more data-intensive applications (WGS, MRD, early cancer detection) is expected to increase utilization of the NovaSeq X installed base, drive consumables demand, and support further system placements. 

2. **Completion of the NovaSeq X transition.** Finalizing the clinical migration and normalization of demand in research should help mitigate the impact of pricing erosion. 

3. **Expansion into multiomics and data/AI solutions.** Growth of service-oriented offerings is expected to create new, less cyclical revenue streams and enhance differentiation of Illumina’s product portfolio. 

#### **Key risks** 

1. **Competitive pressure in NGS.** Rising competition in high- and mid-throughput segments could intensify pricing pressure on consumables and instruments, limiting margin expansion and complicating monetization of the installed base. 

2. **Macro and budgetary headwinds.** Persistent weakness in academic funding may constrain demand for instruments and consumables in the research segment, increasing risk to sustained mid-term revenue growth. 

3. **China geopolitical and regulatory risk.** Inclusion on the UEL continues to restrict instrument sales and creates ongoing downside risk related to potential changes in import/export regulations.

###### **_Freedom Broker's Analyst Rating Calculation Disclosure_** 

The calculation of the Analyst Rating (“AR”) is disclosed below. 

Analyst Rating (AR) = (Expected Return) / (Annual Volatility) 

1. Expected Return is calculated using the formula: Expected Return = (Price Target / Current Price) − 1 

This figure represents the anticipated percentage change from the stock’s current price to the analyst’s target price. 

2. Annual Volatility is calculated as: 

Annual Volatility = (Standard Deviation of Daily Returns over the Past Year) × √252 

Volatility measures how much the stock price fluctuates over time. To calculate this, Freedom Broker uses the standard deviation (a statistical measure of how much daily returns vary from the average) of the stock’s daily price returns over the past year, adjusted to reflect a full year by multiplying it by the square root of 252 – the approximate number of trading days in a year. 

Once calculated, the AR indicates how many standard deviations the analyst’s expected return is away from the current price. Investors should carefully review this entire report, including the definitions of all ratings, and should not base investment decisions solely on a stock’s stated rating.
