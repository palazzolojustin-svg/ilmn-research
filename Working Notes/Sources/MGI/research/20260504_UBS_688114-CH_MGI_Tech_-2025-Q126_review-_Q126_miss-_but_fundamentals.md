## MGI Tech 

2025/Q126 review: Q126 miss, but fundamentals may have bottomed 

###### **Q: How did the results compare with expectations?** 

MGI recorded 2025 revenue of Rmb2.78bn (-7.73% YoY), net loss to the parent/ recurring net loss to the parent narrowed to Rmb222m/390m from Rmb601m/653m in 2024, largely in line with the preliminary results. Q126 revenue rose 24.8% YoY to Rmb585m from a low base in Q125, while recurring net loss to the parent narrowed to Rmb88.5m from Rmb144m in Q125, driven by a YoY improvement in GPM and cost control efforts,  in our view. Q126 results missed our forecasts. 

###### **Q: What were the most noteworthy areas in the results?** 

1) "Seq-all" segment revenue was under pressure in 2025 (down 8.04% YoY) to Rmb2.20bn, but growth rebounded to  23% in Q126 from a low base. By region in 2025, domestic revenue was down 11% YoY, APAC fell 29% YoY, EMEA was up 4% YoY, and the Americas rose 21% YoY. By product, instrument/reagent  revenues were down 17%/6%  in 2025, -4%/+23% YoY in Q126. Sequencer sales volume increased to 1,470+ in 2025 (versus 1,260+ in 2024), although ASP was dragged by pricing pressure and product mix changes, in our view. MGI said its market share of public bids in China's sequencing market rose to c70% in 2025.  2) In 2025 and Q126, omics segment revenue recorded rapid growth of 48%, driven by new products, while Generative Lab Intelligence (GLI) segment revenue was -32%/+10%. 3) MGI announced in February the acquisition of nanopore-sequencing and spatial omics platforms, and the divestment of its US subsidiary (see page 2). 

###### **Q: Has the company's outlook/guidance changed?** 

We lower our operating assumptions for 2026-28E (see page 3) to reflect weak market demand and intensified competition; we still forecast teens percentage revenue growth (mainly driven by the seq-all and omics segments) and NP breakeven in 2026. We expect margin expansion from a higher revenue mix of reagents and continued cost cuttings with further profit upside from the potential closure of its out-licensing and divestment deals in 2026-27E. This should increase MGI's product competitiveness, lower its exposure to geopolitical risks and improve profitability over the long term. 

###### **Valuation: cut price target to Rmb58.00 from Rmb73.80; maintain Neutral** 

Based on the above and assuming completion of the divestment in early 2027E (booking of a milestone payment and an asset disposal gain in 2027E), we now forecast 2026/27/28 EPS of Rmb0.25/1.07/0.70  from Rmb0.16/0.63/1.04. Our new DCF-based PT (WACC of 8.7% from 8.5% after updating risk-free rate and beta) implies 7.0x 2026E P/S, a 23% premium to the medtech peer average to reflect MGI's stronger global positioning.
