### Pacbio 

# **Hustle All Day and Dream of the Islands and the Sea** 

Vega demand the big headline in the Q and opening new customers, which could eventually help drive broader HiFi adoption w/in the clinic. A strong ’25 lays a solid first layer of the foundation for management to build off of. 

_Especially considering how tough the operating environment is, AGBT will be a good testing ground to see if the momentum can turn into an inflection point over the next 12-18mos for the business._ 

With our eyes on the guide, we were already modeling FY26 sales at the high-end of the $165M$180M range that mgmt initiated, and same goes for their GM guide of 41–44%. That's what could make this year different for PACB - where we could see the technology start to unlock the translational market in a more material way continue to shift away from academia. Mgmt called out strength in this part of the market in the Q w/ 55% growth in the clinic/hospital setting.  Vega can help here as well, as 65% of placements were to new-to-PACB customers, and arguably the more researcher’s hands HiFi is in, the better chances of making the shift to clinical applications. SPRQ-Nx resets long-read economics at $300/genome, which helps accessibility as well. To be clear, we are not expecting the funding floodgates to open, and it is still a very tough operating environment affecting all genomics companies for the most part. So the measurable KPIs on penetration and market expansion are likely to come more from channel checks and user feedback than from seeing an inflection in the business. What also clouds the demand is w/ Roche coming with a relatively long-ish read technology that may freeze some of the market for PACB. SPRQ-Nx launch by 2H could help thaw a broader market freeze, or at least lead to a nice pickup in consumables growth in the 2H. Either way, the market factors continue to be less than supportive for PACB, yet they keep hammering away at the opportunities they get. We expect to see this hustle continue through what is likely to be another knife-fight of a 1H. 

While we are encouraged by the strong end to 2025 and are relatively comfortable with the guide range, we remain on the sidelines with an EW rating and maintain our $2 PT, which is based on 5x EV/Sales on our 2026E sales estimate of $178M. Our thesis and model don't change much on this print, and we look for more detailed channel checks from AGBT in 2 weeks. 

**Keeping track of what PACB is doing in the clinic** , adoption is accelerating across rare disease, oncology, carrier screening, and population sequencing.
