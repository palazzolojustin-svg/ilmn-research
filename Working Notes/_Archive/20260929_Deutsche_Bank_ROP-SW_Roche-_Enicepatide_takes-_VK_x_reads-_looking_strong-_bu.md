### Viking VK2735 maintenance study update 

**On 22nd Sept 2026, Viking Therapeutics** **<u>press released</u> new data from their SC GLP-1/GIP VK2735 study showing dosing up 33 weeks on their Q2W and Q4W dosing regimens** after a QW induction phase. The P3 VANQUISH (SC VK2735) in obesity/T2D is fully enrolled with data due H2'27 and the oral P3 trials are expected to begin Q4'26. We note that oral maintenance data planned for Part 2 and will begin later in 2026. 

##### **1. Data summary** 

- n **Study** : exploratory P1 maintenance study in c180 adults with obesity. Patients received weekly SC VK2735 during a 21-week induction phase, followed by 12 weeks of Q2W, monthly, weekly or placebo maintenance. 

- n **Induction** efficacy: VK2735 delivered 16.3%-18.7% absolute weight loss at Week 21, versus -0.1% with placebo, with no evidence of plateau:

##### **3. Data comparison** 

- n **Vs prior VK2735 SC VENTURE** : the original P2 study showed **_14.7%_** absolute and **_13.1% pbo-adj_** WL at Week 13 on 15mg. Today’s data extend this to **_16%-19% pbo-adj_** at Week 21. Viking also cites **_~22%_** at Week 33, although the PR does not provide the underlying absolute or placebo-arm data for this figure. The new study is also smaller, with only 14-15 patients in most induction cohorts. 

- n **Vs other injectable GLP-1/GIP agents** : enicepatide achieved **_22.5%_** pboadj PP WL at Week 48, while Zepbound achieved **_~19%_** at a similar time point. VK2735’s **_18.6%_** pbo-adj WL at Week 21 therefore looks competitive for the shorter timeframe, but comes from a small P1 cohort rather than a larger P2/P3 study. 

Enicepatide/CT-388 Phase II T2D data 

##### **1. Data summary** 

- n **CT-388-104:** P2 trial in 447 adults with T2D and overweight/obesity, evaluating weekly SC enicepatide for 48 weeks. 

- n At 24mg, enicepatide delivered-2.65% **_HbA1c from an 8.1%_** baseline and- **_15.5% body weight_** , with no weight-loss plateau. In patients with baseline HbA1c >8.5%, HbA1c fell 4.13%. 

- n 90% reached HbA1c ≤6.5% and 62% achieved normoglycaemia <5.7%. 

- n Safety was class-consistent, with mainly mild-to-moderate GI AEs and only 2% discontinuing due to AEs vs 0% placebo. 

##### **2. What is new vs the last update?** 

- n This is the first enicepatide dataset in T2D. The prior ADA update was from the separate CT-388-103 obesity study in patients without diabetes, which showed 22.5% placebo-adjusted PP weight loss at Week 48. 

##### **3. Comparison using ADA note** 

- n **The -2.65% HbA1c reduction looks very competitive** vs -2.0% for retatrutide,-1.9% for CagriSema and -1.9% for elecoglipron, although baseline HbA1c and trial designs differ. 

- n The -15.5% weight loss also compares favourably with approximately - 15.3% for retatrutide in T2D and exceeds the weight loss generally reported with CagriSema and oral GLP-1s in diabetes.

Macroeconomic fluctuations often account for most of the risks associated with exposures to instruments that promise to pay fixed or variable interest rates. For an investor who is long fixed-rate instruments (thus receiving these cash flows), increases in interest rates naturally lift the discount factors applied to the expected cash flows and thus cause a loss. The longer the maturity of a certain cash flow and the higher the move in the discount factor, the higher will be the loss. Upside surprises in inflation, fiscal funding needs, and FX depreciation rates are among the most common adverse macroeconomic shocks to receivers. But counterparty exposure, issuer creditworthiness, client segmentation, regulation (including changes in assets holding limits for different types of investors), changes in tax policies, currency convertibility (which may constrain currency conversion, repatriation of profits and/or liquidation of positions), and settlement issues related to local clearing houses are also important risk factors. The sensitivity of fixed-income instruments to macroeconomic shocks may be mitigated by indexing the contracted cash flows to inflation, to FX depreciation, or to specified interest rates - these are common in emerging markets.  The index fixings may - by construction - lag or mis-measure the actual move in the underlying variables they are intended to track. The choice of the proper fixing (or metric) is particularly important in swaps markets, where floating coupon rates (i.e., coupons indexed to a typically short-dated interest rate reference index) are exchanged for fixed coupons. Funding in a currency that differs from the currency in which coupons are denominated carries FX risk.
