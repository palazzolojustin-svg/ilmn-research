##### US Weekly sales/volume trends 

Our weekly integrated sales/volume tracker for the US looks at selected oncology assets and biosimilar penetration for key hospital assets up to 24<sup>th</sup> July 2026. Data in the charts are sourced from IQVIA (SMART US Weekly database) and capture weekly prescription volume units and $ sales across key channels in the US, including (but not limited to) clinics, long-term care, non-federal hospitals, chain stores, food stores and federal facilities.
