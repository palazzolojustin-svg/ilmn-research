## 76. All Eyes on Order Ramp-Up of Illumina NovaSeq System: 1Q Preview 

The order outlook for Illumina's newly launched NovaSeq system will dominate 1Q earnings. Illumina announced the system in January at an investor conference and began shipping the 6000 version in March. The time lines for the reagent kits and pacing of orders through the remainder of the year are likely to be a key focus. Illumina is targeting the NovaSeq to replace its current workhorse system, the HiSeq. About 800 customers globally are using 1,900 HiSeq systems in the field. 

Grail closed out a $900 million round of financing in 1Q. Illumina's guidance provided for 8 cents of 1Q and full-year dilution from Grail. Post financing, Grail's results will no longer be fully consolidated. (04/19/17)
