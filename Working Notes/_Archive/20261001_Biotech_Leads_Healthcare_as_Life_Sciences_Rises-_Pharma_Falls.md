# **Healthcare Scorecard: Biotech Shines, Pharma Falls** 

Read Research Report: European Healthcare Autumn Outlook Laurent Douillet Simbarashe Gumbo S) Team: Strategy Team: Strategy BI Senior Equity Strategist BI Associate Analyst |&1 

Biotech Leads Healthcare as Life Sciences Rises, Pharma Falls 

(Bloomberg Intelligence) -- Biotech and life sciences are set to lead healthcare into its next phase, powered by rising earnings estimates, robust price momentum and market breadth. Healthcare equipment and providers screen cheaper after recent declines, while pharma lags as weak technicals and persistent policy headwinds weigh on its earnings outlook. (10/01/26) 

## 1. Biotech Tops Scorecard; Life Sciences Rises, Pharma Falls 

Biotechnology remains at the top of our healthcare scorecard, supported by strong price momentum (Argenx and Genmab), relatively healthy price breadth and positive earnings revisions. Life sciences climb from near bottom to second place, driven also by technical indicators and earnings upgrades, though a tough earnings comparison base and elevated valuation weigh on its overall score. Healthcare providers fall from the top two to the middle as earnings revisions turn negative and price action is broadly neutral. 

Pharma drops from neutral to the bottom, reflecting weak technicals (price momentum and breadth) while its forward earnings growth score falters due to a demanding trailing 12-month comparison base. Healthcare equipment and supplies remains weak on technicals and revisions, but with relatively stronger net earnings growth. (10/01/26)

Healthcare providers top the pillar despite relatively modest forward growth, helped by an easier comparison base. Healthcare equipment ranks second, with EPS expected to rise 11% after trailing growth of about 8.5%. Biotechnology sits mid-table despite having the highest next 12-month EPS growth at 39%, reflecting a tough comparison after a 40% rise over the past 12 months.

## 3. Revisions Favor Life Sciences, Biotech Estimates Jump 

Life Sciences tops our revisions pillar, with the strongest revision ratio at 20%, though 2027 EPS estimates are up just 0.6% over the past three months. Biotechnology ranks second, with the largest increase in 2027 estimates at 6%, driven by Argenx and Genmab. A weaker breadth holds back its score, with upgrades matching downgrades. Pharma is third, with 2027 EPS estimates up 0.3% (driven by Roche and Galderma) and a revision ratio of 18%. 

Healthcare Equipment and Supplies comes in fourth, with 2027 EPS estimates cut of 1.6% and a minus 40% revision ratio. Siemens Healthineers downgrades are weighing on the group as Chinese efforts to curb healthcare costs pressure its diagnostics outlook. Healthcare providers rank last, with 2027 EPS estimates cut 2% and every company's estimates downgraded. (10/01/26)

## 5. Biotech Leads Momentum, Life Sciences Tops Breadth 

Biotechnology and life sciences lead on technical indicators, with the former posting the strongest momentum -- a 32% six-month minus two weeks return -- but is second on breadth with 50% of members above their 50-day average and 75% above their 200-day average. Life sciences is the one leading on breadth with all members trading above their 50- and 200-day moving averages but is up "only" 11%. Healthcare providers rank third on both measures, with a 5% return and half of its members above both moving averages. 

Healthcare equipment has the weakest price momentum, with negative returns over both six months and two weeks, while just 30% of members trade above their 50- and 200-day moving averages. Pharma is also weak, with a six-month return of just 1% and only 36% of members above both moving averages. (10/01/26)
