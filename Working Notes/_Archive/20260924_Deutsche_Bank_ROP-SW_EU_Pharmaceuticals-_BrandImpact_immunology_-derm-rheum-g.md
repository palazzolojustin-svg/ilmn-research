## BrandImpact immunology (derm/ rheum/gastro): bim on the charge? 

##### Summary takes 

**Proprietary longitudinal physician survey panel data from IQVIA BrandImpact provides unique insight into prescribing by line of therapy and sub-indication: of most value in settings such as immunology where drugs compete** across multiple settings and lines of therapy, making the conventional prescription data in our weekly trackers of limited help to assess detailed trends. Here, for the first time in over a year _(EU Pharma: BrandImpact immunology: derm/rheum/gastro (NOVN/_ _<u>ROG/SAN/UCB)</u> (21/07/25))_ we publish updated trends, **which we view as marginally net positive for UCB (strength in HS/PsA offset by some weakness in PsO) and broadly neutral for the rest (NOVN/ROG/SAN/GALD)**

### A note on data 

IQVIA Brand Impact data is collated from a longitudinal sample of physicians (panel size is denoted at the bottom of each data chart), capturing their behaviour at point of care. We believe the data provides insight into rapidly evolving markets by indicating how a representative and consistent panel of physicians are behaving over time in that therapeutic space. However, we caution upfront that the data has caveats e.g. attached to the limited sample size and typically askew to specialist (vs. PCP) physicians. In addition, early launch or monthly shifts in trends may not be representative of broader or future sales evolution and should be interpreted accordingly.
