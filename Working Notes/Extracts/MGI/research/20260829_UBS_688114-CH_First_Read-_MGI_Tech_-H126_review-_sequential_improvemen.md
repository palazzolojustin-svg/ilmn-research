## H1 2026 results versus expectations

UBS's answer to "How did the results compare with expectations?":

- H1 2026 revenue was Rmb1.37bn, up 18.9% year on year.
- Net loss attributable to the parent company narrowed to Rmb158m, and the recurring net loss (excluding one-off items) to Rmb161m. A year earlier (H1 2025) these were Rmb168m and Rmb203m respectively.
- UBS views the results as "largely in line."
- Q2 2026 revenue rose 33% quarter on quarter, and net losses also narrowed sequentially.
- UBS's reasons for the narrowing loss: solid revenue growth across all business segments and regions; gross profit margin (GPM) expansion from a higher mix of reagent revenue; and cost-control efforts.

## Noteworthy areas in the results

### 1) "Seq-all" (sequencing) segment
- Revenue rose 17.6% year on year to Rmb1.1bn.
- By region: domestic (China) +8.5%; APAC +8.5%; EMEA (Europe, Middle East, Africa) flat; Americas +126%.
- By product: instrument revenue fell 6.4% and reagent revenue rose 16.3% year on year. UBS says this shows "an accelerated conversion of the installed base into recurring higher-margin reagent revenues" (the machines already placed with customers are generating more repeat consumable sales).
- Management said clinical demand (revenue up 36.6% year on year) drove overall sequencing growth, while revenue from research customers was broadly flat.

### 2) Generative Lab Intelligence (GLI) segment
- Revenue rose 4.1% to Rmb116m.
- Growth was mainly from customized projects in EMEA; the domestic market "remained under pressure."

### 3) Omics segment
- Revenue grew 44.9% to Rmb162m.
- Drivers per the note: formal commercialisation of spatial omics (measuring molecular activity while preserving where it sits in tissue); synergies between short-read and long-read sequencing and spatial omics; and rising demand for multi-omics datasets because of the rise of AI large language models (LLMs).

### 4) Swiss Rockets out-licensing deal
- MGI has not yet recognized the US$20m upfront payment from its out-licensing deal with Swiss Rockets as revenue.
- Management estimates the CoolMPS technology transfer to Swiss Rockets (for outside APAC and China) is roughly 50% complete. (CoolMPS is MGI's sequencing chemistry.)

## Outlook and guidance

- UBS says guidance is "largely unchanged."
- Management's view: as import substitution (domestic products replacing foreign ones) rises in China, industry competition has shifted "from product pricing to broader competitive capabilities."
- Management expects industry fundamentals to improve in 2027, potentially delivering teens-percentage growth, referencing industry reports and competitors' guidance.
- UBS's opinion: it sees MGI gaining share, backed by its capabilities in product and technology integration, ecosystem operations and global commercialisation.

## Risks (UBS)

**Downside risks:** (1) IP litigation and geopolitical tensions; (2) technical road map shifts; (3) macro pressure dragging research and clinical funding; (4) slower downstream adoption of genome sequencing; (5) tightening regulation in the life-science industry; (6) intensifying competition from new entrants.

**Upside risks:** (1) reduced geopolitical tensions; (2) faster technology upgrades and product iterations; (3) macro stimulus boosting research and clinical funding; (4) wider downstream adoption of genome sequencing; (5) loosening life-science regulation; (6) less competition from new entrants.
