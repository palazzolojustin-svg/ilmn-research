# UBS Fast Take on MGI Tech's Rmb365.7mn Acquisition of Nanopore-Sequencing and Spatial Omics Platforms from BGI Tech (Neutral, PT Rmb73.80)

*Source file: `Working Notes/Sources/MGI/research/20260206_UBS_688114-CH_UBS-_Fast_Take-_MGI_Tech_-MGI_to_acquire_nanopore_sequen.md` · Date: 2026-02-02 · UBS Securities Co. Limited (UBS Global Research), analysts Henry Liu, CFA and Chen Chen, PhD (contribution from Jun Deng) · sell-side research*

**Summary.** A short UBS note on MGI Tech's announced purchase of nanopore-sequencing and spatial omics platforms from BGI Tech for Rmb365.7mn. UBS expects limited near-term financial impact but sees the deal as strategically important, because it adds long-read nanopore sequencing to MGI's short-read business. UBS keeps a Neutral rating and a Rmb73.80 price target (DCF-based), with no change to the target shown, and projects MGI to turn profitable in 2026.

## The News

**Headline [UBS, 2026-02-02]:** MGI Tech will acquire nanopore-sequencing and spatial omics platforms from BGI Tech for Rmb365.7mn (365.7 million Chinese yuan). The note does not give further deal terms, such as timing, financing, approvals, or what the assets generate.

Terms in plain English:
- **Nanopore sequencing** reads DNA or RNA by passing a molecule through a tiny pore and detecting the changes it causes. It typically produces long reads (long continuous stretches of sequence). The note describes it as "long-read."
- **Short-read sequencing (NGS, next-generation sequencing)** reads DNA in small fragments. The note describes it as "short."
- **Spatial omics** measures molecules such as genes or proteins while preserving their location within a tissue. The note does not elaborate on it.
- **BGI Tech** is the seller. The note does not describe its relationship to MGI.

## UBS's Take

**Financial impact [UBS, 2026-02-02]:** UBS expects "limited direct financial impact near term from the acquisition."

**Strategic importance [UBS, 2026-02-02]:** UBS says the deal is "key for strengthening MGI's product (NGS+nanopore-sequencing) and tech barrier (short +long-read)." A tech barrier here means a technical advantage that is hard for rivals to copy.

**Competitive position [UBS, 2026-02-02]:** "MGI is the only supplier in China and one of two globally with such capability," meaning the ability to offer both short-read and long-read sequencing. The note does not name the other global supplier, and it does not mention Illumina, Oxford Nanopore, or any other competitor by name.

## Company Description (UBS)

UBS describes MGI Tech as "China and the world's leading genome sequencer and lab automation equipment producer." It focuses on R&D and the production and sale of life sciences and biotech instruments and reagents. Its main businesses are:
- **Genome sequencing:** the major revenue contributor and where UBS says its competitive edge shows.
- **Lab automation:** complements the sequencing business.
- **New business:** explores digitalisation opportunities in life sciences.

## Risks (UBS)

**Downside risks:**
1. IP (intellectual property) litigation and geopolitical tensions.
2. Technical road map shifts.
3. Macro pressure dragging research and clinical funding.
4. Slower genome sequencing downstream adoption.
5. Tightening regulations in the life science industry.
6. Intensifying competition from new entrants.

**Upside risks:**
1. Reduced geopolitical tensions.
2. Faster tech upgrades and product iterations.
3. Macro stimulus boosting research and clinical funding.
4. Wider genome sequencing downstream adoption.
5. Loosening regulations in the life science industry.
6. Less competition from new entrants.
