*Source file: `Working Notes/Sources/MGI/consensus/MGI_Consensus_Annual.md` · Date: 2026-10-10 · Bloomberg consensus estimates · consensus estimates*

**Summary.** Bloomberg consensus financial projections for MGI Tech (Chinese ticker 688114 CH), a genomics and laboratory automation company. Data covers 2019–2025 actuals and 2026–2028 estimates. Shows sharp revenue contraction (4.2 billion CNY in 2022 to 2.8 billion in 2025) driven by major asset impairments totaling 700+ million CNY across 2023–2025, with recent segment restructuring into Long Read Sequencing, Intelligent Automation, and Multi-Omics Products. Consensus forecasts recovery to profitability in 2027 as revenue rebounds and operating leverage improves.

## Overview

This is a Bloomberg consensus financial sheet for MGI Tech (Shanghai listing, ticker 688114 CH), reporting in Chinese Yuan (CNY). All figures are in millions of CNY unless otherwise stated. Data covers 2019–2025 as actuals and 2026–2028 as consensus estimates from multiple analysts.

The document contains consolidated income statements, balance sheets, cash flow statements, and a new business-segment breakdown effective 2025, alongside historical segment data (2019–2024) under an older classification structure.

## Summary and Implications

**Narrative:** MGI Tech experienced a dramatic reversal from a highly profitable 2022 (47.9% net margin, 5.26 CNY EPS) to cumulative losses of 1.4 billion CNY across 2023–2025. The collapse was driven by major asset impairments (637 million CNY total), likely from unsuccessful acquisitions or business combinations (the "Laboratory Automation" and "New Business" segments imploded), combined with sharp revenue contraction (4.2 billion → 2.8 billion CNY).

**Restructuring:** MGI reorganized its reporting segments starting 2025, consolidating into Long Read Sequencing (core business, 2.2 billion CNY), Intelligent Automation (lab automation, 254 million), and Multi-Omics Products (fastest growth, 276 million). The new structure suggests a pivot away from the failed automation/new business strategy toward its original genomics core, with expansion into multi-omics to compete with Illumina and other genomics-plus players.

**Recovery Path:** Consensus expects a gradual return to profitability starting 2027, with:
- Revenue recovering from 2.8 billion (2025) to 4.3 billion (2028E), nearing but not matching the 2022 peak
- Net income margins improving from –8% to +4.65% by 2028E
- EPS at only 0.46 CNY by 2028E, less than 10% of the 2022 peak, reflecting reduced share count or recovery starting from a deeper hole

**Constraints:** The slow return to profitability—with ROE at only 2.5% by 2028E and continued capex requirements—suggests consensus does not expect MGI to regain its prior market position or margin profile. This is consistent with either market share loss to competitors (Illumina, Oxford Nanopore, PacBio) or a permanent downgrade in the economics of its markets. Multi-Omics Products show the strongest growth (35% CAGR), but from a small base, and may not be sufficient to offset maturation or competition in the core sequencing business.
