# Leading banks back Oxford Nanopore after London Calling showcases maturing platform

*Source file: `Working Notes/Sources/ONT/news/20260526_Proactive_Investors_Ltd_ONT-LN_Leading_banks_back_Oxford_Nanopore_after_London_Calling.md` · Date: 2026-05-26 · Proactive Investors Ltd; author Ian Lyall · news*

**Summary.** Following Oxford Nanopore's annual London Calling conference, RBC Capital Markets and Citi reiterated positive ratings on the stock. The conference showcased new product launches including an upgraded PromethION Plus flow cell with ~20% higher throughput, RNA transcriptomics chemistry, vaccine quality control workflows, and a protein sequencing system. Management hires signal a focus on clinical applications. Analysts noted compelling valuation and growing traction in biopharma and clinical segments.

## Analyst Ratings and Valuations

RBC Capital Markets maintains an outperform rating with a 225p price target [RBC, 26 May 2026]. Citi rates Oxford Nanopore buy with a high-risk designation, finding the stock compelling at 2.3 times estimated 2027 enterprise value to sales (a valuation metric that divides the firm's total market value minus net cash by projected annual revenue) [Citi, 26 May 2026]. Both ratings were reiterated following the London Calling conference.

## Stock Price

Oxford Nanopore shares closed flat at 128.98p on the day of the article [26 May 2026].

## PromethION Plus Flow Cell

Oxford Nanopore launched a higher-throughput version of its PromethION sequencer's flow cell (the consumable component where DNA strands are read). The PromethION Plus delivers approximately 20% more data per flow cell without requiring user intervention, improving the cost-per-gigabase competitive position [London Calling conference, May 2026]. A new barcoding system accompanies the upgrade. The combination implies a sequencing cost of roughly $400 per human genome—above the cost of short-read sequencing (a cheaper, faster method that reads only small DNA fragments) but offset by a significantly richer output that captures structural variation (large DNA rearrangements) and methylation (chemical tags that affect gene expression) [26 May 2026].

## RNA and Protein Sequencing

Oxford Nanopore is developing RNA transcriptomics chemistry, allowing it to sequence RNA molecules that reflect which genes are active in a cell [London Calling, May 2026]. 

The company demonstrated its first amino acid caller for protein sequencing, a machine-learning system that directly identifies the 22 amino acids in a protein by analyzing nanopore readings. In testing, it successfully identified all 22 amino acids in each of 22 randomly selected *E. coli* proteins (a model organism) against a database of 4,000 reference proteins. This represents a "step change from previous classifier-based approaches," which used less direct methods [London Calling, May 2026].

## Vaccine Quality Control and Biopharmaceutical Applications

Oxford Nanopore is developing quality control workflows for the vaccine industry, including mRNA vaccines (vaccines based on genetic instructions for making a protein) and testing for adventitious virus agents (unintended viral contaminants in manufacturing). Partners already engaged with this technology include Lonza, Moderna, BioNTech, Pfizer and Regeneron [London Calling, May 2026].

A compliance-ready GridION Q-line system—designed to meet regulatory requirements for biopharmaceutical manufacturing—is scheduled to launch in June 2026 [26 May 2026]. Citi noted it came away "more confident in Oxford Nanopore's value proposition and traction in both biopharma and clinical segments" following the conference [Citi, 26 May 2026].

## Management Appointments

Oxford Nanopore appointed new officers in chief medical officer, chief information officer and chief people officer roles. Analysts interpreted these hires as signals that the new chief executive is strengthening operational capability and doubling down on clinical applications [26 May 2026]. RBC expects the new chief executive to provide detailed strategic commentary at the first-half 2026 results presentation, which RBC views as "an important event for the stock" [RBC, 26 May 2026].

## Product Portfolio Strategy

The London Calling conference featured a "streamlined product portfolio," suggesting Oxford Nanopore has simplified its product range [26 May 2026].
