# RBC raises Oxford Nanopore target price to 300p on AI-driven demand for biological data

*Source file: `Working Notes/Sources/ONT/news/20260930_Proactive_Investors_Ltd_ONT-LN_Oxford_Nanopore_target_price_raised_by_RBC_on_AI_opportu.md` · Date: 2026-09-30 · Proactive Investors Ltd; analyst Ian Lyall · news*

**Summary.** RBC Capital Markets raised its target price for Oxford Nanopore Technologies by 33% to 300p, retaining an 'outperform' rating, citing growing demand from AI drug discovery for differentiated genomic data. The note highlighted a near-term revenue test via the UK Biobank's 50,000-sample sequencing project and a larger $180 million opportunity from follow-on sequencing of the remaining 450,000 samples, while cutting 2026–2028 revenue forecasts by 5–14% on near-term headwinds.

## RBC Rating and Valuation

RBC Capital Markets raised its target price for Oxford Nanopore Technologies PLC (LSE:ONT) to 300p from 225p, a 33% increase [Proactive Investors, 2026-09-30]. The analyst retained an "outperform" rating on the stock. RBC based the 300p target on a 7× 2027 enterprise value-to-sales multiple and an additional 10% premium to account for potential positive announcements related to the company's AI opportunities [RBC, 2026-09-30].

## Stock Performance Context

Oxford Nanopore's shares rose 19% over the two weeks preceding the note, underperforming US-listed sequencing peers, which gained about 30% over the same period [RBC, 2026-09-30].

## Oxford Nanopore's Differentiated Technology

The company generates differentiated biological data by combining three capabilities in a single sequencing assay [RBC, 2026-09-30]:

- **Long reads** (DNA sequences read as longer continuous stretches, compared to the shorter reads produced by other sequencing methods)
- **Native methylation detection** (identifying chemical modifications to DNA bases without additional preparation steps)
- **Structural variant detection** (identifying large insertions, deletions and rearrangements in the genome)

## AI as a Driver of Demand for Richer Biological Data

RBC argues that AI models used in drug discovery increasingly require richer and more detailed biological inputs to train effectively [RBC, 2026-09-30]. The broker cited DeepMind's AlphaGenome model as evidence that long-range genomic context—the relationship between distant genetic elements—has significant value for predicting gene function [RBC, 2026-09-30].

## UK Biobank Project: Near-Term Revenue Opportunity

The UK Biobank's 50,000-sample sequencing project, being executed by deCODE Genetics (an Icelandic genetics company), is expected to provide a near-term validation of demand for this type of data [RBC, 2026-09-30]. Results are due by the end of 2026 [RBC, 2026-09-30].

RBC estimates the 50,000-sample project will generate approximately $20 million in revenue, assuming $400 per genome (the cost per complete set of genetic information sequenced) [RBC, 2026-09-30]. A planned follow-on project to sequence the remaining 450,000 samples in the biobank could represent approximately $180 million in additional revenue at the same assumed price [RBC, 2026-09-30].

## Broader AI-Driven Market Opportunities

Beyond the UK Biobank, RBC identifies several addressable markets [RBC, 2026-09-30]:

- Population biobanks (large collections of genetic samples from diverse populations)
- Pharmaceutical company databases (genetic information housed by drug developers)
- AI-native drug discovery companies (startups and established firms built around AI-driven drug design)

The AI drug discovery sector attracted more than $4 billion in investment during 2026 [RBC, 2026-09-30].

## Technology and Infrastructure

Oxford Nanopore's PromethION devices (its most advanced sequencing instrument) already integrate Nvidia GPUs for real-time basecalling (the computational step that converts raw sequencing signals into genetic sequences) [RBC, 2026-09-30].

## Investor Ownership and Strategic Alignment

The Ellison Institute of Technology owns approximately 17.5% of Oxford Nanopore and has stated strategic priorities in health and generative biology [RBC, 2026-09-30].

## Revised Revenue Forecasts

RBC cut its revenue forecasts for the next three years [RBC, 2026-09-30]:

| Year | RBC Forecast | Reduction |
|------|--------------|-----------|
| 2026 | £256 million | 5% cut    |
| 2027 | £299 million | 11% cut   |
| 2028 | £353 million | 14% cut   |

Despite these reductions, RBC stated that its forecasts remain broadly in line with consensus estimates [RBC, 2026-09-30].
