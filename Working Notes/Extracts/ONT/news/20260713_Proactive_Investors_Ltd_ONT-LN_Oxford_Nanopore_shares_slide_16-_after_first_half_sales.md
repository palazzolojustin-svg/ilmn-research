# Oxford Nanopore H1 2026 Results Miss; 16% Share Decline on China and Middle East Headwinds

*Source file: `Working Notes/Sources/ONT/news/20260713_Proactive_Investors_Ltd_ONT-LN_Oxford_Nanopore_shares_slide_16-_after_first_half_sales.md` · Date: 2026-07-13 · Proactive Investors Ltd, author Ian Lyall · news*

**Summary.** Oxford Nanopore reported first-half 2026 revenue of £116.5 million, growing 10% reported or 12% at constant currency, below management expectations. Shares fell 16% to 101.2 pence. Sharp geographic headwinds—China revenue down 16% due to export controls and operational changes, Middle East down 14% due to geopolitics—depressed overall results; stripping these two regions showed 16% constant-currency growth. Applied markets (clinical up 35%, biopharma up 25%) grew strongly at 22%, while research revenue rose only 5%. The company maintained full-year constant-currency guidance of 21–25% growth (pending one-off collaboration revenue in H2), with underlying growth of 16–20% excluding those deals, and reaffirmed 62% gross margin and 2027 adjusted earnings breakeven target.

## Financial Results: First Half 2026

### Revenue and Growth

Oxford Nanopore reported first-half revenue of around £116.5 million for the six months to 30 June 2026. This represented growth of about 10% on a reported basis, or 12% at constant currency—below management expectations and the primary driver of a 16% share price decline to 101.2 pence on 13 July 2026. [Proactive Investors, 13 Jul 2026]

### Geographic Performance and Headwinds

The shortfall was principally attributable to sharp revenue declines in two specific regions:

- **China**: Revenue fell around 16%, driven by tighter export control restrictions and changes to the company's commercial operations in the market. [Proactive Investors, 13 Jul 2026]
- **Middle East**: Sales dropped about 14%, reflecting the ongoing geopolitical situation. [Proactive Investors, 13 Jul 2026]

Stripping out these two troubled regions, group revenue grew around 16% at constant currency. The Americas also weighed on near-term performance due to the timing of customer orders and contract wins. [Proactive Investors, 13 Jul 2026]

### Product Segment Performance

Applied markets revenue—the company's higher-margin segment encompassing clinical diagnostics and pharmaceutical research—grew about 22% in the first half:
- Clinical sales: Up 35%
- Biopharma: Up 25%

Research revenue rose around 5%, held back by the roll-off of several large projects. [Proactive Investors, 13 Jul 2026]

## Financial Guidance and Outlook

### Full-Year 2026 Expectations

Oxford Nanopore maintained its full-year constant-currency revenue growth guidance within the previously stated range of 21% to 25%. However, the company explicitly noted that this range depends on additional collaboration and licensing revenue expected in the second half of the year, some of which is described as one-off in nature. Excluding those opportunities, underlying growth is expected to be 16% to 20%. [Proactive Investors, 13 Jul 2026]

The company expects a materially stronger second half, supported by momentum in applied markets and the ramp-up of secured business. [Proactive Investors, 13 Jul 2026]

### Profitability and Balance Sheet

Gross margin guidance remains around 62% for the full year. The company reaffirmed its target of reaching adjusted earnings breakeven during the 2027 financial year. [Proactive Investors, 13 Jul 2026]

Cash and liquid investments stood at around £234.5 million at the end of June 2026, down from £302.8 million at the end of 2025, reflecting cash usage of approximately £68.3 million in the first half. [Proactive Investors, 13 Jul 2026]

## Management Commentary

Chief executive Francis Van Parys stated that operational progress continued, with further gains in gross margin and disciplined cost control. [Proactive Investors, 13 Jul 2026]

## Upcoming Events

Interim results (full financial statements and details) are due to be published on 19 August 2026. [Proactive Investors, 13 Jul 2026]
