# Oxford Nanopore 2025 revenue guidance ahead of forecast; 8% stock gain

*Source file: `Working Notes/Sources/ONT/news/20260112_Proactive_Investors_Ltd_ONT-LN_Oxford_Nanopore_rises_on_strong_2025_revenue_growth.md` · Date: 2026-01-12 · Proactive Investors Limited · news*

**Summary.** Oxford Nanopore announced 2025 revenue of £223–224 million, up 22–24% year-on-year, slightly exceeding its prior guidance range of 20–23% growth at constant currency. Growth was broad-based across all customer segments (Clinical up 60%, BioPharma up 30%, Applied Industrial up 27%, Research up 15%) and geographies. The PromethION platform for large-scale sequencing users achieved standout growth of over 40%. The company ended 2025 with £302 million in cash and liquid investments. Full results will be published 2 March 2026.

## Financial Performance

**Annual Revenue and Growth**

Oxford Nanopore expects 2025 revenue of £223 million to £224 million, representing growth of 22% on a reported basis and 24% at constant currency (accounting for foreign exchange movements). This slightly exceeds the company's prior guidance of 20% to 23% growth at constant exchange rates.

For comparison, 2024 revenue was £183.2 million, providing the baseline for year-on-year calculations.

## Revenue by Customer Segment

**Clinical Market**  
Clinical revenues grew approximately 60% during 2025, representing the strongest growth segment. The company attributed this to "broad-based growth" across customer markets.

**BioPharma Market**  
BioPharma revenues grew 30% during the year, outpacing research-related sales but substantially below the clinical segment's growth rate.

**Applied Industrial**  
Applied Industrial sales rose 27% during 2025.

**Research Market**  
Research-related sales grew 15% despite what the company described as "ongoing funding pressures in the academic sector," suggesting headwinds in this traditionally important segment for sequencing companies.

## Product Performance

**PromethION Platform**  
The PromethION line, positioned for large-scale users, delivered "standout growth of over 40% year on year," representing the strongest product-level performance disclosed.

## Balance Sheet and Capital Position

At the end of 2025, Oxford Nanopore held approximately £302 million in cash and liquid investments. The company attributed this strong cash position partly to "better working capital management" and described it as supporting "continued balance sheet strength" for executing its strategy. The cash position was also noted to be ahead of market expectations.

## Stock Price

Oxford Nanopore's share price rose 8% to 152p on the announcement (Monday, 12 January 2026).

## Upcoming Events

Full preliminary results for the year ended 31 December 2025 will be published on 2 March 2026, with a webcast and Q&A session for investors scheduled for the same morning.
