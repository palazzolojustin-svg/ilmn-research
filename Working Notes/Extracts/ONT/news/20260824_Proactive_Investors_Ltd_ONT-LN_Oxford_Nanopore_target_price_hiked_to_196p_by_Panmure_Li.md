# Panmure Liberum upgrades Oxford Nanopore to 'buy', raises target to 196p on diagnostics deal and strategic pivot to biopharma

*Source file: `Working Notes/Sources/ONT/news/20260824_Proactive_Investors_Ltd_ONT-LN_Oxford_Nanopore_target_price_hiked_to_196p_by_Panmure_Li.md` · Date: 2026-08-24 · Proactive Investors, analyst: Panmure Liberum · news*

**Summary.** Panmure Liberum upgraded Oxford Nanopore to 'buy' and raised its price target to 196p from 170p, citing a strategic review by the CEO that refocuses the company on long-read sequencing in the high-value biopharma market. The upgrade was triggered by an undisclosed diagnostics cross-licensing deal worth $20 million upfront plus $15 million in committed purchases over two years. Half-year results showed a narrowed adjusted core loss of £22.1 million with scaling gross margins, and the company targets cash flow breakeven by 2028, though success depends on biopharma customers transitioning from validation to full commercial adoption.

## Analyst Action and Valuation

Panmure Liberum upgraded Oxford Nanopore to a 'buy' rating on 24 August 2026, citing a sweeping strategy review led by the company's CEO. The target price was raised to 196 pence from 170 pence, a 15% increase. At the time of the report, the stock traded at 165.90p, down 2.41% on the day [Panmure Liberum, 24 Aug 2026].

## Strategic Pivot

The analyst highlighted a strategic shift toward deploying the company's long-read sequencing technology in the high-value biopharma market, a departure from its previous diversified approach. This reorientation is seen as an effort to capture more lucrative applications for the technology.

## Diagnostics Deal

Central to the upgrade is a global cross-licensing agreement with an unnamed diagnostics firm. The deal includes a $20 million upfront payment and $15 million in committed product purchases over the next two years. Panmure Liberum noted that this cash injection is expected to cushion near-term operating expenses and offset headwinds from product rationalisation [Panmure Liberum, 24 Aug 2026].

## Financial Performance and Outlook

Oxford Nanopore's first-half 2026 results showed a comfortable earnings beat. The adjusted core loss narrowed significantly to £22.1 million, described as resilient, with gross margins noted as scaling—indicating improving profitability on each unit sold as the business scales [Panmure Liberum, 24 Aug 2026]. 

The company has set a long-term target for cash flow breakeven by the 2028 financial year, reflecting management's commitment to disciplined cash conservation.

## Key Risks and Dependencies

The analyst flagged a critical strategic dependency: the company's ambitious revenue targets rely heavily on rapid adoption by biopharma customers. Specifically, key customers must transition from the validation phase (testing the technology) to full commercial deployment to generate the revenue needed to fund the group's growth. This transition is not guaranteed and represents a material execution and market adoption risk [Panmure Liberum, 24 Aug 2026].
