**Summary.** Semi-annual consensus revenue, profitability and cash flow estimates for Oxford Nanopore Technologies (ONT LN), a UK-listed sequencing technology company, covering actual results for 2025–H1 2026 and consensus estimates through end-2029. The consensus shows revenue growth from GBP 105.6m (H1 2025) to GBP 236m (H2 2029), gross margin expansion from 58.2% to 67.2%, and a path to profitability: EBITDA moves from negative GBP 48.3m (H1 2025) to positive GBP 38m (H2 2029), and net income reaches GBP 1.46m by H2 2029. Operating expenses decline sharply as a percentage of revenue as the company scales. The business is presently unprofitable but the consensus expects breakeven in EBITDA by late 2027 and profitability by late 2029.

## Key Trends and Observations

**Path to profitability on track per consensus:** The consensus projects Oxford Nanopore will reach adjusted EBITDA breakeven by H2 2027 and modest profitability (GBP 1.5m net income, ~0.00 EPS) by H2 2029. This reflects two key drivers: (1) revenue growth of ~21% half-annual CAGR, and (2) severe operating expense leverage, with SG&A declining from 90% to 43% of revenue as the company scales.

**Gross margin expansion reflects manufacturing/pricing improvements:** The 9-percentage-point improvement in gross margins from H1 2025 to H2 2029 suggests the company is achieving unit economics gains—either through manufacturing efficiency, favorable product mix (higher-margin offerings), or pricing power. This is consistent with a company moving from early-stage to commercial scale.

**Cash burn and balance sheet tightening:** The company is burning cash aggressively in 2025–2026 (GBP 48m and GBP 43m per half), funded by liquidating financial assets (down from GBP 137m to GBP 12m).
