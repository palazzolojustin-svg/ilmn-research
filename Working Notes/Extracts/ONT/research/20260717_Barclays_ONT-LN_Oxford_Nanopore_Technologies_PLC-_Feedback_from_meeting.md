## Headline view

Barclays summarises the meeting this way:

- Confidence in H2 (the second half of 2026) comes from a "converted, ramping business," meaning customer contracts that have already been signed and are now ramping up. There is also "some headroom baked into AMR expectations." AMR here appears to mean the Americas region; the note uses "Americas" and "AMRs" for the same region.
- APAC ex-China (Asia-Pacific excluding China) is another swing factor in the updated guidance.
- Revenue is weighted slightly more to H2 than expected.
- Barclays does "not see fundamental performance issue."
- The adjusted EBITDA breakeven target for 2027 is "still achievable." Adjusted EBITDA is earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation, with one-off items removed.

## Three key takeaways

### 1) 2026 revenue expectations

- Management's confidence in H2 growth comes from business that has already been converted (signed) and is ramping.
- The new 2026 growth guidance is a range of 16-20%. The note does not say what period or currency basis this growth is measured on, nor what the prior guide was.
- Americas and APAC ex-China are expected to be the swing factors within the 16-20% guide. EMEAI (Europe, Middle East, Africa and India, as the note uses the term) and China expectations are "relatively fixed."
- New opportunities could push growth back into the 21-25% range. This range is presumably the earlier guidance, though the note does not say so. Management will discuss these opportunities in more detail once they are signed.
- Revenue at the midpoint of the new guide is slightly more H2-weighted than the previously communicated 45:55 split (45% of revenue in H1, 55% in H2), "but not drastically."

### 2) 2027 and beyond growth

- Management does not see a fundamental issue that changes its view on the business's continuing growth rate.
- Management can see growth reaccelerating to 21-25% in 2027.
- The China comparison will be easier in 2027, because the 2026 base is depressed.

### 3) Earnings

- Adjusted EBITDA breakeven is still targeted for 2027, despite the expansion of the C-Suite (senior executive team).
- Management is still comfortable with its guidance of a 62% gross margin for the year, and in both H1 and H2. Gross margin is revenue minus the direct cost of producing the goods sold, as a share of revenue.
- ONT will publish its full H1 2026 results on 19 August 2026.

## Barclays' detailed notes from the meeting

### China

- H1 performance was below expectations. Barclays says this largely reflects China, where the company is in the middle of restructuring its commercial organisation and where the market is weak. "Competitors have all shown a decline in the market."
- The commercial reorganisation is still under way and has weighed on performance. Management does not expect an immediate rebound from it.
- China was 10% of revenue in 2025 and is now 7% in H2 2026 (as written in the note).
- Management "always expected low-to-no growth for China."
- China is still declining in H2 2026.

### Americas

- Performance was below expectations, which Barclays characterises as "largely timing issue."
- The company has signed the contracts it needs to see H2 acceleration, and these are in the ramp phase.
- Within the 2026 guide there is some headroom in the Americas expectations. Management is "not assuming all the opportunities are going to happen."

### Applied Industrial

- Growth is slower in this segment, for two reasons given in the note:
  - Plasmid growth is slowing, due to multiplexing and higher utilisation per flow cell. Multiplexing means running several samples together on one flow cell, the consumable that holds the nanopores. This means customers need fewer flow cells for the same work.
  - There is more pricing intensity between service providers.

### EMEAI

- Management's expectation is "more of a fixed expectation," with less swing than other regions.

### UK Biobank

- UK Biobank has ramped and is "performing strongly," with a significant contribution expected in H2 2026.

### New opportunities

- Management will talk in more detail once deals are signed. It has "talked for some time about penetrating different markets."

### Beyond 2026 and EBITDA

- Management does not think there is a fundamental issue with performance or the market. It expects to see reacceleration of growth in FY27.
- It remains confident of achieving adjusted EBITDA breakeven despite the C-Suite build-out.
