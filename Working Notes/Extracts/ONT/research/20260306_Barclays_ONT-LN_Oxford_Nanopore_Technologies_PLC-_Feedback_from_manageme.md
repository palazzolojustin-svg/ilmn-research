**Summary.** Barclays attended Oxford Nanopore's management presentation after its FY25 results on 2 March 2026 and reports three takeaways: 2026 growth guidance (low end reflects a subdued US, risk from P2 Solo discontinuation and China; top end needs more customers converting to P2i), the path to adjusted EBITDA breakeven in 2027 via gross margin (62% in FY26, about 64% achievable in 2027), and early biopharma contract wins. # Barclays: Feedback from Oxford Nanopore (ONT LN) management presentation, 2 March 2026

## Rating, price target and headline

- **Rating:** Overweight (Barclays' relative rating: the stock is expected to beat the average total return of the analysts' coverage universe over 12 months). Industry view: Positive (fundamentals and valuations in the coverage universe are improving). The rating is reiterated.
- **Price target:** 170p (GBp 170). The closing price on 27 Feb 2026 was 133p, implying potential upside of +27.7%.
- **Report date:** completed and released 2 March 2026 (15:42–15:43 GMT), after Oxford Nanopore published FY25 (full-year 2025) results the same day. It follows Barclays' note "FY25 first take: Growth guide below expectations, adj EBITDA breakeven on track" (2 March).
- **Market reaction:** shares fell 12% on the day versus a 1% fall for the SXDP (European sector index), which Barclays attributes to the reduction in 2026 and 2027 growth expectations. Barclays views the maintained target of adjusted EBITDA breakeven by 2027E as a positive.
- **Barclays' stance:** "We reiterate our OW rating and continue to see an attractive opportunity in the shares over the medium term." [Barclays, 2 Mar 2026]
- **Headline view:** the US and P2 Solo discontinuation / P2i conversion are the key factors in landing at the top or bottom of guidance; APAC could provide upside; a 64% gross margin is achievable in 2027, with PromethION flow cell recycling yet to provide a net tailwind.

## Barclays' three key takeaways from the management presentation

### 1) 2026 growth guidance

- **Low end of the range** reflects a more subdued US, some risk from the P2 Solo discontinuation (P2 Solo is a smaller PromethION sequencer being withdrawn), and China.
- **Top end** largely mirrors the same factors, with more customers converting to P2i (the P2 model Oxford Nanopore is focusing on instead).
- **Regional view:** management sees growth strongest in the Americas. A PRECISE 2 headwind plus specific factors in China could mean slower growth in APAC (Asia-Pacific), but APAC could also provide upside to guidance.
- **Not included:** guidance does not account for recent Middle East developments.
- **2027:** growth could accelerate, but that is not needed to reach adjusted EBITDA breakeven.

### 2) Adjusted EBITDA breakeven

- Adjusted EBITDA (earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation, with one-off items stripped out) breakeven is targeted by 2027.
- A big part of the path is gross margin (revenue minus direct cost of goods, as a percentage of revenue). A 62% gross margin for FY26 brings the target forward by one year. Further improvement is expected in 2027, with around 64% achievable.
- PromethION flow cell recycling (reusing the consumable cells that hold the sequencing nanopores) has yet to provide a tailwind, because it was offset in 2025 by lower MinION recycling.

### 3) Biopharma

- One contract has been signed in mRNA, and management hopes to sign one more in 2026.
- A large biopharma company currently uses Oxford Nanopore products for sterility testing.
- There is a pipeline of companies evaluating the products.

## Detailed notes from the presentation

### Restructuring and costs
- **Two restructuring events:** a January restructuring to allocate capital to higher-growth areas, and a November restructuring to align the business with new growth areas. The reduction in average R&D headcount is still to come through in the numbers.
- **Cost base:** IT, logistics and internal working practices can be improved, excluding headcount-related expense.

### Adjusted EBITDA
- The adjusted EBITDA loss improved 26% in FY25 versus FY24, and 32% in H2 2025 (second half).
- Barclays calls it a "solid path" to breakeven.
- Currency has been a £9m headwind since 2024.
- Management points to a loss of roughly £45–55m for 2026.

### Gross margin
- 62% for FY26 (target brought forward by one year), continued improvement in 2027, around 64% achievable.
- PromethION flow cell recycling is yet to provide a net tailwind, as it was offset by the reduction in MinION recycling in 2025.
- Phasing: margins have a few moving parts, but the aim is a consistent gross margin of around 62% in both H1 and H2.

### Balance sheet and cash items
- **Assets at customers** (instruments placed with customers that remain on Oxford Nanopore's books) were reduced owing to a change in pricing model. The H2 increase was mainly the UK Biobank plus a small number of devices to other customers.
- **Capex** (capital spending): it is unlikely that the low level seen in 2025 will be repeated in 2026.
- **Inventory:** a big focus "from the board all the way down." The £80m reported includes provisions, so the gross number is higher and can be reduced further. Yield improvement and increased recycling should benefit inventory.

### Products
- Discontinuation of P2 Solo, with focus on P2i.
- Development of Elysion (Oxford Nanopore's sample-preparation and sequencing automation platform) is paused.
- GridION Q 2.0 is to be launched in 2026.
- 2025 innovation was described as more focused, more commercially aligned and more scalable.

### 2026 guidance details
- Low end: subdued US, some risk from P2 Solo discontinuation, and China. Top end largely mirrors this, with more customers converting to P2i.
- Growth strongest in the Americas, which "has momentum," with a good pipeline in Clinical and Biopharma.
- PRECISE 2 headwind plus China-specific factors could slow APAC, which could also be the upside to guidance.
- Applied end markets are driven by Clinical and Biopharma.
- Middle East changes are not taken into account.
- **Phasing:** revenues are broadly 45:55 weighted between H1 and H2, as usual.

### 2027
- Could be higher than 2026, but only needs to be level with 2026 to achieve adjusted EBITDA breakeven.
- "Nothing in particular" limits the 2027 guide.

### Biopharma
- One mRNA contract signed, hoping for one more in 2026; a large biopharma company uses the products for sterility; a pipeline of companies is evaluating them.

### Clinical definition
- Management defines "clinical" as anyone using the technology to provide a diagnosis to patients.
- Management looks predominantly at how the customer gets paid, because it lacks complete visibility on what customers do.
- The bulk of clinical revenue comes from lab-developed tests (LDTs) and similar, not from Oxford Nanopore providing a diagnostics product.

## Valuation and risks

- **Method:** the price target rests on about 5.7x EV/Sales 2026E (enterprise value divided by expected 2026 sales), roughly a 20% premium to peers.
- **Why the premium:** its growth profile, a now-clearer commercial strategy, improving execution, and potentially significant upside to numbers from opportunities in applied markets.
- **Cross-check:** a DCF (discounted cash flow; WACC 7.5%, terminal growth rate 3%) gives a similar valuation.
- **Risks:** poor execution on expansion, difficulty penetrating clinical markets, and increased competition.
