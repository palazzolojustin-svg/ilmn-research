**Summary.** JPM's first read of Oxford Nanopore's 2H 2025 results and 2026 guidance. 2H 2025 adjusted EBITDA loss beat consensus by 9% and revenue was about 1% ahead. # Oxford Nanopore (ONT): 2H 2025 first take and update

**Publisher and author context.** This is a J.P. Morgan Cazenove research note dated 2 March 2026, covering the London-listed stock ONT.L. It is by Zain Ebrahim (the lead analyst) and Richard Vosser. The share price quoted is 133p as of 27 Feb 2026. The note was written on the morning ONT published its detailed results, ahead of the company's conference call at 10.45am GMT (5.45am ET). The note contains no analyst Q&A or call transcript; it is the analyst's own commentary. [JPM, 2026-03-02]

## Bottom line

- **Rating and price target:** JPM reiterates **Overweight** (meaning it expects the stock to outperform the average total return of the analyst's coverage universe over the price-target period). The price target is **190p, dated June 2027**. It is based on **5.5x 2026 EV/Sales** (enterprise value divided by sales, a way of valuing a company that is not yet profitable), equivalent to about 5x 2027 EV/Sales, and is "underpinned by our DCF" (discounted cash flow valuation). [JPM, 2026-03-02]
- **Results:** 2H 2025 revenue was about 1% ahead of "Company Consensus" (the analyst-estimate average compiled by the company, dated 27 Feb 2026). The adjusted EBITDA loss was 9% better than consensus. (Adjusted EBITDA is earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation, excluding one-off items.)
- **Guidance:** The 2026 revenue guide implies about 5% less revenue than consensus. The 2027 growth outlook implies cuts of up to about 11% to 2027 consensus revenue. ONT reiterated that it will reach adjusted EBITDA breakeven in 2027, which JPM says implies upside to consensus EBITDA, because consensus models a £14m loss in 2027.
- **JPM's overall view:** The cuts to consensus revenue are "at least somewhat anticipated", given that ONT shares underperformed by about 24 percentage points in the prior month (ONT down 22%, while the SXDP, the Stoxx Europe 600 Health Care index, was up 2%). JPM sees the reiteration of 2027 adjusted EBITDA breakeven as "reassuring". [JPM, 2026-03-02]

## 2H 2025 results versus consensus

JPM's text and its table (the table was garbled in extraction) cover the following points. All figures are for the second half of 2025 (2H'25) unless stated otherwise.

**Revenue.** LSRT (life science research tools) revenue was £118.3m, about 1% above company consensus. It was broadly in line with the £117.9m JPM had inferred from ONT's earlier trading update. That update said full-year 2025 revenue would be £223-224m, or 24% growth in constant currency (LC, meaning growth with exchange-rate effects removed). Full-year 2025 revenue was £223.9m, versus a consensus of £223.2m. Second-half growth was 21% LC, driven by:

- Clinical revenue up 80% LC.
- Biopharma revenue up 52% LC.
- Applied Industrial revenue up 8% LC.

**Gross profit.**

- Reported gross profit was £70.0m, about 1% below consensus. The reported gross margin was 59.1%.
- The shortfall was largely due to a £1.8m restructuring cost, a write-down of inventory for the ElysION platform. ONT decided to stop selling ElysION and focus on the P2i product.
- Adjusted gross profit was £71.7m, 2% above consensus, with an adjusted gross margin of 60.6%. JPM states this was about 66-70bps (basis points, hundredths of a percentage point) ahead of consensus. The note gives both figures in different places.

**Adjusted EBITDA.** The loss was £38.4m, 9% better than consensus and 8% better than JPM's own estimate (JPMe). JPM calls this "a continued beat on Adj. EBITDA".

**Operating expenses.**

- Adjusted operating expenses (OpEx) grew 1% year on year to £137m, about 1% above JPMe.
- Adjusted R&D was £45m and fell 9% year on year. It was 25% (£15m) below JPMe. The note contains an inconsistency: the summary says R&D was 25% "higher" than JPMe, while the detailed section says "below". The detail section, with the decline of 9% year on year, supports "below".
- Adjusted SG&A (selling, general and administrative costs) grew 6% year on year and was 22% above JPMe.

**Cash.** ONT ended the period with cash and other liquid investments of £302.8m, versus a consensus of £294.5m.

## 2026 guidance

All of the following are company guidance as interpreted by JPM. [JPM, 2026-03-02]

**Revenue.**

- Guidance is for 21-25% LC revenue growth.
- JPM uses a midpoint of 23% (the note also writes 23.5% in one place) and a 2% FX (foreign exchange) headwind. This implies 2026 revenue of about £270m (£269.9m). That is about 5% below company consensus. About 2 percentage points of the gap is because consensus did not reflect the FX headwind.
- Consensus expected 27.5% LC growth in 2026, though off a slightly lower 2025 base.

**Gross margin.**

- Guidance is for a 62% gross margin, 110bps above consensus of 60.9%.
- This implies gross profit of £167.3m, about 3% below consensus.
- JPM says the improvement is driven by continued operational improvements, namely flow cell recycling (reusing the consumable chip at the heart of each sequencing run), and by tailwinds from the changes to the pricing model.

**Operating expenses.** Adjusted OpEx is guided to grow 0-5% in 2026.

**Regions.**

- The Americas should be strongest. This reflects continued progress in non-Research end markets, with the NIH (US National Institutes of Health) market stabilising, though it is still "muted".
- EMEAI (Europe, Middle East, Africa and India) should grow strongly but below 2025, given the conclusion of a number of strategic projects in Research and the timing of new projects beginning.
- APAC (Asia-Pacific) demand remains strong. However, factoring in the impact from PRECISE II (a project), the timing of new projects starting and "ongoing specific market challenges", growth could moderate slightly from 2025 levels.

**End markets.** Growth will be weighted towards the Applied end markets (Clinical, Biopharma and Industrial).

## Medium-term guidance (2027 and 2028)

**Adjusted EBITDA breakeven in 2027, and cash breakeven in 2028.**

- ONT reiterated its targets for adjusted EBITDA breakeven in FY'27 and for positive cash flow in FY'28. It said these are reinforced by the restructuring events of FY'25.
- Consensus has a £14m adjusted EBITDA loss for 2027, so JPM sees upside to consensus.
- JPM says the 9% EBITDA beat in 2H 2025, together with the revenue and margin guidance, should "reassure on the achievability" of 2027 breakeven.

**2027 revenue.**

- ONT expects above-market constant-currency revenue growth in FY'27 "at a rate broadly similar to FY'26". JPM infers this means 21-25% LC growth (JPMe).
- This replaces the previous target of a 2024-27 revenue CAGR (compound annual growth rate) of more than 30% LC.

**2027 gross margin.** ONT expects continued improvement from FY'26 levels. This replaces the previous target of a 2027 gross margin above 62%. JPM models 64% (200bps of expansion), against consensus of 62.2%.

| Scenario | 2027 revenue | vs. consensus (£365.2m) | Of which FX | 2027 gross profit | vs. consensus |

## Investment thesis (JPM)

- **2026 drivers.** JPM expects ONT's 2026 performance to depend on executing its expansion into Biopharma and Clinical/Applied use of nanopore sequencing, which should improve confidence in a more than 20% revenue CAGR from 2025 to 2030.
- **Revenue growth forecasts.**
  - A 22% revenue CAGR for 2025-30, driven by Biopharma and Clinical (37% CAGR) and by Research (11% CAGR).
  - For 2026, 23% LC revenue growth, supported by 42% LC growth in Biopharma/Clinical and Applied, and 14% LC growth in Research.
- **Profitability.** A £47m adjusted EBITDA loss in 2026, driven by topline growth plus more than 250bps of gross margin expansion, which should build confidence in 2027 breakeven.
- **Valuation versus peers.**
  - On JPM's forecasts ONT trades on 3.5x 2026 EV/Sales, a discount to Illumina (about 4x) and PacBio (about 5x), despite a better growth profile.
  - JPM forecasts a 2025-28 sales CAGR of 23% for ONT, versus 5% for Illumina and 12% for PacBio. (Illumina is covered by US Life Sciences and Tools analyst Casey Woodring.)
  - JPM sees a 5.5x 2026 EV/Sales target multiple as "undemanding for a strong mid-term growth story".

## Risks to rating and price target (JPM)

1. Competition from established short-read optical sequencing companies, particularly Illumina, given its very large installed base and its competitive pricing and accuracy.
2. Future competition from other medium-to-long-read sequencing companies, such as PacBio (competing optical approach) or Roche with its SBX nanopore product. JPM notes this is "at present just short read and doesn't capture methylation".
3. Eventual competition from other long-read electrical sequencing companies when ONT patents expire.
4. Failure to execute on margin improvement or reaching profitability, from pricing pressure (slower gross margin improvement) or higher OpEx as ONT continues to invest.
