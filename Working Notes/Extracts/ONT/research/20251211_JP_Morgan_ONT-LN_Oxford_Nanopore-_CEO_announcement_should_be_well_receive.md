# Oxford Nanopore (ONT): J.P. Morgan note on CEO appointment, 8 December 2025

## Document basics

This is a short J.P. Morgan Cazenove Europe Equity Research note dated 8 December 2025 (completed and disseminated 07:45 GMT that day). The publisher is J.P. Morgan Securities plc. The lead analyst is Zain Ebrahim, with Richard Vosser and Sophia Graeff Buhl Nielsen also named. The note covers Oxford Nanopore Technologies (ONT, London-listed as ONT.L), a company that makes DNA/RNA sequencing devices based on "nanopore" technology, which reads genetic material by passing it through tiny pores and measuring electrical signals.

**Rating and price data**
- Rating: **Overweight** (J.P. Morgan's "buy" equivalent: it expects the stock to beat the average total return of the analyst team's coverage universe over the price-target period).
- Share price: 130p (as of 4 December 2025 close, per the cover page).
- Price target: **190p, set for June 2027** [JPM, 2025-12-08].
- Zain Ebrahim's coverage universe in the disclosures includes Bachem, EUROAPI, GSK, Genmab, Hikma, Lonza, Oxford Biomedica and Oxford Nanopore.
- Illumina, PacBio and Danaher are covered by a different J.P. Morgan analyst, US Life Sciences & Tools analyst Casey Woodring, which the note flags explicitly.

## The news: new CEO

[JPM, 2025-12-08] reports that ONT announced "this morning" the appointment of **Francis van Parys as CEO, effective 2 March 2026**.

**Francis van Parys's background (as described by J.P. Morgan)**
- For the last 2.5 years he has been CEO of **Radiometer**, a Danish subsidiary of Danaher. Radiometer is a business with more than $1bn of revenue. In that role he was responsible for building Radiometer's positioning in **point-of-care diagnostics** (testing done at or near the patient) in acute care settings (urgent, hospital-type care).
- Radiometer develops, manufactures and markets solutions for blood sampling, blood gas analysis and immunoassay testing (immunoassays are tests that detect specific molecules, such as proteins, in a sample).
- Before that he was President of Biotechnology Integrated Solutions at **Cytiva** for one year. Before that he was VP Commercial for Asia-Pacific at Cytiva for 2.5 years.
- Earlier he held various regional leadership roles at **GE Healthcare** for 11 years.

**Outgoing CEO**
- Gordon Sanghera will step down from the ONT Board at close of business on 2 March 2026. He will remain in the business in an advisory capacity through to early 2027 to ensure a smooth handover.

## J.P. Morgan's reaction and view

- The analysts write: "Overall, we are encouraged by the appointment of Francis van Parys as CEO, given his strong and extensive track record in Life Sciences leadership roles and the overlap between his most recent role, focusing on Point of Care Diagnostics, and ONT's expansion into the Biopharma, Clinical and Applied Setting."
- Their stated expectation: "We therefore expect ONT shares to outperform by >5% today." (This is an opinion and a forecast of the share-price reaction, not a reported outcome.)
- The headline frames the appointment as one that "should be well received given track record in Life Sciences leadership and commercially focused roles."

## Investment thesis (J.P. Morgan opinion and forecasts)

**What should drive 2026.** The analysts expect ONT's performance in 2026 to be driven by operational execution on expanding into **Biopharma** (drug-company customers) and **Clinical/Applied** uses (patient testing and non-research uses such as applied markets) of nanopore sequencing. They say this should raise confidence in ONT's ability to deliver strong double-digit revenue growth beyond its 2024-27 mid-term growth outlook (management's own medium-term target, whose figures are not given in this note).

**Revenue forecasts**
- 2025-30 revenue CAGR (compound annual growth rate) of **27%**, driven by expansion into Biopharma and Clinical and by growing use in Research, specifically increasing use of **methylation** (chemical marks on DNA that affect gene activity, which nanopore sequencing can read directly).
- 2025-28 sales CAGR of **31%** for ONT.
- 2026 revenue growth of **more than 30%**. The reasoning: academic and government funding headwinds (reduced research budgets from those customers) are expected to abate and "should now be reflected in the base"; Biopharma/Clinical and Applied, plus strong Research demand outside the US, are expected to underpin an acceleration in revenue growth.

**Profitability forecasts**
- 2026 Adj. EBITDA (adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization, a measure of operating profit) of **-£56m**, i.e. a loss, but a **£34m year-on-year improvement**.
- Drivers: top-line growth plus **200 basis points (2 percentage points) of gross margin expansion**.
- J.P. Morgan says this should increase confidence in ONT's ability to reach its **Adj. EBITDA break-even target by 2027**.

## Valuation

- On J.P. Morgan's forecasts, ONT trades on **3.5x 2026 EV/Sales** (enterprise value divided by sales, a way of comparing company value to revenue).
- That is a discount to **Illumina (about 5x 2026 EV/Sales)** and **PacBio (about 6x 2026 EV/Sales)**, even though J.P. Morgan sees ONT as having a superior growth profile.

The comparison below shows the 2025-28 sales growth rates J.P. Morgan forecasts against the valuation multiples. ONT is expected to grow fastest yet trades on the lowest multiple.

| Company | 2025-28 Sales CAGR (JPM forecast) | 2026 EV/Sales |
|---|---|---|
| Oxford Nanopore | 31% | 3.5x |
| Illumina | 5% | c. 5x |
| PacBio | 13% | c. 6x |

- Methodology and target: the analysts "move to a Jun-27 PT of 190p, 5.5x 2026 EV/Sales, which we see as undemanding for a strong mid-term growth story."
- Price target history from the disclosure table (all ratings Overweight): 3 Jan 2023: price 246p, PT 460p; 23 Mar 2023: 198p, PT 390p; 5 Dec 2023: 192p, PT 325p; 6 Mar 2024: 129p, PT 210p; 3 Sep 2024: 122p, PT 200p; 4 Mar 2025: 105p, PT 190p. Coverage was initiated 10 Nov 2021. The earlier 190p target (March 2025) is the same level as the new one; the change is described only as a move to a June 2027 target date based on 5.5x 2026 EV/Sales.

## Risks to the Overweight rating and price target

1. **Competition from established short-read optical sequencing** companies, in particular Illumina, which has a very large installed base (the number of instruments already placed with customers), competitive pricing and accuracy.
2. **Future competition from other medium-to-longer read companies**, such as PacBio with its competing optical sequencing approach, or Roche with its **SBX** nanopore product. The note adds that at present Roche's product "is just a short read and doesn't capture methylation."
3. **Eventual competition from other long-read electrical sequencing companies** as and when ONT's patents expire.
4. **Failure to execute on margin improvement or reach profitability**, either because of pricing pressure (and hence slower gross margin improvement) or because of higher operating expenses as the company continues to invest.
