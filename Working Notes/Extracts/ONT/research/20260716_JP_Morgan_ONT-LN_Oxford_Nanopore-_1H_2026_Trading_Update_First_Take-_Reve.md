## Headline takeaways (J.P. Morgan's "Our Take")

Oxford Nanopore (ONT) makes DNA/RNA sequencing instruments and consumables based on nanopore technology. It reported first-half 2026 revenue of **£116.5m**, with growth of **12% at constant currency** ("LC," meaning growth with foreign-exchange effects removed). Key comparisons:

- It was **7% (about £9m) below BBG consensus**.
- It was **2% (about £2m) below JPMe**.
- A headwind of about **4 percentage points** came from disruption in China and MENA (Middle East and North Africa). J.P. Morgan said this was "broadly in-line with our expectations."
- The shortfall against J.P. Morgan's estimate was attributed to the timing of customer orders and contract wins in the Americas, where growth of 12% LC was below management's expectations.

J.P. Morgan's overall view: although first-half revenue was 7% below consensus, "this should have been at least in part anticipated," given revenue was only 2% below its own estimate. It found the reiteration of 2026 guidance, now including new revenue opportunities that offset the China, Middle East and US headwinds, "reassuring."

## Noteworthy Area 1: Revenue miss and its causes

- **China:** revenues fell about 16% year on year. J.P. Morgan cited enhanced export control restrictions and changes to commercial operations in the region.
- **Middle East:** revenues fell 14%, because of the ongoing geopolitical situation.
- **Americas:** timing of customer orders and contract wins held back growth, which was below management expectations.
- Together, China and MENA disruption cost about 4pp of group growth.

## Noteworthy Area 3: Revenue by end market

The note says Applied revenue grew 22% year on year, with Research growth of 5%. Applied markets are the non-academic uses: Clinical, Biopharma and Applied Industrial. J.P. Morgan's figures are inferred where stated.

- **Applied total:** +22% LC. J.P. Morgan infers revenue of about **£41m**, roughly **12% below JPMe**, "likely due to the timing of customer orders and contract wins."
- **Clinical:** +35%, "continued strong growth." Implied revenue is **£17.6m**, about 8% below JPMe.
- **Biopharma:** +25% to **£9.5m**, about 20% (£2m) below JPMe.
- **Applied Industrial:** +5% to **£13.5m**, about 10% (£2m) below JPMe.
- **Research:** +5%.

## 2026 outlook and guidance

- Management **reiterated** 2026 revenue growth guidance of **21-25% LC**.
- The guidance now reflects extra **collaboration and licensing revenues** (J.P. Morgan estimates about £11m), some of which is non-recurring. These additional revenues are expected to offset three things:
  - the China and Middle East weakness;
  - first-half customer order timing in the Americas, which is not expected to be fully recovered by year-end.
- **Excluding** the additional collaboration and licensing revenue, 2026 growth is expected to be **16-20% LC**.
- **Gross margin** guidance of **62%** for FY2026 was reiterated.

## Likely changes to consensus

J.P. Morgan applied the guidance midpoint plus a 2% foreign-exchange headwind and arrived at:
- 2026 revenue of **£269.9m**, about 1% below BBG consensus.
- 2026 gross profit of **£167.3m**, about 1% below BBG consensus.

## 2027 profitability target

- Management reiterated its expectation of **Adjusted EBITDA breakeven in 2027** (adjusted earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation, a measure of operating profit before certain costs), noting "strong progress made in 1H 2026."
- J.P. Morgan infers this implies Adj. EBITDA at least in line with BBG consensus of **-£32m**, a sequential improvement on **-£38m** delivered in "2H 2026." (The note's wording says 2H 2026, although that half-year had not yet occurred as of the report date. This is likely a typo in the source for 2H 2025, and the note does not clarify.)

## Cash

ONT ended the period with **£234.5m** in cash, down from **£302.8m** at FY2025 year end (a fall of about £68m).

## Upcoming events

- Full 1H 2026 results, including the profit and loss statement, will be published on **19 August 2026**.
- A virtual presentation follows at 9:30am BST / 10:30am CEST / 4:30am EDT. **New CEO Francis van Parys** will review the first-half results and provide a **strategic update**.

## Risks and watch-points implied by the note

- Continued China export-control and commercial-operations disruption.
- Middle East geopolitical instability.
- US Research funding pressure.
- Lumpy contract and order timing in the Americas that may not be fully recovered in 2026.
- Reliance on non-recurring collaboration and licensing revenue to hold the guidance range (underlying growth ex-these items is 16-20%).
- Completion of large projects (two in EMEAI, PRECISE II in Singapore) creating tougher comparisons.
