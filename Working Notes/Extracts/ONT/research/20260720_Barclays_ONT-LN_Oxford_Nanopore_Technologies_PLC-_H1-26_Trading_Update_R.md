# Barclays H1'26 Trading Update Review on Oxford Nanopore (Overweight, PT cut to 160p) with CFO meeting notes

*Source file: `Working Notes/Sources/ONT/research/20260720_Barclays_ONT-LN_Oxford_Nanopore_Technologies_PLC-_H1-26_Trading_Update_R.md` · Date: 2026-07-14 · Barclays Equity Research (European Medical Technology & Services); analysts Jonathon Unwin, Hassan Al-Wakeel (CFA), Marco Pires-Cox · sell-side research*

**Summary.** Barclays reviews Oxford Nanopore's 13 July 2026 H1 trading update, in which management cut 2026 underlying revenue growth guidance to 16-20% because of weakness in China, the Middle East and the Americas. Barclays keeps its Overweight rating but lowers its price target to 160p from 172p, trims revenue forecasts 2-8% for 2026-2028, and expects underlying growth to re-accelerate in 2027. Notes from a post-update CFO meeting say management still targets adjusted EBITDA breakeven in 2027 and a 62% gross margin, while Barclays thinks breakeven comes in 2028.

# Oxford Nanopore (ONT LN): Barclays H1'26 Trading Update Review

Publication: 14 July 2026 (completed 13 July 2026, 20:58 GMT; released 14 July, 03:00 GMT). Firm: Barclays Equity Research, European Medical Technology & Services. Analysts: Jonathon Unwin, Hassan Al-Wakeel (CFA) and Marco Pires-Cox. Rohit Rajan is also listed on the final page.

## Rating, price target and headline summary

**Rating and target.** Barclays reiterates its **Overweight** rating (the stock is expected to beat the average expected return of the analysts' covered universe over 12 months). The sector view is **Positive** (unchanged). The **price target falls 7%, to 160 GBp from 172 GBp**. The price on 13 July 2026 was 116 GBp, so the target implies **+37.9% upside**. **What happened.** Oxford Nanopore (ONT, a UK-listed maker of DNA/RNA sequencing devices) published its H1'26 trading update on 13 July 2026. Barclays titled its own note on that update "H126 Trading update: Sales miss, 2026 guidance adjusted". The update cut the guide for 2026 underlying growth (growth excluding the new collaboration and licensing deals) to **16-20%**. Barclays attributes the cut to weakness in China, the Middle East and the Americas. [Barclays, 2026-07-14]

Barclays says the cut "weighed on shares", but that "additional opportunities provide support to numbers". Full H1'26 results are due on **19 August 2026**.

**Additional opportunities.** Management highlighted potential extra collaboration and licensing deals that could lift growth back to the previous guidance range of **21-25%**. Some of this revenue would be non-recurring. Barclays notes there is "limited disclosure around the phasing of these contracts and how much is recurring."

## Barclays' thesis

- **Re-acceleration in 2027.** Barclays expects underlying growth to accelerate in 2027, driven by the Clinical and Biopharma end-markets. It also cites self-help (management's own fixes) and easier comparisons in China.
- **Biopharma upside.** Barclays sees potential upside to its medium-term Biopharma estimates, which sit below management's expectations. Management has said Biopharma could be **20% of Group sales by 2027**. Barclays' risk-adjusted forecast has it at just **14% of sales by 2028** (see Barclays' 25 March 2026 note, "Guidance cut a clearing event?").
- **Valuation gap.** Despite "a slew of guidance cuts", Barclays believes the valuation "does not accurately reflect the growth opportunity relative to peers."
- **Why Overweight (Barclays' stated rationale).** It views market fundamentals as robust. It believes ONT's outlook in Biopharma and Clinical can support an acceleration of growth over the medium term. It says "Execution against updated targets should drive a re-rating in the shares."

## Barclays' modelling assumptions

- Barclays assumes the new collaboration and licensing opportunities add about **£11m of extra revenue in 2026**, equal to about 5 percentage points of extra growth. It assumes **50% of that is recurring in 2027**.
- On that basis it forecasts **21.5% constant-currency growth for 2026** (growth with exchange-rate effects removed), of which **16.5% is underlying**. It sees growth accelerating across all regions in H2'26.
- For 2027 it expects a headwind from the non-recurring part of these agreements. Underlying growth nonetheless accelerates **from 16.5% to 21.1%**, helped by self-help, easier China comparisons, and conversion of Clinical and Biopharma revenue in EMEAI (Europe, Middle East, Africa and India) and AMR (the Americas).
- It forecasts a **21% constant-currency revenue CAGR (compound annual growth rate) for 2025-2028E**. The summary table gives a revenue CAGR of 21.6%.
- Revenue forecasts are lowered by **2-8% for 2026E-2028E**.
- Adjusted EBITDA (earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation, adjusted for one-offs) is forecast at **-£55m for 2026** (-£28.2m in H1, -£25.4m in H2). Barclays still sees EBITDA breakeven as more likely in **2028**, not 2027 as the company guides.

## Valuation method

- The price target is based on a **c.5.2x EV/Sales (enterprise value divided by sales) multiple on 2026E**, "in line with the peer average".
- The multiple was reduced from 5.5x to 5.2x. The 5.5x figure included a previous **35% premium to the sector**, which Barclays has now removed.
- The reasons for the change are the sector re-rating and uncertainty about the outlook after successive guidance cuts.
- Barclays says the company "would merit a premium versus peers in light of its growth profile, but successive guidance cuts mean we risk adjust our conviction and value ONT in line with peers."

**Scenarios (to 2030).**
- **Upside case: 220 GBp.** ONT keeps growing strongly and reaches more than **£1.1bn in sales by 2030**, against about £0.7bn in the base case.
- **Downside case: 85 GBp.** Growth slows and revenue reaches about **£0.3bn by 2030**.
- **Base case: 160 GBp.**

## Risks (Barclays)

Risks that may impede the price target are "poor execution, further guidance cuts, and Biopharma contracts not materialising."

## Notes from the CFO meeting

After the trading update, Barclays joined a meeting with ONT's CFO. The notes below are Barclays' record of that meeting, so they represent management claims.

### Three key takeaways

1. **2026 revenue.** Confidence in H2 growth comes from business that has already been converted and is ramping.
   - The Americas (AMR) and APAC (Asia-Pacific) excluding China are the swing factors within the 16-20% guide.
   - EMEAI and China expectations are relatively fixed.
   - New opportunities that could push growth back into the 21-25% range will be discussed in more detail once signed.
   - Revenue at the mid-point of the new guide is slightly more H2-weighted than the previously communicated 45:55 H1:H2 split, but "not drastically".
2. **2027 and beyond.** Management does not see a fundamental issue that changes its view on the business's continuing growth rate. It can see growth re-accelerating to **21-25% in 2027**. The China comparison will be easier.
3. **Earnings.** Adjusted EBITDA breakeven is still targeted for **2027**, despite the expansion of the C-suite (senior executive team). Management is still comfortable with its **62% gross margin** guidance for the year, and in both H1 and H2.

### Barclays' detailed notes by region and segment

- **China.**
  - H1 performance was below expectations. This largely reflects China, where ONT is in the middle of restructuring its commercial organisation and where the market is weak.
  - Competitors have all shown a market decline.
  - The reorganisation has weighed on performance, and management does not expect an immediate rebound.
  - China was **10% of revenue in 2025** and is expected to be **7% in H2'26**.
  - Management "always expected low-to-no growth" in China.
- **Americas.**
  - Performance was below expectations, which Barclays records as "largely timing issue."
  - ONT has signed the contracts it needs for an H2 acceleration, and they are in the ramp phase.
- **Applied Industrial.**
  - Growth is slower because plasmid sequencing (reading circular DNA used in lab and manufacturing work) is slowing. Customers are multiplexing (running several samples at once) and using each flow cell (the consumable chip that does the sequencing) more fully.
  - There is also more price competition between service providers.
- **2026 swing factors.**
  - China is still declining in H2.
  - Some headroom is built into the Americas expectations. Management is not assuming all the opportunities will happen.
  - EMEAI is a more fixed expectation.
- **Beyond 2026.** Management thinks there is no fundamental issue with performance or the market, and expects growth to re-accelerate in FY27.
- **EBITDA breakeven.** Management is still confident it can achieve this despite the C-suite build-out.
- **New opportunities.** Management will give detail once deals are signed, but notes it has "talked for some time about penetrating different markets."
- **UK Biobank.** The project has ramped, is performing strongly, and should make a "significant contribution in H2'26."
