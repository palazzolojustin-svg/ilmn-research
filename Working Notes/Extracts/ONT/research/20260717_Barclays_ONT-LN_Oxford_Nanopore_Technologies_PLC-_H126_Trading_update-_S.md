**Summary.** Barclays reacts to Oxford Nanopore's first-half 2026 trading update, in which revenue of about £116.5m missed consensus (£125.4m) by about 7% on weakness in China, the Middle East and the Americas. # Barclays on Oxford Nanopore: H1 2026 trading update

This is a short Barclays note dated 13 July 2026 (completed 06:48 GMT and released 06:49 GMT). It reacts to the half-year trading update Oxford Nanopore Technologies (ONT, the UK-listed maker of nanopore DNA/RNA sequencing devices) published that morning. A trading update is an interim announcement of headline results before the full report. The full H1 2026 results are due on 19 August 2026 [ONT H1'26 trading update, via Barclays, 2026-07-13].

## Headline view and rating

- The note's title is "H126 Trading update: Sales miss, 2026 guidance adjusted" [Barclays, 2026-07-13].
- Barclays says revenue came in about 7% below consensus (the average analyst forecast), at 12% growth at constant currency (growth with exchange-rate effects stripped out, "ccy").
- Reduced 2026 guidance is 16-20% growth on a like-for-like basis (excluding the extra opportunities). Including additional collaboration and licensing opportunities it is 21-25%.
- Barclays says this implies full-year revenue about 3% below consensus at the mid-point.
- Barclays names three drags: China, the Middle East and the Americas (AMR).
- Barclays' opinion: "We expect the print to weigh on shares." It calls the update "a negative trading update for ONT."
- Rating: Overweight (Barclays' term for expecting the stock to outperform the average of its sector coverage over 12 months), reiterated. Industry view: Positive.
- Barclays says it continues "to see an attractive opportunity in the shares over the medium term," citing its earlier note "2025 outlook: 5 themes for '25" (21 November).
- Price target: 172p (GBp, pence). The share price on 10 July 2026 was 120p, implying 43.0% potential upside [Bloomberg, Barclays Research].

## What ONT reported for H1 2026

### Revenue

- Revenue was about £116.5m, up about 12% at constant currency. Consensus was £125.4m.
- Barclays' own estimate was £126.7m. Actual revenue was 8.1% below Barclays and 7.1% below consensus.
- The prior-year comparison is H1 2025 revenue of £105.6m, which had grown 28.0% at constant currency.
- Constant-currency growth of 12.0% compares with Barclays' 21.9% estimate (9.9 percentage points lower) and consensus of 20.5% (8.5 points lower). Consensus growth is implied from Bloomberg's reported-revenue growth and Barclays' FX estimate.
- Revenue fell 16% in China and 14% in the Middle East.
- Excluding China and the Middle East, revenue grew 16% at constant currency.

### Product and market lines

- PromethION (ONT's high-throughput sequencing instrument) grew about 15% year on year.
- The Research market (academic and discovery customers) grew about 5%.
- Applied Markets (clinical, industrial and biopharma uses) grew about 22%.
- Barclays' table breaks out H1 2025 sales by segment (the company did not give H1 2026 segment figures in this update):
  - Research: £72.1m.
  - Applied Industrial: £12.9m.
  - Clinical: £13.0m.
  - BioPharma: £7.6m.
- Barclays' H1 2026 estimates were Research £82.2m, Applied Industrial £15.1m, Clinical £19.9m and BioPharma £9.4m.

### Geography (constant currency)

- EMEAI (Europe, Middle East, Africa and India) grew about 23%.
- AMR (the Americas) grew about 12%. Barclays calls this below expectations. The company said growth was slower than anticipated because of the timing of customer orders and contract wins, largely in the Clinical market.
- APAC (Asia-Pacific) declined about 8%. This reflects weakness in China and the completion of the PRECISE II contract in Singapore.

### Gross margin

- Gross margin (the share of revenue left after direct costs) was not disclosed. Consensus was 61.7%, Barclays' estimate 62.1%, and H1 2025 was 58.0%.
- Management noted further gross margin improvement.
- Management reiterated FY26 gross margin guidance of about 62%.

### Adjusted EBITDA

- Adjusted EBITDA (earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation, adjusted for one-offs) was not disclosed. Consensus was a loss of £32.4m, Barclays' estimate a loss of £24.4m, and H1 2025 was a loss of £48.4m.
- Management highlighted disciplined cost control.
- Management said ONT remains on track for adjusted EBITDA breakeven in FY27.

### Cash

- Cash was about £234.5m at 30 June 2026, versus £302.8m at FY25 year-end.
- The company said this provides "continued balance sheet strength to execute the Group's strategy."

## FY26 guidance

- **Headline:** revenue growth of 21-25% at constant currency, versus consensus of 23.0%. Barclays describes the guidance as "adjusted."
- **What changed:** management expects growth within the previously guided range, but "supported by additional collaboration and licensing revenue opportunities expected in H2." Barclays notes that some of these are one-off in nature.
- **Underlying growth:** excluding those opportunities, FY26 constant-currency growth is expected at about 16-20%. Management cites three reasons:
  - continued weakness in China;
  - disruption in the Middle East;
  - customer order timing in AMR that is not expected to be fully recovered by year-end.
- **AMR outlook:** management expects a materially stronger second half, supported by the ramp-up of secured business. First-half order timing headwinds are not expected to be fully recovered by year-end.
- **Mix:** growth is increasingly weighted towards Applied Markets, particularly Clinical and BioPharma.
- **Gross margin:** guidance reiterated at about 62% for the LSRT line (consensus 61.8%), supported by continued operational improvements.
- **Operating expenses:** management continues to highlight cost control and efficiency initiatives. No figures were given.
- **Adjusted EBITDA outlook (from Barclays' table):** a loss of £45m to £55m for FY26.

## Mid-term outlook (reiterated)

Adjusted EBITDA breakeven during FY27 is maintained. Consensus for FY27E is a loss of £10.5m. Management says this rests on:

- above-market constant-currency revenue growth, with Applied Markets driving it;
- further gross margin improvement from current levels (consensus FY27E: 63.3%);
- continued tight cost control and operational leverage;
- a strong balance sheet and ongoing cash discipline.

## Valuation and risks

- **Method:** the price target is based on about 5.5x EV/Sales 2026E (enterprise value divided by estimated 2026 sales), a premium of about 35% to peers.
- **Why a premium:** Barclays says ONT merits it given its growth profile, a "now-clearer commercial strategy," improving execution, and "potentially upside potential to numbers given opportunities in applied markets."
- **Risks to the target:** poor execution, further guidance cuts, and BioPharma contracts not materialising.
