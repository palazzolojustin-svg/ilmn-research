# J.P. Morgan Cazenove flash note on Oxford Nanopore FY25 trading update (24% constant-currency revenue growth, 2H25 revenue beat)

*Source file: `Working Notes/Sources/ONT/research/20260115_JP_Morgan_ONT-LN_Oxford_Nanopore-_FY25_Trading_Update-_24-_LC_LSRT_growth.md` · Date: 2026-01-12 · J.P. Morgan Cazenove (Europe Equity Research), analyst Zain Ebrahim · sell-side research*

**Summary.** J.P. Morgan reacts to Oxford Nanopore's FY25 trading update, in which the company expects 2025 revenue of £223-224m, or 24% growth at constant currency, above its own 20-23% guidance. The analyst calculates that second-half 2025 revenue of about £117.9m beat consensus by about 3% and J.P. Morgan's own estimate by about 1%. Clinical and Biopharma growth accelerated while Applied Industrial and Research slowed. Cash of £302m also beat expectations. The analyst keeps an Overweight rating and expects the shares to outperform on the day.

## What the company announced

[ONT FY25 trading update, as summarised by JPM, 2026-01-12] ONT expects 2025 revenue of £223-224m. That is 24% growth at constant currency (LC, meaning growth with foreign-exchange effects removed). It is above ONT's FY25 guidance of 20-23% LC growth.

## Second-half 2025 revenue versus expectations

Using the £223.5m midpoint of the guided range, J.P. Morgan calculates implied second-half 2025 (2H'25) revenue of £117.9m.

| Comparison | Result |
|---|---|
| vs. Bloomberg consensus (dated 12 Jan 2026) | about 3% above |
| vs. J.P. Morgan estimate (JPMe) | about 1% above |

- **Growth rate:** 2H'25 revenue grew about 21% at constant currency.
- **Deceleration:** That is about 7 percentage points slower than 1H'25. The analyst attributes this to a tougher comparison base: 2H'24 underlying revenue grew 36% LC, versus 12% LC in 1H'24.
- **Table 1:** The note includes a "2H 2025 Consensus Comparison" table. Its content was garbled in the source and could not be read. The visible source line cites company reports, company consensus dated 12 Jan 2026, and JPM estimates.

## Growth by end market

The note says growth accelerated for Clinical and Biopharma. It decelerated for Applied Industrial (tougher comparisons) and for Research (continued funding pressure). The 2H'25 figures below are J.P. Morgan's own estimates, derived from the reported full-year growth rates.

| End market | FY25 reported growth | JPM-estimated 2H'25 revenue | JPM-estimated 2H'25 LC growth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clinical | about 60% | £16.8m | more than 70% |
| Biopharma | about 30% | £10.5m | 52% |
| Applied Industrial | 27% | £14.5m | 7% |
| Research | 15% | £76m | 11% |

- **Research:** The analyst notes the 11% LC growth came "despite end-market funding pressures."
- **Applied Industrial:** The slowdown reflects tougher comparisons.

## PromethION and geography

- **PromethION:** This is ONT's high-throughput sequencing platform. Revenue grew more than 40% year over year on a reported basis. Assuming 41% growth, J.P. Morgan estimates 2H'25 PromethION growth of 30% LC.
- **Geography:** ONT saw more than 20% LC growth in each region: EMEAI (Europe, Middle East, Africa and India), APAC (Asia-Pacific) and the Americas.
- **Americas acceleration:** J.P. Morgan estimates Americas growth accelerated in 2H to at least 26% LC, from 17% LC in 1H.

## Cash

ONT ended the year with £302m of cash, cash equivalents and other liquid investments. That compares with J.P. Morgan's estimate of £287m and consensus of £280m.
