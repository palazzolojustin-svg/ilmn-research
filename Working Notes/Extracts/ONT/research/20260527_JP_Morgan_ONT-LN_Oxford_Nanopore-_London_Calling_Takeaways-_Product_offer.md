## The event and the overall view

The analysts attended ONT's London Calling Analyst Event on 21 May 2026 ("yesterday" in the note). ONT was represented by CEO Francis Van Parys, CFO Nick Keher and Chief Scientific Officer Lakmal Jayasinghe.

Overall, J.P. Morgan said it was "encouraged" by ONT's progress across its portfolio. It cited four items:
1. The launch of a higher-throughput PromethION (PromethION Plus).
2. An improved GridION Q-line product with additional features.
3. Continued interest in Biopharma QC (quality control in drug manufacturing).
4. An updated, AI-developed, higher-accuracy basecaller, v6.0 (software that turns the raw electrical signal from a nanopore into DNA letters).

The analysts were also encouraged by the two executive appointments, saying they add "to the strength and depth of the Executive Committee." [JPM, 2026-05-22]

## Executive appointments

ONT announced two senior hires at the event. [London Calling, May 2026, as reported by JPM]

**Davide Manissero, first Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**
- Effective 3 August 2026.
- He joins from Radiometer, where he is currently CMO. He was previously CMO at QIAGEN and held roles at the ECDC (European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control) and the WHO, focused on infectious diseases and public health.
- His focus will be leading ONT's global clinical strategy, drawing on his experience in regulated clinical environments.

**Andrew Watson, Chief Information Officer (CIO)**
- Effective 15 June 2026.
- He joins from Mimecast, a cybersecurity company, where he leads AI and threat-protection engineering. He previously held senior roles at Healx and Dyson, where he established machine learning and biomedical research teams.
- His focus will be scaling ONT's customer-facing platforms, improving user experience and embedding machine learning across product development and technology.

**Other leadership points**
- This follows the earlier appointment of Tina St Leger as Chief People Officer. J.P. Morgan notes that further Executive team appointments are expected this year.
- Responsibility for ONT's commercial function (sales and marketing) will move from CFO Nick Keher to CEO Francis Van Parys, effective 1 July 2026.

## Product announcements

### PromethION Plus (launch July 2026)
PromethION is ONT's high-throughput sequencer, aimed at high-volume customers. ONT announced that PromethION Plus will launch from July 2026. [London Calling, as reported by JPM]
- It removes the wash-and-reload step that the current PromethION requires.
- Yield (data output per run) rises about 20%, to 177GB from 149GB.
- J.P. Morgan says this will reduce the price per genome and improve throughput.
- It will be available alongside High Throughput Barcoding (tagging many samples so they can run together).

### Updated GridION Q-line (available from June 2026)
GridION is ONT's mid-sized benchtop sequencer. The updated Q-line will be available from June 2026 with these added features: [London Calling, as reported by JPM]
- R10.4.1 flow cell (the consumable chip holding the nanopores).
- A rapid barcoding kit.
- A rapid PCR barcoding kit.
- An RNA flow cell.
- A direct RNA sequencing kit (RNA004), which reads RNA molecules directly.

### Biopharma QC
ONT highlighted increasing interest in end-to-end workflows for Biopharma QC. The reason given is that fewer separate assays are needed for quality control. [London Calling, as reported by JPM]
- **mRNA vaccine QC, in development with Lonza.** Available now under mRNA QC: sequence confirmation, reads of the modified base N1mpU, and poly(A) tail length. In development: 5' cap detection and double-stranded RNA detection.
- **Adventitious viral agent (AVA) QC.** AVAs are unwanted viruses that can contaminate biological manufacturing. ONT is developing tests for these, including non-endogenous viruses (those not naturally part of the production cell line), and tests for antibody production. The aim is to cut the number of tests and assays needed to a single platform.

### New basecaller HAC v6.0
ONT announced HAC v6.0 (a "high accuracy" basecaller developed with machine learning), with better performance on insertions/deletions, variant calling and methylation detection. [London Calling, as reported by JPM]
- **Accuracy:** Q23.5, up from Q22.6 with previous HAC models. J.P. Morgan equates Q22.6 to 99.4%. Q-scores are a log scale of base-call error, so a higher number means fewer errors. It runs at high speed.
- **No SUP model needed.** ONT believes HAC is now good enough that a SUP ("super accuracy") basecalling model is not required. It cites comparable performance for bacterial assemblies, superior performance on insertions/deletions and SNPs (single-letter DNA changes), superior methylation detection (including 4mC, 5mC, 5hmC and 6mA, which are chemical marks on DNA bases), and improved detection of short tandem repeats (STRs, short repeated DNA stretches).
- **AI positioning.** ONT noted the emphasis of machine learning on protein design, LLM (large language model) training and manufacturing. It reiterated that, because AI depends on the quality of the information it is given, ONT is "uniquely positioned to benefit from increased AI use."
- The new model is available on GitHub.

### Protein sensing and peptide sequencing
ONT highlighted further progress using two approaches: (i) peptide threading and (ii) direct protein sequencing. [London Calling, as reported by JPM]
- In the peptide approach, a full-length protein is digested into peptides. Each peptide is attached to DNA handles, which offers Q20+ DNA accuracy.
- The method can also detect post-translational modifications (chemical changes to proteins after they are made), including phosphorylation, acetylation and succinylation.
- The note gives no details on direct protein sequencing, timelines or commercial plans.
