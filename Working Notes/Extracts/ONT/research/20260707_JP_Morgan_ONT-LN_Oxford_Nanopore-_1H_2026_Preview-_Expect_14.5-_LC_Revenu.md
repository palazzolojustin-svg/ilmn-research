# Oxford Nanopore (ONT) 1H 2026 preview: J.P. Morgan Cazenove, 2 July 2026

## Document basics

This is a sell-side research note (written by a brokerage's analysts for investors) from J.P. Morgan Cazenove's European Pharmaceuticals & Biotechnology team, dated 2 July 2026. Zain Ebrahim is the lead analyst. Co-contributors listed are Richard Vosser, Sophia Graeff Buhl Nielsen, Dhitee Goel and Khushi Bihani. The note was completed 1 July 2026 and disseminated 2 July 2026. The share price used was 131p at 1 July 2026. The Bloomberg (BBG) consensus used is dated 30 June 2026.

Key dates: Oxford Nanopore (ONT) will publish a trading update on 1H 2026 revenues on 13 July 2026, followed by detailed 1H 2026 results on 19 August 2026.

## Rating and valuation

- **Rating:** Overweight [JPM, 2026-07-02]. Overweight means J.P. Morgan expects the stock to outperform the average total return of the analyst's coverage universe over the price-target period.
- **Price target:** The note does not state a current price target. The rating history table in the disclosures shows earlier targets only: 5 Dec 2023, Overweight, price 192p, target 325p; 6 Mar 2024, 129p, target 210p; 3 Sep 2024, 122p, target 200p; 4 Mar 2025, 105p, target 190p. Coverage was initiated 10 Nov 2021. No change to rating or target is made in this note.
- **Relative valuation argument:** J.P. Morgan estimates (JPMe) a 21% revenue compound annual growth rate (CAGR) for ONT over 2027-30, versus 14% for PacBio and 7% for Illumina. Against that growth, ONT trades at about 3x 2027 revenue, versus about 6x for Illumina and 5x for PacBio. The analysts call ONT's valuation "undemanding" and see ONT as "on track to outperform their peers."

## Headline view

- The analysts expect 1H 2026 revenues to be modestly below ONT's implied guidance. They also see a potential 2026 guidance revision that would imply roughly 2% cuts to consensus 2026 revenues. They say this "should be reasonably well anticipated by the market."
- They still see ONT as well positioned to hit its Adj. EBITDA breakeven target in 2027 (adjusted EBITDA is earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation, with some items removed). They expect a sequential improvement in the Adj. EBITDA loss in 1H 2026, which they say should reassure investors on achievability.
- Underlying performance is described as on track. The shortfall is attributed to external factors in China and MENA.

## 1H 2026 revenue forecast

J.P. Morgan forecasts 1H 2026 revenue growth of 14.5% at constant currency (LC, meaning growth with currency effects stripped out) to £118.9m. This compares as follows:

- About 5% below a "stale" BBG consensus. Table 1 shows consensus at £125.4m, so JPM is 5.1% below.
- About 2% below ONT's implied guidance for 1H of £121.5m. That figure is derived from the expected 45% weighting of revenue towards 1H 2026 and the implied midpoint of the FY26 guide of £270m. The FY26 guide is 21-25% LC revenue growth with a 2% FX (currency) headwind.
- Table 1 shows a year-ago 1H 2025 base of about £105.6m, giving reported growth of about 12.6% and LC growth of 14.5%.

### Research (academic and government sequencing customers)

- Forecast: 3% LC revenue growth to £72.8m (Table 1 consensus is £72.4m, so JPM is slightly ahead, by about 0.9%). This is a deceleration from 15% growth in 2H 2025.
- Driver: a slowdown in China and MENA (Middle East and North Africa).
- **China:** The analysts expect continued deterioration due to export control restrictions (limits on exporting sequencing technology to China). Evidence cited from peers in 1Q 2026: Illumina's China revenues fell 29% LC (analyst Casey Woodring covers Illumina at J.P. Morgan), and PacBio's APAC (Asia-Pacific) revenue fell 16% LC, partly due to customers deferring purchases ahead of the launch of PacBio's SPRQ-Nx.
- **MENA:** The analysts note the potential impact of geopolitical disruption, which has likely hurt sequencing demand in 2Q 2026.

### Applied Markets (Biopharma, Clinical and Industrial)

- Forecast: 40% LC revenue growth to £46.2m. Table 1 shows consensus at £33.5m, i.e. JPM is about 37.8% above consensus on this line, and the year-ago base implies 39.7% LC growth.
- Rationale: the analysts are "encouraged by the strong momentum seen in 2H 2025," which they expect to continue into 1H 2026.

## Profitability forecast

- **Adj. EBITDA loss:** £32.6m in 1H 2026, "broadly in-line with" consensus, and a sequential improvement on the £38.4m loss delivered in 2H 2025.
- **Drivers:** gross margin forecast of 61.5% and continued operating-expense (OpEx) control, with the analysts anticipating low-single-digit OpEx growth.

## FY 2026 guidance outlook

- J.P. Morgan estimates that geopolitical disruption in MENA plus continued deterioration in China could create an additional headwind of roughly 3 percentage points (pp) to FY26 growth, with underlying performance in line with expectations.
- They therefore anticipate the FY26 revenue guidance could be revised to 18-22% LC growth, from the current 21-25%.
- **Peer precedent:** PacBio cut its FY26 guidance by about 2pp at 1Q 2026 on softer instrument demand, from 3-12% revenue growth to 3-9%.
- **FX:** Based on latest exchange rates, the analysts now see a 1% FX headwind to FY26 revenues (previously 2%).
- **Implied revenue:** a 20% LC growth midpoint with a 1% FX headwind implies about £266m, roughly 2% below BBG consensus of £272.7m. (ONT's current guide midpoint implies about £270m.)
- **Breakeven in 2027:** Adj. EBITDA breakeven in 2027 is still seen as achievable. Revenue growth is likely to see a modest acceleration in 2027 because the MENA-impacted 2026 base should be more favourable (easier to grow against).

## Risks and caveats mentioned

- Continued China export-control restrictions and demand deterioration.
- Geopolitical disruption in MENA affecting sequencing demand.
- Possible guidance cut (a JPM expectation, not a company statement).
- The BBG consensus is described as "stale," so the apparent gap to consensus may overstate the true shortfall.
- Full valuation methodology and risks are not given in this note. The disclosures refer readers to J.P. Morgan's most recent company-specific report.
