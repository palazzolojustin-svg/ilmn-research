# Barclays note on Oxford Nanopore's new CEO appointment (Francis van Parys from Radiometer), Overweight, PT 170p

*Source file: `Working Notes/Sources/ONT/research/20251212_Barclays_ONT-LN_Oxford_Nanopore_Technologies_PLC-_New_CEO_appointed.md` · Date: 2025-12-08 · Barclays Equity Research (European Medical Technology & Services); analyst Hassan Al-Wakeel, CFA, with Jonathon Unwin, Marco Pires-Cox and Rohit Rajan listed as contacts · sell-side research*

**Summary.** Barclays reacts to Oxford Nanopore's 8 December 2025 announcement that Francis van Parys, currently CEO of Danaher's Radiometer, will succeed co-founder Gordon Sanghera as CEO. Barclays calls the appointment an "incremental positive", citing his clinical and biopharma experience and large-company commercial background, and keeps its Overweight rating and 170p price target (about 36% upside from 125p). The note contains no estimate changes; it includes the valuation method (about 5.7x 2026E EV/Sales) and the price-target history.

## Document overview

This is a short Barclays Equity Research flash note, dated 8 December 2025 and released at 07:49 GMT that day. It covers Oxford Nanopore Technologies PLC (ONT; London ticker ONT.L, Bloomberg ONT LN). Oxford Nanopore is a UK company that makes DNA and RNA sequencing devices based on nanopore technology, a competitor to Illumina. The note is titled "New CEO appointed". It is written by Hassan Al-Wakeel, CFA, in Barclays' European Medical Technology & Services team. The other listed contacts are Jonathon Unwin, Marco Pires-Cox and Rohit Rajan. Only Al-Wakeel gives the analyst certification.

## The CEO announcement

### What happened

- ONT announced on the morning of 8 December 2025 that it had appointed **Francis van Parys** as its new CEO. He joins from **Radiometer**, a Danaher company.
- Radiometer is described as an acute care diagnostics company (tests used in urgent, hospital-based care) and part of Danaher Corporation, a large US life-science and diagnostics group.
- The departure of the current CEO and co-founder, **Gordon Sanghera**, was already announced in August 2025 [Barclays]. Barclays' headline says he will step down on 2 March 2026 and stay on in an advisory capacity until early 2027.
- Per the release, Sanghera will leave the Board at close of business on 2 March 2026. He will remain an employee in an advisory capacity through to early 2027 "to ensure a smooth handover".

### Details from the company release

- Van Parys has more than 20 years of experience leading multi-billion-dollar life science businesses. The release credits him with a strong track record of scaling innovation-driven organisations through commercial and operational excellence.
- He is currently President and CEO of Radiometer.
- Previously he held senior leadership roles at **Cytiva** and **GE Healthcare**, "driving sustained growth and building high-performing teams across Europe, Asia, and North America."

### Barclays' view

Barclays reports having spoken to the company. Its points are:

- The new CEO will continue to focus on innovation but also brings global commercial experience of running companies at a larger scale than ONT.
- He has experience across both clinical (patient diagnostics) and biopharma (drug-industry customers) end-markets.
- Barclays says: "We view the appointment of a CEO as an incremental positive in the ONT story."
- Barclays also views his experience in regulated environments (markets where products need regulatory approval, such as clinical diagnostics) as positive. It says ONT has been "progressively moving towards" such environments in recent years, including through partnerships with **bioMerieux** and **Cepheid** (the latter part of Danaher).
- The headline summary says his clinical and biopharma experience is "a positive".

Danaher is the parent of both Radiometer and Cepheid, so the new CEO comes from the same group as one of ONT's partners. The note does not draw this link beyond naming the two.

## Valuation methodology

- Barclays' price target rests on about **5.7x EV/Sales for 2026E**. EV/Sales is enterprise value divided by revenue, a way of valuing a company relative to its sales. 2026E means the estimate for fiscal 2026.
- That multiple is about a **20% premium to peers**.
- Reasons given for the premium: ONT's growth profile, a "now-clearer commercial strategy", improving execution, and "potentially significant upside to numbers given opportunities in applied markets" (markets outside traditional research, such as clinical and other practical uses of sequencing).
- A discounted cash flow (DCF) model, which values a company from its projected future cash flows, is used as a sense-check, with a **WACC of 7.5%** (the discount rate) and a **terminal growth rate (TGR) of 3%**. Barclays says it "arrive[s] at a similar valuation".

## Risks to the price target

Barclays names three risks that could prevent its target from being reached:

- Poor execution on expansion.
- Difficulty penetrating clinical markets.
- Increased competition.
