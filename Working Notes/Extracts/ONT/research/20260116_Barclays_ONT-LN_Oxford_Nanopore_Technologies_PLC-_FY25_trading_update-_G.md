# Barclays on Oxford Nanopore FY25 trading update: growth ahead of guidance, GridION Dx approved in UK/Europe, Overweight, PT 170p

*Source file: `Working Notes/Sources/ONT/research/20260116_Barclays_ONT-LN_Oxford_Nanopore_Technologies_PLC-_FY25_trading_update-_G.md` · Date: 2026-01-12 · Barclays Equity Research (European Medical Technology & Services); analysts Hassan Al-Wakeel, CFA, Jonathon Unwin, Marco Pires-Cox, Rohit Rajan · sell-side research*

**Summary.** Barclays reacts to Oxford Nanopore's FY25 trading update, in which revenue of about £223-224m and 24% constant-currency growth beat the guided 20-23% range and consensus. Cash of £302m was also ahead of consensus, and the GridION Dx sequencer received UK and European approval, a step toward launching regulated clinical tests, starting with a tuberculosis test. Barclays reiterates Overweight with a 170p price target, expects the update to be taken well, and notes that no 2026 guidance was given. The mid-term targets (to FY27) were reiterated.

## Headline and rating

Barclays published this note on 12 January 2026 (completed and released 08:15 GMT) after Oxford Nanopore (ONT) issued its FY25 trading update, a short company statement on full-year performance released ahead of the full results. Barclays titled the note "FY25 trading update: Growth ahead of guidance". Its summary line states: "Expect +ve update to be taken well".

- **Rating:** Overweight (a relative rating: Barclays expects the stock to outperform the average expected return of its industry coverage universe over 12 months). Industry view: Positive, meaning sector fundamentals and valuations are improving.
- **Price target:** 170p (GBp 170), unchanged from the 24-Nov-2025 target of 1.70 in the rating history.
- **Share price:** 141p at the 09-Jan-2026 close, so potential upside is +20.7%.
- **Barclays' conclusion:** "We reiterate our OW rating and continue to see an attractive opportunity in the shares over the medium term." Barclays expects the above-guidance growth, the improving working capital and the GridION Dx CE and UKCA approval "to be taken well".
- **Next catalyst:** ONT publishes full FY25 results on 2 March 2026. Barclays will look for more detail then. (The Barclays text also says "March" generally.) ONT gave no 2026 guidance, and Barclays expects an update at the full-year results.

## What the trading update showed

### Revenue and growth

Barclays describes the update as a c.0.5% beat against consensus on the top line in the second half of 2025 (H2).

- **FY25 revenue:** ONT expects to report about £223-224m, against £183.2m in FY24. That is growth of about 22% as reported and 24% at constant currency (ccy, which strips out exchange-rate effects).
- **Versus guidance:** ONT had guided to the top end of a 20-23% constant-currency growth range. 24% is slightly above that range.
- **Versus expectations:** Consensus was £220.3m (the table shows £220.4m, from company-compiled consensus dated 11 January 2026) and Barclays' own estimate was £222.9m. In the results table, the 2025 trading figure is £223.5m. That is 0.3% above Barclays and 1.4% above consensus. Barclays' estimate for ccy growth was 23.5%, so the actual 24.0% is 0.5 percentage points higher. Consensus ccy growth was 21.2%.
- **H2 2025 (implied):** Revenue was £117.9m, against a Barclays estimate of £117.3m (+0.5%) and consensus of £114.8m (+2.7%). Implied H2 ccy growth was 19.8%, which is Barclays' estimate figure shown in that table cell.
- **Gross margin:** There was no update. ONT's guidance remains about 59% for 2025. Barclays estimates 59.0% for FY25 (consensus 59.0%) and 59.8% for H2 (consensus 59.9%).

### Regional performance

Growth was broad based, with more than 20% constant-currency growth in each region: EMEAI (Europe, Middle East, Africa and India), APAC (Asia-Pacific) and AMR (Americas).

Barclays spoke to the company (its CFO, per the note's summary) about the Americas. Growth there accelerated to above 20% in ccy, compared with 16.9% in H1 2025. In Barclays' account, the biggest driver was customers outside academic and government Research ("ex-Research"). There was also "some positive activity" in Research in the Americas at the end of Q3 and into Q4. The summary stresses that regional growth was driven by ex-Research.

### Growth by customer end market

| End market | FY25 growth (ONT release) |
|---|---|
| Clinical | about 60% (reported) |
| BioPharma | about 30% |
| Applied Industrial | about 27% |
| Research | about 15% |

ONT said Research grew about 15% "despite end-market funding pressures". Research is the traditional academic customer base.

### Growth by product

Growth came in all product types. It was led by the PromethION range (ONT's high-throughput sequencers), which grew by more than 40% year on year as reported. MinION (the small portable sequencer) returned to growth, which Barclays says was largely driven by BioPharma.

### Cash

ONT ended 2025 with about £302m of cash, cash equivalents and other liquid investments at 31 December 2025, against £403.8m at FY24. This was ahead of consensus of £280.9m (a 7.5% beat), helped by improving working capital (the cash tied up in inventory and receivables). ONT said this gives "continued balance sheet strength to support execution of the Group's strategy". The release also says the business "remains well capitalised to deliver on its goals". Barclays' press summary says it "continued progress on path to profitability".

### GridION Dx approval

GridION Dx (a diagnostic-grade version of ONT's GridION sequencer) has been approved in the UK and Europe, via UKCA and CE marking (the UK and EU regulatory approvals). Barclays calls this "a key step in launching regulated IVD products (Clinical segment) in the region". IVD means in vitro diagnostic, meaning regulated medical tests. The first test planned for launch is the AmPORE-TB test, a tuberculosis test developed with bioMérieux, which was launched as research use only (RUO, not for clinical decisions) in November 2025.

## Mid-term outlook (reiterated)

ONT reiterated its mid-term targets. Barclays' own estimates sit alongside them.

- **Revenue growth ("LSRT"):** More than 30% compound annual growth (CAGR) between FY24 and FY27, against Barclays at 26.8%. The release says this is underpinned by continued penetration of existing markets (Research) and expansion into emerging end markets, "in particular across BioPharma, Clinical and Applied Industrial". LSRT is not defined in the note.
- **Gross margin:** More than 62% by FY27, against Barclays and consensus both at 62.3% for FY27E. The company attributes this to ongoing manufacturing efficiency gains, higher volumes and further penetration of new end markets.
- **Operating expenses:** Growth at a CAGR of 3-8% between FY24 and FY27. The company says this reflects "a continued focus on financial discipline to leverage the infrastructure the Group has already built and to modulate investment relative to the outlook for growth".
- **Adjusted EBITDA:** Breakeven by 2027, against Barclays at -£13.9m and consensus at -£15.7m for FY27E. Cash flow positive in FY28.

## Valuation and risks

**Method:** Barclays' price target is based on about 5.7x EV/Sales for 2026E (enterprise value divided by sales), a c.20% premium to peers. Barclays argues the premium is warranted by the company's growth profile, a "now-clearer commercial strategy", improving execution and "potentially significant upside to numbers given opportunities in applied markets". As a sense check it uses a discounted cash flow (DCF) model with a 7.5% WACC (weighted average cost of capital, the discount rate) and 3% terminal growth rate (TGR), and arrives at a similar valuation.

**Risks to the target:** Poor execution on expansion, difficulty penetrating clinical markets, and increased competition.
