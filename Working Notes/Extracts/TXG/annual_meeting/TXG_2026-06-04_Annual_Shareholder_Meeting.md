# 10x Genomics 2026 Annual Meeting of Stockholders

*Source file: `Working Notes/Sources/TXG/annual_meeting/TXG_2026-06-04_Annual_Shareholder_Meeting.md` · Date: 2026-06-04 · Seeking Alpha transcript · annual_meeting*

**Summary.** 10x Genomics held its seventh annual shareholder meeting as a public company on June 4, 2026, conducted virtually. The meeting covered three routine governance matters: election of three Class I directors (CEO Serge Saxonov, Ben Hindson, and John Stuelpnagel), ratification of Ernst & Young as the independent auditor for fiscal 2026, and a non-binding advisory vote on executive compensation. All three proposals passed. The meeting included no business commentary, financial results, strategy discussion, or Q&A on company operations.

## Meeting Structure and Attendance

This was 10x Genomics' seventh Annual Meeting of Stockholders as a public company [Jun 04, 2026]. The meeting was held virtually, allowing stockholders to participate regardless of location and to submit questions and vote online.

**Voting participation**: Of 219.7 million total votes entitled to vote (118.9 million Class A shares and 10.1 million Class B shares), 198.8 million votes (90.49% of voting power) were represented in person or by proxy, establishing a quorum [Jun 04, 2026].

The record date for determining voting eligibility was April 8, 2026.

## Board and Leadership

**Company leadership present**: Serge Saxonov (Co-Founder, CEO and Chairperson of the meeting), Adam Taich (Chief Financial Officer), Randy Wu (General Counsel), Cassie Corneau (Senior Director, Investor Relations and Strategic Finance), and James Bryant (Senior Director, Corporate Legal, serving as Secretary of the meeting).

**External parties present**: Casey Hayes from Ernst & Young (external auditor) and John Lundberg from Equiniti Trust Company, LLC (transfer agent and Inspector of Election).

## Proposals and Voting Results

**Proposal 1: Election of Class I Directors**

The Board recommended election of three Class I director nominees to serve three-year terms expiring at the 2029 Annual Meeting or until successors are elected and qualified:
- Serge Saxonov
- Ben Hindson  
- John Stuelpnagel

All three were duly elected [Jun 04, 2026]. The company's bylaws include an advanced notice provision that closed further nominations.

**Proposal 2: Auditor Ratification**

The Board recommended ratification of Ernst & Young LLP as the company's independent registered public accounting firm for the fiscal year ending December 31, 2026. This proposal was approved [Jun 04, 2026].

**Proposal 3: Say-on-Pay Advisory Vote**

The Board recommended a non-binding advisory resolution to approve the compensation of named executive officers. This proposal was approved [Jun 04, 2026].

## Stockholder Proposals

No stockholder proposals were submitted for consideration. The company's bylaws require stockholder proposals to be submitted between the 120th and 90th day prior to the meeting date; no such proposals were received for the 2026 meeting.
