**Summary.** Bloomberg consensus financial forecasts for PacBio (PACB) spanning ten quarters from Q1 2025 actual results through Q2 2027 estimates. The sheet shows a company with flat to slightly declining revenues (~$37–44 million per quarter), deeply negative profitability across all periods, persistent negative cash flow from operations and free cash flow, rapidly depleting cash reserves (from $343 million to $129 million), and shareholder equity turning negative by Q1 2026 and worsening through 2027. ## Key Observations and Red Flags

1. **Persistent unprofitability and cash burn**: The company loses money on every metric every quarter and burns $20–48 million in free cash flow quarterly. The consensus shows no path to profitability through Q2 2027.

2. **Cash runway concern**: With $129.2 million in cash and investments at Q2 2027 and a burn rate of ~$25 million per quarter, the consensus implies the company has less than 5 quarters of runway remaining—a critical constraint that demands either a dramatic operational turnaround or financing.

3. **Negative shareholder equity**: Balance sheet insolvency (negative equity) arrives in Q2 2026 and deepens, suggesting mounting financial distress and potential covenant violations if the company has bank covenants on its debt.

4. **Debt service burden**: The company carries $644 million in long-term debt while equity erodes. Interest expense is modest (~$1.7–2.1 million per quarter), but maintaining debt service while burning cash is unsustainable without a material improvement in operations.

5. **Geographic volatility**: Asia Pacific revenue estimates collapse in 2026 (to $3.6 million in Q4 2026E), suggesting regional weakness or market loss, though recovery is assumed in 2027 estimates.

6. **High inventory levels**: Days inventory outstanding remains in the 150–215 day range, suggesting either build-ahead of demand (an optimistic signal) or demand weakness requiring write-downs (a pessimistic signal).

7. **Modest revenue scale and limited leverage**: At ~$40 million per quarter, the revenue base is too small to absorb the operating cost structure ($50–60 million in SG&A and R&D combined). Gross margins of 35–40% do not cover opex.

8. **Flat-to-declining topline**: The consensus shows no meaningful revenue growth, with 2027 estimates essentially at 2026 levels, ruling out the "growth story" that might justify current costs.
