**Summary.** Bloomberg consensus quarterly financial estimates for PacBio (PACB) covering nine quarters from Q3 2026 through Q4 2028. All estimates. Shows a company approaching profitability by late 2028 with improving gross margins (35% to 46%) and shrinking operating losses, fueled by consumables-driven revenue growth. Two major forecast anomalies: a sharp $91M revenue spike in Q1 2028 followed by a deep drop to $34M in Q2 2028, and a $223M cash increase in Q3 2027 (likely a financing event). Company maintains negative shareholder equity throughout the period.

## Summary of Key Trends and Anomalies

### Positive Signals

1. **Consumables-driven growth:** Consumables revenue grows 75% from Q3 2026 to Q4 2028, outpacing total revenue growth and building a more stable, recurring revenue base.
2. **Margin expansion:** Adjusted gross margin improves from 35% to 43.5%, likely driven by mix (consumables) and manufacturing efficiency.
3. **Operating leverage:** OpEx as a percentage of revenue shrinks from 145% to 96%, creating a path to profitability.
4. **Cash position:** The Q3 2027 financing event ($223M raise) extended the runway into 2028 and beyond.
5. **Improving cash flow:** Operating and free cash flow turn positive by Q4 2028 (barely), suggesting the business model is maturing.
6. **International growth:** Europe revenue roughly doubles from Q3 2026 to Q4 2028, indicating global adoption.

### Concerning Signals

1. **Persistent losses:** The company is unprofitable throughout the forecast, with no clear path to significant profitability by end of 2028.
2. **Negative equity:** Shareholder equity is negative in every quarter, ranging from -$51M to -$152M. The company is technically insolvent (liabilities exceed assets) on a book value basis.
3. **Instrument revenue weakness:** Instrument (hardware) revenue is lumpy and flat ($11M–$14M per quarter), suggesting slow capital equipment adoption or market saturation at current penetration.
4. **High cash burn:** Even Q4 2028 shows free cash flow of only $0.47M—minimal and easily wiped out by rounding or small changes in assumptions.
5. **Massive accumulated deficit:** At -$3 billion, the deficit reflects decades of losses and is unlikely to be erased by 2028.

### Major Forecast Anomalies (Unexplained)

1. **Q1 2028 revenue spike to $91M:** A dramatic 82% jump from Q4 2027 ($50M). This is abnormal and suggests either a one-time order, a major contract start, or a data error. The consensus sheet provides no explanation.
2. **Q2 2028 revenue collapse to $34M:** Following the Q1 spike, revenue drops 63%, falling below Q3 2026 baseline. Again, unexplained.
3. **Q3 2027 cash raise:** A $223M cash influx in one quarter, likely from equity or debt financing, which stabilizes the balance sheet.

These anomalies significantly reduce confidence in the 2028 forecast and suggest either material corporate events not reflected in the consensus detail, or data quality issues.
