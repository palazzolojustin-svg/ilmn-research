# Barclays on Pacbio (PACB) Q4 2025 results and 2026 guidance: "Hustle All Day and Dream of the Islands and the Sea" (Equal Weight, $2 PT)

*Source file: `Working Notes/Sources/PACB/research/20260220_Barclays_PACB_Pacbio-_Hustle_All_Day_and_Dream_of_the_Islands_and_the.md` · Date: 2026-02-12 · Barclays Equity Research (U.S. Life Science & Diagnostic Tools), analysts Luke Sergott, Salem Salem, Anna Kruszenski (published 12 Feb 2026, released 13 Feb 2026 GMT) · sell-side research*

**Summary.** Barclays reviews Pacbio's Q4 2025 results (revenue beat, gross margin miss) and its initial 2026 guidance of $165M-$180M revenue and 41-44% gross margin. It keeps an Equal Weight rating and a $2 price target (5x 2026E sales of $178M). The analysts see strong Vega instrument demand, growing clinical and hospital adoption of HiFi long-read sequencing, and the $300/genome SPRQ-Nx as positives, but warn that a tough funding environment and Roche's new long-read technology cloud demand, and that the stock trades at a premium to peers.

## Summary of the call

Barclays (analyst Luke Sergott, with Salem Salem and Anna Kruszenski) published this note on 12 February 2026, after Pacbio reported fourth-quarter 2025 results. The title is "Hustle All Day and Dream of the Islands and the Sea." The note keeps the **Equal Weight** rating (the stock is expected to perform in line with the other stocks Barclays covers in the industry over 12 months). The **price target stays at $2.00**. The industry view for U.S. Life Science & Diagnostic Tools stays **Positive**. The stock closed at $1.84 on 12 Feb 2026, so the target implies about **+8.7%** upside.

The report says its thesis and model "don't change much on this print." It plans to look for "more detailed channel checks" (conversations with customers and users to gauge real demand) at **AGBT** (a major genomics industry conference) two weeks later.

## Headline view

- **Vega demand** is "the big headline in the Q" (the quarter). Vega is Pacbio's lower-priced benchtop long-read sequencer. It is opening doors to new customers, which Barclays says "could eventually help drive broader HiFi adoption w/in the clinic." HiFi is Pacbio's accurate long-read sequencing method, in which long stretches of DNA are read with high accuracy.
- A "strong '25 lays a solid first layer of the foundation for management to build off of."
- Barclays says that "especially considering how tough the operating environment is," AGBT "will be a good testing ground to see if the momentum can turn into an inflection point over the next 12-18mos for the business."
- Barclays says it is "encouraged by the strong end to 2025 and relatively comfortable with the guide range," but stays on the sidelines.

## Guidance view and what could make 2026 different

- Before the print, Barclays was already modeling FY26 sales at the **high end of the $165M-$180M** range that management initiated. It was also modeling gross margin at the high end of the **41-44%** guide. Gross margin is the share of revenue left after the direct cost of making products and services.
- Barclays says this is what "could make this year different for PACB." It could see the technology "start to unlock the translational market in a more material way," meaning research aimed at clinical use, and "continue to shift away from academia."
- **Clinic and hospital growth:** Management called out strength in this part of the market, with **55% growth** in the clinic/hospital setting in the quarter [Q4 2025 call, as cited by Barclays].
- **New customers:** **65% of Vega placements** were to customers new to Pacbio [as cited by Barclays]. Barclays argues: "arguably the more researcher's hands HiFi is in, the better chances of making the shift to clinical applications."
- **SPRQ-Nx:** Barclays says it "resets long-read economics at $300/genome," which "helps accessibility." It expects a launch "by 2H" (second half of 2026).

## Caveats and risks Barclays raises

- Barclays is "not expecting the funding floodgates to open." It calls the environment "still a very tough operating environment affecting all genomics companies for the most part."
- Because of this, the measurable indicators of penetration and market expansion "are likely to come more from channel checks and user feedback than from seeing an inflection in the business."
- **Roche competition:** Roche is "coming with a relatively long-ish read technology that may freeze some of the market for PACB." Barclays says the SPRQ-Nx launch by 2H "could help thaw a broader market freeze, or at least lead to a nice pickup in consumables growth in the 2H." Consumables are the reagent kits customers must buy for each sequencing run.
- Barclays says "the market factors continue to be less than supportive for PACB, yet they keep hammering away at the opportunities they get." It expects "this hustle" to continue "through what is likely to be another knife-fight of a 1H" (first half of 2026).

## Rating rationale ("Why Equal Weight?")

- Barclays says it "remain[s] on the sidelines." It likes the underlying technology, the revamped business and story, the strong management team, and the innovative launches.
- However, the stock "is trading at a 20% premium to peers."
- It also notes that, "considering a volatile macro backdrop, we could see multiple contraction" (investors paying a lower multiple for the stock).

### Valuation method

The **$2 price target applies a 5x EV/Sales multiple** to Barclays' **FY26 revenue estimate of $178M**. EV/Sales compares a company's enterprise value (equity value plus net debt) to its annual revenue.

### Scenarios

| Case | Price | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Upside | $3.00 | An improved macro backdrop encourages investors to return to growth names, and a healthy funding environment supports customer spending. |

The stock's 52-week range was $0.85 to $2.73.

### Risks listed in the report

- **Upside risks:**
  1. Macro conditions ease and investors return to growth names.
  2. The funding environment improves and customer spending accelerates.
  3. Demand and use cases for long-read sequencing ramp faster than expected, and scientists "flock to PACB as the premier provider of long-read tech."
- **Downside risks:**
  1. Macro headwinds persist longer than expected or worsen, causing investors to shy away from high-multiple names.
  2. Customers tighten their belts further and demand weakens.

## Clinical adoption: what Pacbio is doing in the clinic

Barclays says adoption is "accelerating across rare disease, oncology, carrier screening, and population sequencing." The examples below come from the report, apparently drawn from company commentary on the quarter.

### Rare disease and oncology

- **Barry Genomics:** received NMPA approval (China's drug and device regulator) for the Sequel II sequencer for thalassemia testing, with possible menu expansion to fragile X, SMA (spinal muscular atrophy) and DMD (Duchenne muscular dystrophy).
- **Children's Mercy:** implemented HiFi as a first-line diagnostic to improve yields and turnaround.
- **Radboud UMC:** consolidated several standard-of-care assays onto one HiFi test.
- **Stanford Medicine Group:** uses Revio (Pacbio's high-throughput sequencer) to personalize prescriptions, identify adverse reactions and improve adherence.
- **Ambry Genetics:** uses HiFi to validate improved diagnostic yields.
- **HiFi Solves Consortium:** now has more than 15 members and enables federated data sharing (analysis across institutions without pooling raw data).

### Carrier screening

Customers are validating HiFi-based carrier assays alongside short-read genome tests, aiming to replace legacy methods. Market launches are anticipated in 2026.

### Population sequencing

- HiFi is being used in the **All of Us** program (first data published in October 2025).
- It is also used in the **Long Life Family Study** (about 8,000 samples) and the **Asian Pan-Genome Consortium** (more than 10,000 samples, building a comprehensive pan-genome reference).
- Additional projects in the pipeline are now planning for **hundreds of thousands of samples**.

### Guide notes (other 2026 commentary)

- **Revio placements:** flat to slightly up.
- **Revio pull-through:** $225k-$250k. Pull-through is the consumables revenue generated per installed instrument per year.
- **Vega placements:** to grow year over year.
- **Vega pull-through:** $25k-$40k.
- **Cash burn:** to "slightly tick up."
- **Operating expenses:** below $230M.
- **Q1 2026 expectations:** revenue growth up year over year, but below Q4 2025 levels (the report says "below 4Q26," apparently meaning Q4 2025); gross margin at the low end of the range, about 41%.
