## Bottom line (UBS thesis)

UBS's Neutral rating balances two things [UBS, 2026-02-13]:

1. An appreciation for the "unique capabilities" of PacBio's technologies.
2. The reality that building support for those features is hard "in the face of persistently unfavorable relative cost and throughput differentials vs. Illumina." UBS says this "will remain a challenge." Throughput means how much DNA data a machine produces per run.

UBS says the Q4 results and 2026 guidance were "incrementally positive but do not change our view." It gives three reasons:
- Instrument sales were partly driven by Q3 procurement delays.
- Consumables sales were above both Wall Street ("Street") consensus and UBS's own expectations.
- The 2026 guidance "seems conservative but faces an unfavorable academic/government (Aca/gov.) end market." Separately, the near-term financial impact of SPRQ-Nx chemistry and multi-use cells "remains uncertain."

## Q4 2025 results

### Headline sales

PacBio's Q4 2025 sales were about $44.6M, roughly in line with expectations. In January the company had already pre-announced a beat, so the final number held few surprises. The mix was:

- **Instruments:** about $17.3M. The note says this was driven partly by Vega shipments in Europe that had been delayed from Q3. (Vega is PacBio's lower-cost benchtop long-read sequencer.)
- **Consumables:** a record quarter at about $21.6M. UBS says this was driven by higher utilization from hospital and clinical customers, a cohort whose sales grew 55% in full-year 2025. Consumables are the reagents and SMRT cells customers buy for each run.

The remainder of the $44.6M (about $5.7M by simple subtraction, which the note does not itemize) would be other revenue such as service. This is an arithmetic inference, not a figure stated in the note.

### Vega (benchtop system)

- PacBio placed 42 Vega systems in Q4, bringing cumulative shipments to 147 systems.
- UBS says strength in Vega was partly due to the shipment delays from Q3, and that management also noted momentum in the placement pipeline.
- Vega average selling prices (ASPs) stayed roughly flat in Q4.
- About 65% of 2025 placements went to new customers.

### Revio (high-throughput system) and pull-through

Pull-through means the consumables revenue each installed instrument generates per year.

- Annualized Revio pull-through per system was about $240k in Q4, up sequentially from about $236k in Q3.
- Full-year 2026 Revio pull-through was cited as about $242k, which UBS says was slightly below 2025. This appears to be a reference to the full-year figure for the period just ended, since the sentence sits within the Q4 recap.
- Revio ASP was about $482k, roughly flat versus Q3.
- Several new Revio instruments were placed in key accounts at a lower ASP in Q4. UBS notes this is the same dynamic seen in Q3. The belief is that those customer accounts "will generate above-average consumable pull-through." In other words, PacBio discounted the instrument to win accounts that should buy more consumables later.

### Customer mix and geography

- Management continues to highlight growing momentum in clinical applications, particularly in EMEA (Europe, Middle East and Africa).
- Management is de-emphasizing the academic/government channel because of funding uncertainty headwinds.

## SPRQ-Nx chemistry and multi-use SMRT cells

- SPRQ-Nx chemistry (PacBio's next sequencing chemistry, a new reagent set) is moving from beta access (about 4-5 users) to early access (about 15 users).
- It will expand into EMEA "in several weeks" and ultimately APAC (Asia-Pacific), with a full launch later in 2026.
- UBS says SPRQ-Nx is anticipated to be accretive to sales growth and to improve incremental margins (the profit earned on each extra dollar of sales).
- UBS flags "uncertainty remains on its capacity to meet the volume required for sales growth and overall influence on PacBio's overall financials." This is the basis for its statement that the near-term financial impact of SPRQ-Nx and multi-use SMRT cells is uncertain. (SMRT cells are the consumable chips on which sequencing happens. The multi-use version is a new design that can be used more than once.)

## 2026 guidance

PacBio guided 2026 sales of $165M-$180M and a gross margin of 41-44% [company guidance, as reported by UBS]. For context, UBS's table shows 2025 revenue of about $160.0M and a gross margin of 40.1%. The guidance:

- Incorporates uncertainty in U.S. academic/government demand and a ramp in Revio utilization.
- For Q1 2026, management expects a normal seasonal downturn from Q4 2025, but sales still above Q1 2025. Seasonality is more pronounced in instruments than in consumables.
- Growth acceleration in 2026 is expected to come from clinical adoption of HiFi (PacBio's high-accuracy long-read data) and continued consumables strength.
- Management expects more Vega and Revio shipments year over year, with Revio pull-through in a range similar to 2025 at about $225k-$250k.
- SPRQ-Nx chemistry and multi-use SMRT cells are expected to drive some of the clinical growth and, ultimately, margins.

UBS's view: the guide looks conservative, but the academic/government end market is unfavorable.

## Valuation and estimate changes

- UBS updated its FY26 adjusted EPS estimate to $(0.46) from $(0.50). The note gives no explicit reason beyond the post-results update. The loss per share is a bit smaller than before.
- The price target stays at $1.50. It is based on a next-twelve-months (NTM) EV/Sales multiple (enterprise value divided by sales) of about 5x in one year.
- UBS's rating history for PacBio: Neutral since November 2024 (target $2.00), with a $1.50 Neutral target recorded on 2025-02-13 (share price $1.48). Earlier it was Buy from November 2023 to March 2024, with targets of $10.00-$12.50, then no rating on 2024-03-28 (price $3.75). Before that, Neutral in 2023 with targets of $12-$13.

## Risks (UBS)

- **Tech risk:** failure to bring new products or features to market on time at promised specifications.
- **Commercial risk:** inability to manufacture new products so they perform consistently as promised in the field with minimal downtime.
- **Economic risk:** a slowdown in global GDP growth, including escalating trade tensions.
- **China and emerging markets risk:** worsening economic conditions or tariffs.
- **Industry risk:** reduced pharma spending (due to big M&A, less funding for small companies, or trial failures hitting drug classes), or pressure on academic/government budgets from recession cost-cutting or political change.
- **Competition:** shifts in competitive dynamics, including disruptive new entrants.
- **Balance sheet:** growth plans fail and the company makes no progress toward profitability, creating financing pressure.
- **FX:** currency swings or tax policy changes.

## Company description (as given by UBS)

PacBio designs and manufactures DNA sequencing solutions for scientists and clinical researchers working on genetically complex problems. It was founded in 2000 and is headquartered in Menlo Park, CA. It reports three segments: instruments, consumables and services. The description in the note is dated: it mentions the Sequel II and IIe and "planned launches" of Revio and Onso in the first half of 2023, which UBS did not update.
