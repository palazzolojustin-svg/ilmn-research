**Summary.** Barclays keeps its Underweight (expected to underperform) rating on Pacbio but raises its price target 50% to $1.50 from $1.00. # Barclays on Pacbio, 8 May 2026: "Why Go to the Top When the Party is at Basecamp"

## Rating, price target and headline view

Barclays (analysts Luke Sergott, Salem Salem and Anna Kruszenski) published this note on 8 May 2026, after Pacbio reported first-quarter 2026 results. Pacbio (PACB) is a maker of long-read DNA sequencing instruments, meaning machines that read much longer continuous stretches of DNA than the short-read machines from competitors such as Illumina.

- **Rating:** Underweight, unchanged. Barclays' rating is relative: Underweight means the stock is expected to underperform the average expected return of the analysts' coverage universe over 12 months.
- **Industry view:** U.S. Life Science & Diagnostic Tools, Positive, unchanged.
- **Price target:** $1.50, raised 50% from $1.00.
- **Share price:** $1.65 at the 7 May 2026 close, so the target implies about 9.1% downside.
- **52-week range:** $0.85 to $2.73.
- **Market capitalization:** about $512M, on 310.49M shares outstanding. Free float is 97.1%. Average daily volume over 52 weeks is 7.9M shares.
- **Other Bloomberg data:** return on equity over the trailing twelve months is -213.45%, and book value per share is $0.02.

**Headline thesis [Barclays, 2026-05-08]:**
- The Basecamp deal is "an impressive opportunity that could deliver material upside to next year."
- Outside of the deal, the quarter was "unsurprisingly difficult." Pacbio is exposed to U.S. academic and government (A&G) funding, and the market froze ahead of the SPRQ-Nx launch in May.
- Barclays says it "clearly underappreciated" the mid-March press release about the Basecamp deal.
- Basecamp is "more of a '27 driver that we do not have a lot of visibility on right now."
- Given the tough macro backdrop, Barclays remains Underweight and raises the target "on the AI foundational biology model opportunities."
- The note is cautious about the new market: "it is still very early in this new market development and we look for more proof points before diving in both feet."

## The Basecamp Research deal

### What the initiative is
Barclays describes the deal as follows.
- Basecamp Research is private and not covered by Barclays. It leads a scientific initiative called the **Trillion Gene Atlas**.
- The goal is to scale the world's largest biological dataset for AI from 10 billion genes to 1 trillion genes.
- Partners named are Anthropic, Ultima Genomics (a sequencing competitor) and Pacbio. The project is powered by NVIDIA AI infrastructure.
- Pacbio's piece is to perform "human-based depth" whole-genome sequencing (WGS) metagenomics on 100,000 samples collected from around the world. Metagenomics means sequencing the mixed DNA of an environmental sample, rather than one organism.
- Basecamp's stated rationale: foundational biology models (large AI models of biology) need to be trained on the most diverse dataset ever, so that they can predict disease, drug function "and everything else."
- The note's commentary: "The project thinks big. 1 trillion genes." It adds that, like the gap between a million seconds and a billion seconds, "at some point the numbers stop having any meaning."

### What it means for Pacbio
- Pacbio will begin sequencing internally. Barclays says it will get "human-style economics" on each sample through SPRQ-Nx (its new chemistry and flow cell, described in the quarter section), multi-use chips and other internally developed technologies.
- Barclays calls this "the biggest deal in the company's history."
- **Revenue estimate [Barclays estimate]:** about $10M-$20M over the next 18 months. The arithmetic is 100,000 samples at $100-$200 per sample.
- Barclays says this is not the only foundational biology model under development. It could see Pacbio partner with more such companies and capture more economics on a data- and model-sharing basis. Under that structure, Pacbio would help develop an AI company's model, and the model could then be used with Pacbio's software for downstream data analysis.
- One possible structure: the AI company gets a discount on sequencing in exchange for sharing downstream economics. Barclays says "the frontier is open."
- Barclays sees "plenty of upside potential here to change the face of the business," but stresses that it is early.

## First-quarter 2026 commentary

Barclays calls it "a tough one given the US A&G funding environment."

- **Placements:** Pacbio placed 27 Vega instruments (its lower-cost benchtop sequencer), which Barclays says reflects the funding environment. The note says Vega "expects" were somewhat disappointing, given the more attractive average selling price (ASP); the original text is partly garbled here.
- **Revio consumables:** the SPRQ-Nx launch in May "froze some of the market on Revio consumables" (Revio is Pacbio's high-throughput sequencer). This produced year-over-year declines in pull-through, meaning the consumables revenue per installed instrument.
- **Middle East:** the Middle East crisis cost $1.5M of hardware revenue because Pacbio could not ship into the region. On a roughly $40M quarterly revenue base, Barclays says "$1.5m moves the needle."
- **Bright spots:** strong demand in Europe and in clinical markets. Clinical revenue rose 100% year over year.
- **Outlook:** "It feels more like a waiting game" for demand to recover, with the SPRQ-Nx launch in May building momentum through the year.

**Guidance notes [Pacbio guidance, as summarized by Barclays]:**
- Revio pull-through is expected at $225K-$250K per instrument.
- Guidance assumes no meaningful recovery in academic and government funding, particularly in the U.S.
- Gross margin is likely to land at the lower end of the range because of rising compute costs.
- Vega ASPs are expected to return to normalized levels in Q2.
- Non-GAAP operating expenses are guided to $220M-$225M, versus less than $230M previously.

### Valuation basis for the price target
- The $1.50 target applies about 4x EV/revenue (enterprise value divided by sales) to Barclays' 2027 revenue estimate of $187M.
- The previous $1.00 target used 3x on $190M, so the multiple rose and the revenue estimate fell slightly.
- The stated reason for the higher target is the AI foundational biology model opportunity.

## Why Underweight

Barclays says that, while it likes "the underlying technology, revamped business & story, strong team, and innovative launches," the stock "has a tough setup NT" (near term). The reasons:
- It sees the potential for a demand pause on capital equipment purchases because of competitive launches across the sequencing space.
- It could see an "air pocket" on consumables in the first half of the year, as customers wind down research in anticipation of SPRQ-Nx.

## Scenarios and risks

- **Upside case ($3.00):** an improved macro backdrop that encourages investors to return to growth names, plus a healthy funding environment supportive of customer spending.
- **Downside case ($1.00):** macro headwinds persist longer than expected or worsen, or a guidance miss causes multiple contraction.
- **Upside risks to the Underweight view [Barclays]:**
  1. Macro conditions ease and investors return to growth names.
  2. The funding environment improves and customer spending accelerates.
  3. Demand and use cases for long-read sequencing ramp faster than expected, and scientists flock to Pacbio as the premier provider of long-read technology.
