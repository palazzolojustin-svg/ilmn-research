**Summary.** Barclays reviews Pacbio's Q2 2025 results (reported around 7 Aug 2025) and keeps its Equal Weight rating and $1.50 price target. The analysts call the quarter relatively strong given the macro backdrop: revenue beat, helped by Asia-Pacific and Europe/Middle East/Africa strength and strong demand for the low-cost Vega sequencer, and diagnostics/translational interest is rising. # Barclays on Pacbio (PACB), 7 August 2025: Q2 2025 review

## Rating, price target and headline view

**Rating and target.** Barclays (Luke Sergott, with Salem Salem and Anna Kruszenski) kept Pacbio at **Equal Weight** (a relative rating meaning the stock is expected to perform in line with the other companies the analyst covers over 12 months). The industry view on U.S. Life Science & Diagnostic Tools stayed **Neutral**. The **price target stayed at $1.50** [Barclays, 2025-08-07].

**Share price context.** The stock closed at $1.26 on 7 Aug 2025, so the target implies about **19.0% upside**. Other market data in the report:
- Market capitalization: $378 million.
- Shares outstanding: 300.08 million.
- Free float: 97.36%.
- 52-week range: $0.85 to $2.72.
- 52-week average daily volume: 9.5 million shares.
- Trailing-twelve-month return on equity: -177.29%.
- Book value per share: $0.30.
- Dividend: none.

**Headline takeaway.** The report's summary line is: "Trends consistent with rest of peers on US A&G side. Strength in APAC/EMEA drove the beat. Strong demand for Vega highlights the interest in HiFi. Dx and translational demand starting to pick up. So overall, a good Q with all things considered on the macro volatility." (A&G means academic and government customers; APAC is Asia-Pacific; EMEA is Europe, Middle East and Africa; Vega is Pacbio's lower-priced benchtop sequencer; HiFi is Pacbio's high-accuracy long-read sequencing; Dx means diagnostics.) Barclays says it came away "incrementally more positive on PACB's quarter with all things considered."

## The thesis in the analysts' words and reasoning

**1. US academic and government demand is soft, as expected.** Barclays says "We all knew that US A&G demand for high end genomics instrumentation is soft and that played out accordingly." It describes the trend as consistent with the rest of its peer group.

**2. Vega offset some of that weakness.** Barclays reads Vega demand as showing interest in HiFi sequencing from this same customer base "just at a more approachable price point." The Q2 variance table shows 38 Vega placements against Barclays' estimate of 24.

**3. Ex-US demand was strong.** APAC and EMEA were up 45% year over year. Barclays says this strength drove the revenue beat.

**4. Translational and diagnostic applications are gaining traction.** Barclays calls this "the most notable update on the print." (Translational means research aimed at clinical use.)
- In its diagnostics lab checks and a recent whole-genome sequencing (WGS) non-deal roadshow (NDR, an investor marketing meeting), the analysts heard more about long-read adoption for carrier screening (testing healthy people for gene variants they could pass to children) and other targeted panel applications. One example given was "Ataxi w/ DGX" (Quest Diagnostics).
- Barclays thinks this is partly a result of the many large-scale long-read sequencing projects completed over the past few years, and it sees more of these projects continuing to ramp. Management highlighted a large global ALS study.

**5. The full diagnostics opportunity depends on cost.** Barclays sees the current progress as "a good start," but says "the true Dx opportunity unlocks when PACB gets the cost of sequencing down close to ILMN's $200." At those prices, "the economics work for larger scale vols of HiFi + SBS for a Dx test." (SBS is sequencing by synthesis, Illumina's chemistry, which Pacbio has been developing as a complement; the note does not expand on it.)
- The analysts say the cost issue "is not a biochemistry issue, it is an engineering problem around the chip and the supporting system, which the team has been working on for 2-3 years."
- They add: "We are not discounting the challenge, we are just noting that engineering issues tend to be more easily solved than chemistry issues."

**Conclusion.** Barclays calls it "a relatively strong quarter with the current demand environment" and says it is incrementally more positive on diagnostic progress and Vega demand.

## Why Equal Weight

Barclays says it remains "on the sidelines." It likes "the underlying technology, revamped business and story, strong management team, and innovative launches," but the stock "is trading at a 20% premium to peers." It also says that, given a volatile macro backdrop, "we could see multiple contraction." (The source text is partly garbled here, but this is the clear meaning.)

## Valuation and scenarios

- **Method:** The $1.50 target applies a 4x multiple of enterprise value to revenue (EV/revenue, a measure of the company's total value relative to its sales) to Barclays' 2026 revenue estimate of **$173 million**. The prior target also used about 4x, on **$165 million** of 2026 revenue, so the target is unchanged while the revenue estimate rose.
- **Upside case: $3.00.** This assumes an improved macro backdrop that encourages investors to return to growth names and a healthy funding environment that supports customer spending.
- **Downside case: $1.00.** This assumes macro headwinds persist longer than expected or worsen, or the company misses guidance, causing multiple contraction.

**Risks listed by Barclays.**
- Upside risks: (1) macro conditions ease and investors return to growth names; (2) the funding environment improves and customer spending accelerates; (3) demand and use cases for long-read sequencing ramp faster than expected and scientists flock to Pacbio as the premier long-read provider.
- Downside risks: (1) macro headwinds last longer or worsen, causing investors to shy away from high-multiple names; (2) customers tighten budgets further and demand weakens.

**Rating history (price target changes).** The target was $14.00 on 10 May 2023 (initiation shown as Equal Weight, price $12.03), then $9.00 on 28 Sep 2023 (price $8.36), $8.00 on 31 Oct 2023 ($7.11), $3.00 on 9 Apr 2024 ($3.45), $2.00 on 9 May 2024 ($1.68), and $1.50 on 24 Jun 2025 ($1.27). The rating stayed Equal Weight throughout.

## Things to note on interpretation

- The thesis that cost is an "engineering, not chemistry" problem is Barclays' opinion; the note does not cite a management statement for it.
- The channel checks (diagnostics lab checks and a WGS NDR) are described qualitatively, with no survey figures.
- The "20% premium to peers" is Barclays' own valuation statement and its main reason for not being more positive.
