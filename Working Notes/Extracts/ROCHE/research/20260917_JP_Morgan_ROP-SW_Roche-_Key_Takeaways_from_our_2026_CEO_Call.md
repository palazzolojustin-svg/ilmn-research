## Pharma outlook (the pipeline and growth)

- **19 new molecular entities (NMEs, meaning brand-new drug candidates rather than reformulations of existing drugs) with launch potential by 2030.** Schinecker reiterated that Roche's pipeline has 19 NMEs expected to read out Phase III trials (the large final-stage trials used to seek approval) through 2030. He said this should support Pharma growth through the end of the decade and well into the 2030s.
- **Margin guidance reiterated:** Roche's guidance is for "at least stable" Pharma margins.
- **No near-term patent cliff (the sudden sales loss when a drug's patent protection ends).** Schinecker said Roche does not face one soon and that he does not see a scenario in which the on-market Pharma portfolio would decline. He expects the marketed base business to sustain growth, with the pipeline adding incremental growth on top.
- **Business development:** Roche has "significant firepower" for M&A. It will keep selectively evaluating late-stage opportunities and will also invest in earlier-stage assets, including Phase I and preclinical programs.

## Diagnostics (including sequencing)

This section is the most relevant for Illumina and sequencing competition.

- **Margin target:** Schinecker said the Diagnostics business is expected to return to a margin of **"at least 20%"** over time. Margins have been hurt by significant price cuts in China over the past two years, which followed China's healthcare pricing reform. He remains confident despite that pressure.
- **Growth target:** He expressed confidence in delivering **mid to high single-digit top-line growth**, from the combination of the base business and new launches.
- **New launches each seen as 1bn+ dollar opportunities:** He highlighted recent diagnostics launches in three areas, each described as a potential opportunity worth more than one billion dollars:
  - next-generation sequencing (reading DNA at scale), via the **Axelios 1** platform;
  - mass spectrometry (measuring molecules by their mass, used for testing);
  - continuous glucose monitoring (CGM, wearable sensors that track blood sugar continuously).

### Axelios 1 sequencer: early placements

- Axelios 1 was **launched in June 2026**.
- Roche had guided to about **100 instrument placements (instruments installed at customers) in the first year**. Roughly **one-third** have already been placed.
- Placements to date have been with **external customers only** and with **no "teaser offers"** (that is, no promotional or discounted come-on deals, as reported by J.P. Morgan).
- Schinecker cited **positive early customer feedback**.
- He expects **reagent revenue** (the recurring consumable kits needed to run the instrument) to build over time as customers **migrate existing assays** (tests) onto the new sequencing platform.

## Key drug programs

### Giredestrant (breast cancer)

- Roche expects to **launch giredestrant this year**. The **PDUFA date** (the US FDA's target decision date) is **30 November 2026**, following use of a **priority review voucher** (a voucher that speeds FDA review).
- Most revenue is expected from **lidERA** (the trial in adjuvant HR+/HER2- breast cancer, meaning treatment after surgery in hormone-receptor-positive, HER2-negative disease). A smaller contribution is expected from **evERA** (patients with ESR1-mutant disease after a CDK4/6 inhibitor, in 1st/2nd-line-plus metastatic breast cancer).
- Schinecker said lidERA already addresses about **50% of early breast cancer patients**.
- Roche plans to broaden the opportunity with clinical trials of giredestrant in combination with CDK4/6 inhibitors, and in patients completing initial CDK4/6 plus aromatase inhibitor adjuvant treatment.
- Ahead of those trials, he sees potential use in lower-risk patients and in patients unable to tolerate CDK4/6 inhibitor side effects, who may switch.

### Remibrutinib (Rhapsido) vs. fenebrutinib in multiple sclerosis

- Remibrutinib (brand name Rhapsido, a Novartis drug, per the document's context of comparison) reported positive headline results from the **REMODEL-1 and REMODEL-2** trials in **relapsing multiple sclerosis (RMS)**.
- Schinecker said the **full dataset at MSToronto2026 (21–23 October 2026)** is needed to assess its profile against Roche's **fenebrutinib**.
- He stressed that **fenebrutinib has set a high efficacy bar**.
- He pointed out that current consensus (the average analyst forecast) for fenebrutinib appears to reflect **only the primary progressive MS (PPMS) opportunity**. If fenebrutinib's RMS data compare favorably with remibrutinib's, there could be **upside to consensus forecasts**.
- On labeling: because BTK inhibitors (the drug class) are known to affect liver enzymes, he expects **liver monitoring** to likely be included in the label.

### Ocrevus (MS)

- Schinecker reiterated existing guidance for **high-single-digit (HSD) Ocrevus sales growth in 2026**.
- He is confident in continued uptake of **Ocrevus Zunovo** (a subcutaneous version).
- He noted an upcoming launch of a **high-concentration formulation paired with an on-body injector**, which could support growth and protect the franchise's durability.
- On competition from **Briumvi (TG Therapeutics)**, he said Ocrevus continues to offer **higher efficacy**, even if Briumvi may be priced lower.

### Obesity

- Roche still sees the obesity market as worth **more than $100bn**, and is building a portfolio aimed at distinct patient segments.
- **Existing exposure:** orforglipron (Foundayo) is a **Chugai-originated molecule licensed to Eli Lilly**; the associated royalties are recorded in Other Revenues.
- **CT-388 (enicepatide):** Schinecker emphasized its efficacy. **Week-48 weight loss appears broadly comparable with the current leader (tirzepatide/Zepbound) at week 72**, and higher doses might show even more weight loss. He suggested this could potentially place it in a "triple-G" efficacy range (a reference to triple-agonist-class efficacy).
- **Combination with petrelintide:** the rationale is to pair the incretin's (CT-388's) competitive efficacy with the amylin's (petrelintide's) near placebo-like tolerability.
- **Hanmi Pharma deal:** Roche recently licensed a **urocortin-2 analog** from Hanmi, a potential differentiator through better **muscle preservation**.
- CT-388 could also be a combination backbone in other indications, for example **MASH** (a liver disease), where it could be combined with Roche's FGF-21 drug **pegozafermin**.

### Divarasib (KRAS G12C inhibitor, lung cancer)

- Schinecker reiterated the strength of divarasib data in **2L+ NSCLC** (second-line-or-later non-small cell lung cancer).
- This underpins guidance of **$1–2bn in peak sales**, driven primarily by the metastatic setting, with possible upside from expansion into earlier lines.
- BE flagged key upcoming trials:
  - **KRASCENDO-2** (Phase III): divarasib plus pembrolizumab (Keytruda) in 1st-line NSCLC; primary completion **November 2028**.
  - **KRASCENDO-3** (Phase III): adjuvant study in resected stage II–III NSCLC; primary completion **January 2032**.

### Afimkibart (anti-TL1A, ulcerative colitis)

- Phase III studies **AMETRINE-1 and AMETRINE-2** in ulcerative colitis (UC) have primary completion in **January 2027**, so data are expected in 2027.
- Schinecker said that across multiple molecules, Phase II data have been **strongest in UC**, so if Phase III efficacy replicates Phase II, the likelihood of success "should be high".
- He said afimkibart data appear **broadly similar to Merck's tulisokibart** and expects both to be the **first entrants** to market. (J.P. Morgan notes that Merck is covered by US analyst Christ Schott.)
- He also pointed to Roche's **bispecific and multispecific** pipeline, including the disclosed **p40 × TL1A bispecific**, now in the Phase II **SUNCREST** trial with primary completion **August 2027**.
