# Roche (ROP) Consensus Quarterly Financials: Q3 2025 – Q4 2027 Estimates

*Source file: `Working Notes/Sources/ROCHE/consensus/ROCHE_Consensus_Quarterly.md` · Date: 2026-10-10 · Bloomberg Professional Service, Consensus Estimates · consensus estimates*

**Summary.** Bloomberg consensus estimates for Roche's quarterly revenues and product-level sales from Q3 2025 (actual) through Q4 2027 (estimate), in Swiss francs. The sheet covers pharmaceuticals (ca. 12 billion CHF per quarter), diagnostics and devices (ca. 3.5 billion CHF per quarter), individual product performance with geographic breakdowns, constant-currency growth rates, and selected balance-sheet and cash-flow items. Key themes: established pharma franchises (Ocrevus, Hemlibra, Vabysmo, Tecentriq) driving overall revenue, significant declines forecast for aging blockbusters (Actemra, Herceptin, Avastin), and a diagnostic-device business growing modestly.

## Overview

This is a Bloomberg consensus sheet of quarterly financial estimates for Roche Holding AG (ticker ROP SW Equity), denominated in Swiss francs (CHF). Data covers actual results for Q3 2025 through Q2 2026, with consensus estimates for Q3 2026 through Q4 2027. The sheet includes consolidated revenue, business-unit breakdown (pharmaceuticals and diagnostics & devices), product-level sales by geography (United States, Europe, Japan, International), constant-currency growth rates, and selective balance-sheet and cash-flow snapshots. No company guidance, management commentary, or analyst reasoning is provided—only consensus numbers.

## Key Trends and Observations

1. **Portfolio transition**: Consensus expects a sharp shift from older, declining franchises (Actemra –37%, Herceptin –24%, Avastin –29%, Perjeta –34%) to newer growth drivers (Vabysmo +42%, Gazyva +51%, Phesgo +46%, Polivy +41%, and pipeline ramps).

2. **HER2 oncology consolidation**: Phesgo (fixed-dose combination) is expected to cannibalize Herceptin, Perjeta, and Kadcyla, which collectively decline sharply. This reflects industry trend toward convenient combination therapies.

3. **Immunology challenged**: Actemra faces particularly steep declines (–15% to –20% CAGR 2026–2027), likely driven by loss of exclusivity and/or therapeutic competition. Xolair volatility and Pulmozyme decay are also notable.

4. **Tecentriq sustained growth**: Anti-PD-L1 checkpoint inhibitor shows resilience with ~15% growth over 2.5 years, suggesting broad clinical adoption and expanding indications (data shows breakouts by cancer type: NSCLC, HCC, melanoma, TNBC, etc.).

5. **Ophthalmology strength**: Vabysmo is a star; its dual mechanism (VEGF + Ang-2 inhibition) and convenient dosing are expected to drive market share gains from legacy anti-VEGFs (Lucentis near-exit, Avastin declining).

6. **Diagnostics modest growth**: D&D division growing ~4–6% annually (constant currency), less dynamic than pharma. Core Lab is largest but slower-growing; Molecular Lab outpacing others.

7. **Seasonality and volatility**: Certain products (Xofluza, Tamiflu, Xolair) show large Q-to-Q swings, likely reflecting influenza season, reimbursement timing, or clinical event-driven patterns.

8. **Early-stage pipeline inclusion**: Consensus includes Q4 2027 estimates for late-stage pipeline products (Giredestrant 400.5, Fenebrutinib 42.5, RG6641/6640/6652 at 196–240 CHF m each), suggesting confidence in near-term launches; however, these are highly speculative and subject to regulatory and uptake uncertainty.
