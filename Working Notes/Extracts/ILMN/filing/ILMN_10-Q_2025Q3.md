# Illumina Q3 2025 10-Q—Revenue Flat YoY; Tariffs and China Restrictions Pressure Margins; SomaLogic Acquisition Pending

*Source file: `Working Notes/Sources/ILMN/filing/ILMN_10-Q_2025Q3.md` · Date: 2025-10-31 · SEC filing (Form 10-Q for quarter ended September 28, 2025) · SEC filing*

**Summary.** Illumina's Q3 2025 results show flat revenue yoy but a 3% year-to-date decline, with gross margin pressure from tariffs and China restrictions. The company spun off GRAIL in June 2024 and is now a single-segment business focused on sequencing and array-based genomics. Illumina is acquiring SomaLogic for $350M to enter the proteomics market. Operating leverage is improving despite China headwinds, which management expects will persist through 2025 and beyond with uncertain duration.

## Financial Position and Results

### Revenue

Consolidated revenue was **$1,084 million in Q3 2025**, essentially flat compared to $1,080 million in Q3 2024 (up less than 1%). Year-to-date (YTD), revenue declined to **$3,184 million** from $3,268 million, a **3% decrease**. 

The revenue composition shifted: product revenue (which includes consumables and instruments) was 85.5% of total in Q3 2025 versus 84.6% prior year, while service and other revenue declined to 14.5% from 15.4%.

**Revenue by Product (Core Illumina only, nine months ended):**

| | Q3 2025 | Q3 2024 | YTD 2025 | YTD 2024 | Change YTD |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Consumables** | $816M | $807M | $2,395M | $2,390M | +$5M (flat) |
| **Instruments** | $111M | $107M | $324M | $342M | -$18M (-5%) |
| **Services and other** | $157M | $166M | $465M | $550M | -$85M* |

*The service decline includes $55M from GRAIL's spin-off in June 2024.

**Consumables:** increased $9 million (+1%) in Q3 2025, driven by demand for high-throughput consumables as customers transition to the NovaSeq X platform. YTD consumables were essentially flat.

**Instruments:** increased $4 million (+4%) in Q3, primarily from MiSeq i100 Series shipments (which launched in Q4 2024). However, YTD instruments declined $18 million (-5%) due to "fewer shipments of our high- and mid-throughput instruments, as capital and cash flow constraints continue to impact our customer's purchasing behavior." This was partially offset by the new MiSeq i100 Series.

**Service and other revenue:** declined $9 million (-5%) in Q3 and $85 million YTD. Management attributed the decline primarily to decreased revenue from strategic partnerships. Excluding the GRAIL spin-off impact of $55 million, service revenue declined an additional $30 million YTD.

### Gross Profit and Margin

Consolidated gross profit was **$733 million in Q3 2025** (67.6% margin) compared to $745 million in Q3 2024 (68.9% margin). YTD gross profit was **$2,111 million (66.3% margin)** versus $2,133 million (65.3% margin) in YTD 2024.

Management noted the Q3 margin decline was "primarily due to higher tariff costs" and an increase in inventory reserves, partially offset by lower strategic partnership revenue (which carries lower margins). The YTD decline included a $23 million impairment of an intangible asset (recorded in Q2 2025 when the planned use of a finite-lived intangible asset changed). Excluding these items, core Illumina gross margin decreased "primarily due to higher costs related to tariffs."

**Key margin drivers cited by management:**
- Market pricing conditions
- Sales mix (consumables vs. instruments vs. services)
- Product mix (new vs. established products)
- Excess and obsolete inventory reserves
- Royalties
- Manufacturing cost structure relative to volume
- **Freight costs and tariffs** (emphasized as new headwinds)
- Product support obligations

## Operating Expenses

### Research and Development

Consolidated R&D expense was **$229 million in Q3 2025** (21.1% of revenue) compared to $253 million in Q3 2024 (23.4% of revenue), a decrease of $24 million or 9%. YTD R&D was **$728 million** (22.9% of revenue) versus $913 million (27.9%) in YTD 2024, a decrease of $185 million or 20%.

Management attributed the Q3 decrease to "decreases in employee-related compensation costs, including share-based compensation expense related to our optimization efforts, and a decrease in outside professional services," partially offset by increases in lab supply costs. The YTD decrease was primarily due to the GRAIL spin-off (GRAIL had R&D of $189 million in YTD 2024 through June).

### Selling, General and Administrative

Consolidated SG&A expense was **$277 million in Q3 2025** (25.5% of revenue) compared to $239 million in Q3 2024 (22.2%), an increase of $38 million or 16%. YTD SG&A was **$777 million** (24.4% of revenue) versus $813 million (24.8%) in YTD 2024, a decrease of $36 million or 4%.

The Q3 increase was primarily driven by an unfavorable $52 million impact from remeasurement of contingent consideration liabilities related to GRAIL Contingent Value Rights (CVRs), which resulted in a loss of $3 million in Q3 2025 versus a gain of $49 million in Q3 2024. Excluding this impact, SG&A would have decreased, reflecting lower employee compensation (including share-based compensation optimization) and lower acquisition-related costs.

The YTD improvement included a lower net gain on contingent consideration liabilities of $275 million (gains of $29 million in YTD 2025 versus $304 million in YTD 2024), lower acquisition costs (including $53 million of spin-off costs in YTD 2024), and decreased employee compensation, partially offset by the absence of the $474 million legal contingency gain recognized in YTD 2024 (reversal of European Commission fine related to GRAIL acquisition).

### Income from Operations

**Q3 2025:** Operating income was **$227 million** (20.9% margin) versus $741 million (68.6%) in Q3 2024, a decline of $514 million. 

**YTD 2025:** Operating income was **$606 million** (19.0% margin) versus a loss of **$1,008 million** (-30.8%) in YTD 2024, an improvement of $1,614 million. 

The YTD improvement was primarily driven by the absence of $1,886 million in goodwill and intangible impairment charges recorded in YTD 2024 related to GRAIL, and the $474 million legal contingency gain from the EC fine reversal. Excluding these items, management noted that "Core Illumina operating expense decreased in YTD 2025, primarily due to a decrease in acquisition-related costs."

## Profitability and Tax

### Net Income

**Q3 2025:** Net income was **$150 million** ($0.98 per diluted share) compared to $705 million ($4.42 per diluted share) in Q3 2024.

**YTD 2025:** Net income was **$516 million** ($3.29 per diluted share) compared to a loss of **$1,410 million** (-$8.86 per diluted share) in YTD 2024.

### Effective Tax Rate

The effective tax rate was **31.8% in Q3 2025** and **27.2% YTD** compared to 2.1% and -3.2% in the prior-year periods, respectively.

The higher rates resulted from U.S. tax legislation signed on July 4, 2025, which eliminated the requirement to capitalize U.S.-based research and development expenses. While this was favorable, management recorded a **$42 million valuation allowance in Q3 2025** against deferred tax assets related to certain U.S. foreign tax credits "that were generated in prior years that were not previously utilized," as it became "more likely than not" these credits will not be realized. The effective tax rate benefited from earnings in lower-tax jurisdictions such as Singapore.

## Strategic Items

### GRAIL Spin-Off

On June 24, 2024, Illumina completed the spin-off of GRAIL, distributing approximately 85.5% of GRAIL's outstanding shares to Illumina shareholders (1 GRAIL share for every 6 Illumina shares held on the record date). Illumina retained approximately 14.5% of GRAIL's shares, which are now reflected in short-term investments at fair value of $228 million as of September 28, 2025 (versus $93 million at year-end 2024).

**GRAIL Contingent Value Rights (CVRs):** In connection with the original GRAIL acquisition, Illumina issued CVRs that entitle holders to future cash payments representing a portion of GRAIL-related revenues for 12 years through August 2033:
- **2.5% payment right** on the first $1 billion of annual GRAIL revenue
- **9% payment right** on revenue above $1 billion

Fair value of the GRAIL CVR liability was **$43 million** as of September 28, 2025 (down from $73 million at year-end 2024). In YTD 2025, the company paid $994 thousand in Covered Revenue Payments against Covered Revenues of $106 million for Q4 2024 through Q2 2025, driven primarily by sales of GRAIL's Galleri cancer detection test.

Management uses a Monte Carlo simulation to estimate CVR fair value, with inputs including forecasted GRAIL revenues (derived from public filings and analyst reports), a revenue risk premium (3% as of Sept 28, 2025), and revenue volatility. Management noted sensitivity: a 20% increase or decrease in forecasted revenues would result in a +$13 million or -$11 million change in liability, respectively.

### SomaLogic Acquisition

On June 22, 2025, Illumina entered into a Stock Purchase Agreement with Standard BioTools to acquire **SomaLogic and other specified assets for $350 million in cash**, subject to customary adjustments. The deal also provides for:
- **Royalty streams** on specified products and services
- **Up to $75 million in potential milestone payments** tied to revenue generation

**Transaction timing and terms:**
- Expected close: First half of 2026 (subject to regulatory approvals)
- Termination rights: If closing does not occur by March 23, 2026, subject to three automatic three-month extensions for regulatory approvals
- Termination fee: **$14.5 million** if terminated due to failure to obtain regulatory approvals

Management stated: "We believe our acquisition of SomaLogic will enhance our presence in the expanding proteomics market and advance our multiomics strategy."

**Antillary agreements contemplated:**
- Transition services agreement (Standard BioTools to provide services post-closing)
- License agreement (Illumina to grant non-transferable, non-sublicensable IP license to Standard BioTools)

## Regulatory and Legal Matters

### China Restrictions

In Q1 2025, Illumina was **added to the "unreliable entities list" by Chinese regulatory authorities** and received notice that it is **not permitted to export sequencing instruments into China**. 

Management cited this as a significant headwind: "In Q1 2025, we were added to the unreliable entities list by regulatory authorities in China and received notice that we are not permitted to export sequencing instruments into China." Total revenue impact in Q3 was $23 million and YTD was $41 million.

Management expects "these factors to continue to impact our sales and results of operations for the remainder of 2025 and beyond, the size and duration of which is significantly uncertain." The company attributes this to efforts to "help mitigate the expected impact of a reduction in revenue and related operating income from our Greater China business."

### Tariffs

Beginning in April 2025, the U.S. government and several countries imposed tariffs. Management noted: "the largest cost impact to us relates to importation from our manufacturing facility in Singapore. The remainder is a mix of importation of parts and subassemblies to our manufacturing operations in the United States and importation into China."

**Mitigation efforts:** The company has "taken several actions to fully mitigate the impact of these tariffs," expecting to "partially mitigate the impact in 2025, through supply chain optimization, cost measures, and pricing actions" and aims to "more fully mitigate the impact in 2026."

Management estimated tariff cost impact: "$350 million in cash estimate of the cost impact on Illumina of the tariffs announced by the U.S. Government and other countries beginning in April 2025" (from forward-looking statements section).

### Legal Proceedings

#### Shareholder Derivative Actions

Four consolidated shareholder derivative lawsuits allege that certain directors and officers breached fiduciary duties by "knowingly causing Illumina to unlawfully close the GRAIL acquisition" and concealing material facts:

1. **_Icahn Partners LP v. deSouza, et al._** (filed Oct 17, 2023)
2. **_City of Omaha Police and Firefighters Retirement System v. deSouza, et al._** (filed Feb 26, 2024)
3. **_City of Roseville General Employees Retirement System v. deSouza, et al._** (filed Apr 16, 2024)
4. **_The Pavers and Road Builders Benefit Funds v. deSouza, et al._** (filed Dec 23, 2024)

**Status:** On March 27, 2025, the parties filed a stipulation to consolidate actions 2-4. The Icahn Partners action reached a **settlement in principle on April 11, 2025**, with a release of claims and no payment by any party. The consolidated case now focuses on the Pavers action, with defendants' motion to dismiss due February 13, 2026.

Management stated: "In light of the fact that these lawsuits are in an early stage, we cannot predict the ultimate outcome of the suits. We deny the allegations in the complaints and intend to vigorously defend the litigations."

#### Federal Securities Class Actions

Three securities class actions consolidated into **_In re Illumina, Inc. Securities Litigation No. 23-cv-2082_** allege that Illumina and GRAIL made "materially false and misleading statements and omitted material facts" related to the GRAIL acquisition. Lead plaintiffs were appointed in April 2024.

**Status:** On September 26, 2025, the Court granted defendants' motion to dismiss for failure to state a claim but granted leave to file an amended complaint. Plaintiffs filed a Third Amended Complaint on October 27, 2025; company response due December 11, 2025.

#### State Securities Class Actions

Two state court actions in California alleged similar claims related to the November 2020 and February 2021 GRAIL acquisition registration statements. Consolidated complaint filed August 12, 2024.

**Status:** On September 3, 2025, the Court overruled Illumina's demurrer. On September 16, 2025, Illumina filed its answer denying allegations. On October 28, 2025, Illumina filed a writ with the California Court of Appeal seeking interlocutory appellate review.

Management stated: "In light of the fact that the lawsuits are in an early stage, we cannot predict the ultimate outcome of the suits. We deny the allegations in the complaints and intend to vigorously defend the litigation."

### Litigation Demands

On May 1, 2024, shareholder Michael Warner sent a litigation demand requesting the Board pursue claims against officers and directors involved with the GRAIL acquisition. On July 30, 2024, the Board deferred a final decision "given, among other things, the pending stockholder lawsuits" and stated it would "monitor the stockholder lawsuits and revisit the demand as warranted."

A second demand from shareholder Jane Davidson was sent August 21, 2024, making similar requests. On October 29, 2024, the Board again deferred a final decision for the same reasons.

## Risk Factors and Outlook

### Macroeconomic and Market Headwinds

Management identified several pressures: "Macroeconomic factors such as tariffs, inflation, exchange rate fluctuations and concerns about an economic downturn, competitive challenges in our China region, the sanctions imposed on Russia as a result of the armed conflict between Russia and Ukraine, and reductions in the U.S. government's funding of the NIH have impacted both Illumina directly and our customers' behavior."

Management noted: "Some customers, specifically research customers, continue to manage inventory and capital more conservatively and some labs are delaying projects due to funding concerns." The company "expects these factors to continue to impact our sales and results of operations for the remainder of 2025 and beyond, the size and duration of which is significantly uncertain."

### Cost Reduction Initiatives

In Q1 2025, Illumina implemented an incremental **$100 million cost reduction program for 2025**, including:
- Optimizing stock-based compensation and non-labor spending
- Accelerating certain productivity measures
- Workforce reductions

This was to "help mitigate the expected impact of a reduction in revenue and related operating income from our Greater China business and the uncertainty in the U.S. government's funding of the NIH."

Cumulative restructuring charges since inception totaled $255 million, with YTD 2025 charges of $41 million ($23 million in SG&A, $15 million in R&D, remainder in cost of revenue).

### NIH Funding Risk

Forward-looking statements cited as a risk: "any reductions or potential reductions in funding for the National Institutes of Health (NIH), or targeted cancellations by the U.S. federal government of certain grants or contracts, could negatively impact our customers and reduce demand for our products and services."
