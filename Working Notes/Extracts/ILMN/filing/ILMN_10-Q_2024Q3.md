# Illumina Q3 2024 10-Q: GRAIL spin-off, EC fine reversal, goodwill impairments, strengthening core margins

*Source file: `Working Notes/Sources/ILMN/filing/ILMN_10-Q_2024Q3.md` · Date: 2024-09-29 · SEC filing (10-Q) · SEC filing*

**Summary.** Illumina reported Q3 2024 results following the June 2024 completion of the GRAIL spin-off and the September 2024 European Commission's withdrawal of its €432 million fine. The company achieved strong gross margins of 68.9% (Core Illumina) driven by operational efficiency gains, though overall revenue declined 3% YTD due to reduced instrument shipments offsetting consumables growth. A $481 million net gain from the EC fine reversal and a $1.9 billion goodwill/intangible impairment charge dominated P&L items; Core Illumina generated operating income of $1.3 billion YTD on strengthening execution, while GRAIL (now independent) accounted for the vast majority of impairment charges. Facing continued macro headwinds, customer capital constraints, and significant litigation related to the GRAIL transaction, management emphasizes cost discipline and sustainable long-term growth.

## Financial Summary

### Total Revenue and Profitability

**Q3 2024 vs. Q3 2023** [Sep 29, 2024 10-Q]

Total consolidated revenue declined to **$1,080 million** in Q3 2024 from **$1,119 million** in Q3 2023, a decrease of **3%** [§ Revenue]. The decline was driven primarily by a decrease in sequencing instruments revenue, though offset by increases in sequencing consumables and service and other revenue, primarily from strategic partnerships and extended maintenance service contracts.

Core Illumina generated **$1,080 million** of this revenue (Q3 2023: $1,106 million, down 2%). GRAIL contributed zero revenue in Q3 2024 following the completion of its spin-off on June 24, 2024.

**Nine Months Ended September 29, 2024 (YTD)** [§ Results of Operations]

Year-to-date 2024 revenue totaled **$3,268 million**, down **3%** from **$3,382 million** in the nine months ended October 1, 2023. This decline reflects decreased sequencing instrument revenue partially offset by increased consumables and service revenue.

Segment breakdown YTD 2024:
- Core Illumina: $3,228 million (down 3% from $3,341 million YTD 2023)
- GRAIL: $55 million (down 11% from $62 million YTD 2023; spin-off completed June 24)
- Eliminations: $(15) million (vs. $(21) million YTD 2023)

**Drivers of Gross Margin Improvement** [§ Results of Operations, Gross Margin]

Management attributes the improvement to four factors:
1. **Operational excellence priorities**: Cost savings in freight and improved manufacturing productivity
2. **Favorable product mix**: A more favorable mix of sequencing consumables (higher-margin vs. instruments)
3. **Warranty and field service**: Decrease in warranty and field service costs
4. **Strategic partnerships** (YTD only, offset): Higher strategic partnership revenue, which carries lower margins

The company notes that gross margin depends on: market conditions affecting pricing; sales mix changes among consumables, instruments, services, and development/licensing; product mix between established and new products; excess/obsolete inventory; royalties; manufacturing cost structure relative to volume; freight costs; and product support obligations [§ Financial Overview].

### Goodwill and Intangible Asset Impairment

**Goodwill and Intangible Impairment Charges** [§ Results of Operations, Operating Expense; § Supplemental Balance Sheet Details]

In Q2 2024, Illumina performed its annual goodwill impairment test in May and identified significant impairment triggers for GRAIL. Management recorded two major charges:

1. **GRAIL Goodwill Impairment: $1,466 million** (Q2 2024)
   - GRAIL's carrying value exceeded its estimated fair value of $580 million
   - Fair value estimated using enterprise value estimates prepared by investment bankers for the spin-off pricing process
   - Fair value methodology: estimated revenues for two-year period based on assumed growth rates and implied revenue multiples for comparable companies
   - Sensitivity: an increase of 100% in estimated enterprise values would still have resulted in full impairment

2. **GRAIL IPR&D Impairment: $420 million** (Q2 2024)
   - In-process research and development asset exceeded estimated fair value
   - Fair value determined using discounted cash flow model
   - Discount rate: **46.5%** (derived by reconciling GRAIL's long-range plan—contemplating FDA approval with 15-year cash flow projections—to observed market values based on when-issued trading activity)
   - Sensitivity: increase of 300 basis points to the discount rate would result in additional impairment of $20M
   - Management notes "substantial risk inherent in forecasting revenues and spend associated with research and development" made more challenging by the spin-off and Disposal Funding

**Historical Context**: In Q3 2023, Illumina recorded a GRAIL goodwill impairment of $712 million and IPR&D impairment of $109 million following an interim impairment test. At that time, management estimated GRAIL's fair value using a discounted cash flow approach at **$3.6 billion**, using a discount rate of 24.0%. The significant deterioration from $3.6 billion (Q3 2023) to $580 million (Q2 2024) reflects additional information available in GRAIL's amended Form 10 filings and management presentation in early 2024, including updated disclosure of GRAIL's business and anticipated near-term financial trends. By May 2024, analyst and banker valuations ranged between $400–$770 million, consistent with the impairment recorded [§ Supplemental Balance Sheet Details - Impairment of Goodwill and Intangible Assets].

Core Illumina recognized a $3 million IPR&D impairment in Q1 2024, unrelated to GRAIL.

## GRAIL Spin-Off Transaction

### Completion and Structure

On **June 24, 2024**, Illumina completed the separation of GRAIL into a separate, independent publicly traded company through a tax-free distribution. The structure was: **1 share of GRAIL common stock for every 6 shares of Illumina common stock held** on the Record Date (June 13, 2024) [§ GRAIL Spin-Off].

**Shares Distributed**: 26,547,021 shares of GRAIL common stock, representing **85.5%** of GRAIL's outstanding common stock as of the Record Date. Illumina retained approximately **14.5%** of GRAIL common stock immediately following the spin-off [§ GRAIL Spin-Off].

### Post-Spin-Off Investment Loss

Illumina retained an investment in GRAIL initially valued at $397 million (representing 14.5% of net assets). However, the fair value of this investment has deteriorated significantly. As of September 29, 2024, the investment is valued at **$70 million**, reflecting an **unrealized loss of $332 million** recorded in YTD 2024 [§ Investments and Fair Value Measurements; § Other Income (Expense)].

This loss represents an 82% decline in the Illumina-retained GRAIL stake value in the three months from the June 24 spin-off to September 29, 2024.

### GRAIL Contingent Consideration Rights (CVRs)

**Structure and Terms** [§ Investments and Fair Value Measurements - Contingent Consideration Liabilities]

Illumina issued contingent value rights (CVRs) in connection with the original GRAIL acquisition. These entitle holders to receive quarterly cash payments representing a pro rata portion of "Covered Revenues" each year through August 2033 (a 12-year period). The payment structure is:
- **2.5% payment right** on the first **$1 billion of GRAIL revenue each year**
- **9% payment right** on GRAIL revenues **above $1 billion each year**

**Fair Value and Recent Changes** [§ Investments and Fair Value Measurements - Contingent Consideration Liabilities]

| Date | Fair Value |
|---|---:|
| December 31, 2023 | $387M |
| September 29, 2024 | $82M |
| **Change** | **-$305M (-79%)** |

The significant decrease from $387M to $82M reflects two factors:
1. **Revised revenue projections announced by GRAIL in May 2024** (downward)
2. **Substantial restructuring announced by GRAIL in August 2024**, including "reduction in headcount and planned hires and a substantial decrease in certain R&D projects and investments"

**Valuation Methodology**: Illumina uses a Monte Carlo simulation to estimate the CVR fair value. Key assumptions include:
- Forecasted revenues for GRAIL through 2033
- Revenue risk premium: **18% selected** (as of Sept 29, 2024), derived from reconciling Illumina's forecasted revenues to GRAIL's market capitalization based on a 60-day trailing average
- Revenue volatility estimate
- Operational leverage ratio
- Counterparty credit spread

**Post-Spin-Off Challenge**: "Subsequent to the Spin-Off of GRAIL, we no longer have access to GRAIL management's forecasts and therefore must rely on information made public by GRAIL's management to estimate forecasted revenues through August 2033" [§ Investments and Fair Value Measurements - Contingent Consideration Liabilities]. This introduces additional estimation uncertainty.

**Sensitivity Analysis** [§ Investments and Fair Value Measurements - Contingent Consideration Liabilities]

Small changes in assumptions could materially impact the liability:
- **20% variance in forecasted revenues** (up or down each year): ±$20–21M impact on liability as of Sept 29
- **250 basis point variance in revenue risk premium** (up or down): ±$15–16M impact
- Management expects "certain levels of volatility in the GRAIL contingent consideration liability are possible in future quarters"

**Covered Revenue Activity** [§ Debt - Summary of Term Debt Obligations]

| Period | Covered Revenues | Covered Revenue Payments |
|---|---:|---:|
| Q4 2023 – Q2 2024 (YTD 2024) | $89M aggregate | $836K aggregate |
| Q4 2022 – Q2 2023 (YTD 2023) | $65M aggregate | $609K aggregate |

The increase in covered revenues reflects sales of GRAIL's Galleri test for early cancer detection. At the current run rate, annual covered revenue payments would be modest relative to the fair value liability.

## Legal Proceedings and Regulatory Matters

### European Commission GRAIL Acquisition

**Timeline and Resolution** [§ Legal Proceedings - Acquisition of GRAIL]

**April 2021**: EC sought and accepted referral from France (joined by Belgium, Greece, Iceland, Netherlands, Norway) under Article 22(1) of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 to review GRAIL acquisition. Management notes this was unprecedented: "The European Commission had never solicited referrals to take jurisdiction over an acquisition of a U.S. company that had no revenue in Europe" [§ Legal Proceedings].

**April 28, 2021**: Illumina filed action in the EU General Court seeking annulment of the EC's jurisdictional assertion.

**July 13, 2022**: EU General Court ruled in favor of the EC, holding it has jurisdiction.

**September 22, 2022**: Illumina appealed to the EU Court of Justice.

**October 12, 2023**: EC issued decision requiring Illumina to unwind GRAIL acquisition.

**April 12, 2024**: EC approved Illumina's divestment plan.

**June 24, 2024**: Illumina completed the Spin-Off of GRAIL.

**September 3, 2024**: **EU Court of Justice ruled in Illumina's favor**, confirming that the EC had unlawfully asserted jurisdiction, annulling the EU General Court judgment and the EC's decisions accepting the referral. The judgment concludes these proceedings and is not subject to further appeals [§ Legal Proceedings].

**September 6, 2024**: EC issued **Withdrawal Decision** withdrawing all prior decisions, including:
1. July 22, 2021 decision opening investigation
2. September 6, 2022 decision prohibiting acquisition
3. October 29, 2021 and October 28, 2022 interim measure decisions
4. October 12, 2023 decision requiring unwinding
5. **July 12, 2023 decision imposing €432 million fine**

The Withdrawal Decision resolves all ongoing regulatory proceedings in the European Union [§ Legal Proceedings].

### EC Fine Reversal (Financial Impact)

**September 6, 2024 Fine Reversal** [§ Legal Proceedings]

As a result of the EC withdrawing its €432 million fine and accrued interest (5.5% per annum), Illumina recognized a **net gain of $481 million in Q3 2024**:

The guarantees provided in October 2023 to satisfy the fine obligation in lieu of cash payment (while appealing) are no longer outstanding.
