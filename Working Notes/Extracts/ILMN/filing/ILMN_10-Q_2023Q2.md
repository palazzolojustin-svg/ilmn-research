# Illumina Q2 2023 10-Q — Cash declined, revenue fell, GRAIL legal pressures mounted, EC imposed €432M fine

*Source file: `Working Notes/Sources/ILMN/filing/ILMN_10-Q_2023Q2.md` · Date: 2023-07-02 · SEC filing (10-Q for fiscal quarter ended July 2, 2023) · SEC filing*

**Summary.** Illumina's Q2 2023 results show total revenue of $1.176B (flat YoY, -5% YTD), with product revenue under pressure as customers transition from NovaSeq 6000 to the newly launched NovaSeq X; gross margin fell to 62.2% from 66.0% due to lower volumes, new-product-launch margin pressure, and higher service costs. The company posted a net loss of $234M in Q2 vs. $535M loss a year prior, improved partly by a $597M reduction in legal contingency charges as the European Commission's €432M fine was substantially accrued in Q2 2022. Cash fell $458M to $1.553B, driven by $500M repayment of maturing term notes; the company also remains under strict hold-separate orders from the EC and is defending itself in FTC appeals. Full-year 2023 revenue guidance lowered to 1% growth vs. 2022. GRAIL continues to operate at substantial losses ($204M in Q2) but Galleri test revenue grew +83% YoY.

## Financial Performance

### Revenue and Growth

Total consolidated revenue was $1.176 billion in Q2 2023, essentially flat versus $1.162 billion in Q2 2022 (+1% YoY), but down 5% year-to-date to $2.263 billion from $2.386 billion a year prior. This slowdown represents a significant deceleration from historical growth rates.

The decline was driven primarily by sequencing consumables and instruments revenue, partially offset by growth in GRAIL service revenue. Core Illumina revenue of $1.159 billion in Q2 was essentially unchanged from $1.156 billion; GRAIL contributed $22 million, up from $12 million a year prior (+83%). After eliminations of intra-company sales ($5 million), reported total was $1.176 billion.

**By product category (Q2 2023 vs. Q2 2022):**
- Sequencing consumables: $734 million (down $5 million or 1% from $739M). The decline reflects lower pull-through on NovaSeq 6000 systems as high-throughput customers transition to the newly launched NovaSeq X, combined with tighter customer inventory management amid macroeconomic uncertainty.
- Microarray consumables: $70 million (down $4 million or 5% from $74M).
- Sequencing instruments: $193 million (up $3 million or 2% from $190M), supported by early NovaSeq X shipments, which offset lower shipments of NovaSeq 6000, NextSeq 550, and MiSeq systems.
- Microarray instruments: $4 million (up $1 million from $3M).
- Service and other revenue: $175 million (up $19 million or 12% from $156M), driven by increased revenue from extended maintenance service contracts on a growing installed base, partially offset by declines in development and licensing agreements.

**Year-to-date (first six months of 2023):**
- Product revenue fell 7% to $1.923 billion from $2.076 billion.
- Sequencing consumables declined $96 million or 6% to $1.419 billion, with the NovaSeq 6000 transition the primary driver.
- Sequencing instruments fell $55 million or 13% to $346 million.
- Service and other revenue grew 10% to $340 million from $310 million.

**Geographic revenue (Q2 2023):**
- Americas: $640 million (flat vs. $639M in Q2 2022)
- Europe: $303 million (up from $274M, +11%)
- Greater China: $115 million (down from $118M, -3%)
- Asia-Pacific, Middle East and Africa (AMEA): $118 million (down from $131M, -10%)

Note: The company implemented a new regional structure in Q1 2023, consolidating Asia-Pacific with emerging markets (Middle East, Africa, Turkey, CIS). Prior-year results have been reclassified to reflect this structure.

**YTD 2023 geographic revenue:**
- Americas: $1.256 billion (down from $1.288B, -2%)
- Europe: $564 million (essentially flat, up $4M)
- Greater China: $206 million (down from $245M, -16%)
- AMEA: $237 million (down from $293M, -19%)

**Headwinds to revenue:** The company reported unfavorable foreign exchange rate impacts of $11 million in Q2 2023 and $35 million YTD, net of $2 million and $3 million, respectively, reclassified to revenue from cash flow hedges.

**2023 Outlook:** Management lowered its full-year 2023 revenue guidance to 1% growth versus 2022 (previously expected higher growth), citing "continued impact of challenging macroeconomic factors" including customer inventory discipline, delayed lab expansions, and competitive pressures in China. The company noted that COVID-19 pandemic mitigation measures, Russia-Ukraine conflict sanctions, inflation, and exchange rate volatility are expected to continue impacting results in 2023 with "significantly uncertain" magnitude and duration.

### Gross Margin and Cost of Revenue

Gross margin declined to 62.2% in Q2 2023 from 66.0% a year prior, a 380-basis-point contraction. Year-to-date, gross margin fell 500 basis points to 61.3% from 66.3%.

Gross profit in Q2 was $732 million (down $35 million or 5% from $767M); YTD gross profit fell $197 million or 12% to $1.386 billion from $1.583 billion.

**Cost of revenue breakdown (Q2 2023 vs. Q2 2022):**
- Cost of product revenue: $305 million (up from $286M, +7%)
- Cost of service and other revenue: $91 million (up from $69M, +32%)
- Amortization of acquired intangible assets: $48 million (up from $40M, +20%)
- Total cost of revenue: $444 million (up from $395M, +12%)

**YTD 2023:**
- Cost of product revenue: $591 million (vs. $586M, +1%)
- Cost of service and other revenue: $190 million (vs. $138M, +38%)
- Amortization: $96 million (vs. $79M, +22%)
- Total cost: $877 million (vs. $803M, +9%)

**Margin drivers:** Management attributed the decline primarily to:
- Less fixed-cost leverage on lower manufacturing volumes (lower production spreading fixed costs across smaller revenue base)
- Lower instrument margins due to NovaSeq X platform launch (typical margin pressure during new product ramp before manufacturing scale and operational efficiencies are achieved)
- Increased field services and installation costs
- GRAIL gross loss of $(24) million in Q2 2023 (and $(50) million YTD), driven primarily by $33 million and $67 million, respectively, of amortization of acquired GRAIL intangible assets

## Contingent Consideration (GRAIL Acquisition)

As of July 2, 2023, the fair value of contingent consideration liabilities related to the GRAIL acquisition was $440 million (up from $412 million on January 1, 2023). The fair value change of $28 million is recognized in SG&A.

**Contingent Value Rights structure:**
- Entitle holders to quarterly Covered Revenue Payments based on certain GRAIL-related revenues
- Structure: 2.5% of first $1 billion of GRAIL revenue annually for 12 years; 9% on revenue above $1 billion
- Q2 2023 Covered Revenues: approximately $22 million
- Estimated Q3 2023 Covered Revenue Payments: approximately $209,000
- YTD 2023 Covered Revenue Payments: approximately $400,000 (related to Q4 2022 and Q1 2023 Covered Revenues of $42 million aggregate)
- Of the $440 million liability, $439 million is included in other long-term liabilities and approximately $1 million is in accrued liabilities

The company uses a Monte Carlo simulation to estimate fair value, with assumptions including forecasted GRAIL revenues, revenue risk premium, revenue volatility, operational leverage ratio, and counterparty credit spread. These are Level 3 fair value measurements.

## GRAIL Acquisition — Legal and Regulatory Status

The acquisition of GRAIL remains subject to ongoing legal and regulatory proceedings that impose significant costs, operational restrictions, and legal uncertainty. This is the most material non-financial risk facing the company.

### Regulatory History

**August 18, 2021:** Illumina closed acquisition of GRAIL for consideration including equity and contingent value rights.

**March 30, 2021:** FTC filed administrative complaint alleging the acquisition would violate Section 7 of the Clayton Act.

**April 19, 2021:** European Commission accepted Article 22(1) referral from France (joined by Belgium, Greece, Iceland, Netherlands, Norway) to review acquisition under EU Merger Regulation (unprecedented for US company with no EU revenue).

**April 20, 2021:** US District Court for DC granted FTC's motion to transfer venue to Southern District of California.

**May 28, 2021:** District court granted FTC's motion to dismiss without prejudice.

**October 29, 2021:** EC adopted Initial Interim Measures Order imposing hold-separate requirements.

### Current Status and Obligations

**Hold-Separate Arrangement (New Interim Measures Order):**
- Illumina and GRAIL must operate as independent legal entities with arm's-length transactions
- GRAIL management has sole day-to-day operational responsibility
- Illumina management must have no involvement in or influence over GRAIL
- Illumina must implement measures to preserve GRAIL's viability, marketability, and competitiveness (resource provision, key personnel retention/replacement)
- Confidential information restrictions between companies
- Independent monitoring trustee appointed to monitor compliance
- Hold-separate obligations characterized as "burdensome to implement and administer"
- Additional legal, financial advisory, regulatory, and professional services costs expected to continue
- Failure to comply could result in additional fines or penalties from EC

**European Commission Fine (Article 14(2)(b) Decision):**
- €432 million fine for breaching EU Merger Regulation by acquiring and exerting influence during review
- Accrued as of July 2, 2023: $471 million (included in accrued liabilities)
- Company intends to appeal
- Planning to issue guarantee and defer payment pending outcome of EU General Court's jurisdiction appeal
- Fine represents 10% of company's 2022 consolidated annual revenues (maximum amount)

**FTC Proceedings (US):**
- Fifth Circuit Court of Appeals review underway
- Company intends to "vigorously defend"
- Outcome uncertain; if FTC ultimately prevails, will require divestiture of GRAIL

**Pending EU General Court and Court of Justice Proceedings:**
- Illumina's appeal on jurisdiction pending in Court of Justice
- Multiple motions for annulment of Prohibition Decision, New Interim Measures Order pending in EU General Court (some stayed)
- Timeline for EC Divestment Decision unknown

## Summary of Key Themes

**Revenue and Market Pressures:** Revenue growth essentially flat YoY and -5% YTD, driven by sequencing consumables and instruments decline as customers transition from maturing NovaSeq 6000 to new NovaSeq X platform. Macroeconomic headwinds (inflation, FX, supply chain disruption), COVID-19 pandemic effects, Russia-Ukraine sanctions, and China competitive pressures cited as ongoing. Full-year 2023 guidance reduced to 1% growth.

**Margin Compression:** Gross margin declined 380–500 basis points YoY/YTD due to lower fixed-cost leverage on declining volumes, new NovaSeq X platform launch margin depression, and increased field service costs. Management expects margin pressure to continue in near term.

**Legal and Regulatory Burden:** The GRAIL acquisition remains the company's dominant non-financial issue, with ongoing FTC appeals (Fifth Circuit oral argument September 12, 2023), EU jurisdiction appeals, and EC-imposed hold-separate restrictions. The €432 million EC fine (10% of 2022 revenues) was imposed July 12, 2023, after the quarter closed. Divestiture may be ordered; if so, could result in tax liabilities of $500M–$1B and loss of synergies. Hold-separate restrictions preventing operational integration create ongoing administrative and professional services costs and constrain management focus.

**GRAIL Segment:** GRAIL revenue growing (+83% Q2, +91% YTD) driven by Galleri multi-cancer blood test, but segment loss deepening ($(204)M Q2, $(408)M YTD) due to continued clinical and commercial investment, hold-separate costs, and amortization of intangible assets. Fair value of contingent consideration liability $440M.

**Restructuring:** Q2 2023 included $33M restructuring charge for workforce reductions and facility consolidation (i3 campus exit by end 2023, Foster City campus under evaluation).

**Cash and Debt:** Cash declined $458M to $1.553B, primarily from $500M repayment of maturing 2023 Term Notes. Net debt now approximately $1.5B in term notes outstanding (2025, 2027, 2031 maturities). $750M convertible notes (0% coupon) mature August 2023; as of August 9, none converted. Company has $750M undrawn credit facility.

**Tax Position:** Effective tax rate negative (expense on loss) due to R&D capitalization impacts and GRAIL pre-acquisition NOL limitations; company changed interim tax accounting methodology in Q2 2023 to year-to-date effective rate method due to volatility.

**Share Count and Buybacks:** Share count stable at ~158M shares outstanding. No repurchases in 2023; only $15M remaining under authorization; company stated no intent to repurchase in 2023.
