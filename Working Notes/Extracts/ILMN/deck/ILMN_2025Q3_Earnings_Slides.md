# Illumina Q3 2025 Earnings Presentation (slides, with GAAP/non-GAAP appendix)

*Source file: `Working Notes/Sources/ILMN/deck/ILMN_2025Q3_Earnings_Slides.md` · Date: 2025-10-30 · Illumina, Inc. investor relations earnings slide deck (no individual speakers named) · slides*

**Summary.** Illumina's Q3 2025 deck reports revenue of $1.084B, roughly flat year over year, with non-GAAP operating margin up to 24.5% and non-GAAP EPS up 18% to $1.34. Clinical sequencing consumables grew 12% ex-China while Research & Applied fell 8%, and Greater China revenue dropped 31%. The company hit its NovaSeq X transition targets, launched new multiomics products, and raised its 2025 profit guidance (EPS $4.65-$4.75) while trimming the revenue range (0.5%-1.5% constant-currency growth).

# Illumina Q3 2025 Earnings Presentation Notes

All figures below come from Illumina's slide deck dated October 30, 2025 [Q3 2025 deck]. The deck contains no analyst questions, ratings, price targets or spoken quotes, because it is a presentation rather than a call transcript. Fiscal Q3 2025 ended September 28, 2025; the comparison quarter, Q3 2024, ended September 29, 2024. "YoY" means versus the same quarter a year earlier. "CC" means constant currency, meaning growth with the effect of exchange rates removed. "Non-GAAP" means results adjusted to exclude items such as acquisition-related costs and amortization, which management says helps investors judge underlying operating performance.

## Framing notes from the deck

- Illumina divested GRAIL (its cancer-detection spinoff) in June 2024 (the footnotes give the spin-off date as June 24, 2024). Year-to-date 2024 "consolidated" figures include GRAIL; "Core Illumina" figures exclude it. For 2025 the two are identical.
- "China" in this deck means the Greater China region, which also includes Taiwan and Hong Kong.
- Q3'25 figures include the pricing actions Illumina announced in Q1 2025. FX effects are measured as of 9/28/2025.
- "AMEA" means Asia Pacific, Middle East and Africa; "AMR" (Americas) is shown in the appendix tables. "HT" means high-throughput (large sequencers), and "Gb" means gigabases (billions of DNA letters read).

## Q3 2025 results summary (slide 3)

- **Total revenue:** $1.08B (appendix: $1,084M vs $1,080M in Q3 2024), roughly flat YoY on both a constant-currency and reported basis.
- **Revenue excluding China:** grew about 2% YoY in constant currency.
- **Non-GAAP operating margin:** 24.5%. **Non-GAAP diluted EPS (earnings per share):** $1.34.
- **NovaSeq X placements:** more than 55 instruments placed in the quarter. NovaSeq X is Illumina's newer, higher-throughput sequencer.

Regional revenue (share of the $1.08B total):

| Region | Q3'25 revenue | Share | YoY growth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Americas | $612M | 56% | +1% CC and reported |
| Europe | $309M | 29% | +3% CC, +6% reported |
| AMEA | $111M | 10% | +8% CC, +6% reported |
| Greater China | $52M | 5% | (31%) CC and reported |

Greater China is the clear weak spot; the other three regions grew modestly. Europe's reported growth exceeds constant-currency growth because currency movements helped.

## Long-term growth pillars (slides 4-5)

The deck frames three key growth drivers: (1) core sequencing and the NovaSeq X transition, (2) scalable entry into multiomics (measuring more than DNA, such as proteins), and (3) expansion of services, data and software.

Progress claimed for Q3'25 [Illumina deck]:

- **Core sequencing and NovaSeq X:** more than 55 placements. Illumina says it "achieved HT transition targets": over 75% of high-throughput gigabases shipped, and over 50% of high-throughput revenue, now come from NovaSeq X.
- **Multiomics:** Illumina Protein Prep launched in September 2025. The 5-Base Solution launched in October 2025.
- **Services, data and software:** "BioInsight," a new business announced in October 2025, with population sequencing, data partnerships, and software and AI listed beneath it.

## Revenue detail (slides 6, 7, 10)

Illumina reports revenue in three lines: sequencing consumables (the reagent kits customers buy for every run), sequencing instruments (the machines), and sequencing service and other.

| Line | Q3'25 | Q3'24 | YoY |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sequencing consumables | $747M | $741M | ~flat CC (+1% reported; appendix CC $741M vs $739M) |
| Sequencing instruments | $107M | $104M | +3% CC and reported |
| Sequencing service and other | $147M | $150M | (3%) CC, (2%) reported |

**Consumables excluding China** were $707M vs $684M, up 3%. Within that, **Clinical** (hospital, testing and diagnostic uses) grew 12% and **Research & Applied** fell 8%. **Instruments excluding China** were $104M vs $98M, up 6% CC and reported.

Ex-China sequencing trend (dollars in millions; growth rates are actual):

| | Q1'24 | Q2'24 | Q3'24 | Q4'24 | FY2024 | Q1'25 | Q2'25 | Q3'25 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Consumables | 642 | 679 | 684 | 640 | 2,645 | 640 | 690 | 707 |
| Instruments | 100 | 111 | 98 | 144 | 453 | 103 | 92 | 104 |
| Total consumables YoY | 1% | 2% | 9% | 2% | 4% | (0%) | 2% | 3% |

The takeaway is that clinical growth has accelerated through 2025 (8%, 10%, 12%) while research and applied has stayed negative all year. The deck notes that Q3'25 total consumables actual growth equals constant-currency growth.

## High-throughput transition to NovaSeq X (slides 8-9)

Illumina set targets for how much of its high-throughput business runs on NovaSeq X rather than the older NovaSeq 6000, and says both were achieved in Q3'25: **over 75% of HT gigabases shipped** and **over 50% of HT consumables revenue**.

The charts (Q2'24 to Q3'25) show NovaSeq X share of HT gigabases shipped and of HT consumables revenue, split into Total, Research & Applied, and Clinical. The extracted chart text is jumbled, so the series cannot be reliably assigned to each line. Values visible on the charts include 91%, 82%, 80%, 78%, 70%, 68%, 69%, 66%, 64%, 59%, 55%, 53%, 51%, 49%, 45%, 44%, 43%, 40%, 36%, 35% and 27%. The revenue-share chart appears to end near 51% for the total and 43-44% for subsets, and the gigabase chart appears to end at roughly 82% for the total, with 91% likely the highest segment. Treat these assignments as unverified; check the original deck for exact values.

**Transitioned clinical accounts** (slide 9): Illumina analyzed 40 clinical customers (as of 9/28/2025, including some top-tier accounts) that have fully or largely moved (all or the vast majority of volume) to NovaSeq X. Chart labels, for 2023, 2024 and 2025E (annualized year-to-date actuals), show consumables revenue and gigabase volume growing at double-digit rates (+DD) for these accounts, with one label reading +LSD (low single digit) and the rest +DD. The chart text does not make clear which period each label belongs to. Management's implied claim is that customers who complete the move to NovaSeq X buy more, in both revenue and sequencing volume.

## GAAP results and reconciliation items

- **Q3'25 GAAP:** gross profit $733M (67.6% margin) vs $745M (68.9%); R&D $229M (21.1%) vs $253M; SG&A $277M (25.5%) vs $239M (22.2%); operating profit $227M (21.0%) vs $741M (68.6%) in Q3'24. Q3'24 GAAP was inflated by a $488M gain from reversing the European Commission (EC) fine, including accrued interest, which is excluded from non-GAAP.
- **GAAP EPS:** $0.98 in Q3'25 vs $4.03 Core Illumina in Q3'24 ($4.42 consolidated). GAAP net income $150M vs $642M Core ($705M consolidated). Nine-month GAAP EPS $3.29 vs $4.88 Core ($(8.86) consolidated, which included large GRAIL impairments). Nine-month GAAP net income $516M.
- **Q3'25 acquisition-related costs** (excluded from non-GAAP) comprised: $16M amortization of intangibles (cost of revenue), $4M lease impairment, $4M legal expenses mainly for the pending SomaLogic acquisition, and $3M fair-value adjustments on contingent consideration (an obligation to pay more depending on future results). Transformational initiatives costs were mainly an ERP (enterprise software) upgrade. Nine-month 2025 transformational costs were mostly severance from restructuring plus the ERP upgrade. The nine-month 2025 gross profit also excludes a $23M intangible impairment in Core Illumina.
- **Other income:** GAAP other expense, net was $(7)M in Q3'25, including a $6M strategic investment gain, vs $(21)M a year earlier. Nine-month GAAP other income was $103M, including a $141M strategic investment gain.
- **Tax:** GAAP tax provision was $70M (31.8% rate) in Q3'25 vs $77M (10.8%) Core in Q3'24. The non-GAAP tax expense line includes a one-time $42M valuation allowance (a write-down of deferred tax assets) against certain US foreign tax credits, resulting from US tax legislation signed July 4, 2025. The Pillar Two global minimum top-up tax no longer applies in 2025 because GRAIL's pre-acquisition net operating losses were fully used in prior years.
- **Cost-structure note:** GAAP SG&A is a net figure that includes other items such as $3M for board membership changes and $2M for a legal contingency accrual in nine-month 2025.

## Risks and strategic flags (from the forward-looking statement list)

The risk list names, among others: slowing growth in served markets, including proteomics; the pending acquisition of SomaLogic (and certain other assets) from Standard BioTools, which requires regulatory approvals such as Hart-Scott-Rodino in the US; integrating that business; Illumina's inclusion on China's "unreliable entities list" and China's decision not to permit export of Illumina sequencing instruments into China; US and counter-tariffs; supply chain and manufacturing risks; competing products; payor reimbursement and regulatory clearance; and possible further litigation tied to the GRAIL acquisition. These are standard cautionary risks, not new management statements.
