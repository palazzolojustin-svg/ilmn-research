*Source file: `Working Notes/Sources/ILMN/deck/ILMN_2025Q4_Earnings_Slides.md` · Date: 2026-02-05 · Illumina, Inc. investor presentation (no speakers named) · slides*

**Summary.** Illumina's slide deck for the quarter ended Dec 28, 2025. Revenue was $1.16B (+4% constant currency, +5% reported), non-GAAP operating margin rose to 23.7% and non-GAAP EPS to $1.35 (+42%). # Illumina Q4 2025 Earnings Presentation (Feb 5, 2026)

This is a slide deck, so it states results and guidance but includes no analyst questions, ratings or price targets. All claims below are management's own presentation [Q4 2025 deck, 2026-02-05]. Notes on terms: "constant currency (CC)" strips out the effect of exchange-rate moves; "YoY" compares with the same quarter a year earlier; "QoQ" compares with the prior quarter; "non-GAAP" figures exclude items such as acquisition costs and intangible amortization. "China" here means Greater China (China, Taiwan, Hong Kong). All values include pricing actions Illumina announced in Q1 2025. Growth rates and values are described as approximate.

## Headline Q4 2025 results (slide 3)

- **Revenue:** $1.16B (exact: $1,159M versus $1,104M in Q4 2024), up 4% YoY in constant currency and 5% as reported.
- **Ex-China growth:** revenue excluding Greater China grew 7% YoY in constant currency.
- **NovaSeq X placements:** more than 100 instruments placed in the quarter. NovaSeq X is Illumina's newest high-throughput sequencer.
- **Non-GAAP operating margin:** 23.7% (operating profit as a share of revenue).
- **Non-GAAP diluted EPS:** $1.35 (earnings per share).

Regional revenue for Q4 2025, with share of total in parentheses:

| Region | Q4'25 revenue | YoY change | Share of total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Americas | $638M | +8% CC, +8% reported | 55% |
| Europe | $353M | +5% CC, +8% reported | 30% |
| Asia Pacific, Middle East and Africa (AMEA) | $113M | +6% CC, +3% reported | 10% |
| Greater China | $55M | (30%) CC, (30%) reported | 5% |

The table shows that growth came from the Americas and Europe, while China was a heavy drag. Prior-year regional figures: Americas $589M, Europe $326M, AMEA $109M, Greater China $80M. (AMEA includes Russia and Turkey.)

## Clinical strength (slide 4)

Clinical sequencing consumables ex-China (the reagent kits used on every sequencing run for clinical testing) grew reported YoY by 8% in Q1'25, 10% in Q2'25, 12% in Q3'25 and 20% in Q4'25, a steady acceleration through the year.

## Strategic pillars (slide 5)

Illumina lists three growth pillars: (1) core sequencing and the NovaSeq X transition, (2) scalable entry into multiomics (measuring more than DNA, such as proteins), and (3) expansion of services, data and software.

## SomaLogic acquisition (slide 6)

Illumina closed the acquisition of SomaLogic in January 2026. It brings all existing SOMAmer-related technology and products (SOMAmers are the binding reagents used to measure proteins), including SomaScan kits and services, SomaSignal Tests, and relevant technology-development capabilities. The cautionary notes describe this as SomaLogic, Inc. plus certain other assets acquired from Standard BioTools.

- **Purchase consideration:** $350M cash, up to $75M in near-term revenue-performance-based milestones, and performance-based royalties.
- **Rationale:** it builds on the existing partnership, pairing SomaLogic's proteomics (protein-measurement) technology and expertise with Illumina's scalable next-generation-sequencing (NGS) ecosystem. It accelerates product timelines by enabling a more seamless proteomics development roadmap, advances Illumina's multiomics strategy, and positions it in a large, expanding proteomics market.
- **SomaLogic financials:** end markets growing high-single-digit-plus to double-digit; a high-margin consumables business as enabled on NGS. Management expects the business to be profitable in 2027 on a non-GAAP operating income basis and expects its non-GAAP operating margin to match Illumina's in 2028.

## BioInsight (slide 7)

BioInsight is presented as Illumina's AI-enabled applications effort: aggregating biology at scale to train proprietary foundation models (large AI models) for discovery and insights. The slide's building blocks are genome-wide Perturb-Seq (experiments that disrupt genes to see their effects), sequencing at scale, compute power and artificial intelligence. Stated outputs are causal maps of gene function, large-scale longitudinal datasets (data tracked over time), and standardized pipelines delivering model-ready data. Applications named: advancing drug discovery for pharma partners and clinical insights.

## NovaSeq X transition (slide 10)

Two charts track the shift from the older NovaSeq 6000 to NovaSeq X. "HT" means high-throughput.

- **NovaSeq X share of HT gigabases shipped** (volume of sequencing output) climbed from the mid-30s% in Q2'24 to about 90% by Q4'25. Labelled points include 91% and 90% at the top end, with intermediate points of 82%, 80%, 78%, 71%, 70%, 69%, 68%, 66%, 64%, 59%, 55%, 53%, 51%, 49%, 45%, 43% and 35%. The chart labels are not fully attributable to individual quarters in the source.
- **NovaSeq X share of HT consumables revenue** by Total, Research & Applied and Clinical: figures shown are 55%, 51%, 44%, 43%, 40%, 36% and 27%, with Q4'25 total at roughly 51-55% range and Clinical lagging behind; the source chart does not cleanly map each label to a segment.
- **Management statement:** "With transition milestones achieved, we will phase out transition-specific metrics after Q4 earnings."

## Full-year 2025 results (appendix)

- **Revenue:** $4,343M, flat versus $4,332M for core Illumina in 2024 (consolidated 2024 was $4,372M, including GRAIL until its June 24, 2024 spin-off; consolidated change (1%)). Constant currency $4,327M versus $4,317M, flat. Ex-China revenue $4,100M versus $4,024M, up 2%.
- **By source (FY25 vs FY24):** sequencing consumables $2,939M vs $2,872M (+2%); instruments $465M vs $484M (-4%); services and other $581M vs $597M (-2%, -3% CC). Ex-China: consumables $2,750M vs $2,645M (+4%); instruments $449M vs $453M (-1%); services $559M vs $565M (-1%).
- **By region (FY25 vs FY24):** Americas $2,406M vs $2,401M (flat); AMEA $430M vs $438M (-2%, +1% CC); Greater China $243M vs $308M (-21%, -20% CC); Europe $1,264M vs $1,185M (+7%, +5% CC).
- **Q4 hedge and currency effects:** Q4 revenue included a $(4)M hedge effect and a $16M favorable exchange-rate effect; CC revenue was $1,147M versus $1,099M (+4%). Ex-China CC revenue was $1,092M versus $1,021M (+7%).
- **Q4 regional CC detail:** Americas $637M (+8%); AMEA $114M (+6%); Greater China $55M versus $78M (-30%); Europe $341M versus $324M (+5%).

### GAAP to non-GAAP reconciliation

- **Q4'25 EPS:** GAAP diluted EPS $2.16 versus $0.73 (core, Q4'24); non-GAAP $1.35 versus $0.95 core ($0.86 consolidated). The gap is mostly a $(1.25) per share strategic-investment gain adjustment, plus $0.30 SG&A and $0.12 cost-of-revenue items.
- **FY25 EPS:** GAAP $5.45 (versus $5.61 core and $(7.69) consolidated in 2024, the latter hit by GRAIL impairments); non-GAAP $4.84 versus $4.16 core and $2.45 consolidated.
- **Net income:** Q4'25 GAAP $334M, non-GAAP $208M (versus $152M core). FY25 GAAP $850M, non-GAAP $756M (versus $663M core, $390M consolidated).
- **Q4'25 margins (GAAP versus non-GAAP):** gross profit $759M (65.5%) versus $777M (67.0%); R&D $239M versus $238M; SG&A $310M (26.7%) versus $264M (22.8%); operating profit $202M (17.4%) versus $275M (23.7%); other income $176M GAAP versus $(16)M non-GAAP, after removing a $192M strategic investment gain. GAAP operating profit in Q4'24 was $175M (15.8%) versus non-GAAP $218M (19.7%).
- **FY25 margins:** non-GAAP gross margin 68.2% (versus 68.6% core 2024); non-GAAP R&D $950M (21.9%); non-GAAP SG&A $1,009M (23.2%); GAAP operating profit $807M (18.6%) versus non-GAAP $1,004M (23.1%, versus 21.3% core 2024). FY25 non-GAAP other expense, net was $(54)M.
- **Notable adjustments:** Q4'25 acquisition-related costs were $16M amortization of intangibles (cost of revenue), $11M contingent-consideration fair-value adjustments and $10M mostly SomaLogic and GRAIL legal and other expenses. FY25 acquisition costs were $66M amortization plus $33M of legal/other (including a lease impairment), offset by an $18M fair-value benefit. Transformational initiatives were mainly severance ($47M in FY25) and ERP upgrade costs ($14M in FY25). A $19M donation to the Illumina Foundation hit Q4 (plus $3M for board changes in FY25). FY25 also included a $23M intangible impairment in core Illumina.
- **Tax:** Q4'25 GAAP tax provision $44M (11.6%); non-GAAP $50M (19.5%) versus 23.7% core a year earlier. FY25 non-GAAP tax $194M (20.5%) versus 23.6% core. Non-GAAP tax includes a one-time $42M valuation allowance recorded in Q3 2025 against deferred tax assets tied to certain U.S. foreign tax credits, following U.S. tax legislation signed July 4, 2025. The Pillar Two global minimum top-up tax no longer applies for 2025 because GRAIL's pre-acquisition losses were fully used.

## Risks listed in the cautionary notes

Management lists risks including proteomics market growth, order timing and mix, integrating SomaLogic and its manufacturing, managing partner and customer relationships in proteomics, Illumina's inclusion on China's "unreliable entities list", US tariffs and trade measures, competing products, new-product and supplier risks, further GRAIL-related litigation, payor reimbursement, regulatory clearances, and macroeconomic or armed-conflict uncertainty.
