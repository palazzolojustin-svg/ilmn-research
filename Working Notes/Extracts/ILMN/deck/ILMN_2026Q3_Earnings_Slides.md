## How to read the numbers (definitions in the deck)

- **Non-GAAP** means results adjusted to leave out items such as amortization of acquired intangibles, acquisition costs and investment gains and losses. All headline metrics are non-GAAP and also **exclude tariff refunds**.
- **Reported growth** is year-over-year (YoY) change against Q3 2025. **Organic growth** also removes currency effects and acquisitions. **Rest-of-world (ROW) organic growth** additionally excludes Greater China (which includes Taiwan and Hong Kong).
- **Sequential** means versus the prior quarter.
- Regions: USCAN (U.S. and Canada), EMEALA (Europe, Middle East, Africa, Latin America), APAC (Asia Pacific), Greater China.
- FX effects are measured as of 09/27/2026.

## Headline results (slide 4)

- Revenue was **$1.17B**, up **4.8% organic** and **7.6% reported**. ROW organic growth was **+4.7%**.
- Non-GAAP operating margin (profit as a share of revenue) was **25.0%**; diluted EPS (earnings per share) was **$1.44**.
- **More than 80 NovaSeq X instruments** were placed in Q3 2026 (NovaSeq X is Illumina's newest high-throughput sequencer).

Regional revenue (all excluding tariff refunds):

| APAC | $86M | 7% | +5% | +5% |

The US drove growth; Europe-Middle East-Africa-Latin America shrank organically; China grew in the quarter after a steep nine-month decline (see below).

## Revenue detail (slides 6-9)

Total revenue was **$1,166M** versus **$1,084M** in Q3 2025 (+5% organic, +8% reported).

**Sequencing consumables** (reagent kits customers must buy for every sequencing run) were **$792M** versus **$747M** a year earlier, +6% organic and +6% reported. ROW sequencing consumables were **$748M** versus **$707M**, +5% ROW organic and +6% reported.

**Sequencing instruments** (the machines) were **$106M** versus **$107M**: (1%) organic, roughly flat reported, and (2%) ROW organic.

**Sequencing service and other** revenue (service contracts and related items) was **$158M** versus **$147M**: +8% organic, +7% reported, +8% ROW organic.

## Tariff refunds

Illumina recognized a one-time benefit from refunds of IEEPA tariffs (tariffs imposed under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act). It booked a **$79M** reduction to cost of revenue in Q3'26 and a related **$37M** reduction to revenue for the portion it expects to credit to customers. The refund lowered revenue growth: it was a 3.4-point drag on the quarter's growth (reported 4.2% vs 7.6% excluding it).

Revenue impact by product ($M): sequencing consumables 28, instruments 4, service and other 3 (total sequencing 35), microarray 2; total 37. By region: USCAN 28, EMEALA 9; total 37.
