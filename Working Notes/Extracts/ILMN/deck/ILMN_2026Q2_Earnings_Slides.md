**Summary.** Illumina's Q2 2026 earnings deck reports revenue of $1.16B (+6.5% organic, +9.5% reported), non-GAAP operating margin of 22.5% and non-GAAP EPS of $1.31 (+10%). # Illumina Q2 2026 Earnings Presentation: Detailed Notes

This is the slide deck Illumina used for its second-quarter 2026 earnings presentation on July 30, 2026. The fiscal quarter ended June 28, 2026. The deck contains presentation slides, a Q&A divider slide (no Q&A content is included in the document), and appendix tables reconciling company-adjusted ("non-GAAP") figures to standard accounting ("GAAP") figures. Everything below is from the company's own materials, so all statements are [Q2 2026 slides] and reflect management's presentation, not independent views.

## Definitions and reporting conventions used in the deck

- "Year-over-year" means versus the same quarter of the prior fiscal year (Q2 2025). "Sequential" or "quarter-over-quarter" means versus the prior quarter (Q1 2026).
- "China" in the deck means the Greater China region, which includes Taiwan and Hong Kong.
- Unless stated otherwise, growth rates are year-over-year on a reported basis.
- "Organic" growth strips out the effects of currency movements and acquisitions. "ROW organic" (rest-of-world, meaning everything outside Greater China) also strips out Greater China, to show underlying growth in the rest of the business.
- Regions: USCAN = U.S. and Canada; EMEALA = Europe, Middle East, Africa and Latin America; APAC = Asia Pacific (excluding Greater China).
- Non-GAAP figures exclude items such as amortization of acquired intangibles, acquisition costs, restructuring and strategic investment gains or losses. Illumina says non-GAAP operating margin and EPS are key metrics used by its board to judge and pay management. Foreign-exchange impacts are as of 06/28/2026. All values on the summary slides are described as approximate.

## Q2 2026 results summary (slide 4)

Total revenue was about $1.16 billion, up 6.5% organically and 9.5% as reported versus Q2 2025. ROW organic growth (excluding Greater China) was +8.1%. Non-GAAP operating margin (operating profit as a share of revenue, excluding adjustment items) was 22.5%, and non-GAAP diluted EPS (earnings per share) was $1.31. The company placed more than 95 NovaSeq X instruments in the quarter (NovaSeq X is its newest high-throughput sequencer).

Regional revenue and growth:

| Region | Q2'26 revenue | Share of total | Organic growth | Reported growth |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| USCAN | $653M | 56% | +13% | +15% |
| EMEALA | $368M | 32% | +0% | +4% |
| APAC | $82M | 7% | +9% | +14% |
| Greater China | $56M | 5% | (19%) | (12%) |

Takeaway: the U.S. and Canada drove growth and made up more than half of revenue. Europe/Middle East/Africa/Latin America was flat organically, with the reported gain coming from currency. Greater China shrank sharply; the deck's footnotes cite uncertainty from Illumina's inclusion on China's "unreliable entities list" (see Risks).

## Revenue overview (slide 6)

- Total revenue: $1,159M in Q2'26 versus $1,059M in Q2'25, +6% organic, +9% reported; +8% ROW organic.
- Sequencing consumables (the reagent kits and flow cells customers must buy for every sequencing run, and the recurring core of the business): $775M versus $740M in Q2'25, +4% organic, +5% reported.
- ROW sequencing consumables (outside Greater China): $734M versus $690M, +5% organic, +6% reported.

The reconciliation tables show the roughly 2.1-point gap between organic and reported total growth came from acquisitions (-2.1 points, mainly SomaLogic, a protein-measurement business acquired from Standard BioTools) and currency (-0.9 points). Excluding those, organic growth was 6.5%; removing China's drag (+1.6 points) gives ROW organic of 8.1%.

Instruments revenue was $103M, $92M, $104M, $150M, $449M (FY2025), $114M (Q1'26) and $122M (Q2'26).

Total ROW consumables growth of 6% in Q2'26 compares with 2% a year earlier.

"High-throughput" (HT) refers to Illumina's large production sequencers. The slide tracks how far customers have moved to NovaSeq X, the newer model, from older NovaSeq 6000 machines. Two measures are shown, each split into Total, Research & Applied and Clinical (the chart text is jumbled, so exact attribution of each point to a line is uncertain):

- NovaSeq X as a percent of HT gigabases shipped (Gb = gigabases, a measure of sequencing data volume), Q2'24 through Q2'26. Values appearing on the chart range from the 30s-40s% in mid-2024 up to about 90-92% at the high end by Q2'26. Values listed include 35%, 43%, 45%, 49%, 51%, 53%, 55%, 59%, 64%, 66%, 68%, 69%, 70%, 71%, 76%, 78%, 80%, 82%, 83%, 90%, 91% and 92%.
- NovaSeq X as a percent of HT consumables revenue, Q2'24 through Q2'26: values appearing are 27%, 36%, 40%, 43%, 44%, 51%, 55%, 55% and 59%. The 59% figure appears to be the latest total or highest point.

Takeaway: NovaSeq X's share of data volume is well ahead of its share of revenue, consistent with the newer machine delivering more data per dollar, and both shares have risen steadily over two years. The latest-quarter Total values cannot be confidently separated from the segment lines in the garbled chart.

## Instruments and services (slide 9)

- Sequencing instruments revenue (the machines themselves): $125M in Q2'26 versus $96M in Q2'25, +30% organic, +31% reported, +31% ROW organic. Currency was a 1-point headwind, and China contributed a 1-point drag to the organic figure.
- Sequencing service and other revenue (service contracts, training and similar): $154M versus $136M, +13% organic, +14% reported, +14% ROW organic.
- Microarray revenue (older chip-based genotyping products; table only): +21% reported, -6% organic, -4% ROW organic. Acquisitions added 25 points to reported growth, so the underlying business declined. Six-month organic was -13% (ROW -12%).

## Profitability (slide 10 and appendix)

| Non-GAAP measure | Q2'26 | Q2'25 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gross margin | 68.2% | 69.4% | down 120 bps |
| Operating margin | 22.5% | 23.8% | down 130 bps |
| Diluted EPS | $1.31 | $1.19 | up 10% |

(A "bp" or basis point is one-hundredth of a percentage point.) Margins fell year over year even as EPS grew 10%. In dollars, non-GAAP gross profit was $790M (vs $735M) and non-GAAP operating profit was $260M (vs $252M).

Non-GAAP expense lines, as a share of revenue: R&D (research and development) was $252M or 21.7% (vs $243M, 22.9%), a lower share; SG&A (selling, general and administrative) was $278M or 24.0% (vs $241M, 22.7%), a higher share. This SG&A increase explains much of the operating margin decline. Non-GAAP other expense, net was $(8)M versus $(10)M. The non-GAAP tax rate was 20.5% (vs 22.2%); the GAAP rate was 20.5% (vs 23.4%).

Six-month figures: non-GAAP gross margin 68.2% (vs 68.4%), operating margin 22.2% (vs 22.1%, $499M vs $464M), non-GAAP EPS $2.46 (vs $2.16).

## GAAP versus non-GAAP reconciliation

GAAP (the standard accounting measure) results for Q2'26: diluted EPS $1.35 (vs $1.49 in Q2'25); gross profit $770M, 66.4% margin (vs $695M, 65.6%); operating profit $245M, 21.1% margin (vs $214M, 20.2%); R&D $252M; SG&A $273M; other income, net $15M (vs $92M).

EPS bridge from GAAP $1.35 to non-GAAP $1.31 in Q2'26 (Q2'25 in parentheses): acquisition-related costs +$0.07 (+$0.03); transformational initiatives +$0.03 (+$0.06); strategic investment gain, net -$0.15 (-$0.65); intangible impairment none (+$0.15); tax provision +$0.01 (+$0.11). GAAP EPS was reduced a year ago by a large investment-gain effect, which fell sharply this year.

Explanations of adjustments (footnotes):
- Acquisition-related, Q2'26 and year-to-date: intangible amortization $18M and $35M; SomaLogic inventory step-up amortization $2M and $8M; SomaLogic and GRAIL acquisition expenses $7M and $22M; net gains on contingent consideration (payments owed on acquisitions, remeasured) of $(16)M and $(32)M. Prior year: amortization $16M and $33M; acquisition expenses $9M and $15M; contingent consideration gains $(21)M and $(32)M.
- Transformational initiatives in 2026: mainly costs of upgrading the ERP (enterprise software) system; in 2025: mainly employee severance from restructuring.
- Strategic investment gains and losses are realized and unrealized gains, losses and impairments on investments.
- "Other" in 2025: $3M for board membership changes and $2M legal accrual.
- Intangible impairment of $23M in Q2'25 (none in 2026).

Six-month GAAP: EPS $2.22 (vs $2.31); operating profit $454M, 20.2% (vs $378M, 18.0%); gross margin 66.2% (vs 65.6%); GAAP tax rate 18.4% (vs 25.1%).

## Guidance (slide 12, as of July 30, 2026)

Illumina's guidance is non-GAAP and year-over-year. It says it cannot reconcile forward non-GAAP guidance to GAAP because items such as acquisition costs, contingent consideration changes, investment gains and losses, impairments, restructuring and litigation outcomes are unpredictable.

Context from the deck: Q2'26 operating margin of 22.5% sits below the full-year guided range, implying margin expansion in the second half (Q3 guided at about 24%). The deck does not state a change from prior guidance, so whether it was raised, lowered or reiterated cannot be determined from this document.

## Risks and strategic factors flagged

The deck's forward-looking statement lists risk factors, of which these are specific to Illumina's current situation:
- Growth of proteomics (protein measurement) markets, and the integration of SomaLogic and certain Standard BioTools assets, including manufacturing SomaLogic products and managing partner and customer relationships in proteomics.
- Illumina's inclusion on China's "unreliable entities list" and its uncertain effect.
- U.S. tariffs and trade measures, related court proceedings and possible refund or relief programs.
- Possible further litigation tied to the GRAIL acquisition (GRAIL is a cancer-detection company Illumina bought).
- Competing products, new product launches affecting existing ones, reliance on third-party suppliers, payor reimbursement, regulatory clearances, and general economic conditions or armed conflict.
