## Headline view

[Bloomberg Intelligence, Palmer, 2025-11-13] Illumina's reach in genomic tools (the instruments and consumables used to read DNA) was "unparalleled for more than a decade." The company serves a global market that spans research customers and clinical customers. It offers an ever-broadening lineup of systems intended to provide "a solution for every setting."

On competition, the analyst says it is increasing from new entrants such as Element Biosciences and Ultima Genomics, and that Roche's return to the sequencing market "on the horizon." The report does not give details of the competitors' products, pricing or share.

## Section 1: Customer base expands with new applications

### Who the customers are

[Bloomberg Intelligence, 2025-11-13] Illumina's customers are diverse both geographically and by application. Genetic sequencing is increasingly common in commercial markets such as oncology (cancer), agriculture and reproductive health, and these markets are bringing Illumina new customers.

### Geography

[Bloomberg Intelligence, 2025-11-13] The majority of Illumina's sales are still domestic (in the United States). The market abroad is growing as more countries fund genomic research. A chart in the report, "Revenue by Geography," splits sales by region and shows categories including Greater China, Asia Pacific and Europe. The chart text was garbled in the source, so no figures can be read from it.

### Government funding and its risks

[Bloomberg Intelligence, 2025-11-13] Government funding accounts for almost half of Illumina's sales. U.S. government action can temporarily cap growth. The analyst's example is that budget sequestration (automatic across-the-board federal spending cuts) and a federal shutdown did this in 2012.

### Covid-19 effect

[Bloomberg Intelligence, 2025-11-13] Covid-19 "added another wrinkle." The timeline for academic and research labs to reopen was thought to dictate the pace of Illumina's recovery, but 2021 instead saw record demand across instruments.

### Shift toward clinical (updated 03/11/26)

[Bloomberg Intelligence, update 2026-03-11] Growth is shifting toward clinical end-markets. Consumables (the reagent kits customers must buy for every sequencing run) are now 60% of sequencing revenue. The analyst attributes this to NovaSeq X volume and oncology demand. The source gives no prior-year comparison for the 60% figure.

## Section 2: Genetic sequencing for every customer

### Positioning

[Bloomberg Intelligence, 2025-11-13] Illumina leads the market in DNA sequencing with a broad array of systems. Researchers can choose depending on the speed, data output and cost per run they need.

### Product lineup

- **NovaSeq X series:** This is the latest flagship. The analyst says it was launched at Illumina's 2022 Genomics Forum at its lowest cost per gigabase (a gigabase is one billion DNA letters read). It is described as "a critical catalyst for the portfolio" with 2.5x the throughput of existing offerings.
- **iSeq and MiSeq:** For smaller labs, these use Illumina's chemistry in a small footprint at a competitive price.
- **NextSeq line:** This is the mid-throughput line. The analyst says demand for it is a key driver of Illumina's expansion into clinical applications.
- **HiSeq X Ten:** First offered in 2014, it broke the $1,000-per-genome barrier.
- **XLEAP-SBS:** This is a chemistry upgrade to SBS (sequencing by synthesis, Illumina's core method of reading DNA). It is projected to push costs below $200 per genome. This is the analyst's projection, not a stated company result.

A graphic titled "Illumina's Portfolio of Sequencers" (tagged 03/11/26) shows the range of instruments, but its contents were not legible in the source.

## Section 3: Diverse, growing set of market opportunities

### Size and penetration

[Bloomberg Intelligence, 2025-11-13] Illumina's sequencing technology is gradually being deployed into new applications, which gives the company a long runway of growth. The company divides its addressable markets into two categories with more than $125 billion in potential sales. It significantly penetrates only the research market, its current core business, with an estimated 34% captured by 2027, "as of the 2022 investor day." This is a company estimate from 2022, not a current measurement.

### Clinical path

[Bloomberg Intelligence, 2025-11-13] The plan to penetrate the clinical market depends on increased biomarker profiling (testing patients for specific molecular markers) and on physicians' use of downstream sequencing applications.

### Liquid biopsy

[Bloomberg Intelligence, 2025-11-13] The next opportunity is the $800 million liquid biopsy market (blood tests that detect disease such as cancer from DNA in the blood). Products here would be commercialized in areas like cancer screening and monitoring, where Illumina "hoped to compete via Grail." The past tense signals that the Grail route is no longer available as originally planned; the report does not explain why.
