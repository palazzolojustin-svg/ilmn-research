# Barclays on Illumina (ILMN), 5 February 2026

## Rating, price target and valuation

Barclays analyst Luke Sergott keeps Illumina at **Underweight** (the stock is expected to underperform the average expected total return of the analyst's coverage universe over 12 months). The industry view on U.S. Life Science & Diagnostic Tools is **Positive**, also unchanged. The price target is **$110, unchanged**. The share price at the 5 February 2026 close was $133.61, so the target implies about **17.7% downside**. [Barclays, 2026-02-05]

**Valuation method.** The $110 target applies a **21x price-to-earnings multiple** (the share price divided by one year of earnings per share) to Barclays' FY26 EPS estimate of **$5.14**. The prior target used **22x on $5.00** of EPS. The target is unchanged because the estimates barely moved. [Barclays, 2026-02-05]

**Scenario values.**
- Upside case: **$150**. This assumes the multiple expands on the "Core ILMN" business if management delivers on its high-single-digit long-term growth framework.
- Downside case: **$60**. This assumes the multiple contracts on the Core ILMN business as competitors eat into Illumina's competitive moat.
- The 52-week range is $68.70 to $155.53. [Barclays, 2026-02-05]

**Stated reason for Underweight.** Illumina leads next-generation sequencing (NGS, the technology that reads DNA at scale), where Barclays projects significant growth from large research studies and clinical diagnostics. But near-term growth issues, emerging competitors and an extended valuation mean the analyst "struggle[s] to see much upside to current prices." [Barclays, 2026-02-05]

## Core thesis

**Headline view.** The 1Q guide sets up a second-half-weighted ramp (more of the year's growth is expected late in the year) just as stiff competition is expected in 2H. Shares have held in relatively well versus the group over the past two weeks. The analyst sees downside from headline risk at AGBT (the Advances in Genome Biology and Technology industry conference) at the end of the month. The title frames it as a "Hulk Hogan vs. The Big Show" matchup at this year's AGBT. [Barclays, 2026-02-05]

**Positives the analyst acknowledges.**
- There is "plenty of momentum" on the clinical side. Illumina exited 4Q25 with close to **20% growth in clinical sequencing consumables** (the reagent kits used for every sequencing run). This is ex-China.
- The oncology NGS testing market shows little sign of slowing.
- Growth was driven by liquid biopsy (cancer blood tests) and NGS-based assays across oncology testing.
- The macro environment is stable, and research demand improved as 2025 progressed.
- With research budgets expected to be better in 2026, the analyst's thinking is that sequencing consumables should grow at least mid-single digits.

**Concerns.**
- *Soft 1Q and guide.* The analyst says Illumina "keeps up the trend" of a 1Q guide much softer than expected, producing a 2H-weighted ramp. Management's guide is for only a **modest improvement in sequencing consumables** for the year and for **instruments to be slightly down to flat**. A normal reading would assume "a healthy dose of conservatism" is baked into the guide.
- *How investors are reading it.* With many competitive launches coming this year, investors are reading the guide as signalling a market freeze (customers waiting for competing products) and/or a slowdown in clinical. There was also a steep drop-off in high-throughput (HT) instrument placements in 1Q after a very strong 4Q. The market's thinking is that there were pull-forwards or a backlog flush ahead of competitive launches.
- *Management's response.* Management was clear that pull-forward is **not** the reason for the guide. It also does not see a fully recovered market dynamic through the year.
- *Research funding.* There are still funding issues on the research side. Some of Illumina's biggest accounts are institutions "in the cross-hairs of funding cuts w/in the current administration," so "everything is not back to normal."
- *Investment priorities.* Management continues to invest to diversify into multi-omics (measuring several types of biological molecules, not only DNA), workflow simplification and automation, and a comprehensive analytical software ecosystem around DRAGEN (Illumina's data-analysis software).
- *Investor posture.* Given the guide, the macro, and expected meaningful announcements at AGBT, investors are likely hitting pause to see how the year shakes out. As with the rest of the group, investors have "little risk appetite for a 2H-weighted guide," especially one that already bakes in some improvement across sequencing consumables and a normal instrument cadence (a bigger 4Q placement seasonality).

**Competition and pricing.**
- The analyst cites reports that **Roche is looking to price a $100 genome** (the cost of sequencing one human genome). The note says Roche is "supposedly coming in at $100/genome." How meaningful Roche's launch will be "remains to be seen."
- At those prices, Illumina would have to fend off a "well capitalized competitor that can aggressively launch in both research and clinical markets."
- The analyst thinks there could be **another round of pricing headwinds in 2027 and beyond**, which would weigh on outyear growth.
- The analyst thinks the **research market is more at risk in the near term than clinical**, because the clinical customer base is sticky.

**Conclusion.** Barclays thinks Illumina is likely to achieve the guide. It struggles to see upside, which is "what is needed at these valuations," especially as the stock has held in well versus the group over the past two weeks.

### Guide details (analyst's notes)

- **SomaLogic** (a proteomics company, i.e. protein measurement, that Illumina is acquiring/integrating; it adds to 2026): a **1.5%–2% tailwind** to revenue growth.
- **Multiomics (OMx):** a 100 bps headwind.
- **EPS:** an 18 cent headwind.
- **Organic growth ex-China:** +2% to +4%. Overall demand is expected to be similar to 2H25.
- **Clinical consumables:** growth from the low double digits to the mid-teens percent.
- **Academic:** a mid- to high-single-digit decline in this market. No change in markets is assumed year over year, and NIH (National Institutes of Health) improvement would be upside.
- **Research and applied consumables:** flat to slightly down.
- **Instruments:** flat to down, with 50–60 NovaSeq X instruments per quarter.
- **Foreign exchange:** a 1% tailwind.
- **China sales:** $210–220mn, a roughly 1% headwind.
- **Rest of world (ROW) organic sequencing revenue growth:** low- to mid-single digits.
- **Operating margin:** 23.3%–23.5%, which is up 130 bps excluding M&A (mergers and acquisitions).
- **1Q26 organic growth ex-China:** 1%–3%.
- **1Q26 EPS:** $1.02–1.07, including 4 cents of dilution from SomaLogic. (Barclays' own 1Q26 estimate and the consensus are both $1.14, so the guide sits below both. This is the "much softer than expected" 1Q guide referred to in the text.)

## Key takeaways from the call (4Q25 call; summarised by Barclays)

**BioInsight.** Illumina launched BioInsight in 4Q25 for AI-enabled drug development, with interest and key partnerships from large pharma.
- BioInsight supports discovery and drug development through data, software and AI, and with tools to perturb biology (deliberately alter genes) using CRISPR (gene-editing technology) at genome-wide scale. It uses AI to analyze data and build predictive biological models.
- The "Billion Cell Atlas" is claimed to be the most comprehensive map of human biology for drug discovery. It was built using single-cell technology, CRISPR-based perturbation and AI. It has seen strong interest from biopharma, with initial collaborations with AstraZeneca (AZN), Merck (MCK) and Eli Lilly (LLY). (Tickers as given in the source.)

**Multiomics as a long-term growth pillar.** Multiomics investment is increasingly a long-term growth pillar, using the scale of the platform to capture more of the workflow.
- Illumina launched **Illumina Connected Multiomics**, a software platform integrating genomics, proteomics, single-cell and ABE genomics data. (Written as "ABE genomics" in the source.)
- It remains on track to introduce its **spatial transcriptomics** solution (mapping gene activity within tissue) and its **Constellation mapped-read technology** in **1H26**.
- Management expects multiomics combined with BioInsight to drive **at least 1%–2% revenue growth in 2027**, with momentum accelerating in subsequent years. [4Q25 call, as summarised by Barclays]
