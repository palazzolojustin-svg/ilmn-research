**Core argument [Freedom Broker]:** Illumina delivered a strong Q1, with revenue, margin and earnings per share (EPS, profit per share) all above expectations. Q1 revenue of $1.091B beat consensus (the average analyst forecast) by $18M and beat Freedom Broker's own estimate by $16M. Non-GAAP EPS (earnings excluding certain items such as acquisition-related costs and stock compensation, as the company defines them) of $1.15 beat both consensus and Freedom Broker by $0.1.

The after-hours stock reaction was mixed, roughly -1%. The analyst says this looks unjustified given the combination of NovaSeq X strength (NovaSeq X is Illumina's newest high-throughput DNA sequencing instrument), margin leverage (profit margins rising faster than revenue), and higher FY26 EPS guidance. The analyst expects positive momentum over the next 1-2 trading days, because the market should focus more on clinical-led demand for instruments and consumables (the reagent kits customers must buy for every sequencing run) and less on the still-weak research/applied trends.

## Q1 2026 results: why the beat is "high quality"

The analyst states that the beat was broad enough to be considered high quality. The supporting facts are:

- **Revenue:** up 4.8% year over year (YoY) to $1.091B.
- **Organic growth outside China:** "ROW" (rest of world, meaning excluding China) organic growth was 3.5%, which the note says was above management's guidance range.
- **Instrument demand was the key driver:** Illumina placed more than 80 NovaSeq X systems in the quarter, versus a quarterly target of 50-60 systems. Sequencing instruments revenue rose 9% YoY.
- **Clinical demand:** clinical remained the main demand engine. Ex-China clinical sequencing consumables grew 20% for the second consecutive quarter.
- **Margin:** non-GAAP operating margin (operating profit as a share of revenue, on the company's adjusted basis) expanded 150 basis points (1.5 percentage points) YoY to 21.9%.
- **EPS:** non-GAAP EPS rose 19% YoY to $1.15.
- **Weak spot:** ex-China research and applied consumables were down 12% (research and applied covers customers such as academic labs and industrial users, as opposed to clinical).

## Thesis and interpretation [Freedom Broker]

- **The real surprise:** the analyst says it was the evidence that NovaSeq X demand is stronger than assumed, and that the instrument outlook is moving from stabilization toward modest growth.
- **Growth mix:** the consumables segment remains the primary growth engine in 2026, and instruments is likely to stabilize rather than grow (the note frames instruments growth as a modest upside to that base case).
- **Growth bridge:** the print improves confidence in the 2026-2027 growth bridge, especially because new NovaSeq X placements should support future consumables revenue (each installed system generates recurring reagent purchases).
- **Research/applied weakness:** this remains a constraint (ex-China research and applied consumables down 12%), but the analyst thinks the market is likely to look through it given the stronger clinical signal, the buyback authorization (a plan to repurchase company shares; the note gives no size), and the upgraded EPS guidance.

## Valuation method [Freedom Broker]

The Buy rating and $140 target rest on a weighted valuation:

- 50% weight: a 5-year discounted cash flow model (DCF, which values a company on the present value of its future cash flows) using the Free Cash Flow to the Firm (FCFF) method.
- 25% weight: relative valuation on EV/Sales NTM (enterprise value divided by next-twelve-months sales) at 7x.
- 25% weight: relative valuation on P/E NTM (price divided by next-twelve-months earnings) at 30x.

## Key risks [Freedom Broker]

1. **Competitive pressure in NGS (next-generation sequencing).** Rising competition in high- and mid-throughput segments could intensify pricing pressure on consumables and instruments, limiting margin expansion and complicating monetization of the installed base.
2. **Macro and budgetary headwinds.** Persistent weakness in academic funding may constrain demand for instruments and consumables in the research segment, increasing risk to sustained mid-term revenue growth.
3. **China geopolitical and regulatory risk.** Inclusion on the UEL (China's Unreliable Entity List) continues to restrict instrument sales and creates ongoing downside risk related to potential changes in import/export regulations.
