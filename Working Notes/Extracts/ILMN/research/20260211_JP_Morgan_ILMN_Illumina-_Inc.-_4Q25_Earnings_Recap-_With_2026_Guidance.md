# J.P. Morgan 4Q25 Earnings Recap for Illumina – 2026 Guidance Initiated, Neutral Rating, Price Target Cut to $120

*Source file: `Working Notes/Sources/ILMN/research/20260211_JP_Morgan_ILMN_Illumina-_Inc.-_4Q25_Earnings_Recap-_With_2026_Guidance.md` · Date: 2026-02-06 · J.P. Morgan North America Equity Research; analysts Casey Woodring (lead, certifying analyst), Marta Nazarovets Zaremba, Jaden Rismay, Sebastian Sandler · sell-side research*

**Summary.** J.P. Morgan reviews Illumina's fourth-quarter 2025 results (revenue of about $1.16B, up 5% y/y, with a beat versus the Street) and its first 2026 guidance (organic growth of +1-3%, adjusted EPS of $5.05-5.20). The firm keeps a Neutral rating and cuts its December 2026 price target to $120 from $130, citing competitive uncertainty (especially Roche's expected consumables pricing, to be signalled at the AGBT conference), pricing pressure, and a back-half-weighted margin ramp. The report also gives 1Q26 guidance, instrument pull-through assumptions, JPM's forecasts, a DCF valuation, and risks.

## Overview and Rating

**Publication:** J.P. Morgan equity research note dated 6 February 2026 (completed 03:14 AM EST), titled "4Q25 Earnings Recap: With 2026 Guidance Embedding Similar Trends Seen in 2H25, Competitive Uncertainty Comes into Focus Ahead AGBT". It covers Illumina (ILMN), a maker of DNA sequencing instruments and the reagent kits ("consumables") that customers must buy for each run. AGBT is a major genomics technology conference held at the end of February.

**Rating and price target (JPM, 2026-02-06):**
- Rating: **Neutral** (JPM defines this as expecting the stock to perform in line with the average total return of the analyst team's coverage universe over the price-target horizon). The rating is unchanged; JPM has rated ILMN Neutral continuously since at least February 2023.
- Price target (Dec-26): **$120**, down from **$130** prior. JPM says the cut is "based on our updated forecasts and DCF assumptions."
- Share price on 5 Feb 2026: **$133.61**. The $120 target therefore sits about 10% below the then-current price.
- Valuation method: discounted cash flow (DCF, which values a company by discounting its projected future cash flows to today), using a CAPM-derived WACC (weighted average cost of capital, the discount rate) of **10.2%** and terminal growth (the assumed perpetual growth rate after the forecast period) of **4.0%**.

**Core stance (JPM):** JPM still sees strong long-term clinical traction for next-generation sequencing (NGS), with possible growing contributions from the multiomics pipeline (products that measure several biological layers, not just DNA). But "ongoing competitive concerns and pressured pricing dynamics" keep it on the sidelines. It says it will "remain on the sidelines until there is more visibility on how the landscape could evolve."

**Key framing by JPM:**
- The clinical transition "continues to see encouraging momentum," but there is "pushback on the 2H margin ramp implied by the guide."
- Investor focus now turns to AGBT at the end of February, where JPM expects Roche to indicate where it intends to price its sequencing consumables. JPM describes "competitive noise heating up."
- The 2026 guidance assumes the academic end market looks like 2025, while the clinical end market is expected to grow strongly on rising adoption of sequencing-based diagnostic tests and volumes.

## 4Q25 Results (reported early Feb 2026)

### Headline numbers
- **Revenue:** about **$1.16B**, up **5% y/y reported**, **+4% in constant currency (cc)**, and **+7% cc excluding China**. This was roughly in line with the company's January preannouncement and above the then-Street estimate of **$1.10B**.
- **Why it beat:** more than **100 NovaSeq X placements** (the NovaSeq X is Illumina's newest high-throughput sequencer) versus the preannounced ">95". The total installed base reached about **890** instruments. Over **60%** of X systems placed in 4Q went to clinical customers.
- **Adjusted gross margin:** fell **40 bps** (0.4 percentage point) y/y to **67.0%**, below Street of 68.1% (68.2% in another passage). Tariffs cost **205 bps**. Excluding tariffs, gross margin improved **165 bps q/q**.
- **Operating expenses (core ILMN):** fell **$24M** y/y to **$502M**, the result of multiyear cost reduction programs and prioritizing growth investments.
- **Adjusted operating margin (OPM):** rose **400 bps** y/y to **23.7%**, versus Street of 22.5% and an implied guide of about 22.8% at the midpoint. JPM credits improved operating leverage from a better cost structure.
- **Adjusted EPS:** **$1.35**, above the preannouncement range of $1.27-1.30, the then-Street of $1.21, and the guide midpoint of $1.20.
- **Free cash flow:** **$267M**, versus Street of $412M (a miss on this line).
- **Tax rate:** 19.5%. **Buybacks:** 337k shares repurchased for $42M.

### Revenue by segment (4Q25)
- **Sequencing instruments:** **$154M**, up **3% y/y cc ex-China**, driven by the stronger NovaSeq X placements.
- **Sequencing consumables:** **$755M**, up **8% cc**; excluding China, **$713M**, up **11%** cc. Drivers: continued customer transition to the NovaSeq X and clinical customers growing **20% ex-China** on broader NGS-based testing. Management noted traction from customers converting current assays into ones needing "significantly more data," such as whole genome sequencing (WGS).
- **Sequencing activity:** total activity on high- and mid-throughput instruments rose **more than 30% y/y**, following more than 30% growth in 3Q25.
- **Service and other:** **$157M**, up **4%** y/y cc ex-China, driven by the timing of strategic partnerships and data deals, which were "lumpy" (uneven) in 2025.

## 2026 Guidance (initiated at 4Q25 report)

### Revenue
- **Organic (cc) growth:** **+1.0-3.0%** y/y (JPM estimate had been +2.8%). **Ex-China:** **+2.0-4.0%**.
- **Reported revenue:** **$4.50-4.60B**, versus Street of $4.41B.
- **Bridge from organic to reported:** M&A adds +1.5-2.0%, FX adds +1.0%, and China subtracts 1.0%.
- JPM's own 2026E revenue is $4,549M (+4.7% y/y) against 2025 revenue of $4,343M.
- **Management's method [4Q25 call, per JPM]:** the company took the average of 2H25 to bracket guidance, with the 3Q25 growth rate as the low end (a quarter in which clinical still grew at a double-digit rate). Management said it is not seeing a change in momentum despite the step down from the 4Q exit rate to the 2026 guide, particularly in clinical.

### Instruments and consumables
- Sequencing instruments: low-single-digit decline to flat cc y/y ex-China.
- Assumes **50-60 NovaSeq X placements per quarter** in 2026.
- Consumables: low-to-mid-single-digit cc growth y/y ex-China, driven by strong clinical growth.
- **NovaSeq X transition:** pricing headwinds (price declines as customers move to the X) should begin to ease for research customers in **2H26**, because about **90%** of academic customers' high-throughput sequencing volume is already on the X. Clinical volumes are now **more than two-thirds** converted, so clinical pricing dynamics will be tied to new applications such as WGS that bring higher volumes. The company expects the conversion to be **substantially complete by end of 2026**.

### End markets
- **Research / academic and government (A&G):** management sees signs of stabilization, including better visibility on U.S. policy and funding, but expects 2026 dynamics similar to 2025, meaning **mid-single-digit to high-single-digit declines** in research and applied consumables revenue. Recent NIH (National Institutes of Health) budget announcements and resumed funding flows could prove more favorable than assumed. Still, details are needed before many customers can spend on instruments as normal, and there is caution on grant disbursement. Management said research improvement would provide upside to the guide.
- **Clinical:** guidance assumes **double-digit to mid-teens** clinical consumables growth, continuing the strong 2H25. Customers are making progress with on-market tests and new sequencing-intensive whole genome approaches.
- **China:** expected sales of **$210-220M**, with little or no step-up in instrument sales in 1H26. Illumina will revisit the assumption as it works with the government to import instruments into the country.
- **Product launches:** on track to unveil its **spatial transcriptomics** solution (measuring gene activity within tissue location) and **constellation mapped reads** technology in 1H26.

### Margins and EPS
- **Adjusted OPM:** about **23.3-23.5%**, versus Street of 23.7%, including roughly a **-1.0 point** impact from the **SomaLogic** acquisition (a protein-measurement business Illumina acquired).
- **Adjusted EPS:** **$5.05-5.20**, versus Street of $5.07, including **$0.18 dilution** from SomaLogic. Excluding that dilution, EPS growth would be about **+10%** y/y.
- Other items: net other income/expense about **-$65M**; capex about **-$225M**; adjusted diluted share count about **155M**; tax rate **20.5%**.

### 1Q26 guidance (below JPM's initial expectations)
- Organic cc growth of **-1.0% to +1.0%** y/y (JPM had 1.4%), with FX +2.0%, M&A +1.5%, China -2.0%.
- Adjusted OPM about **20.2-20.7%** (Street 22.1%), including a -1.0 point acquisition impact.
- Adjusted EPS **$1.02-1.07** (Street $1.14), including a -$0.04 acquisition impact.
- Net other income/expense about -$15M, share count about 155M, tax rate 20.5%.
- JPM's own 1Q26E: revenue $1,076M, adjusted EPS $1.05.

### 2027 targets (reiterated)
- Management says it is on track for long-term 2027 targets: core growth of **high-single-digit** and **+500 bps** of OPM expansion, reaching **26.0% adjusted OPM** by 2027.
- It expanded adjusted OPM by **+200 bps** in 2025 despite a **-200 bps** headwind from "tariffs and other things," and is now committing to a further **+130 bps** in 2026 (excluding acquisitions).
- SomaLogic is dilutive at the start, but management is working through synergies and will get "as close to" the 2026 and 2027 OPM targets as possible.

### Instrument pull-through assumptions for 2026
Pull-through is the annual consumables revenue a placed instrument generates. Management gave:
- NovaSeq X: **$1.35-1.40M** per year
- NovaSeq 6000: **$600-650k**
- NextSeq 1k/2k: **$80-100k**
- NextSeq 500/550: **$90-110k**

### Competition
Management said on the call that, ahead of AGBT, it believes cost per genome is not the only parameter customers use, and that customers value the entire workflow, where Illumina has "the portfolio and pipeline to win." JPM, by contrast, expects Roche to signal its consumables pricing at AGBT and flags that as the next catalyst.

## DCF Detail
JPM's DCF (Figure 1) values the company using revenue growth, tax-affected EBIT margins and perpetuity growth, with sensitivity grids for discount rate (about 9.7-10.7%) and terminal growth (3.0-5.0%). The base case (about 10.2% and 4.0%) maps to JPM's $120 target. The grid's equity value per share ranges roughly from the high $90s to $170s across scenarios, and terminal value represents about 75-86% of enterprise value (the figures are partly garbled in the source). Projected revenue: $4,549M (2026), about $4,886M (2027), $5,147M (2028), $5,429M (2029), $5,685M (2030).

## Risks (JPM)
- **Upside:** (1) faster-than-expected recovery in academic markets; (2) stronger clinical growth from more sequencing-based clinical applications; (3) broader uptake of whole genome sequencing; (4) expanding use cases for multiomic offerings.
- **Downside:** (1) ongoing uncertainty or further pressure in academic research funding; (2) intensifying competition and potential pricing pressure in sequencing; (3) execution risks in launching and getting adoption of new products.
