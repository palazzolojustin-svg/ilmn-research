# UBS Research Note on Illumina (ILMN) after Q4 2025 Results and 2026 Guidance: Neutral Rating, Price Target Raised to $135

*Source file: `Working Notes/Sources/ILMN/research/20260211_UBS_ILMN_Illumina_Inc_-Encouraging_Clinical_Signal_the_Story_of_t.md` · Date: 2026-02-05 · UBS Global Research (UBS Securities LLC); analysts Dan Leonard, Lu Li, Nathan Bolanos (associate analyst) · sell-side research*

**Summary.** UBS keeps its Neutral rating on Illumina and raises its 12-month price target to $135 from $120, based on about 15x its 2027 EBITDA estimate (up from 14x). The analysts like the 20% Q4 growth and mid-teens 2026 guide for clinical (medical-use) sequencing consumables, but say Illumina's +1-3% organic sales growth guide for 2026 compares poorly with peers. UBS cut 2026/2027 EPS slightly (to $5.07/$5.74), now including dilution from the SomaLogic acquisition, while raising its longer-term clinical growth outlook.

## About this document

This is a short UBS Global Research note on Illumina Inc. (ticker ILMN, the largest maker of DNA sequencing instruments and the reagent kits and services that go with them). It was dated 5 February 2026, the day Illumina's share price closed at US$133.61. The rating was finalized on 6 February 2026 at 04:15 AM GMT. The analysts are Dan Leonard, Lu Li and associate analyst Nathan Bolanos. The report was prepared by UBS Securities LLC. The note was written after Illumina reported fourth-quarter 2025 results and gave its 2026 guidance. The text of the note is brief. Most of the content is forecast tables, valuation and disclosures. The note gives few explanatory details beyond its headline conclusions.

## Core thesis ("Bottom Line")

[UBS, 2026-02-05] The Neutral rating "balances a muted sales growth outlook (lesser than peers') with outsized margin expansion opportunity." The analysts say the Q4 results and the 2026 guidance "have mixed implications for our thesis."

**Encouraging (upside) points:**
- Clinical next-generation sequencing (NGS) consumables grew 20% in Q4 2025. NGS is the high-throughput DNA-reading technology Illumina sells, and consumables are the reagent kits customers must buy for every run. "Clinical" means use in patient testing rather than research.
- Illumina guided to mid-teens (roughly 15%) growth for clinical NGS consumables in 2026. UBS calls both the Q4 figure and the 2026 guide "encouraging to the upside."

**Unfavorable points:**
- Illumina's assumption of +1-3% organic sales growth for 2026 in aggregate "compares unfavorably" to peers' growth, even though it is in line with UBS's own forecast. Organic means excluding acquisitions.

**Changes to forecasts:**
- UBS increased its 2027-and-beyond outlook to reflect better clinical growth.
- UBS says its EPS forecast "isn't meaningfully changed" and now includes dilution from the acquisition of SomaLogic. The cash flow forecast shows a US$350 million net acquisitions outflow in 2026. The note does not say that figure is SomaLogic, and gives no deal terms.

## Valuation method and reasoning

- The price target of US$135 rests on an enterprise value (market cap plus net debt, using projected debt and cash levels) of about 15x UBS's 2027 EBITDA estimate. The prior multiple was 14x. The note's method statement says the target is "based on our TTM Dec-27E EV/EBITDA," meaning trailing-twelve-month EBITDA as of December 2027.
- UBS raised the multiple "to reflect greater confidence in the growth trajectory." It keeps a discount to the Tools peer group (life-science tools companies) "to reflect the lower relative growth outlook vs peers."

## Company description (from the note)

Illumina is described as "the largest provider of next-generation sequencing instruments and related consumables/services, as well as microarrays." It sells to academic and government research labs, clinical labs, pharmaceutical companies, agricultural companies and consumer genomics companies. UBS says it has differentiated itself with rapid product cycles that substantially increased throughput and decreased the cost of DNA/RNA sequencing. It is based in San Diego, California.

## Risks (UBS)

Company-specific: the NovaSeq ramp (NovaSeq is Illumina's high-throughput sequencer line) could slow significantly; the sequencing market could become saturated; pricing pressure could push sequencing prices down faster than expected; the clinical diagnostics market could fail to open up; and academic funding could be cut.

Industry-wide:
1. A decline in pharma spending, due to significant M&A, reduced funding for small companies, or prominent trial failures affecting classes of drugs.
2. Slowing global GDP growth, including escalating trade tensions.
3. Pressure on academic and government budgets, from recessionary cost-cutting or political change.
4. Pricing pressure in diagnostics from the Protecting Access to Medicare Act (PAMA), a US law governing Medicare lab-test pricing.
5. Changes in competitive dynamics, including disruptive new entrants in certain technologies.
6. Inability of companies to compete effectively in China, called "a critical growth market."
7. Foreign exchange fluctuations or tax policy changes.

## Rating definitions and disclosures worth noting

- UBS's 12-month ratings: Buy if forecast stock return (FSR) exceeds the market return assumption (MRA) by more than 6%; Neutral if within ±6%; Sell if more than 6% below. The MRA is the one-year local interest rate plus 5%. Because of ILMN's volatility, the bands are ±15% (note 20).
- Global rating distribution as of 31 December 2025: Buy 52%, Neutral 41%, Sell 7%.
- Disclosures: UBS Securities LLC makes a market in ILMN securities (note 16). UBS holds a long or short position of 0.5% or more of the company's listed shares (note 28). UBS says it does and seeks to do business with covered companies, which may create a conflict of interest.
