# J.P. Morgan alert: Illumina preannounces 4Q25 results (13 January 2026)

## The preannouncement: what Illumina reported

Illumina (a maker of DNA sequencing instruments and the reagent kits, called consumables, used on them) released preliminary fourth-quarter 2025 results on the morning of the note. J.P. Morgan described them as "solid" [JPM, 2026-01-13]. The figures below are as J.P. Morgan reported them.

### Total revenue

- Revenue was about **$1.16B**.
- The Street (consensus of Wall Street analysts) expected about $1.10B.
- Illumina's own guidance was $1.09-1.13B.
- Growth was **+4% year over year in constant currency** ("cc", which removes the effect of exchange-rate changes). Illumina had guided to flat growth year over year in constant currency.

### Revenue excluding China

- Revenue outside China was about **$1.10B**, against guidance of $1.06-1.10B, so it came in at the top of the range.
- That was **+7% year over year in constant currency**, against guidance of about +4%.
- The note does not give China's own revenue. Subtracting the two figures implies roughly $0.06B, but that is an inference and the note does not state it.

### Earnings per share

- Non-GAAP diluted EPS (earnings per share, excluding items such as acquisition-related costs and stock compensation as defined by the company) is expected to be **$1.27-1.30**.
- The Street expected **$1.21**.
- Illumina's guidance was **$1.20 at the midpoint**.
- The phrase "expects... to be in the range" means these are preliminary figures, not final reported results.

## What investors would focus on at the conference

The note says Illumina was due to present at J.P. Morgan's conference the same day at 7:30AM PST (10:30AM EST). J.P. Morgan listed what it expected investors to focus on:

- **The pace of customer transitions to the NovaSeq X platform**, Illumina's newer high-throughput sequencer, which replaces older instruments.
- **Progress on new product launches.**
- **The company's strategy for entering the multiomics market.** Multiomics means measuring several kinds of biological molecules together, such as DNA, RNA and proteins.
- **Updates on China, including the unreliable entity list.** This is a Chinese government list that can restrict foreign companies. The note names it but gives no details.
- **Medium-term growth targets.**

The note gives no analysis of these topics and does not record what management said.
