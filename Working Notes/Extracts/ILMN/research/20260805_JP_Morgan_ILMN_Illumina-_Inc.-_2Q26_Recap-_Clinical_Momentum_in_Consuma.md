# J.P. Morgan 2Q26 Recap on Illumina: Clinical Consumables and NovaSeq X Placements Drive Beat and Raise; Overweight, PT raised to $230 (Dec-27)

*Source file: `Working Notes/Sources/ILMN/research/20260805_JP_Morgan_ILMN_Illumina-_Inc.-_2Q26_Recap-_Clinical_Momentum_in_Consuma.md` · Date: 2026-07-31 · J.P. Morgan North America Equity Research; analysts Casey Woodring (lead, AC), Marta Nazarovets Zaremba, Jaden Rismay · sell-side research*

**Summary.** J.P. Morgan reviews Illumina's second-quarter 2026 results, which beat Street revenue and EPS expectations on strong clinical sequencing consumables growth (+15% ex-China) and more than 95 NovaSeq X placements. Illumina raised full-year 2026 guidance for revenue, organic growth, clinical consumables and EPS, and guided 3Q26 above Street on revenue. J.P. Morgan kept its Overweight rating and rolled its DCF forward to a Dec-2027 price target of $230 (from $220 for Dec-2026), citing +HSD revenue growth and +mid/high-teens EPS growth as achievable.

## Overview and rating

J.P. Morgan (Casey Woodring and team) published this recap on 31 July 2026, after Illumina (ILMN) reported second-quarter 2026 results. The firm kept its **Overweight** rating (it expects the stock to outperform the average total return of the analyst's coverage universe). It **raised the price target to $230 from $220**, and the target date moved from Dec-2026 to **Dec-2027**. The stock closed at $205.09 on 30 July 2026. [JPM, 2026-07-31]

The headline view is that the quarter showed continued momentum in clinical consumables (the reagent kits clinical labs must buy for every sequencing run). The growing base of NovaSeq X instruments (Illumina's newest high-throughput sequencer) installed in clinical labs is expected to drive sustainable consumables growth. J.P. Morgan sees "+HSD" (high-single-digit percent) topline growth and "+mid/high-teens" adjusted EPS growth going forward as "highly achievable." Upside could come from a sharper recovery in research end-market demand and from emerging opportunities in multi-omics (measuring several kinds of biological molecules together) and BioInsight (Illumina's AI-drug-discovery data business). [JPM, 2026-07-31]

The note refers readers to J.P. Morgan's May upgrade note (which moved the rating to Overweight) and to an NGS (next-generation sequencing) customer survey the firm ran in May 2026. It also points to its 2Q preview note, a customer survey deck and its 1Q note. The survey results themselves are not included in this document.

## Valuation and thesis

**Valuation method.** The $230 target comes from a discounted cash flow (DCF, which values a company by discounting its projected future cash flows) model. It uses a CAPM-derived WACC (weighted average cost of capital, the discount rate) of **9.8%** and terminal growth of **4.5%**. [JPM, 2026-07-31]

**Thesis.**
- Illumina's clinical franchise is anchored by major CLIA labs (U.S. clinical laboratories certified under federal quality rules). These labs have largely moved to the NovaSeq X and face meaningful switching friction, which J.P. Morgan says makes the user base "stickier-than-appreciated."
- Together with a multi-year test-volume runway, this underpins J.P. Morgan's +HSD organic growth forecast through 2030E.
- Structural tailwinds come from therapy selection (choosing cancer drugs from a tumor's genetics), MRD (minimal residual disease, detecting leftover cancer after treatment) and MCED (multi-cancer early detection blood tests). These are amplified by rising "sequencing intensity" as labs adopt whole-genome sequencing (WGS), RNA and multiomic workflows.
- Competition from Roche's Axelios 1 sequencer "appears manageable near-term." J.P. Morgan cites unresolved concerns about reusable flow-cell contamination and higher DNA input requirements.
- Incremental upside comes from a recovering U.S. academic and government (A&G) end market into 2H26/2027 and from Illumina's multiomics portfolio (Fluent, SomaLogic, StrataMap).
- Valuation: with +mid/high-teens adjusted EPS growth and a leading ~4-5% free-cash-flow yield, J.P. Morgan says ILMN offers "scarcity value." It trades at a **4.1x EV/EBITDA premium to Core Tools**, well below its historical ~15x premium, which the firm calls attractive "even after the recent run-up."

**Risks to rating and price target.**
1. Worse clinical and research customer attrition to Axelios.
2. Slower clinical sequencing consumables growth because of delayed guidelines or approvals in key diagnostics markets such as MRD and MCED.
3. Pricing pressure on sequencing instruments and consumables from competition.
4. Minimal improvement in the research funding environment.

## 2Q26 results versus guidance and Street

Total revenue was **$1.16B** (reported figure $1,159M), above the Street (consensus analyst estimate) of $1.13B and above Illumina's guide of $1.12-1.14B. Organic growth (excluding acquisitions, divestitures and currency effects) was **+6.5%**, versus guidance of +3-5% and J.P. Morgan's estimate of +4.1%. Growth excluding China ("ROW", rest of world) was **+8.1% organic**, versus guidance of +4-6%. J.P. Morgan attributes the beat to high-throughput consumables revenue as the NovaSeq X installed base grew and pull-through (consumables bought per instrument) rose, plus instrument strength. [2Q26 report, via JPM 2026-07-31]

### By product group

- **Sequencing instruments:** revenue $125M (Street $118M), up +30% organic (+31% ROW organic). Strength came from both the NovaSeq X and the MiSeq i100.
- **Sequencing consumables:** $775M (Street $768M), up +4% organic. ROW sequencing consumables were $734M, up +5% organic, driven by sequencing-based diagnostics and other sequencing-intensive applications.
- **Clinical consumables:** +15% ROW (vs. about +20% in 1Q). Growth exceeded +20% in USCAN (U.S. and Canada) but was slower in EMEALA (Europe, Middle East, Africa, Latin America).
- **Research and applied consumables:** -7% ROW (vs. -12% in 1Q).
- **Microarray and other:** $105M, up +21% reported but down -4% organic ROW. SomaLogic (a protein-measurement business Illumina acquired) revenue tracked at the high end of deal expectations.
- **Service and other:** $177M (Street $165M). Sequencing service and other revenues rose +13% organic (+14% ROW organic).

## Clinical franchise

- Clinical consumables grew +15% ROW, a step down from the +20% seen in each of the prior two quarters. J.P. Morgan says this reflected "pockets of softness in the Middle East and Latin America," offset by U.S. clinical consumables growth above +20%.
- **78% of clinical volumes are now on NovaSeq X.** Management expects **80-85% conversion by end of 2026** [Illumina management, 2Q26].
- Clinical sequencing Gb (gigabase, a unit of sequencing output) on connected mid- and high-throughput instruments grew "well above" the +30% seen across the whole company.
- Clinical customers placed several multi-unit capacity-expansion orders, some for new clinical trial starts.
- The full-year clinical consumables growth guide was raised to **+mid-teens** from **+DD (double-digit)/mid-teens**. J.P. Morgan says this supports a path to +HSD growth in 2027.

## Research and applied end markets

Illumina saw encouraging demand signals, including some U.S. funding releases late in the quarter. It cautioned that customers are still navigating funding uncertainty and that it is "too early to call a recovery." The full-year research sequencing consumables outlook was kept unchanged at **-MSD/HSD (mid- to high-single-digit) declines**. The 2026 guide does not assume research improvement relative to prior expectations. [Illumina management, 2Q26, via JPM]

## NovaSeq X placements and instruments

- Illumina placed **more than 95 NovaSeq X** instruments in 2Q, above the guide of about 80 (similar to 1Q) and above 1Q26's >80. Placement mix remained about **70% clinical / 30% research**.
- Several multi-unit capacity-expansion orders were noted, including some tied to new clinical trials.
- On J.P. Morgan's follow-up, management said **as much as half of clinical placements appear to be incremental** rather than upgrades from the older NovaSeq 6000 ("6k").
- Illumina also placed over 10 NovaSeq 6000 instruments in the quarter, which J.P. Morgan called "interesting."
- Management said it continues to offer volume discounts on NovaSeq X instruments.
- Recent quarters have tracked above Illumina's original framework of 50-60 NovaSeq X placements per quarter, and management expects the elevated level to carry into 2H.
- On J.P. Morgan's follow-up, management said 2H NovaSeq X instrument *revenue growth* is not expected to match the elevated 1H rates. Placements are expected to step down q/q in 3Q and rebound q/q in 4Q.

## 3Q26 guidance

- Organic revenue growth of about **+4.5%** (JPMe ~+2.8%), with ROW organic about +4.5%.
- Reported revenue of **$1.14-1.16B** (Street $1.14B).
- Adj. OPM about **24%** (Street 24.2%), implying roughly +150bps q/q expansion, driven by stronger consumable mix, more benefit from cost actions and better efficiency.
- Adj. EPS **$1.33-1.38** (Street $1.36).

## BioInsight

BioInsight combines sequencing, perturbation tools (methods that alter genes to see effects), compute and AI to build multi-omics datasets for AI-enabled drug discovery. Its flagship **Billion Cell Atlas**, a genome-wide perturbation dataset, has delivered over **300M cells** and is generating revenue. The business now has **six biopharma partners**, three of them added after quarter-end. Management called these "early but encouraging signals" and expects more updates later in 2026.
