Glossary of recurring terms:
- **NovaSeq / NovaSeq X ("X")**: Illumina's flagship high-throughput DNA sequencing instruments. The original NovaSeq launched in 2017. The newer NovaSeq X launched in 2023.
- **Consumables**: the reagent kits and flow cells customers must buy for every sequencing run. They are the recurring revenue stream.
- **Pull-through**: annual consumables revenue generated per installed instrument.
- **Placements**: instruments sold or installed at customers.
- **Clinical vs. research customers**: clinical customers (hospitals, diagnostic labs) run sequencing as a routine workflow and are seen as stickier. Research customers (academia, government, pharma) depend on grants and budgets.
- **Core**: Illumina excluding Grail, the cancer-detection company it acquired and later spun off.
- **bps**: basis points, where 100 bps is one percentage point.
- **Operating margin**: operating profit as a share of revenue.
- **Consensus**: the average of analyst forecasts.
- **EPS**: earnings per share.

## 1. Post-2Q 2026 Earnings Outlook (2026-07-31), the newest note

**Source:** Jonathan Palmer and Fallon Helmon, Bloomberg Intelligence.

### Thesis
- Illumina's target of high-single-digit revenue growth in 2027 "appears increasingly visible." The reason is that 2026 guidance was raised to 5% revenue growth, excluding China.
- NovaSeq X placements stayed robust: 95 in 2Q 2026, after 80 in 1Q 2026.
- The company's goal of 60 placements per quarter is described as "increasingly less meaningful," because actual placements are well above it.
- Clinical customers still make up most system buyers. They accounted for 65% of sequencing consumables in 2Q 2026. That share is up 10 percentage points over two years. BI says it "adds ballast to the revenue stream, given the sticky nature of clinical workflows."
- **Primary negative:** the cadence of the second half. 4Q 2026 will need "a more marked step-up" to reach full-year goals, because 3Q guidance is "a touch softer than consensus."

### 2Q 2026 results versus expectations
- Revenue was $1.16 billion, beating consensus by 2.5%.
- EPS was $1.31, which topped estimates by 8 cents.
- Total sales rose 9% year over year. Service and other revenue grew 20% and product sales grew 7%.
- Operating margin was 22.4%, ahead of consensus by 20 bps.
- Full-year EPS guidance was raised to $5.30-$5.40, from $5.15-$5.30.
- Full-year revenue growth guidance is now about 5% excluding China. It was previously 4-6% in the preview, and 2-4% at the time of the 1Q 2026 report.

## 2. 2Q 2026 Preview (2026-07-23)

**Title:** "Illumina Charting a Path to 2H Acceleration."

- BI said Illumina's heavy exposure to academic and government end-markets positions it for an inflection. Peer commentary, however, suggested demand was stable but "yet to normalize."
- Management had signaled 2Q NovaSeq X placements would be similar to the 80 in 1Q. Visibility into 2H would push the year above the 200-240 systems guided at the start of the year.
- Sequencing consumables growth was expected to be muted, at 4% in 2Q. Clinical customers were expected to "dramatically outpace" research customers.
- Sequential margin improvement was expected to be modest. The heavy lifting toward the 23.4-23.6% full-year target would come in 2H, from cost efforts and from mitigating downward pressure from SomaLogic. SomaLogic is the proteomics (protein measurement) company Illumina acquired.
- Competition: Roche is now in the market with its Axelios system, but BI judged it "likely too early to see any real impact."

Key points:
- Consensus for 2Q was revenue of $1.13 billion and EPS of $1.23.
- Full-year guidance called for revenue growth of 4-6% and operating margin of 23.4-23.6%.
- Consumables were expected up 4% in 2Q, and instruments up 22% year over year.
- Analysts expected a 2Q operating margin of 22.2%, versus guidance of 22%.

## 3. Post-1Q 2026 Earnings Outlook (2026-05-01)

**Title:** "NovaSeq X Momentum; Caution Remains." **Analyst:** Adeline Zandi.

- Illumina beat 1Q consensus across the board. Its updated outlook and limited commentary raised questions about competitive dynamics.
- NovaSeq X placements were expected to exceed the initial guidance range. 2Q was projected to match 1Q at about 80 system sales.
- The guidance increase was modest despite the strong quarter. Revenue guidance was raised by only $20 million.
- BI believed the company was "in the early stages of trading price for volume" as it defended share against an emerging Roche offering. It also saw some conservatism in the outlook, given placement strength and clinical growth.
- Cost containment was noteworthy. Year-over-year gains in gross and operating margin added visibility to full-year targets.

Results:
- Revenue was $1.09 billion, beating consensus by 1.7%.
- EPS was $1.15, 10 cents above consensus.
- Product revenue rose 4.2% to $917 million.
- Service revenue fell 20% to $174 million.
- Operating margin was 21.9%, about 140 bps above consensus.
- Illumina reaffirmed 2026 revenue growth guidance of 2-4%. The EPS view was raised to $5.15-$5.30.

## 4. 1Q 2026 Preview (2026-04-24)

**Title:** "Illumina Awaits Recovery Beyond Clinical Strength." **Analyst:** Adeline Zandi.

- 4Q 2025 was a step in the right direction. The question was whether the momentum could last in a "stepped-down" 1Q.
- Clinical strength needed to keep offsetting softness elsewhere. Research was still pressured, and instrument demand was normalizing from a strong 4Q.
- Questions on NovaSeq X utilization, the pace of the remaining transition, and whether research pricing headwinds were abating would "carry more weight than the quarter's results."
- The 2026 setup looked better than a year earlier, but the story needed "broader support beyond clinical to feel durable."
- It was likely too early to see any impact from Roche's Axelios.

Key points:
- 1Q core revenue consensus was $1.07 billion and EPS $1.05.
- Analysts estimated a 1Q core operating margin of 20.5%, up from 20.4% in 4Q.
- Guidance then was 2026 core revenue growth of 1-3%, adjusted operating margin of 23.3-23.5%, and core EPS of $5.05-$5.20.

## 5. Post-4Q 2025 Earnings Outlook (2026-02-06)

**Title:** "Illumina Clinical Momentum Drives 2026 Outlook." **Analyst:** Adeline Zandi.

- This was a clean 4Q beat. With Roche's entrance on the horizon and "some hesitancy on 2027 profitability targets," the medium-term trajectory was "still unclear."
- Consensus had asked for little in 2026, and guidance landed modestly above expectations.
- Management was confident that double-digit clinical growth would extend into 2026. The outlook still assumed a soft research market, with upside if NIH funding loosens.
- Margin expansion was targeted at 30 bps for 2026, net of 100 bps of dilution from SomaLogic. Whether Illumina can reach its 26% operating margin hurdle in 2027 was "an open question."
- China is a lever, but at under 5% of sales it is less important to the narrative.

Results and guidance:
- Formal 2026 guidance: revenue of $4.5-$4.6 billion, EPS of $5.05-$5.20, and core operating margin of 23.4%.
- 4Q revenue was $1.16 billion, beating consensus by 4%. EPS was $1.35, topping consensus by 9%.
- Sales rose 4% in constant currency in 4Q, and were flat for full-year 2025.
- 4Q operating margin was 23.7%, versus 19.7% a year earlier.

## 6. 4Q 2025 Preview (2026-01-29)

**Title:** "Illumina Enters 2026 With Structural Momentum." **Analyst:** Adeline Zandi.

- Preliminary 4Q results put Illumina ahead of consensus. BI said the composition mattered: the beat came from clinical demand, which has more durable consumables growth, and from NovaSeq X volume starting to offset pricing drag. This sets up cleaner operating leverage into 2026.
- At the J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference, management reaffirmed the 2027 algorithm of high-single-digit revenue growth and about 500 bps of margin expansion. BI said the math leaves room for a normalization year in 2026.
- Consensus for 2026 was modest, at about 3% revenue growth and roughly 70 bps of margin gain. BI said this sets a low bar if Illumina sustains consumables momentum, avoids incremental China disruption, keeps research deterioration at bay, and holds cost control.

Key points:
- 2025 consensus was revenue of $4.3 billion, operating margin of 22.9%, and EPS of $4.74.
- Preliminary 2025 revenue was roughly $4.34 billion, with EPS of $4.76-$4.79.
- 4Q estimates were revenue of $1.10 billion and EPS of $1.22.
- The consensus 4Q operating margin was 22.7%, versus 19.7% in 4Q 2024.

## 7. Post-3Q 2025 Earnings Outlook (2025-10-31)

**Title:** "Illumina Shows Signs of Stability Nearing 2026." **Analyst:** Adeline Zandi.

- Revenue was stable and cost cuts delivered margin gains ahead of expectations. Roche's expected 2026 launch makes a more pronounced recovery less likely, but "green shoots" were emerging.
- Sequencing consumables looked to be at an inflection point, where the NovaSeq X transition drives enough volume to offset pricing headwinds.
- Profitability gains gave greater visibility on the long-term 26% operating margin target for 2027, particularly if sales growth can be kick-started toward the high-single-digit goal (excluding China).
- 2026 end-markets were projected to mirror 2H 2025. Coming multi-omics launches (products measuring more than DNA, such as proteins) add a growth vector.

Results:
- Revenue was $1.08 billion, topping consensus by 2%.
- EPS was $1.34, exceeding consensus by 17 cents.
- Core sales were flat. Within sequencing, instrument revenue grew 3%, consumables grew 3%, and service fell 2%.
- Core operating margin was 24.5%, 190 bps above the prior year.
- 2025 EPS guidance was raised to $4.65-$4.75, from $4.45-$4.55.

## 8. 3Q 2025 Preview (2025-10-24)

**Title:** "Illumina Turns to Consumables for Durable Growth." **Analyst:** Adeline Zandi.

- Cost discipline and mix boosted 2Q 2025 margins. The next step was turning efficiency gains into visible growth across the installed base and consumables.
- NovaSeq X was then 44% of high-throughput consumables revenue. Clinical customers were shifting from validation to production, and the growing backlog underpinned 2H consumables growth that could offset research weakness.
- 2H would show whether Illumina's push into data, multiomics and proteomics can drive incremental growth. This push was reinforced by its new BioInsight data and AI unit, and it faces a competitive launch from Roche in 2026.
- A streamlined cost base cushions margins. Potential tariff relief is an additional tailwind.

Key points:
- Consensus was 3Q revenue of $1.07 billion and EPS of $1.17.
- Guidance called for sequencing instruments to fall 4-6% in 2025, with consumables growing 1-3%.
- Analysts projected a 3Q core operating margin of 21.9%, against guidance of 22-22.5%.
- 2025 EPS guidance was steady at $4.45-$4.55.

## 9. Post-2Q 2025 Earnings Outlook (2025-08-01)

**Title:** "Illumina Shows Margin Upside Amid Mixed Growth." **Analyst:** Adeline Zandi.

- Margin execution masked continued top-line pressure. The guidance raise suggested the company was regaining control through cost rigor and a rebalanced commercial mix.
- Research was still constrained, the instrument guidance was lowered, and clinical consumables did most of the heavy lifting. Growth was "concentrated rather than broad-based."
- Clinical consumables were then 60% of sequencing revenue and outpacing research. This suggested durability despite NIH funding jitters slowing lab purchases.
- NovaSeq X supplied 44% of high-throughput consumables, and the shift has runway.
- Leaner operating expenses and a broader multiomics slate could give 2026 firmer footing, assuming X uptake climbs and China (a small but uncertain slice of sales) stabilizes.

Results:
- Core EPS guidance for 2025 was raised to $4.45-$4.55, from $4.20-$4.30.
- Revenue was $1.06 billion, beating consensus by 1%. EPS was $1.19, topping consensus by 18 cents.
- Sales fell 3%. Service and other fell 21% and product sales fell 2%.
- Operating margin was 23.8%, ahead of consensus by 280 bps.

## 10. 2Q 2025 Preview (2025-07-24)

**Title:** "Illumina Counts on NovaSeq Amid Soft Demand." **Analyst:** Adeline Zandi.

- 1H 2025 had done little to ease concerns about reaccelerating growth.
- The pending SomaLogic purchase was a tactical extension of the multiomics roadmap. It adds a faster-growing proteomics line and could cushion stalled academic instrument budgets.
- Holding NovaSeq X placements near 1Q's 60 units would keep X on track to drive over half of high-throughput revenue in 2H. That would ease pressure from a projected 15% decline in academic and government demand.
- Visibility into the $100 million of cost cuts and supply-chain efforts was key to confidence in 45 bps of margin expansion despite a tariff drag.

Key points:
- Consensus was 2Q revenue of $1.05 billion and EPS of $1.01.
- 2025 guidance was a core revenue decline of 1-3% and operating margin of 21.5-22%.
- 1Q instrument sales grew 0.2% and consumables rose 1.1% from the prior year.
- Analysts expected a 2Q operating margin of 20.9%, against guidance of 21%.

## 11. Post-1Q 2025 Earnings Outlook (2025-05-09)

**Title:** "Headwinds Tick Illumina's 2025 Guidance Lower." **Analyst:** Adeline Zandi.

- The 2027 target of high-single-digit growth "looks further away than ever."
- The EPS guidance reduction embeds a 25-cent net tariff headwind after mitigation. Half of the pricing, supply-chain optimization and additional cost-cutting would be completed in 2025, with the remainder in 2026.
- Constrained research demand arose in the second part of 1Q, with double-digit revenue declines within that customer base projected through year-end.
- Management called out clinical strength as an offset. It expected research funding to be "more rational" in 2026, though BI said "we don't see that as a given."

Results:
- Revenue was $1.04 billion, beating consensus by 0.8%. EPS was 97 cents, topping consensus by 3 cents.
- Product revenue rose 0.5% to $880 million. Service fell 20% to $161 million.
- Operating margin was 20.4%, about 7 bps above consensus.
- 2025 core revenue guidance was lowered to a 1-3% decline, and EPS to $4.20-$4.30.

## 12. 1Q 2025 Preview (2025-05-01)

**Title:** "Illumina Navigates China Risk, Looks to Margin Gains." **Analyst:** Adeline Zandi.

- Revenue expectations were muted following China's export restrictions. These had led to an EPS guidance cut to $4.50.
- The new outlook assumed limited China contribution. Any resolution or further deterioration could materially shift guidance.
- NovaSeq X adoption momentum was expected to continue. Attention would turn to whether rising pull-through and clinical uptake could offset lower instrument sales and potential pricing adjustments for volume-driven deals.
- Operating margin was expected to expand 170 bps in 2025, which underscores the execution risk of balancing structural costs against high R&D intensity.

Key points:
- 1Q core revenue consensus was $1.03 billion and EPS 94 cents.
- Analysts estimated a 1Q core operating margin of 20.3%, up from 19.8% in 4Q.
- 2025 guidance was core revenue growth in the low single digits and adjusted operating margin of 23%.
- Core EPS guidance had been lowered to $4.50, from $4.50-$4.65.

## 13. Post-4Q 2024 Earnings Outlook (2025-02-07)

- The pivot to AI partnerships and multiomics reflects an evolution beyond sequencing. Growing competition in these higher-margin frontiers will take time to scale.

## 15. Post-3Q 2024 Earnings Outlook (2024-11-05)

**Title:** "Illumina Awaiting on Customers to Deliver." **Analyst:** Adeline Zandi.

- Management gave little to support an imminent inflection. There was "a healthy amount of skepticism" around the elasticity of demand (how much volume rises as prices fall) for new offerings.
- The 2024 revenue guidance cut was modest in dollars but signals the macro environment had yet to improve. This likely pushes any meaningful uptick deeper into 2025.
- Operating margin guidance rose 50 bps to 21-21.5% on better mix and renewed cost focus. Much improvement is still needed to reach the goal of 500 bps by 2027, which was outlined in the August 2024 strategy update.

Results:
- Revenue was $1.08 billion, in line with consensus. EPS of $1.14 topped consensus by 27 cents.
- Core sales fell 2%. Product revenue fell 3% and service fell 7%.
- Core operating margin was 22.6%, 10 bps above the prior year.
- 2024 core EPS guidance was raised to $4.05-$4.15, from $3.80-$3.95.

## 16. 3Q 2024 Preview (2024-10-28)

**Title:** "Illumina's Outlook Tempered Amid Current Struggles." **Analyst:** Adeline Zandi.

- The long-term strategy of high-single-digit revenue growth by 2027 and 500 bps of margin expansion "appears feasible but it's a 'show me' story."

- Expected call topics: NovaSeq X pull-through, mid-throughput competition, multiomics progress and cost savings.

Key points:
- Consensus was 3Q revenue of $1.08 billion and EPS of 87 cents.
- Guidance called for a mid-30% core sequencing instrument decline in 2024, with consumables at the upper end of low single digits.

## 17. Post-2Q 2024 Earnings Outlook, corrected (2024-08-08)

**Title:** "Wait Is On for Illumina Update."

- The correction concerned the consumables range.

## 18. 2Q 2024 Preview (2024-07-30)

**Title:** "Illumina Looks to Rebound With Grail Off the Books."

## 19. Post-1Q 2024 Earnings Outlook (2024-05-03)

**Title:** "Illumina Is Waiting for a Growth Inflection."

- Illumina was in the "middle innings" of its turnaround. There were few meaningful updates, which fits the CEO's seven-month tenure and the recent hire of a new CFO.
- The wait was for momentum in core sequencing, long-term financial targets, or for Grail to be off the books.
- Full-year guidance was maintained. The 2Q view was slightly below consensus on revenue growth and margin.
- The NovaSeq X throughput inflection was still ramping.
- Management reaffirmed that Grail's filing would be complete in 2Q and that Grail would need to be capitalized with $1 billion in a capital-markets deal.

Results:
- Revenue was $1.08 billion, beating consensus by 2.4%. EPS of 9 cents topped consensus by 3 cents.
- Product revenue fell 5% to $876 million. Service grew 21% to $200 million.
- Operating margin was 3.1%, roughly 106 bps above consensus.
- 2024 core revenue was still seen flat versus 2023.

## 20. 1Q 2024 Preview (2024-04-25)

**Title:** "Illumina Eyes Progress on Its Organizational Reset."

- Expectations were modest. Illumina guided to a 4% core revenue decline and an 18% operating margin for 1Q, on its way to flat revenue and a 20% margin for the year.
- PacBio's weak preliminary results on delayed Revio placements were not an apples-to-apples comparison but added caution.

Key points:

- Guidance: flat 2024 core revenue, a 20% core operating margin, and Grail's divestiture to be finalized by end of 2Q.

## 21. Post-4Q 2023 Earnings Outlook (2024-02-08)

**Title:** "Clock Starting on Illumina's Turnaround."

- The turnaround was in early days, with the new CEO "pushing a conservative narrative." Guidance largely affirmed his early-January preliminary commentary.
- Guidance assumed a flat 2024 top line and a 20% core margin, which BI called appropriate given the heavy lift of scaling the X in 2023.
- Details on Grail's divestiture would improve with the S-1 filing expected by midyear. A sale remained possible under a dual-track process.
- Updated annual pull-through estimates for the portfolio outside NovaSeq X were helpful, but the elasticity of demand for the new flagship stayed unanswered.

Results:
- Revenue was $1.1 billion, beating consensus by 3%. EPS of 14 cents topped consensus by 12 cents.
- Sales fell 7% in constant currency, with sequencing down 11%.
- Operating margin was 4.6%, versus 2.9% a year earlier.

## 22. 4Q 2023 Preview (2024-02-01)

**Title:** "Illumina Looks to Prove Itself Under New CEO."

- Preliminary 4Q put results ahead of consensus for 2023.
- No splashy product news came at the J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference.
- Illumina shipped 79 NovaSeq X systems in 4Q, bringing the 2023 tally to 352, slightly above the company's 330-340 target. Placements were projected to decline in 2024 after the year ended with a 38-instrument backlog.
- With 3Q's qualitative guidance that 2024 would "look very similar" to 2023, the bar was low for the new CEO. Key near-term drivers were the NovaSeq transition and the Grail divestiture, with cost cuts close behind.

Key points:
- 2024 consensus was revenue of $4.5 billion, operating margin of 4.7%, and EPS of 81 cents.
- Preliminary 2023 revenue was $4.5 billion with operating margin of 5.3%.
- Analysts projected 2023 sequencing growth down 4%, with instruments and consumables both down 4%.
- 4Q estimates were revenue of $1.09 billion, EPS of 2 cents, and operating margin of 1.1% (versus 2.9% a year earlier).

## 23. Post-3Q 2023 Earnings Outlook (2023-11-10)

**Title:** "Illumina Searches for Reprieve; 2024 View Dim." **Analyst:** Jordan Dahan.

- Illumina trimmed its 2023 forecast again amid weaker customer spending and challenges of the flagship transition.
- 2024 was expected to look similar to 2023, with a 3-4% core decline. Details were sparse, and the view sets a low bar. The CEO was only 40 days into the role.
- The NovaSeq X delivery target was cut to 330-340 from 390, due to budget constraints and customers delaying consumables orders on legacy systems as part of the upgrade cycle.
- The new CEO was "laser focused" on Grail's resolution, with core sequencing the priority. He committed to a long-term financial outlook in 2H 2024.

Results:
- Revenue was $1.1 billion, 1% shy of consensus. EPS of 33 cents topped consensus by 19 cents.

## 24. 3Q 2023 Preview (2023-11-03)

**Title:** "Illumina Treading Water, Fate of Grail in Balance." **Analyst:** Jordan Dahan.

- New CEO Jacob Thaysen inherited the job of reviving core sequencing and settling Grail. BI expected no sweeping decision, but a key question was whether he would reiterate mid-term margin targets and mid-teens long-term market growth.
- The EU divestiture order allows 15 months to separate Grail and requires it to be capitalized for 2.5 years.
- Illumina can keep screening exposure by retaining a 14.5% ownership stake and reestablishing its royalty agreement. Its jurisdictional challenge was still pending.
- With 2Q results, the company had raised its NovaSeq X shipment target to 390 even as it lowered revenue expectations. The causes were customer transitions to the X, cautious purchasing and China's slowdown.

Key points:
- Consensus was 3Q revenue of $1.13 billion and EPS of 14 cents.
- 2023 guidance: 3% core sequencing instrument growth and a 3% consumables decline.
- Analysts projected a 3Q operating margin of 4.81%, down from 9.15% a year earlier.
- 2023 EPS guidance was 75-90 cents, with consensus at 83 cents.

## 25. Post-2Q 2023 Earnings Outlook (2023-08-10)

**Title:** "Illumina Is Latest Casualty of Softer Demand." **Analyst:** Jordan Dahan.

## Cross-cutting observations (BI's recurring themes, as stated in the notes)

- **Guidance history 2024-2026:** the 2026 revenue growth view moved from 1-3% (core, pre-4Q 2025 report), to 2-4% (reaffirmed at 1Q), to 4-6% (as of the 2Q preview), to about 5% excluding China (post-2Q, 2026-07-31). 2026 EPS guidance moved from $5.05-$5.20, to $5.15-$5.30, to $5.30-$5.40.
- **Clinical mix shift:** clinical customers were 60% of sequencing revenue (2Q 2025) and 65% of sequencing consumables (2Q 2026). BI repeatedly called clinical workflows sticky.
- **NovaSeq X share:** 44% of high-throughput consumables revenue in 2Q 2025. Placements ran 60 in 1Q 2025, 80 in 1Q 2026 and 95 in 2Q 2026. The 2026 guide was initially 200-240 systems.
- **2027 targets (BI's repeated references):** high-single-digit revenue growth (excluding China), about 500 bps of margin expansion, and a 26% operating margin. BI called the margin target "an open question" in Feb 2026, and later said 2027 visibility was improving.
- **Competition:** Roche's Axelios (launched in 2026) was a recurring concern. BI said in May 2026 that Illumina appeared to be in the early stages of trading price for volume. In July 2026 it said it was too early to see the impact.
- **China:** about 7% of revenue historically (as cited in Feb 2025) and under 5% of sales by Feb 2026. Export restrictions caused a 2025 guidance cut.
- **Tariffs:** a 25-cent net EPS headwind after mitigation was embedded in 2025 guidance (May 2025).
- **Research:** NIH funding concerns constrained research demand from 2025. BI projected a 15% decline in academic and government demand for 2025. The 2026 outlook assumed a soft research market, with upside if NIH funding loosens.

**Data caveats:** a few figures appear as printed in the source and look inconsistent (for example, "EPS of $6.80-$7.70" in the Jan 2020 preview versus $6.80-$7.00 elsewhere, and "Cents" misused in some EPS dollar figures). I did not correct them.
