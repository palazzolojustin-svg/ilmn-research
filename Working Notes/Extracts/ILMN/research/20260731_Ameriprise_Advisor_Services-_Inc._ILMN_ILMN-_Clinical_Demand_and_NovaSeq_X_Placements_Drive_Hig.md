## Q2 2026 Results

- **Revenue:** $1.159B, up 9.4% y/y (6.5% organic, meaning excluding acquisitions and currency effects). Consensus was $1.131B, and consensus organic growth was 4.0% y/y.

## Clinical Demand and NovaSeq X Placements

- **Placements:** More than 95 NovaSeq X systems (Illumina's newest production-scale sequencers) were placed, a second consecutive quarter above Illumina's historical target of 50 to 60 systems per quarter. About 70% went to clinical customers.
- **Why (management):** Customers are adding capacity for expected testing volumes, including multi-unit orders from large clinical laboratories and support for new clinical trials.
- **Clinical consumables:** Clinical sequencing consumables rose 15% y/y in rest-of-world (ROW, meaning excluding Greater China) markets, led by U.S. growth above 20% y/y. In Q1 the ROW figure was 20%. Management attributed most of the slowdown to near-term conditions in the Middle East and Latin America, not to weaker underlying U.S. demand.
- **Applications:** Oncology remains the largest clinical application. Therapy selection is the largest revenue pool within it, and molecular residual disease (MRD, testing for leftover cancer after treatment) is starting to contribute more visibly. Rare disease and screening also continued to grow.
- **Analyst view (Macino):** The expanding NovaSeq X installed base improves visibility into future consumables growth, though normalization (newly installed systems reaching steady utilization) typically takes at least six to nine months.
- **Instrument outlook (management):** Instrument growth could moderate against more difficult comparisons.

## NovaSeq X Transition

- NovaSeq X was 83% of high-throughput gigabases shipped (a measure of sequencing output volume) and 59% of high-throughput consumables revenue in Q2.
- Clinical volume reached 78% on NovaSeq X, versus 76% in Q1'26. Research and applied volume rose to 92%.
- Management continues to expect 80% to 85% of clinical volume to have transitioned by year-end 2026.
- Customers also bought more than 10 NovaSeq 6000 systems (the prior generation) for assays expected to stay on that platform. Macino reads this as evidence that the transition is not an immediate shutdown of older capacity. Management called continued NovaSeq 6000 operation "a normal transition dynamic rather than an identifiable consumables headwind."
- Total gigabase output on connected high- and mid-throughput instruments rose more than 30% y/y, with clinical output rising faster.

## Research and Applied Market

- Research and applied sequencing consumables fell 7% y/y in ROW markets but rose 9% q/q.
- Management saw greater U.S. funding activity and customer inquiries late in Q2, but said it is too early to call a sustained recovery.
- The FY26 outlook still assumes a mid- to high-single-digit decline in research and applied consumables. This limits reliance on a recovery while leaving upside if funding releases turn into spending.
- Report takeaway: demand improved sequentially, but management is not assuming a meaningful end-market recovery.

## Strategic Developments

- **Whole-genome MRD research workflow:** An early-access workflow, run on NovaSeq systems, designed to shorten assay-development timelines and lower development costs. Management said the NovaSeq X roadmap gives customers more confidence the platform can support evolving workflows over several years.
- **StrataMap Spatial:** A newly launched sequencing-based spatial (tissue-analysis) workflow. Management reported customer interest above its internal expectations.
- **SomaLogic acquisition:** The acquired offerings were rebranded SomaScan and SomaSeq. The business is tracking toward the high end of the company's transaction expectations.
- **Multiomics revenue recognition:** Most multiomics products, including single-cell and TruPath, are booked within consumables revenue. Management still expects new products to add an incremental one to two percentage points of revenue growth in 2027.
- **BioInsight:** Combines sequencing perturbation tools, computing and AI to build multiomics datasets and interpretation tools for pharma customers. The Billion Cell Atlas had delivered more than 300M cells by quarter-end. Three partners were added after the quarter, bringing the total to six. Revenue is now being recognized and sits in sequencing service and other revenue. Management called the opportunity early but said the data could support drug discovery and predictive biological models.

## FY2026 and Q3 Outlook

- **Revenue:** Raised to $4.60B-$4.64B from $4.52B-$4.62B. The new range is above the pre-release consensus of $4.571B.
- **Organic growth:** ILMN organic growth guidance raised to "greater than 4.0% y/y."
- **Adjusted EPS:** Raised to $5.30-$5.40 from $5.15-$5.30, implying 10.5% growth at the midpoint. Pre-release consensus was $5.23.
- **Reasons for the raise:** Q2 outperformance, higher 2H'26 expectations, and continued expense discipline.
- **Q3'26 guidance:** Revenue $1.14B-$1.16B; ROW organic growth ~4.5%; adjusted EPS $1.33-$1.38; adjusted operating margin ~24%, about 150 bps (1.5 percentage points) better than Q2, helped by a higher consumables mix, cost actions and efficiency gains.
- **Q4:** Management expects it to remain the largest revenue quarter. An extra fiscal week in Q4 could add ~0.5 percentage point to revenue growth, mainly through consumables.
- **Margin headwinds and offsets:** Higher freight and memory costs weigh on margins, but better consumables mix and planned cost actions could support stronger profitability in 2H'26 (Ameriprise key takeaways).

## Capital Allocation

- Cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments were ~$1.17B at the end of Q2, against ~$1.99B of total debt. Gross leverage was ~1.6x last-12-month EBITDA.
- In Q2 Illumina repurchased ~0.9M shares for ~$122M, at an average price of $129.07. About $1.8B remained under repurchase authorizations, and the company plans to keep buying opportunistically. The $129.07 average is well below the current $205.09 price.
- Analyst view (Macino): the remaining authorization gives capital-return flexibility, though pace could vary with valuation, cash generation, acquisition-related needs and balance-sheet priorities.

## Valuation

- Method: forward P/E (price relative to expected next-12-month earnings). The report gives no price target or explicit model.
- ILMN trades at 36.3x 12-month forward P/E (the market data box shows 36.4x), a discount to its 5-year average forward P/E of 72.2x. Ameriprise says the large discount partly reflects the divestiture of GRAIL Inc (NASDAQ: GRAL; $71.14) on June 24, 2024, and the return to a normalized "Core Illumina" adjusted EPS.
- Macino's framework: balance improving clinical demand, elevated NovaSeq X placements, higher FY26 guidance and potential for better consumables growth against uncertainty over research funding, Greater China, timing of multiomics revenue, cost inflation, and execution on the company's 2027 financial objectives.
- The investment case "increasingly depends on" whether recent instrument placements convert into sustained consumables pull-through, and whether Illumina can deliver higher revenue growth while expanding margins.

## Investment Thesis (Macino)

- Illumina offers exposure to "the genomic medicine of the future" through large diagnostic markets for diagnosing, treating and monitoring diseases with genetic or hereditary origins, including cancer.
- NovaSeq X and X Plus (production-scale systems that can sequence a human genome) could be a multi-year revenue tailwind for the instruments franchise.
- The company believes the next-generation sequencing (NGS) total addressable market is $125B, with only ~$10B served today. Single-cell, spatial and liquid biopsy innovation add further potential.
- For "more aggressive investors with multi-year time horizons," Ameriprise views the improving financial profile and continued recovery as an opportunity to accumulate the stock.

## Risks

- **Thesis risks (Ameriprise):** regulatory challenges to acquisitions or integration difficulties, currency fluctuations, market share erosion, increased generic competition, new product or clinical trial disappointments, delayed regulatory approvals, and reimbursement challenges.
- **Items flagged in the text:** research funding uncertainty, Greater China weakness (revenue down 11.1% y/y), cost inflation (freight, memory), moderation of instrument growth against tougher comparisons, ROW clinical growth slowing to 15% from 20%, and the six-to-nine-month lag before new systems normalize.

## Company Profile

Illumina is described as a global leader in sequencing- and array-based solutions for genetic and genomic analysis. Customers include genomic research centers, academic institutions, government laboratories, hospitals, pharmaceutical and biotech companies, commercial molecular diagnostic laboratories and consumer genomics companies. It was incorporated in California in April 1998.
