# Freedom Broker: Illumina (ILMN) Company Update, 6 May 2026

## Rating, price target and headline view

Freedom Broker (analyst Ilya Zubkov) reiterates its **Buy** rating on Illumina and raises its **price target to $155 from $140** [Freedom Broker, 2026-05-06]. The report says the raise is "mostly due to expected higher instrument growth." Instruments are the sequencing machines Illumina sells; consumables are the reagent kits and flow cells customers must buy for every sequencing run.

The five headline bullets of the report are:
- Instrument upside materially improves the 2026 revenue setup, supporting higher instrument growth assumptions for the rest of 2026.
- Consumables remain the core medium-term growth engine. A growing NovaSeq X installed base (NovaSeq X is Illumina's current flagship high-throughput sequencer) should support consumables acceleration into H2 2026 and 2027.
- Margin performance reinforces the operating-leverage thesis (profits growing faster than revenue), despite near-term mix and cost headwinds.
- Guidance appears conservative even after the upward revision, which supports a potential continued "beat-and-raise" cycle (beating expectations and then raising guidance).
- Buy rating reiterated; PT $140 → $155.

## Instruments: the main driver of the upgrade

- Instrument demand was "meaningfully stronger than management had anticipated at the beginning of the year." Illumina placed **80 NovaSeq X systems in Q1 2026, versus a prior target range of 50–60** [Q1 2026 results, as reported by Freedom Broker].
- Management cited supply constraints tied to higher-than-expected volumes. It guided to **similar NovaSeq X placements in Q2** and expressed a more constructive view for the rest of the year.
- The analyst says these factors "support a sustainable improvement in the instrument segment growth profile."
- The analyst's prior expectation was "largely stable instrument sales." The new view is driven by accelerated NovaSeq X shipments. **The estimate for instrument sales growth for the remainder of 2026 rises from flat to 5%.**
- Key forward consideration: execution on supply scaling. Management was supply constrained in Q1 and continues investing to expand NovaSeq X availability through Q2 and Q3.
- Management raised its full-year rest-of-world (ROW, i.e. ex-US and China-related regional grouping used by Illumina) instrument outlook to **flat to low-single-digit organic growth**. The analyst says this implies upside to the Freedom Broker instrument and total revenue estimates for 2026.

## Consumables: solid, clinically driven beat

- Q1 2026 consumables revenue was **$797M, up 3.8% year over year**. It beat consensus by 2.0% and the analyst's estimate by 0.8%.
- Management attributed growth mainly to higher high-throughput sequencing volumes, helped by the larger NovaSeq X installed base and improving utilization.
- **Clinical sequencing consumables revenue grew 20% excluding China for the second consecutive quarter**, driven by rising clinical volumes and adoption of more sequencing-intensive tests. Clinical was **more than 65% of sequencing consumables revenue** in the quarter.
- Offsets:
  - Research and applied consumables **declined 12% ex-China** amid continued funding uncertainty.
  - **Microarrays (a different, older genotyping technology) fell 20% on a ROW organic basis**, because of softness at several large direct-to-consumer customers.
- Outlook: the analyst's view that consumables are the primary growth engine in 2026 and the mid-term "remains intact." The faster instrument placements in H1 2026 should lead to faster consumables growth in H2 2026, with further acceleration into 2027.
- By the end of 2026 the NovaSeq X transition cycle "may approach its peak, with **82% of high-throughput Gb volume** already generated on the platform" (Gb = gigabases of sequencing output).
- The lagged benefit of installed-base growth should continue through 2027. The longer-term path depends increasingly on clinical NovaSeq X utilization and a research demand recovery.

## Service & Other

- Q1 2026 revenue was **$174M, up 8.1% year over year**, beating consensus by 3.2% and broadly in line with the analyst's estimate.
- The upside was "likely" supported by the **SomaLogic acquisition** (SomaLogic is a protein-measurement business Illumina acquired). Management highlighted **BioInsight** (Illumina's data and partnership business) as a potential growth driver, but noted strategic partnerships and data monetization deals can be lumpy.
- The segment is hard to forecast because of the timing of data-related deals and acquisition mix.
- The analyst sees the segment as reaccelerating, with BioInsight and the **Billion Cell Atlas** initiative creating monetization opportunities, especially while research demand is weak. Segment forecasts are changed only slightly. Upside depends on BioInsight partnerships, data monetization and successful SomaLogic integration.

## Margins

- **Non-GAAP gross margin** (profit after direct costs, excluding certain accounting items) was **68.2%**, versus consensus 67.5% and the analyst's 67.4%. It rose 80 bps (0.8 percentage points) from 67.4% a year earlier, helped by cost efficiencies and higher revenue, partly offset by tariffs.
- Q2 gross margin may be pressured by a higher instrument mix and supply-scaling investments. Some inflationary pressure could appear in Q2, though management expects mitigation initiatives to offset cost increases over the year.
- **Non-GAAP EBIT margin** (operating profit margin) was **21.9%**, well above consensus and the analyst's 20.4%, and up 150 bps year over year. Management said Q1 margin was about **150 bps above guidance**, from solid revenue and disciplined expenses, which also drove the EPS beat. The analyst calls it a "clear beat" that reinforces the operating-leverage thesis.

## Guidance

- **2026 revenue guidance** was raised by only $20M at the midpoint, to **$4.52B–$4.62B**. The analyst says this largely reflects the Q1 beat and "appears cautious" given the improved instrument outlook, and does not exclude a continued beat-and-raise cycle for product sales through 2026.
- **2026 operating margin guidance** rose 10 bps to **23.4%–23.6%**.
- **2026 EPS guidance** rose $0.10 to **$5.15–$5.30**.
- **Q2 operating margin guidance of about 22%** was slightly below the analyst's expectations. Reasons given: higher instrument mix, NovaSeq X supply investments, freight costs, electronics inflation and a full-quarter contribution from SomaLogic. Management still points to margin improvement through the rest of the year and reiterated progress toward its 2027 margin targets.
- **Mid-term 2027 targets** (as referenced): high-single-digit ex-China revenue growth, adjusted operating margin expansion toward 26%, and double-digit adjusted EPS growth (ex-China). The analyst says the setup positions Illumina well to reach them.

## Capital return

Illumina repurchased **2.0M shares for $242M in Q1 2026** and authorized an **additional $1.5B buyback** on top of the remaining prior authorization. The analyst says buybacks add to EPS growth.

## Valuation

The target price is an **equally weighted blend** of (1) a 5-year discounted cash flow model using free cash flow to the firm (FCFF, cash available to all capital providers) and (2) relative valuation multiples: **EV/Sales NTM of 7x** and **P/E NTM of 30x** (NTM = next twelve months). The price-history chart shows the new $155 target against a FactSet analyst target range.

## Investment thesis

- Illumina is the "standard-bearer" in next-generation sequencing (NGS), with a large installed base and a growing clinical mix.
- Growth in consumables and expanding free cash flow are underpinned by expansion into multiomics (measuring several biological layers, not just DNA) and data/AI platforms. The analyst expects revenue re-acceleration and margin expansion.
- Structural drivers: genomics in oncology, neonatal screening, and NGS use in biopharma and population-scale programs. Illumina is described as one of few pure-play exposures to global genomics growth.

### Potential growth drivers
1. **Mid-term revenue acceleration:** more data-intensive applications (whole-genome sequencing, MRD or minimal residual disease testing, early cancer detection) should raise NovaSeq X utilization, consumables demand and placements.
2. **Completion of the NovaSeq X transition:** finishing clinical migration and normalizing research demand should help offset pricing erosion.
3. **Multiomics and data/AI:** service-oriented offerings should create less cyclical revenue and differentiate the portfolio.

## Key risks

1. **Competitive pressure in NGS:** rising competition in high- and mid-throughput could pressure consumables and instrument pricing, limit margin expansion and complicate installed-base monetization.
2. **Macro and budget headwinds:** persistent weak academic funding could constrain research demand for instruments and consumables.
3. **China geopolitical and regulatory risk:** inclusion on China's Unreliable Entity List (UEL) restricts instrument sales and creates downside risk from possible changes to import/export rules.
