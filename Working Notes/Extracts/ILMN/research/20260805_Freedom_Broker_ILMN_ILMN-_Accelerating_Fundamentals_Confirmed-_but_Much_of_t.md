**Summary.** Freedom Broker's Ilya Zubkov reviews Illumina's strong quarter (consumables +5% to $852M, instruments +29% to $130M, services +20% to $177M) and the raised FY26 guidance, and raises estimates for 2026-2028. # Illumina (ILMN): Freedom Broker Company Update, 5 August 2026

- **Market data in the report:** share price $203.01; 52-week range $88.00–$207.00; market capitalization $30,654.51M; enterprise value (market cap plus net debt) $32,419.53M; float 99.7%; 151.30M shares outstanding.

### Three summary points from the analyst

1. The quarter was strong and reinforces Illumina's "accelerating fundamental growth story." Better-than-expected placements of NovaSeq X (Illumina's newest high-throughput sequencer) keep translating into faster consumables growth (consumables are the reagent kits customers must buy for every sequencing run). Clinical remains the main revenue driver. This supports higher 2026–2027 forecasts.
2. Profitability beat expectations on operating leverage (profit growing faster than sales as fixed costs are spread over more revenue) and cost control. Management left operating margin guidance unchanged, which the analyst reads as caution about inflation and ongoing investments. The analyst still expects most margin expansion in 2027.
3. FY26 guidance was raised, confirming the improving trajectory. But after the "recent strong re-rating in ILMN shares," the analyst believes the market "has largely priced in the expected acceleration in fundamentals," hence the downgrade to Hold despite the higher target.

## Q2 2026 results as described in the report

### Consumables: $852M, +5% year over year (YoY)

- Consumables revenue was $852M, up 5% YoY, beating both the analyst's estimate and consensus. It was again the primary growth engine.
- **Drivers:** strong demand for high-throughput sequencing consumables as the NovaSeq X installed base grows and utilization (how heavily each machine is run) rises. This was partly offset by continued weakness in the mid-throughput sequencing portfolio.
- **Clinical:** management said clinical consumables revenue rose 15% YoY excluding China, and the U.S. and Canada kept growing above 20%. Management attributed this to continued adoption of increasingly complex NGS (next-generation sequencing) diagnostic testing.
- **Research and applied markets:** consumables revenue from these markets fell 7% YoY excluding China, reflecting ongoing funding uncertainty, although sequential trends improved. Management emphasized it is "still too early to call a recovery in research spending."
- **NovaSeq X migration:** the platform now accounts for 83% of high-throughput sequencing volumes, 59% of high-throughput revenue and 78% of clinical workflow migrations. The analyst calls the pace "impressive." Clinical applications remain the principal growth driver within consumables.
- **Analyst view:** stronger-than-expected placements in recent quarters are increasingly turning into higher utilization and recurring consumables demand. The results support a "modest upward revision" to consumables forecasts and the thesis that "today's stronger instrument placements are laying the foundation for tomorrow's higher recurring consumables revenue."

### Instruments: $130M, +29% YoY

- Instrument revenue was $130M, up 29% YoY, well above consensus and the analyst's estimate.
- Shipments of NovaSeq X and MiSeq i100 (a newer benchtop, mid-throughput sequencer) continue to beat market expectations and management's original targets.
- Management reported more than 95 NovaSeq X systems deployed in the quarter, following manufacturing improvements, including several large expansion orders from leading clinical customers. For comparison, management's initial expectation at the start of the year was 50–60 placements per quarter, and 80 systems shipped in Q1 2026.
- More than 10 NovaSeq 6000 systems (the older generation platform) shipped in the quarter to customers planning to keep running it for several more years.
- **Analyst view:** this strengthens the positive view of Instruments and supports a modest revenue forecast increase. But "the investment thesis is increasingly shifting away from hardware sales themselves toward the subsequent acceleration in Consumables growth enabled by the expanding installed base."

### Service & Other: $177M, +20% YoY

- Revenue of $177M, up 20% YoY, also beat expectations.
- Management highlighted contributions from the Billion Cell Atlas initiative and the ongoing integration of SomaLogic (an acquired proteomics, i.e. protein-measurement, business).
- **Analyst view:** data, informatics and multiomics (measuring several types of biological molecules together) are gradually becoming meaningful incremental growth drivers, though revenue will likely stay uneven because partnership agreements are project-based. Forecast revisions for this segment are only modest. The higher full-year guidance and management's expectations of further contributions from BioInsight, Billion Cell Atlas and recently launched products add support and "may continue generating upside surprises."

### Margins

- **Adjusted gross margin:** 68.2%, "materially exceeding" the analyst's expectations thanks to stronger sequencing product revenue and disciplined manufacturing execution despite inflationary cost pressure. It declined YoY because of inflation and the SomaLogic acquisition. The analyst expects meaningful gross margin expansion to depend mainly on a richer consumables mix, more visible from 2027.
- **Adjusted operating margin:** beat both the analyst's expectations and consensus, helped by higher revenue and stronger gross margin. The analyst says the quarter validates the operating leverage thesis, even with ongoing investment in manufacturing capacity expansion and SomaLogic integration.
- Because management left operating margin guidance unchanged, the analyst sees little reason to materially change medium-term margin assumptions. Management still expects profitability to improve in 2H26, supporting a stronger earnings profile entering 2027.

## Valuation and rating

- **Multiples raised:** because of higher revenue and EPS forecasts for the next two years, the analyst raised the target multiples from 7x to 8x NTM EV/Sales (enterprise value divided by next-twelve-months sales) and from 30x to 35x NTM P/E (price divided by next-twelve-months earnings).
- **Method:** the target is an equally weighted blend of (a) a 5-year discounted cash flow model using free cash flow to the firm (FCFF, i.e. cash available to all capital providers) and (b) relative valuation using 8x EV/Sales NTM and 35x P/E NTM.
- **Why Hold despite a higher target:** "following the recent appreciation in ILMN shares, we believe much of the anticipated acceleration in Illumina's fundamentals is now reflected in the stock price."
- **Rating mechanics (Freedom's new volatility-adjusted system):** $185 versus the $198.16 previous close implies a -6.64% return. Annualized volatility (from daily log returns, 2 Aug 2025–3 Aug 2026) is 45.87%. Return divided by volatility gives an Analyst Rating (AR) score of -0.14, inside the ±0.35 band, which maps to Hold. Market data come from FactSet and LSEG Data & Analytics.
- **Rating history (Freedom, as of 08/04/2026):** Hold at $150 (2024-02-09); Buy at $130 (2025-02-10); Buy at $140 (2026-02-10); Buy at $155 (2026-05-06). The report charts only a price-target history, with the downgrade now moving to Hold at $185.

## Investment thesis (analyst's)

- Illumina is "the standard-bearer in next-generation sequencing (NGS)," backed by a large installed base and a rising mix of clinical applications. Growth in consumables sales and expanding free cash flow are underpinned by expansion into new verticals, including multiomics and data/AI platforms. The company is positioned for revenue growth re-acceleration and margin expansion.
- The long-term NGS market is supported by broader use of genomics in oncology, neonatal screening, and NGS in biopharma and population-scale programs. Illumina is described as one of the few pure-play exposures to long-term global genomics growth.

### Potential growth drivers

1. **Mid-term revenue acceleration:** a shift to data-intensive applications (whole-genome sequencing, MRD or minimal residual disease testing, early cancer detection) should raise NovaSeq X utilization, drive consumables demand and support further placements.
2. **Completion of the NovaSeq X transition:** finishing the clinical migration and normalization of research demand should help offset price erosion.
3. **Multiomics and data/AI expansion:** service-oriented offerings should create new, less cyclical revenue streams and differentiate the portfolio.

### Key risks

1. **Competition in NGS:** more rivals in high- and mid-throughput could intensify pricing pressure on consumables and instruments, limiting margin expansion and complicating monetization of the installed base.
2. **Macro and budget headwinds:** persistent weakness in academic funding may constrain research-segment demand for instruments and consumables, threatening mid-term growth.
3. **China:** Illumina's inclusion on China's Unreliable Entity List (UEL) continues to restrict instrument sales and carries ongoing downside risk from possible changes to import/export rules.
