## What this document is

This is a three-page Bloomberg Professional "Company Financials" printout for Illumina (ticker ILMN US Equity), generated on 10/10/2026 at 20:52:20. Estimates come from the "Consensus" source, which is Bloomberg's aggregation of sell-side analyst forecasts. No individual analyst, bank, rating or price target appears. Periodicity is annual, and no extra analytics are applied.

Illumina's fiscal years are 52/53-week years that end near the end of December, not exactly on December 31. The sheet labels them as follows:

Some terms the sheet uses:

- **Adjusted** (non-GAAP) figures exclude items such as stock-based compensation and amortization of acquired intangibles. GAAP figures follow standard accounting rules and include them.
- **Consumables** are the reagent kits and chips customers must buy for every sequencing or microarray run. **Instruments** are the machines themselves. **Service & Other** covers service contracts and similar revenue.
- **GRAIL** was Illumina's cancer-detection (blood test) business. It appears only for 2023 and 2024.
- **EBITDA** is earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization, a rough measure of operating cash earnings.
- **Free cash flow (FCF)** is operating cash flow minus capital expenditures.

Several cells are blank (usually in the far-out years) and some cells look garbled by the PDF conversion. Where this happens I say so below. The printout does not state the number of analysts contributing to each estimate.

## Analysis: implied growth, margins and oddities

*These are arithmetic observations from the sheet's own numbers, not statements by Bloomberg or analysts.*

### Revenue growth

Revenue fell 2.9% in 2024 ($4,504M to $4,372M) and 0.7% in 2025 (to $4,343M). Consensus then has growth of about 6.5% in 2026 ($4,624.8M), 6.3% in 2027, 7.0% in 2028, 7.4% in 2029, 7.8% in 2030, 10.1% in 2031 and 9.9% in 2032. The acceleration in 2031–2032 is notable: it is faster than any earlier forecast year and may reflect fewer contributing analysts that far out.

### Mix: consumables up, instruments flat, China down

Sequencing consumables grow from $2,939M in 2025 to $3,104M in 2026E (about +5.6%), with growth of about 8.8% in 2027E and 9.4% in 2028E, and reach $5,675M in 2032E. Total instruments (the machines) fall from $704M in 2023 to $482M in 2025, are forecast to dip to about $465M by 2029E, then recover to $662M by 2032E. Instruments' share of revenue falls from 16% in 2023 to about 7% in 2029–2030E. Microarray consumables are essentially flat at roughly $300–310M throughout. Service & Other revenue dropped to $634M in 2025 from $716M in 2024, then is forecast to rebound to $719M in 2026E (+13%); within it, microarray service jumps from $53M to $95M in 2026E, an unusual step-up the sheet does not explain.

### Geography

Greater China revenue has fallen from $384M (2023) to $308M (2024) and $243M (2025), and consensus has it falling further to $211M in 2026E, $189M in 2027E and $187M in 2028E, before an odd rebound to $230M in 2029E and then $207M, $226M and $245M. Europe is expected to keep growing, from $1,140M in 2023 to $1,360M in 2026E and $1,572M in 2028E. Americas grows from $2,406M (2025) to $2,598M (2026E) and $4,725M (2032E); the jump from $3,140M in 2028E to $3,630M in 2029E (+15.6%) looks large compared with total company growth of 7.4% that year.

### Margins and profitability

Adjusted gross margin rose from 65.3% in 2023 to 68.4% in 2024 and 68.2% in 2025, and is forecast to climb gradually to about 70.8% in 2031E before slipping to 70.4% in 2032E. Adjusted operating margin (operating profit as a share of revenue) rose sharply from 5.5% (2023) to 13.3% (2024) and 23.1% (2025), and is forecast at 23.5% (2026E), 25.2% (2027E), 26.5% (2028E), 28.8% (2030E) and 30.7% (2032E). Adjusted EPS grows about 10.7% in 2026E (from $4.84 to $5.36), 14.0% in 2027E, 14.7% in 2028E, 14.0% in 2029E, 18.9% in 2030E, 15.9% in 2031E and 11.7% in 2032E. Operating expenses are expected to fall as a share of revenue: adjusted SG&A from 25.2% (2025) to 18.7% (2032E), and R&D from 21.9% to about 19.5–19.6% by 2030–2031E.

### GAAP vs. adjusted

GAAP operating income swung from a loss of $833M in 2024 to a profit of $807M in 2025, and GAAP EPS from -$7.69 to $5.45. In 2025 GAAP EPS ($5.45) was above adjusted EPS ($4.84), which is unusual, and GAAP net income ($850M) exceeded adjusted ($756M). GAAP 2026E EPS is $5.02, below adjusted $5.36. Stock-based compensation was $380M in 2023 and $275M in 2025, and is forecast at $282M in 2026E (6.2% of revenue) and $595M in 2032E (7.3%).

### Cash, capital allocation and balance sheet

Free cash flow rose from $283M (2023) to $695M (2024) and $931M (2025), and is forecast at $997M (2026E), $1,102M (2027E) and $1,880M (2032E). Capex runs about 3.5–4.3% of revenue. Debt repayments of $1.2B were made in 2023 and $750M in 2024 (change in debt), with $500M in 2025. Consensus has $250M of debt repayments in 2026E and $167M in 2027E. Share repurchases were $116M in 2024 and $742M in 2025, and are forecast at $420M (2026E), $313M (2027E), $501M (2028E), $600M (2029E) and $1,000M each in 2030E and 2031E. Consensus has an acquisition outflow of $413.5M in 2026E, the only large one in the forecast, and the source does not identify the target. The balance sheet moves from net debt of $356M at end 2025 to net cash (negative net debt) of $222M in 2028E and $4.6B in 2031E. Cash is forecast to jump to $10.3B in 2032E.

### Outliers and data-quality flags

- **2032E looks inconsistent with prior years:** diluted shares rise to 164.85M (from 149.41M in 2031E) despite buybacks; additional paid-in capital falls from $9.13B to $7.04B; cash rises by $3.9B in one year while net change in cash is $1.88B; product revenue growth is +19.7% and cost of products sold +23.3% in 2032E; GAAP net income ($2,064M) exceeds adjusted net income ($2,028M) with GAAP net margin of 28.0%. These likely reflect a very thin set of contributors in the last year.
- **Cash flow statement subtotals:** beginning/ending cash on the cash flow statement for estimate years (for example 2026E ending $1,482.01M) does not match the balance sheet cash ($1,527.29M).
- **Ratio rows:** Net debt per share for 2026E (-1.60) has the wrong sign against positive net debt; working capital for 2030E ($1,387M) dips between $2,331M and $6,547M neighbors.
- **Core Illumina 2025 revenue of $3,709M** equals product revenue and is inconsistent with total revenue of $4,343M.
- **2023–2024 SG&A percentages** (54.60%, 57.75%) are inconsistent with the dollar amounts shown.
- **The "Prior Disclosures" EMEA row** overlaps with the Europe row and uses a different regional grouping, so Europe and EMEA figures should not be added together.
