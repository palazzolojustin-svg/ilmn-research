## Observations and implied trends

All of the following are arithmetic on the consensus figures, not statements by any analyst.

**Revenue.** Consensus revenue rises from $1,154.2M in Q3 2026 to $1,400.2M in Q4 2028, about 21% over nine quarters. Year-over-year comparisons available inside the sheet: Q3 2027 ($1,229.2M) is about 6.5% above Q3 2026; Q4 2027 ($1,292.3M) is about 5.9% above Q4 2026 ($1,219.8M); Q1 2028 ($1,245.8M) is about 7.4% above Q1 2027 ($1,159.6M); Q2 2028 ($1,316.5M) is about 8.1% above Q2 2027 ($1,217.8M); Q3 2028 ($1,333.3M) is about 8.5% above Q3 2027; Q4 2028 ($1,400.2M) is about 8.3% above Q4 2027. Growth is therefore expected to accelerate from the mid-single digits into the high single digits. The pattern is seasonal, with Q4 the strongest quarter and Q1 the weakest in each year.

**Mix.** Product revenue is about 84–86% of the total and rises slowly (84.5% in Q3 2026 to 85.3% in Q4 2028), while service & other drifts down from 15.7% to 14.8%. Consumables grow from 73.6% of revenue to 74.4% (peaking at 75.3% in Q3 2028). Instruments are expected to be roughly flat in dollars ($115.2M in Q3 2026, $137.4M in Q4 2028) and to fall as a share, from 9.9% to 9.5%, with a low of 8.8% in Q1 2028. Instruments are seasonally highest in Q4 each year. Sequencing consumables are the main growth driver, rising from $789.2M to $977.1M. Microarray revenue stays near $100–115M per quarter, essentially flat.

**Regions.** Americas grows from $661.2M to $828.9M and rises as a share of revenue (about 57.3% to 59.2%). Europe grows from $332.2M to $438.5M. Greater China is the one region expected to shrink: $50.9M in Q3 2026 to $47.0M in Q4 2028, with a trough of $42.8M in Q1 2028 (seasonally weak first quarters). APMEA rises from $112.7M to $144.6M.

**Margins and profit.** Adjusted gross margin is expected to rise from 68.84% to a peak of 70.22% in Q3 2028, then dip to 69.57% in Q4 2028. Adjusted operating margin widens from 24.00% to 28.87%, about 4.9 percentage points, driven mainly by operating expenses growing much more slowly than revenue: total adjusted operating expenses go from $530.9M to $563.1M (about 6%) against revenue growth of about 21%. R&D falls from 21.3% to 18.9% of revenue and SG&A from 23.1% to 20.9%. Adjusted EPS rises from $1.36 to $2.05 (about 51%); GAAP EPS from $1.28 to $1.89. The GAAP-to-adjusted EPS gap is about $0.08–$0.18 per quarter. Diluted share count is essentially flat at about 152.5–152.8M, so EPS growth comes from profit, not buybacks.

**Cash and debt.** Bloomberg's "Cash & cash equivalents" row climbs from $1,223.6M to $3,300.7M, with consensus free cash flow of roughly $284–358M per quarter. Quarterly share repurchases are modeled at $90.5M in the next two quarters, $62.7M per quarter in 2027 and $83.5M per quarter in 2028. Debt is modeled as roughly flat ($507.0M current plus about $1.59B long-term) with small paydowns in 2028 (current debt $449.75M and long-term $1,490.4M by Q3 2028).

## Anomalies and data-quality flags

- **Goodwill** is printed as "128400" in every column, an apparent formatting error; it is not a plausible value in millions.
- **Cash definitions conflict.** "Cash & cash equivalents" ($1,223.6M in Q3 2026) differs from the cash-flow statement's end-of-period cash ($1,298.3M), and from the "Cash, equivalents & short-term investments" row ($846.7M), which is lower than the cash line alone. These likely come from different sets of contributing analysts. End-of-period cash also does not equal the next period's beginning cash (for example, Q3 2026 ends at 1,298.30, yet Q4 2026 begins at 1,256.78).
- **Net debt jumps irregularly**: from $516.5M in Q3 2027 to net cash of $539.0M in Q4 2027, then $1,163.0M in each 2028 quarter before net cash of $615.0M in Q4 2028. Net debt per share and book value per share (12.56 in Q3 2026, 19.66 in Q4 2026, 26.91 in Q4 2027, 34.02 in Q4 2028) likewise swing in Q4 each year, which looks like differing analyst contributor sets rather than real changes.
- **Return on equity** of 47.5–48.3% in the first two quarters versus about 19.7–19.9% in the next two is a discontinuity, probably from different bases (such as trailing versus single-quarter).
- **Free cash flow margin** of 38.98% (Q3 2026) is far above any other period and above the 24.60% in Q4 2026. FCF of $335.2M exceeds operating cash flow minus capex ($326.1M − $51.4M = $274.7M), so the FCF contributor set differs from the others.
- **GAAP "other non-operating income"** swings widely (from $0.83M in Q2 2028 to $19.11M in Q1 2027), versus a steady $14–16M on the adjusted basis. GAAP income tax in Q4 2027 ($67.91M) is identical to the adjusted figure.
- **Net income on the cash flow statement** ($193.56M in Q3 2026) differs from both GAAP net income ($194.69M) and adjusted net income ($207.13M).
- **Stock-based compensation** jumps to $87.46M (7.29% of revenue) in Q1 2028 versus about $73–79M in other quarters.
- **Core Illumina gross profit** ($779.4M in Q3 2026) differs from both adjusted ($792.7M) and GAAP ($783.9M) gross profit, because it is on a different segment basis.
- **Total non-current liabilities** are a constant $2,525.00M in every 2026–2028 column through Q3 2028 (and 2028 Q4), a placeholder-like value, as are the lease items.
- **Quarterly tie-outs are imperfect.** For example, in Q4 2027 total assets ($7,959.0M) equal total liabilities plus equity ($3,776.5M + $4,223.0M = $7,999.5M) only approximately; in several quarters components are averages from different analyst sets and do not sum exactly.
