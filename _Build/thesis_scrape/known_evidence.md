## 1A
*Mix shift on the 6000 is not done; ~5 more points of conversion in 2027; the 6000 tail is "permanent"*

- NovaSeq X carried 83% of HT Gb shipped but 59% of HT consumables revenue in Q2'26 (deck, 30 Jul 2026). The 6000 therefore had 17% of Gb and 41% of revenue.
- The 6000 earns ~3.4x the X per Gb (band 3.2–3.6x), so the X is ~70% cheaper. This is our calc on rounded shares, and the shares are Gb shipped, not Gb consumed.
- The 6000/X revenue-per-Gb ratio path was 2.2x (Q2–Q3'24), 2.8x (Q4'24–Q2'25), 3.4x (Q3'25), 3.3x (Q4'25), 3.7x (Q1'26), 3.4x (Q2'26). The gap widened because the tail is the high-price volume, not because the X was repriced.
- The Q1→Q2'26 move in the ratio (0.27→0.30) sits inside rounding bands, so it is not statistically established.
- Quarterly X share of HT Gb / HT revenue: Q2'24 ~45/27; Q3'24 55/36; Q4'24 66/40; Q1'25 68/43; Q2'25 69/44; Q3'25 78/51; Q4'25 80/55; Q1'26 82/55; Q2'26 83/59.
- Clinical X share of HT Gb: 35, 43, 49, 51, 53, 64, 71, 76, 78% (Q2'24→Q2'26). Research: 59, 70, 80, 82, 82, 90, 90, 91, 92%.
- Clinical had 22% of its HT Gb on the 6000 vs 8% for research, so ~80–83% of remaining 6000 Gb is clinical. Clinical is ~64% of HT Gb (range 55–71%, derived).
- Clinical X revenue share is not disclosed. Derived at ~51% (research ~77%) and ~34% at Q3'25, consistent with Wolfe's "less than 50%" (Nov 2025).
- Derived 6000 revenue ~$219M in Q2'26 (~$0.88B/yr), vs $367M in Q2'24. Quarterly: $252M, $234M, $225M, $219M for Q3'25–Q2'26. This assumes HT = 69% of sequencing consumables (65–72% tested).
- Derived X revenue rose from $136M (Q2'24) to $316M (Q2'26). Total HT stayed in a $480–535M band, so X has mostly replaced 6000 dollars.
- Derived clinical 6000 revenue is ~$182M/qtr (~$0.73B/yr), ~36% of clinical sequencing consumables. This assumes the same price gap in clinical as in research, which no source confirms.
- The 6000 y/y revenue decline slowed to −$7M in Q2'26 from −$67M in Q2'25.

## 1B
*Discounting will accelerate: 35B flow cell, Ultima/Element/Roche pricing, bigger extra discounts for large accounts*

- The 35B flow cell (+40% output vs 25B) is on the roadmap for H1 2027 with no price (roadmap page, 23 Feb 2026 release). Thaysen refused to price it ("not ready… to talk about pricing"; "aggressive pricing brings aggressive volume growth").
- The 35B cut estimate is ~20% ($2.50→$2.00/Gb), from one ex-Illumina AD (left Jul 2025). The "January 2027" launch date is hearsay (Mass. genome-center VP).
- UBS: roadmap gives "a path to ~$150/genome" if the 35B is priced like the 25B (~−25% from ~$200 list). One UBS expert pays ~$175/genome after discounts; large users pay ~$125; he expected Roche at $100.
- The 35B's revenue cost at constant Gb on an X base of ~$1.27B: $63M / $127M / $253M (2.0 / 4.1 / 8.2% of FY26 consumables) at 25 / 50 / 100% adoption. A 15%+ cut on existing customers at 50% scope is worth ~$150M of FY27.
- Three price layers fill management's "mid-teens" decline: tail conversion ~6–7%/yr, the 35B ~4%/yr, residual repricing ~4%/yr. That leaves no room for a Roche/Ultima response. Whether management's mid-teens already includes the 35B is unknown.
- 35B stacking: a ~20% cut on a −15% base is −32% effective, requiring clinical Gb ~+47% to hold revenue flat (+30.7% at half scope; +38.9% on a −10% base).
- Management's long-term model: volume mid-20s%, price mid-teens decline, giving HSD revenue (Strategy Day Aug 2024, Bernstein May 2025, JPM Jan 2026). In Aug 2024 price was modeled at −20–25%/yr; Q3'24 came in "closer to 25 rather than 20."
- Historical price/volume: Q3'24 Gb +40%, consumables +5–7%, "largely price… about 25% decline"; Q2'24 Gb +40%, HT revenue ~+1%.
- Implied revenue/Gb, total ROW (Gb = management floor): ≤−25% (Q1'24), −27%, −22%, −21.5%, −23%, −21.5%, −21% (Q3'25), −14.6% (Q4'25), −17.7%, −18.5% (Q2'26). Clinical eased from ~−21 to −25% to ~−8 to −12%.
