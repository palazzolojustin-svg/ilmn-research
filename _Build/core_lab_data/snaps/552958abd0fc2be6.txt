Overview
Offering the $221 human genome
High-quality human whole-genome sequencing with data analysis is available at the UMGC in flexible coverage options (30x, 60x, and 90x). Advances in the Ultima Genomics UG 100 platform enable us to offer the $221 human genome, giving researchers one of the most cost-effective paths to WGS available today (to our knowledge). The UMGC delivers research-grade human WGS on the latest sequencing technologies:
Ultima UG 100 — population-scale efficiency. The UG 100 is an ultra-high-throughput instrument that processes up to 480 human genomes per week at 30x minimum depth. It enables scalable, high-quality human WGS for large cohorts.
Element AVITI24 — exceptional accuracy for precision studies. The AVITI's paired-end chemistry and UltraQ mode deliver exceptional SNV and indel accuracy, making it ideal for clinical research, tumor/normal comparisons, and applications where precision is critical.
Mouse whole-genome sequencing and data analysis are available at the same rates and sequencing options as human WGS.
Analysis Quality
Instrument | Read Type | Raw SNV Quality | Indel Quality |
Ultima UG 100 | Single-end reads (≈200–300 bp) | Q40 | Good |
Element AVITI | Paired-end 2x150-bp reads | Q40 | Excellent |
Element AVITI Ultra Q | Paired-end 2x150-bp reads | Q50 | Excellent |
What’s Included
Sample Quality Control: Rigorous assessment of sample integrity and quantity.
Library Creation: PCR-free workflows to maximize coverage uniformity and minimize bias.
Next-Generation Sequencing: Sequencing is performed on either the UG 100 or AVITI.
Primary Data QC: Comprehensive evaluation of sequencing metrics to verify data completeness and quality prior to analysis.
Data Analysis: Includes mapping to the reference genome and variant analysis (including SNVs and indels) with Google DeepVariant and GATK.
Getting Started
The UMGC is a Certified Service Provider for the UG 100 system, one of a few select labs worldwide with this distinction. This certification underscores our expertise in delivering high-quality, research-grade human WGS on cutting-edge technology. To discuss a human or mouse WGS project, contact us at [email protected].
