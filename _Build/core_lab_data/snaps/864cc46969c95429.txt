Ultima UG100 Sequencing
Services are available now in a pre-launch phase, but are not yet listed in our ordering system. To request more information, including pricing details and how to place an order, please email ‘genomics@cornell.edu’ and include the NGS platform of interest.
These sequencing runs are sent out to external providers but supported by the Genomics Facility (including local ordering/sample drop-off, QC checks and data management/distribution/archive), and without the need to acquire a quote/PO outside Cornell.
Ultima Genomics' UG 100 is a high throughput sequencer that replaces the standard flow cell with an open silicon wafer and a "flow-based," non-terminating chemistry that adds one base at a time across the whole surface. The UG100 enables an output of 10 to 12 billion reads per wafer at a cost of about $0.24 per million reads. Sequencing may be ordered for either ½ wafer (5-6 billion reads) or a whole wafer (10-12B). Importantly, the platform natively produces single-end reads only which are bioinformatically split into paired-end reads. Additionally, standard Illumina-style libraries aren't natively compatible and must be converted, appending Ultima-compatible adapter sequences through an extra PCR step, before they can be sequenced on the UG 100 (service available for additional charge).
