Next Generation Sequencing
The Scripps Research Genomics Core supports next-generation sequencing platforms from Illumina, Oxford Nanopore and Element Biosciences, and PacBio (via local collaborators). We are able to provide a wide range of sequencing solutions to optimize sequencing scale, budget and turnaround time.
In most cases, the sequencing will complete within seven to ten business days after the submission of prepared libraries which pass the initial quality check. If there are urgent sequencing needs, please directly reach out to the Genomics Core for assistance.
Data Release
Internal users:
Demultiplexing and dispatch of sequencing data usually takes 1-2 business days after the sequencing run has completed and passed quality check.
Submitters should receive an email from Genomics Core [email protected] providing SFTP links to sequencing data, together with the sample key information (when applicable).
Please read the dispatch email carefully regarding the downloading instructions.
Please note that complications during demultiplexing process (e.g., wrong barcode information provided, barcode clashes) will delay the sequencing data being released.

Single Cell Genomics
Single-cell genomics is a rapidly evolving technology that studies the genetic material of individual cells, allowing researchers to understand cellular diversity and function within a complex tissue. It provides insights into cellular heterogeneity, revealing unique gene expression patterns and mutations at the single-cell level. Currently the Genomics Core supports 10x Genomics applications using the Chromium X instrument as the matured mainstream platform. We are actively assessing new Single Cell Genomics technologies as they evolve to provide broader and more accessible applications for researchers.
Key 10X Feature
Extensive experience in the comprehensive end-to-end process: cell suspension quality check, GEM generation, library preparation, thorough quality control, sequencing.
Cutting-edge 10X instrument capable of analyzing hundreds to tens of thousands of cells in a single run. Ability to multiplex samples using 10x protocols such as CellPlex, Flex and the new GEM-X Universal 3’ and 5’ (On-chip Multiplexing) kits. Also available is the option to multiplex samples using BioLegend TotalSeq oligo-conjugated antibodies (hashtags).
10X libraries sequenced using either Aviti or Illumina Nextseq 2000, ensuring cost-effective sequencing and scalability for large projects.
Assistance with experimental design and assay selection, including Feature Barcoding and CiteSeq.
Additional support services are available on-campus through the Flow Cytometry Core.

Custom Microarrays
Microarray Scanning
The Genomics Core can provide high resolution (0.5um) imaging of standard microscope slides using an Innopsys 1100AL scanner. This is applicable to most commercial microarray slide options, including DNA/RNA, protein, peptide and glycan microarrays. The scanner can accommodate standard microscope slides and scan/filter at 3 standard channels (488/FITC – 532/Cy3 – 635/Cy5-APC) .
Custom Microarray Fabrication
Using robotic handlers, we can distribute samples onto standard microscope slides in microvolumes from 1nL to 500nL. Contact microarray printing uses solid steel capillary microarray printing pins using a MicroGrid II array printer equipped with ArrayIT Stealth Microarray pins. Non-contact deposition is available using a Dispendix (I.DOT). Additionally, the Genomics Core fabricates the NCFG mammalian and bacterial glycan microarrays, producing the most comprehensive set of available to the public.
