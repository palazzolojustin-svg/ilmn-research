Next-generation sequencing services from the Centre d’expertise et de services Génome Québec
New high-throughput facilities at the Centre d’expertise et de services (CES) Génome Québec, located at CHU Sainte-Justine
Equipped with new state-of-the-art facilities, our CES, located at CHU Sainte-Justine, offers faster turnaround times, with standard deliveries in 4 to 6 weeks and express services available on request.
We have also introduced lower prices, thanks to our position as world leader in the implementation of Illumina’s latest instruments, including NovaSeq X technology and 25B Flow Cell.
A turnkey service for your sequencing projects
Our team supports you at every stage of your project with a turnkey service, from initial consultation through to bioinformatics analysis. We do our utmost to guarantee high-quality results that meet your specific needs.
