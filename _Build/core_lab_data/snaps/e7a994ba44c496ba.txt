GSF FY26 Price Schedule
The fee schedule was formulated on the basis of the operating expenses of the laboratory in addition to a comparison with other academic NGS facilities. A project charge is agreed upon for complex, multifaceted investigations covering a specific time period and the estimated number of analyses. Fees are reviewed annually and adjusted as necessary, with guidance from the Science Advisory Committee. Please contact GSF Director, Dr. Zhao Lai (laiz@uthscsa.edu), for details.
