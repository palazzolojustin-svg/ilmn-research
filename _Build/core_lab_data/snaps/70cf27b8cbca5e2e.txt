Service Fees
We provide support for a broad range of services, including Next Generation Sequencing, sample preparation, DNA/RNA isolation and SNP fingerprinting. UMC Utrecht, and NWO subsidize the facility allowing clients to pay only running and personell costs.
Please contact us for a quotation.
