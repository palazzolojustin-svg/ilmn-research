Preferred Ultima UG 100 Pricing for 10x Genomics Projects
$500 off Ultima UG 100 wafers
For 10x Genomics single-cell and spatial projects
The UMN Genomics Center is pleased to announce new preferred pricing on Ultima Genomics sequencing for 10x Genomics single-cell and spatial projects. 10x customers can send compatible 10x Genomics libraries to the UMGC for sequencing on the Ultima UG 100 platform at preferred rates, with savings of $500 per wafer (10–12B reads) or $250 per half-wafer (5–6B reads). This preferred pricing is available to both UMN and external researchers.
By pairing 10x Genomics’ high-resolution assays with Ultima’s ultra-high-throughput UG 100 sequencer, researchers can analyze their samples more cost-effectively or scale up to larger, more complex single-cell and spatial studies.
Ultima UG 100 Preferred Pricing
UG 100 Sequencing Costs | UMN | External |
Per wafer. 10–12 billion reads. | $2,704 | $3,188 |
Per half wafer. 5–6 billion reads. | $1,857 | $2,175 |
Prices in this table reflect $500 off per wafer (or $250 per half-wafer) compared with standard UMGC Ultima UG 100 pricing.

Contact your local 10x Genomics Account Executive. (For UMN researchers: Benton Veire ([email protected]) is the UMN account manager).
