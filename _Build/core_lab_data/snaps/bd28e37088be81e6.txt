Key Considerations when transitioning from the NovaSeq6000 to the NovaSeqX
When sending premade libraries for NovaSeqX sequencing there are a few things you should consider:
Small fragments form colonies much more efficiently on the NovaSeqX than on the NovaSeq6000. You must remove all adapter dimers from your libraries to prevent over-representation of these and other small fragments.
All libraries in your pool must be the same size. You cannot pool different sized libraries for sequencing on the X. If you have multiple sized libraries, you must use separate lanes or select the NovaSeq6000.
Median insert size shifts to smaller lengths on the NovaX. You may have to size select to a larger fragment size when sequencing on the NovaSeqX.
