1. DNA Sequencing: Whole genome sequencing / Metagenomic sequencing / Shotgun sequencing
This service is used to sequence all of the DNA in a sample (i.e. whole genomes from plants, animals, or all of the DNA from an entire microbial community, etc). This is often referred to as metagenomic sequencing or shotgun sequencing. Both short- and long-read platforms at different depths are available, giving flexibility depending on your aims.

Illumina Sequencing for client-prepared libraries
If you are an advanced user and have made your own libraries, we offer full flow cell sequencing services for libraries that have been prepared outside of our lab for Illumina sequencing. Pricing includes library QC using a bioanalyzer or Lab chip, and cloud based data delivery (e.g. Globus), as well as shipping and customs forms for overseas sequencing. Do NOT order this service if you submit genomic DNA--> THIS IS FOR PREPARED LIBRARIES ONLY. Auckland Genomics takes no responsibility for libraries that fail sequencing, this is a use at your own risk service.
