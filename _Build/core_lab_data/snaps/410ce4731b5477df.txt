Illumina Sequencing Instruments
The Genomics Technology Core has provided next generation sequencing services to the UM system since 2008. The GTC maintain and operate several Illumina sequencing platforms; NovaSeq X Plus and MiSeq (3) instruments.
The NovaSeq X Plus offers Illumina's most advanced sequencer to date while dramatically lowering costs. The release of the 25B flow cell introduced a 2.5-fold increase in output over that of the previous NovaSeq 6000. The new XLEAP-SBS chemistry delivers increased speed and greater fidelity. Each flow cell contains 8 independent lanes, which can be used to load up to 8 separate library pools.
