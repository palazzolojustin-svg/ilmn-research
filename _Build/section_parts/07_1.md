In the expert's view, the binding constraint is adoption of NGS as a clinical test, and it gets stuck in three places: reimbursement (whether insurers pay), regulation (test approval pathways) and prescribing behavior (how oncologists order tests and how readily pathologists adopt them) [Expert: former Illumina senior manager, Jan 2026 est.].

The expert called the sub-$100 genome "a very nice, sexy number" with limited oncology impact.
