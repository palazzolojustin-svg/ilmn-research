life-science tools :: Instruments, consumables and software sold to labs that do biological research, drug development and testing.
consumables :: Supplies and reagents that customers use up and must keep re-buying, such as reagent kits and lab plastics.
Ion Torrent :: Thermo Fisher's DNA sequencing platform, a competitor to Illumina's sequencers.
Olink :: Thermo Fisher's proteomics business, which measures many proteins at once and competes with Illumina's SomaLogic.
proteomics :: The large-scale measurement of proteins in a sample, as opposed to genomics, which reads DNA.
multiomics :: Combining several types of molecular data (DNA, RNA, proteins, metabolites) to study biology.
clinical NGS :: Next-generation DNA sequencing used for medical testing rather than research.
read-across :: What one company's results imply about another company facing similar markets or customers.
organic growth :: Revenue growth excluding the effects of acquisitions, divestitures and currency changes.
adjusted (figures) :: Results that exclude items such as acquisition-related charges to show underlying performance.
GAAP :: Standard U.S. accounting rules; GAAP figures include items that adjusted figures strip out.
EPS :: Earnings per share, a company's profit divided by its share count.
basis point (bp) :: One-hundredth of a percentage point.
operating margin :: Operating profit as a percentage of revenue.
gross margin :: Revenue minus the direct cost of goods sold, as a percentage of revenue.
SG&A :: Selling, general and administrative expenses, the overhead cost of running and selling the business.
volume leverage :: Margin improvement from spreading fixed costs over higher sales.
product mix :: The blend of products sold; selling more lower-margin items hurts margins even if sales grow.
consensus :: The average of Wall Street analysts' forecasts.
guidance :: A company's own forecast of its future results.
free cash flow :: Cash generated from operations minus capital spending.
capital expenditures (capex) :: Spending on long-lived assets such as factories and equipment.
EBITDA :: Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization, a rough proxy for operating cash earnings.
leverage ratio :: Debt divided by EBITDA, a measure of how indebted a company is.
ROIC :: Return on invested capital, profit generated per dollar of capital invested in the business.
dilutive / accretive :: Lowering or raising a metric such as EPS or growth rate.
share buyback :: A company buying back its own shares, which reduces share count and raises EPS.
investment-grade credit rating :: A credit rating signalling low default risk, which keeps borrowing costs low.
divestiture :: The sale of a business unit.
Investor Day :: A company event where management sets out multi-year strategy and financial targets.
authorizations :: New contracted work signed by a contract research business, a leading indicator of future revenue.
bioproduction :: Supplying the media, equipment and consumables used to manufacture biologic drugs.
biologics :: Drugs made in living cells, such as antibodies, rather than by chemical synthesis.
cell culture media :: The nutrient mixture used to grow cells that produce biologic drugs.
single-use technologies :: Disposable bags and components used in drug manufacturing instead of steel equipment.
bioreactor :: A vessel in which cells are grown to produce biologic drugs.
DynaDrive :: Thermo Fisher's single-use bioreactor product line.
purification resins :: Materials used to separate and purify a drug from the cell culture it was made in.
filtration and separation :: Technologies that filter and separate drug products during manufacturing; Thermo bought this business from Solventum.
CRO :: Contract research organization, a company that runs clinical trials for drug developers.
CDMO :: Contract development and manufacturing organization, a company that develops and manufactures drugs for other firms.
clinical trial endpoint :: The measurement used to decide whether a clinical trial succeeded.
sterile fill-finish :: Filling a drug into its final sterile vial or syringe.
drug substance / drug product :: The active drug ingredient versus the finished dosage form patients receive.
oral solid dose :: Pills and tablets.
reshoring :: Moving manufacturing back to the home country, here moving drug production to the U.S.
greenfield facility :: A brand-new plant built from scratch.
tech transfer :: Moving a drug's manufacturing process from one site to another.
patent cliff :: The point at which a key drug loses patent protection and faces cheaper generic competition.
IRA :: The Inflation Reduction Act, a 2022 U.S. law that lets Medicare negotiate some drug prices.
most-favored-nation (MFN) pricing :: U.S. policy proposals to tie U.S. drug prices to the lowest prices paid in other rich countries.
antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) :: An antibody linked to a toxic drug that it delivers directly to cancer cells.
personalized mRNA cancer vaccine :: An mRNA-based therapy designed for one patient's specific tumor.
foundational biological model :: A large AI model trained on lab data to represent how biology works.
mass spectrometry :: A technique that identifies and measures molecules by their mass, used for proteomics and chemistry.
Orbitrap :: Thermo Fisher's family of high-resolution mass spectrometers.
chromatography / UHPLC :: Methods that separate mixtures into components; UHPLC is an ultra-high-pressure liquid version.
PCR / thermal cycler :: PCR is the technique for copying DNA; a thermal cycler is the instrument that runs it.
electron microscopy :: Imaging that uses electron beams to see structures down to the atomic level.
yield ramp :: Raising the share of good chips produced as a new semiconductor fab starts up.
spectroscopy :: Measuring how materials absorb or emit light to determine their composition.
immunodiagnostics :: Blood tests that detect antibodies or antigens.
research and safety market channel :: Fisher Scientific, Thermo's distribution business for lab supplies and safety products.
PPI Business System :: Thermo Fisher's continuous-improvement operating method.
backlog :: Orders received but not yet delivered and recognized as revenue.
