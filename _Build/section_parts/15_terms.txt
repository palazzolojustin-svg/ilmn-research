DNA sequencer :: A machine that reads the order of chemical letters in DNA.
Reagents / consumables :: The chemical kits customers must buy for every sequencing run, which generate recurring revenue.
Installed base :: The machines already placed with customers, which drive repeat reagent sales.
Short-read sequencing (NGS) :: Next-generation sequencing that reads DNA in many small fragments; the approach used by Illumina and MGI.
High-throughput sequencing :: Sequencing that processes very large amounts of DNA at once; UBS uses it as a synonym for NGS.
Long-read sequencing :: Sequencing that reads long continuous stretches of DNA in one go.
Nanopore sequencing :: A long-read method that reads DNA by passing it through a tiny pore and measuring the signal changes.
Spatial omics :: Measuring genes or proteins while preserving where they sit within a tissue.
Multi-omics :: Combining several types of molecular data (DNA, RNA, proteins and so on) about the same samples.
CoolMPS / StandardMPS :: Names of MGI's proprietary sequencing chemistries.
Lab automation (GLI) :: Robotic equipment that handles lab samples; MGI's "Generative Lab Intelligence" segment.
Seq-all :: MGI's sequencing segment, covering sequencers, reagents and related products.
Import substitution :: Chinese buyers replacing foreign-made products with domestic ones, often encouraged by policy.
Public bids (tenders) :: Formal purchasing competitions run by government or hospital buyers.
Out-licensing :: Letting another company use a technology in exchange for upfront fees, milestones and royalties.
Upfront payment :: Cash paid when a licensing deal is signed.
Milestone payment :: Cash paid when a deal reaches an agreed goal.
Royalty :: A percentage of future sales paid to the owner of a licensed technology.
Divestment :: Selling a business or subsidiary.
Earnings commitment :: A seller's promise that acquired assets will hit set profit targets over a defined period.
Net loss attributable to parent :: The loss belonging to the listed company's shareholders, excluding minority interests.
Recurring net loss :: The net loss excluding one-off items.
Preliminary results :: An early, unaudited estimate of results, often given as a range.
Impairment :: A write-down of an asset's book value.
FX gain :: A profit arising from movements in currency exchange rates.
Tax reversal :: The release of a previously booked tax charge, which boosts profit.
Gross margin (GPM) :: The share of revenue left after direct production costs.
Average selling price (ASP) :: Revenue divided by units sold.
EBIT :: Earnings before interest and taxes; operating profit.
EBITDA :: Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization.
Operating margin / EBIT margin :: Operating profit as a share of revenue.
Expense ratio :: A cost line (selling, G&A or R&D) as a share of revenue.
G&A :: General and administrative (overhead) costs.
Non-operating items :: Gains and losses outside the core business, such as deal payments or asset-sale gains.
Asset disposal gain :: Profit booked when an asset is sold for more than its book value.
EPS :: Earnings per share; net profit divided by the number of shares.
Consensus :: The average forecast of analysts covering a stock.
Net cash :: Cash held minus debt owed.
Capex :: Capital expenditure; spending on long-lived assets such as equipment and buildings.
Operating cash flow :: Cash generated by the business's normal operations.
Book value per share :: Accounting net worth divided by the number of shares.
ROIC :: Return on invested capital; profit relative to the capital tied up in the business.
DCF :: Discounted cash flow; valuing a company by discounting its expected future cash flows to today.
WACC :: Weighted average cost of capital; the discount rate used in a DCF.
Beta :: A measure of how much a stock moves relative to the market.
Risk-free rate :: The return on a theoretically riskless investment, such as a government bond, used in valuation.
Price target :: An analyst's estimate of where a share price will be in 12 months.
Neutral rating (UBS) :: UBS's rating for a stock whose expected return is within ±6% of the market return assumption.
Market return assumption :: UBS's expected market return, equal to the one-year local interest rate plus 5%.
Forecast excess return :: The expected stock return minus the market return assumption.
P/E :: Share price divided by earnings per share.
P/S :: Price-to-sales; market value divided by revenue.
P/BV :: Price-to-book; share price divided by accounting net worth per share.
EV/EBITDA :: Enterprise value (market value plus net debt) divided by EBITDA.
EV/revenue :: Enterprise value divided by revenue.
Equity FCF yield :: Free cash flow available to shareholders as a share of market value.
Market capitalization :: The total market value of a company's shares.
Free float :: The share of stock that is freely tradable by the public.
CAGR :: Compound annual growth rate.
Scenario analysis :: Valuing a company under upside, base and downside assumptions.
Fast Take / First Read :: UBS's short reaction notes, issued right after news or results.
YoY / QoQ :: Year on year and quarter on quarter comparisons.
APAC / EMEA :: Asia-Pacific; Europe, Middle East and Africa.
STAR Market :: Shanghai's technology-focused stock exchange board, where MGI is listed.
