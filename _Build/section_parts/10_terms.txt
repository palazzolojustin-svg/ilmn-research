nanopore sequencing :: Reading DNA or RNA by passing a single strand through a tiny protein pore and measuring changes in electrical current.
sequencing-by-synthesis :: Illumina's method of reading DNA by copying short fragments and detecting each added base optically.
short-read sequencing :: Sequencing that reads DNA in short fragments (typically hundreds of bases) that are then pieced together.
long-read sequencing :: Sequencing that reads long continuous stretches of DNA or RNA, often thousands to millions of bases.
universal reads :: J.P. Morgan's term for ONT's ability to produce reads of essentially any length.
methylation :: Chemical tags on DNA that affect gene activity; nanopores can detect them directly.
native RNA sequencing :: Reading RNA molecules directly rather than first copying them into DNA.
structural variants :: Large rearrangements of DNA such as deletions, duplications or inversions.
phasing :: Working out which genetic variants sit on which of a person's two chromosome copies.
basecaller :: Software that converts the raw electrical signal from a nanopore into DNA letters.
Q-score :: A logarithmic measure of base-call accuracy; higher numbers mean fewer errors (e.g. Q22.6 ≈ 99.4%).
flow cell :: The consumable chip containing the nanopores on which each sequencing run takes place.
flow cell recycling :: Reusing returned flow cells to lower manufacturing cost and raise gross margin.
consumables :: Reagent kits and flow cells customers must buy for every sequencing run.
multiplexing :: Running several tagged samples together on one flow cell, lowering consumables needed per sample.
PromethION :: ONT's high-throughput sequencer line, now more than half of group revenue.
P2i / P2 Solo :: Smaller PromethION models; ONT focuses on P2i and discontinued P2 Solo in 2026.
GridION / GridION Q-line / GridION Dx :: ONT's mid-sized benchtop sequencer, its locked-down version for regulated settings, and its CE/UKCA-approved diagnostic version.
MinION :: ONT's small portable sequencer.
ElysION :: ONT's sample-preparation automation platform, stopped in 2025–26.
SBX / Axelios :: Roche's Sequencing by Expansion technology and the sequencer built on it, a short-read system using a nanopore sensor.
IVD :: In vitro diagnostic; a regulated test or device used on patient samples.
IVDR :: The EU regulation governing in vitro diagnostic devices.
CE / UKCA marking :: The EU and UK regulatory approval marks required to sell medical devices.
LDT :: Lab-developed test; a test designed and run within a single laboratory rather than sold as an approved kit.
research use only (RUO) :: A product label meaning a test cannot be used for clinical decisions.
mRNA QC :: Quality-control testing that confirms manufactured mRNA vaccines match their intended sequence and quality.
CQA :: Critical quality attribute; a measurable property (identity, integrity, purity) a drug batch must meet.
BLA :: Biologics License Application; the US regulatory filing to approve a biologic drug or vaccine.
CDMO :: Contract development and manufacturing organisation that makes drugs for pharma companies.
sterility testing :: Checking that a manufactured biologic is free of contaminating organisms.
adventitious viral agents (AVA) :: Unwanted viruses that can contaminate biological manufacturing.
cross-licensing agreement :: A deal in which two companies grant each other rights to their patents.
royalty :: A percentage of a partner's product sales paid to the patent owner.
net present value (NPV) :: Today's value of all expected future cash flows from an asset or deal.
capex model :: ONT's shift to selling larger devices outright rather than leasing them.
installed base :: The number of instruments already placed with customers.
placements :: Instruments sold or installed at customer sites.
utilisation / pull-through :: How much consumables customers buy per installed instrument.
end markets :: Customer groups; ONT reports Research, Clinical, BioPharma and Applied Industrial.
Applied Markets :: ONT's non-academic customers: Clinical, BioPharma and Applied Industrial combined.
LSRT :: Life Science Research Tools; ONT's main reported business line.
EMEAI / AMR / APAC :: ONT's regions: Europe, Middle East, Africa and India; the Americas; Asia-Pacific.
NIH :: US National Institutes of Health, the largest funder of US academic research.
UK Biobank :: A large UK health-research cohort; ONT has a contract to sequence 50,000 genomes for it.
PRECISE / PRECISE II :: Singapore's national population genomics programme, an ONT contract that finished in 2025.
trading update :: A short interim company statement of headline figures before full results.
constant currency (LC/CER/ccy) :: Growth measured with exchange-rate effects removed.
consensus :: The average of analysts' forecasts for a company.
gross margin :: Revenue minus the direct cost of goods sold, as a share of revenue.
basis points (bps) :: Hundredths of a percentage point; 100 bps equals 1 percentage point.
Adjusted EBITDA :: Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation, excluding one-off items; a proxy for operating profit.
breakeven :: The point at which a profit measure such as Adjusted EBITDA reaches zero.
free cash flow (FCF) :: Cash from operations minus capital spending (and, for ONT, capitalised development costs).
operating expenses (OpEx) :: Ongoing costs of running the business, such as R&D and SG&A.
SG&A :: Selling, general and administrative costs.
capitalised R&D :: Development costs recorded as an asset and amortised over time rather than expensed immediately.
working capital :: Cash tied up in day-to-day operations such as inventory and receivables.
operating leverage :: Profits growing faster than revenue because costs grow more slowly.
CAGR :: Compound annual growth rate; the steady yearly growth rate linking two values.
EV/Sales :: Enterprise value (equity value plus net debt) divided by revenue; used to value unprofitable firms.
DCF :: Discounted cash flow; valuation from projected future cash flows discounted to today.
WACC :: Weighted average cost of capital; the discount rate used in a DCF.
terminal growth rate :: The assumed long-run growth rate after a DCF's explicit forecast period.
price target :: An analyst's estimate of where a share price should trade over a set horizon.
Overweight / Buy :: Broker ratings meaning the stock is expected to outperform its peers or coverage universe.
re-rating :: The market paying a higher valuation multiple for a company's shares.
clearing event :: A guidance reset that removes an overhang of doubt from a stock.
corporate broker :: A bank appointed to advise a UK-listed company on markets and investor relations, a potential conflict for its research.
SMID :: Small and mid-capitalisation stocks.
total addressable / serviceable market :: The full revenue opportunity for a product, versus the portion a company can realistically target.
nanopore :: A tiny protein hole that reads DNA or RNA by measuring changes in electrical current as a single molecule passes through.
native DNA/RNA :: The original molecule read directly, without copying or chemical conversion.
long reads :: Very long continuous stretches of DNA sequence read in one piece.
short reads :: Short DNA fragments read at high volume and low cost, Illumina's core approach.
synthetic long reads :: Long sequences reconstructed by stitching short reads together rather than read directly.
flow cell :: The consumable chip holding the nanopores, used for each sequencing run.
consumables :: Flow cells and reagent kits customers must buy for every sequencing run.
MinION :: ONT's small, portable entry-level sequencer.
GridION :: ONT's benchtop sequencer; Q and Dx versions serve QC and diagnostics.
PromethION :: ONT's high-throughput instrument range (P2 Solo, P2i, P24).
Q-Line :: Locked-down, semi-regulated instrument versions for biopharma quality control.
ElysION :: ONT's automated sample-prep and sequencing system, now paused.
methylation :: Chemical tags on DNA that affect gene activity, sometimes called the fifth base.
methylome :: The full pattern of methylation across a genome.
structural variants :: Large rearrangements of DNA that short reads often miss.
adaptive sampling :: Software-driven selection in which the pore ejects DNA strands that are not of interest.
metagenomic sequencing :: Sequencing everything in a sample to find unknown organisms.
base calling :: Converting the raw electrical signal into DNA letters.
T2T genome :: A complete telomere-to-telomere genome with no gaps.
diagnostic yield :: The share of patients for whom a test finds a diagnosis.
reflex test :: A follow-up test ordered after an initial result.
frontline test :: The first-choice test for a condition.
LDT :: Laboratory-developed test that a lab builds and validates itself.
IVD :: In vitro diagnostic, a regulator-certified commercial test kit.
CE-IVD :: European regulatory mark for in vitro diagnostics.
BLA :: Biologics license application, the regulatory filing for a biological drug.
biopharma QC :: Testing that checks a drug batch meets its specification.
sterility testing :: Checking manufactured biologics for contamination.
plasmid :: A circle of DNA used in biotech research and manufacturing.
AAV :: Adeno-associated virus, a vector used to deliver gene therapies.
Sanger sequencing :: An older, low-throughput sequencing method.
multiplexing :: Running several samples on one flow cell.
proteomics :: Analysis of proteins; for ONT, peptide mapping and later protein sequencing.
multiomics :: Combining DNA, RNA, methylation and protein data.
NGS :: Next-generation sequencing, the modern high-throughput sequencing market.
NIH :: US National Institutes of Health, a major funder of academic research.
export controls :: Government limits on selling technology into a country such as China.
