consensus :: The average of forecasts published by sell-side analysts, used as the market's baseline expectation.
sell-side analyst :: A bank or brokerage researcher who covers a stock and publishes forecasts and ratings for clients.
beat/miss :: Whether reported results come in above or below consensus.
fiscal year (52/53-week) :: Illumina's accounting year, which ends on a Sunday near December 31 and occasionally contains an extra week.
GAAP :: Standard US accounting rules; GAAP results include all costs such as stock pay and amortization.
adjusted (non-GAAP) :: Results that exclude items such as stock-based compensation and amortization of acquired intangibles.
stock-based compensation (SBC) :: The non-cash cost of paying employees in company shares.
amortization of acquired intangibles :: The gradual expensing of the purchase value of acquired technology and customer relationships.
EPS :: Earnings per share, net profit divided by the number of shares.
diluted shares :: Share count including options and other securities that could become shares.
consumables :: Reagent kits, flow cells and chips customers must buy for every sequencing or microarray run.
instruments :: The sequencing machines and array scanners, sold once.
service & other :: Revenue from service contracts, genotyping services and similar items.
sequencing :: Technology that reads DNA letters directly.
microarray :: An older, cheaper chip-based technology that tests for known genetic variants.
GRAIL :: Illumina's former cancer-detection blood-test business, present in the data only for 2023–2024.
Core Illumina :: Illumina's business excluding GRAIL.
intersegment revenue :: Sales between a company's own business units.
Greater China :: Mainland China, Hong Kong and Taiwan.
APMEA :: Asia-Pacific outside Greater China, plus the Middle East and Africa.
EMEA :: Europe, Middle East and Africa, an older regional grouping that overlaps Europe and APMEA.
gross margin :: The share of revenue left after the direct cost of making products and delivering services.
operating margin :: Operating profit as a share of revenue.
operating leverage :: Profit growing faster than sales because costs grow more slowly than revenue.
SG&A :: Selling, general and administrative expenses, meaning sales force and corporate overhead.
R&D :: Research and development spending.
EBITDA :: Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization, a rough measure of cash operating profit.
net margin :: Net profit as a share of revenue.
tax rate :: Income tax expense divided by pre-tax income.
free cash flow (FCF) :: Cash from operations minus capital expenditures.
FCF margin :: Free cash flow as a share of revenue.
capital expenditures (capex) :: Spending on equipment, facilities and other long-lived assets.
share repurchase (buyback) :: A company buying back its own stock, which reduces the share count.
goodwill :: The premium paid over book value in past acquisitions, carried as an asset.
net debt :: Borrowings minus cash; a negative figure means more cash than debt.
current portion of debt :: Debt due within one year.
current ratio :: Current assets divided by current liabilities; above 1 means short-term assets cover short-term bills.
working capital :: Current assets minus current liabilities.
days sales outstanding (DSO) :: The average number of days taken to collect payment from customers.
return on equity (ROE) :: Net profit divided by shareholders' equity.
return on assets (ROA) :: Net profit divided by total assets.
book value per share :: Shareholders' equity divided by the number of shares.
retained earnings :: Cumulative profits kept in the business rather than paid out.
additional paid-in capital :: Money shareholders paid for stock above its nominal par value.
deferred tax :: Taxes owed or prepaid that will be settled in future periods.
operating lease right-of-use asset :: The balance sheet value of the right to use leased property.
