## How to read the 2024–2025 numbers

Three accounting features make Illumina's 2024–2025 results hard to compare at face value.

**GRAIL is in some periods and not others.** GRAIL was the cancer-detection company Illumina bought in 2021. It stayed in Illumina's reported results until it was spun off on June 24, 2024. The spin-off was not treated as a "discontinued operation", so earlier periods were not restated to remove it. Illumina therefore reports "consolidated" figures, which include GRAIL until the spin-off, and "Core Illumina" figures, which exclude it. From the second half of 2024 the two are the same.

**GAAP and non-GAAP figures differ a lot.** GAAP results follow official accounting rules. Non-GAAP results are management's adjusted figures, which strip out items such as amortization (the gradual write-off of acquired intangible assets), impairments, legal swings and gains on investments. GAAP results in this period were distorted by very large one-time items:
- a $1.9B GRAIL impairment (a write-down of an asset's book value);
- a $481M net gain when the EU withdrew its fine;
- quarterly revaluations of the GRAIL contingent value rights (CVRs).

**Most figures come from filings.** Unless tagged otherwise, figures below come from 10-Qs (quarterly SEC filings) and 10-Ks (annual filings). Non-GAAP figures and all guidance come from the earnings slide decks [Q2 2025 slides, 2025-07-31; Q3 2025 slides, 2025-10-30; Q4 2025 slides, 2026-02-05].

## 2024: GRAIL exits, the EU fine reverses, buybacks restart

### Q1 2024: flat sales, wider loss

**Revenue.** Revenue was $1,076M, down 1% from $1,087M in Q1 2023. Core Illumina fell 2% to $1,056M. GRAIL grew 35% to $27M on sales of its Galleri cancer-screening blood test [Q1 2024 10-Q].

**Instruments.** Instrument revenue fell 28% to $114M. Management gave two reasons:
- The company "entered 2024 with a lower backlog of NovaSeq X instruments" after heavy launch pre-orders in 2023. NovaSeq X is the flagship high-throughput sequencer.
- Mid-throughput shipments were weaker as "capital and cash flow constraints continue to impact our customer's purchasing behavior."

**Consumables and services.** Consumables were flat at $762M:
- Sequencing consumables rose 1% to $691M.
- Microarray consumables fell 9% to $71M.

Service and other revenue rose 21% to $200M, helped by strategic partnerships.

**Regions.**

| Region | Q1'24 revenue | Change vs Q1'23 |
|---|---|---|
| Americas | $603M | −2% |
| Europe | $279M | +7% |
| Greater China | $78M | −14% |
| Asia-Pacific, Middle East & Africa | $116M | −3% |

**Margins.** GAAP gross margin rose 170 basis points to 62.0%; a basis point (bp) is 0.01 percentage point. Core gross margin reached 65.7%, up from 63.8%. Management credited a favorable consumables mix and savings on freight and productivity. These were partly offset by lower-margin partnership revenue and higher warranty and field-service costs.

**Expenses and loss.** The operating loss widened to $111M from $64M, and the net loss was $126M (−$0.79 per share).
- Core R&D fell 7% to $241M on lower headcount.
- Core SG&A rose 17% to $336M. Drivers were $33M of restructuring charges, a $17M loss on the GRAIL CVR liability, and $7M of interest on the EU fine.

**Cash and buybacks.** Operating cash flow was $77M, up from $10M. Management stated: "We do not intend to make any share repurchases during fiscal year 2024" [Q1 2024 10-Q]. That statement was later overtaken.

### Q2 2024: the GRAIL spin-off and a $1.9B write-down

**Revenue.** Revenue fell 5% to $1,112M, and Core fell 6% to $1,092M.
- Instruments dropped 39% to $120M.
- Consumables were flat at $807M.
- Greater China fell to $75M from $115M.

**Margins.** Core gross margin rose to 68.0% from 65.5% [Q2 2024 10-Q].

**The spin-off.** On June 24, 2024 Illumina distributed 85.5% of GRAIL to its shareholders, one GRAIL share for every six Illumina shares, and kept 14.5%.
- Illumina contributed $974M of "Disposal Funding", cash required by the EU to cover 2.5 years of GRAIL's operations.
- About $968M of cash left Illumina's balance sheet as a result.
- To fund this, Illumina drew a $750M 364-day term loan on June 20 at 6.7%.

**Impairments.** GRAIL's goodwill and in-process R&D were written down by $1,886M ($1,466M goodwill plus $420M IPR&D). The carrying value of GRAIL was cut to an estimated $580M.
- Analyst valuations of GRAIL had fallen from $3–4B over December 2023 to spring 2024, to $400–770M by May 2024.
- The IPR&D valuation used a 46.5% discount rate.

**Other GRAIL effects.**
- The retained GRAIL stake was booked at $397M but fell to $74M by quarter-end, a $328M unrealized loss.
- A $271M gain on remeasuring the GRAIL CVRs pushed Core SG&A down to just $60M.

**Results.** The net loss was $1,988M (−$12.48 per share). Equity fell to $1,436M from $5,745M at year-end 2023.

### Q3 2024: the EU fine reversal and refinancing

**Revenue.** Revenue fell 3% to $1,080M, all of it Core.
- Sequencing consumables rose 8% to $741M, driven by NovaSeq X.
- Sequencing instruments fell 42% to $104M.
- Greater China fell 24%, while Europe rose 12%.

**Margins.** Core gross margin hit 68.9%, up 410 bps.

**The EU fine reversal.** On September 3, 2024 the EU Court of Justice ruled that the European Commission had no jurisdiction over the GRAIL deal. On September 6 the Commission withdrew its €432M fine, which had been accrued at about $481M. Illumina booked a net $481M gain: $489M in operating expense, less an $8M currency loss.

**Results.** GAAP operating income was $741M and net income was $705M.

**Refinancing.** On September 9, 2024 Illumina issued $500M of 4.650% notes due 2026. It repaid the term loan ($761M including interest), recording a $5M loss on extinguishment.

**Buybacks restart.** In August 2024 the board authorized a new $1.5B buyback, which replaced the nearly exhausted 2020 program. Illumina bought 770K shares at an average of $127.71, for $99M.

### FY2024 close

**Revenue.** Revenue was $4,372M, down 3%:
- Core instruments fell 29% to $501M after the "first year of NovaSeq X shipments in 2023".
- Core consumables rose to $3,169M.
- Core services rose 6% to $662M.

**Regions.**

| Region | FY2024 revenue | Change vs FY2023 |
|---|---|---|
| Americas | $2,441M | down from $2,521M |
| Europe | $1,185M | up from $1,140M |
| Greater China | $308M | −20% |
| AMEA | $438M | down from $459M |

**Margins and profit.**
- GAAP gross margin was 65.4%, up 450 bps; the 10-K summary says 490, but the stated figures (60.9% to 65.4%) give 450.
- Core GAAP operating income was $1,473M, inflated by the fine reversal and $315M of CVR gains.
- The tax rate was −3.8%, because the goodwill impairment is not tax-deductible ($308M tax effect).

**Balance sheet at year-end.**
- Cash was $1,127M plus $93M of short-term investments.
- Debt was $2,000M of notes.
- Equity was $2,373M.

**Buybacks and backlog.** Full-year buybacks were 904K shares for $116M, and $1,384M of authorization remained. Backlog (firm orders not yet shipped) was $657M [FY2024 10-K].

**Outlook.** Management said it would focus "on returning to revenue growth" in 2025. Its stated goals are to accelerate growth and expand operating margins [FY2024 10-K].

## 2025: China, tariffs, then a clinical-led recovery

### Q1 2025: new headwinds and a $100M cost program

**China.** On February 4, 2025 China's Ministry of Commerce put Illumina on its "unreliable entities list". On March 4, 2025 Illumina was told it could no longer export sequencing instruments into China. Greater China had produced $308M in 2024.

**Tariffs.** In April 2025, management estimated new tariffs would cost about $85M a year. The largest piece was imports from its Singapore factory. It planned to "partially mitigate" this in 2025 and "more fully" in 2026 through supply-chain, cost and pricing actions.

**Cost program.** Management launched an incremental $100M cost-reduction program for 2025. It covered stock compensation, non-labor spending and headcount, and responded to the China loss and NIH funding uncertainty. The NIH (National Institutes of Health) is the main U.S. funder of academic research customers.

**Revenue.** Revenue was $1,041M, down 3% (Core down 1.4%):
- Sequencing consumables were $696M, up from $691M.
- Instruments were $112M.
- Service fell 7% to $161M on lower partnership revenue.
- Greater China fell 8% to $72M.

**Margins.** Core gross margin held at 65.6% against 65.7%. Management cited "lower product margins, primarily due to reduced pricing" and higher field-service cost.

**Results.**
- GAAP operating income was $164M, against a loss of $111M a year earlier.
- Net income was $131M ($0.82 per share).
- Operating cash flow was $240M and free cash flow $208M.
- Buybacks were 1.73M shares at $115.74, for $200M.

The 10-Q itself gave no numerical full-year guidance.

### Q2 2025: guidance revised and SomaLogic announced

**Revenue.** Revenue was $1,059M, down 3% on a Core basis.
- Sequencing instruments fell 18% to $96M.
- Consumables were flat at $740M.

**Regions.** Europe grew 7% to $310M. The Americas fell to $586M, and Greater China fell 15% to $63M.

**Margins.** Non-GAAP gross margin was 69.4%, flat year on year. GAAP gross margin was dragged down by three items:
- a $23M intangible impairment;
- tariff-related freight and duties;
- field-service costs.

**Profit.** Non-GAAP operating margin was 23.8%, up 160 bps, and non-GAAP EPS was $1.19 (+9%). GAAP net income was $235M, including about $97–102M of strategic-investment gains.

**Cash and buybacks.**
- Free cash flow was $204M.
- Illumina bought back 4.49M shares at $84.66; the 10-Q gives $384M, the slides $380M.
- Gross leverage, debt to trailing earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortization (EBITDA), was about 1.7x.

**SomaLogic.** On June 22, 2025 Illumina agreed to buy SomaLogic, a proteomics (protein-measurement) business, from Standard BioTools.
- Terms: $350M cash, up to $75M in revenue milestones, plus royalties.
- If the deal failed on regulatory grounds, Illumina would owe a $14.5M termination fee.
- Management said SomaLogic would turn profitable on a non-GAAP basis in 2027 and match Illumina's margins in 2028.

**DOJ settlement.** On July 22, 2025 Illumina settled a Department of Justice False Claims Act probe about cybersecurity representations for $9.8M, without admitting liability.

### Q3 2025: profit guidance raised, revenue range trimmed

**Revenue.** Revenue was $1,084M, flat; ex-China revenue grew about 2% in constant currency (CC, growth with exchange-rate effects removed).
- Greater China fell 31% to $52M.
- Ex-China clinical consumables grew 12%, while research and applied consumables fell 8%.
- Illumina said it hit its NovaSeq X transition targets: over 75% of high-throughput gigabases shipped and over 50% of high-throughput revenue.

**Profit.** Non-GAAP gross margin fell 130 bps to 69.2%, mainly because of tariffs. Lower costs still lifted non-GAAP operating margin to 24.5% and EPS 18% to $1.34.
- GAAP tax was 31.8%. The rate included a $42M valuation allowance against foreign tax credits, triggered by the July 4, 2025 U.S. tax law.

**Cash and buybacks.** Free cash flow was $253M. Buybacks were 1.24M shares at $97.10, about $120M.

**China export relief.** On November 5, 2025 China allowed instrument exports again, case by case, effective November 10.

### Q4 2025 and FY2025

**Q4 revenue.** Q4 revenue was $1,159M, up 4% CC and 5% reported; ex-China grew 7% CC.
- Sequencing consumables rose 8% to $755M; ex-China they rose 11%.
- Clinical consumables grew 20%.
- Research and applied flattened after falling all year.
- Over 100 NovaSeq X units were placed.

**Q4 profit.** Non-GAAP operating margin was 23.7%, up 400 bps, and EPS $1.35 (+42%). GAAP EPS of $2.16 included a $192M investment gain.

**FY2025 revenue.** Revenue was $4,343M:
- Ex-China revenue grew 2% to $4,100M.
- Greater China fell 21% to $243M; management attributed $65M of the decline to the entity listing.
- Europe rose 7% to $1,264M.

**FY2025 profit.** Core GAAP gross margin fell 100 bps to 66.1% because of tariffs and the impairment. Operating income was $807M and net income $850M, helped by $328M of investment gains, mostly on the GRAIL stake. The tax rate was 21.7%.

**FY2025 cash and balance sheet.**
- Operating cash flow was $1,079M and free cash flow $931M.
- Backlog rose 12% to $738M.
- Year-end cash plus short-term investments were about $1.63B, against $2.0B of debt; gross leverage was about 1.6x.

**FY2025 buybacks.** Illumina bought 7.79M shares at an average of $95.23, for $742M, leaving $643M of authorization. Diluted share count fell to 156M from 159M in 2024 and about 152.8M outstanding by October 2025.

## Cash flow, debt and capital structure

**Operating cash flow.** It rose from $837M in 2024 to $1,079M in 2025.
- Capex: $128–148M, about 3.4% of revenue.
- Restructuring since the program began in Q2 2023 totaled $261M by end-2025.

**Debt.** Illumina carries $2.0B of notes in four $500M tranches:
- 4.650% due September 9, 2026;
- 5.750% due December 13, 2027;
- 4.750% due December 12, 2030, issued November 25, 2025 to refinance the 5.800% notes repaid on December 12, 2025;
- 2.550% due March 23, 2031.

Interest expense was about $100M in both 2024 and 2025. The $750M revolving credit line (maturing January 4, 2028) was never drawn. Its covenant caps debt at 3.5x adjusted EBITDA.

**Taxes.** Singapore tax holidays saved $37M in 2025 and now run to 2035.

## GRAIL residual items

**CVR liability.** Illumina still owes CVR holders 2.5% of GRAIL's first $1B of yearly revenue and 9% above that, through August 2033. The fair value of that liability fell steadily:

| Q2 2025 | $40M |
| Q3 2025 | $43M |

**Retained GRAIL stake.** The stake fell to $70M by September 2024 and then recovered as GRAIL's share price rose. Illumina sold $103M of it in 2025 and about $104M more by February 11, 2026.

## Source inconsistencies to note

- The Q1 2025 10-Q notes describe the gross margin change as "+40 bps". The stated figures, 62.0% to 65.6%, imply +360 bps.
- The Q3 2025 10-Q notes cite a "$350 million" tariff impact. This is probably an extraction error that confused it with the SomaLogic price; the explicit estimate elsewhere is about $85M.
- FY2024 core consumables appear as $3,169M in the FY2024 10-K and $3,155M in the FY2025 10-K.
- The full-year 2025 foreign-tax-credit valuation allowance is $62M in the 10-K, against $42M recorded in Q3.
