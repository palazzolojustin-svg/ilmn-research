## What "consensus" means and what this section draws on

**Consensus** is the average of the forecasts published by **sell-side analysts**, the bank and brokerage researchers who cover a stock and publish estimates for clients. Investors treat it as the market's baseline expectation. A company "beats" or "misses" depending on how its results compare with consensus, and the share price often reacts more to that gap than to the raw results.

This section rests on three Bloomberg Professional "Company Financials" printouts for Illumina (ILMN US Equity). All three were generated on 2026-10-10 with the estimate source set to "Consensus" and no extra analytics:

- **Annual sheet** [BBG Annuals, 2026-10-10]: reported actuals for fiscal 2023–2025 and consensus for fiscal 2026–2032.
- **Quarterly sheet 1** [BBG Quarterlies, 2026-10-10]: six reported quarters (2025Q1–2026Q2) and four estimated quarters (2026Q3–2027Q2).
- **Quarterly sheet 2** [BBG Quarterlies-2, 2026-10-10]: ten estimated quarters, 2026Q3–2028Q4. Where it overlaps sheet 1 (2026Q3–2027Q2), the figures are identical.

Figures are in millions of US dollars, per-share figures in dollars and share counts in millions, unless stated otherwise. No sheet names an analyst or bank, gives ratings or price targets, explains estimate changes, or says how many analysts contributed.

Thin coverage matters. Each line is an average of whichever analysts chose to model it, so lines often do not tie to each other, and far-out years (especially 2031–2032) or obscure lines may reflect only one or two forecasters. Anything labelled "our arithmetic" is calculated from the sheets' numbers, not stated by Bloomberg or any analyst.

### Fiscal calendar

Illumina's fiscal year is a 52- or 53-week year ending near December 31.

- **Reported years:** FY2023 ended 12/31/2023, FY2024 12/29/2024 and FY2025 12/28/2025.
- **Estimated years:** FY2026 ends 01/03/2027, FY2027 01/02/2028, FY2028 12/31/2028, FY2029 12/30/2029, FY2030 12/29/2030, FY2031 12/28/2031 and FY2032 01/02/2033.
- **Quarter ends:** 03/30/2025, 06/29/2025, 09/28/2025, 12/28/2025, 03/29/2026 and 06/28/2026 are reported. 10/04/2026 (2026Q3), 04/04/2027, 07/04/2027, 10/03/2027, 04/02/2028, 07/02/2028 and 10/01/2028 are estimated.

By date, 2026Q3 (06/28 to 10/04) spans 14 weeks and 2026Q4 (10/04 to 01/03) spans 13, making FY2026 a 53-week year. The extra week may partly explain strong second-half 2026 estimates; this is our inference from the dates, not a reason the sheet gives.

### Adjusted versus GAAP

**GAAP** results follow standard US accounting rules. **Adjusted** (non-GAAP) results chiefly exclude **stock-based compensation (SBC)**, the non-cash cost of paying employees in shares, and **amortization of acquired intangibles**, the gradual write-off of the purchase value of acquired technology and customer relationships. Analysts and Illumina's guidance focus on adjusted figures, so "consensus EPS" normally means adjusted **EPS** (earnings per share: profit divided by shares).

### Revenue lines

- **Consumables** are the reagent kits, flow cells and chips customers must buy for every sequencing or microarray run. They recur and make up about three-quarters of sales.
- **Instruments** are the machines, sold once and lumpy quarter to quarter.
- **Service & Other** covers service contracts, genotyping services and similar revenue.
- **Sequencing** reads DNA letters directly. **Microarray** is an older, cheaper chip technology that tests for known genetic variants.
- **GRAIL** was Illumina's cancer-detection blood-test business. It appears only for 2023 and 2024.

## Annual consensus: the big picture

### Headline expectations

Consensus expects Illumina to return to growth after two years of shrinking sales and to keep widening margins [BBG Annuals, 2026-10-10]. Revenue fell 2.9% in 2024 and 0.7% in 2025; consensus has it up 6.5% in 2026 to $4,624.8M and rising every year to $7,370.5M in 2032. Adjusted **operating margin** (operating profit as a share of revenue) jumped from 5.5% in 2023 to 23.1% in 2025 and is forecast to reach 30.7% by 2032. Adjusted EPS rose from $0.86 (2023) to $4.84 (2025); consensus has $5.36 for 2026 and $12.30 for 2032.

Three points stand out. First, growth sits at 6–8% through 2030, then jumps to about 10% in 2031–2032, faster than any earlier year; that probably reflects a handful of long-range models rather than a broad view. Second, EPS compounds at roughly 11–19% a year against 6–10% revenue growth, thanks to **operating leverage** (costs growing slower than sales, so each extra sales dollar adds proportionally more profit). Third, the big margin step already happened in 2023–2025; consensus adds only about 1–1.5 points a year from here.

### Data-quality warnings on the annual sheet

**2032 looks broken**, likely because very few analysts model it. Diluted shares rise to 164.85M from 149.41M despite buybacks; additional paid-in capital falls from $9.13B to $7.04B; cash jumps $3.9B while net change in cash is only $1.88B; product revenue grows 19.7% and cost of products sold 23.3%; and GAAP net income ($2,064M) exceeds adjusted ($2,028M), at a 28.0% GAAP net margin.

**Other inconsistencies.** Cash-flow-statement cash for estimate years does not match balance sheet cash (2026E: $1,482M versus $1,527M). Net debt per share for 2026E (−$1.60) has the wrong sign against $403M of net debt. Working capital for 2030E ($1,387M) dips between neighbours of $2,331M and $6,547M. Total assets are printed without decimal points, and operating cash flow is not the sum of the lines shown above it.

## How the latest actuals and estimates fit together

The quarterly and annual consensus agree for 2026 but diverge later (all our arithmetic).

**2026 ties out.** First-half 2026 revenue of $2,250M (7.1% above first-half 2025's $2,100M) plus the consensus second half of $2,374M gives $4,624M, matching the annual $4,624.8M. Adjusted EPS ($2.46 reported plus $2.90 expected = $5.36) and adjusted operating income ($499M plus $589M ≈ $1,088M) also match. The implied second-half growth of about 5.8% over second-half 2025's $2,243M is slower than the first half's 7.1%, despite Q3's extra week.

**Later years diverge.** The 2027 quarters sum to $4,899M revenue and $6.08 EPS, slightly below the annual $4,918M and $6.11; the 2028 quarters sum to $5,296M and $7.34, above the annual $5,263M and $7.01. Buybacks don't reconcile: 2026 gives $551M ($370M reported plus $181M expected) against $420M annually, 2027 $251M against $313M, and 2028 $334M against $501M. 2026 FCF of $413M reported plus $619M expected (about $1,032M) compares with the annual $997M. These gaps reflect different analysts feeding the two series.

### Quarterly data-quality warnings

**Reported quarters:**

- 2026Q1 adjusted pre-tax income is printed as $131M, but operating plus other income implies about $254M; $131M equals 2025Q1 GAAP net income, suggesting a data error.
- 2026Q2 adjusted net income of $200.43M does not match pre-tax income of $260M less $52M tax ($208M).
- 2025Q1 cost of revenue ($358M) exceeds products plus services ($341M).
- 2025 depreciation is inconsistent across quarters, and 2025Q1 returns on equity and assets are negative despite profits.
- SBC and "other assets" are printed unscaled (for example, 7300 for about $73M).

**Estimate quarters:**

- Goodwill is printed as "128400" throughout, most likely $1,284M.
- Cash-plus-short-term-investments (for example, $846.7M in 2026Q3) is impossibly below cash alone, and cash-flow-statement ending cash matches neither balance sheet cash nor the next quarter's opening cash.
- Net debt swings irregularly, from $516.5M (2027Q3) to net cash of $539M (2027Q4), then $1,163M in every 2028 quarter; net debt per share turns negative while net debt stays positive.
- Book value per share jumps around ($12.56, $19.66, $26.91, $34.02), and return on equity falls from 47.5–48.3% to about 19.7–19.9%.
- The 2026Q3 FCF margin of 38.98% stands out against a 19–25% range, and its FCF of $335M exceeds operating cash flow minus capex ($274.7M).
- Non-current liabilities are a placeholder-like constant $2,525M; GAAP other income swings between $0.83M and $19.11M against a steady $14–16M adjusted; and assets and liabilities tie out only approximately.

Treat estimated balance sheet and ratio lines as rough. The income statement lines are far more reliable.
